**The Pickup** is Act 1's most branching job. Dex sends you to get back the **Flathead**, the Militech spider-bot **Maelstrom** stole from a convoy. The gang, led by **Royce**, holds it at the **All Foods Factory** (Talbot Place, Northside, Watson). Beforehand you can contact **Meredith Stout**, a Militech internal affairs agent, who offers you a credchip to pay Maelstrom with… and it's rigged. What you do here decides who walks out of the factory alive, who waits for you at the gate, which jobs and weapons open up, and how Maelstrom greets you in **Act 2** (in **Second Conflict**, part of Kerry's chain). IGN recommends **level 3**. You can do it before or after **The Information**; both are needed for **The Heist**.

> **Order:** (optional) call Meredith Stout and meet her in the storm channel under the overpass → meet Jackie at All Foods → use the intercom and talk to Dum Dum → reach the main production floor → ride the elevator up → sit and get Jackie to sit → talk to Royce and pay (or fight) → leave by one of three paths → follow Jackie and talk to him → call Dex.

> **Get ready:** decide **before you go in** what you want out of the factory (see "Which path to pick"), because calling Meredith or not already changes the possible outcomes. **Save manually** before calling Meredith, before hacking her chip, and before going through the All Foods gate. The peaceful deal needs **€$10,000 of your own**. If you plan to fight, bring **grenades** (**EMP** works very well on the Militech mechs) and quickhacks such as **Reboot Optics**, **Overheat**, **Weapon Glitch** or **Short Circuit** for Royce.

## Which path to pick

The wiki numbers the outcomes as "paths". Path 1 is just the instant death (taking too long to answer Royce). The other three:

- **Path 2, peaceful deal:** you **don't** meet Meredith, threaten nobody, get Jackie to sit and pay Royce **€$10,000 of your own**. It is the **only** way to unlock the **Maelstrom Weapon Vendor** and it keeps **Dum Dum** alive (in Act 2 he carries his iconic gun, **Doom Doom**). You miss out on looting the factory.
- **Path 3, fight Militech:** you meet Meredith, there is no hostility, and you pay with the cleaned chip, with the chip while warning Royce (**Corpo only**), or with your €$10,000. Militech storms the factory and you fight alongside Maelstrom. **Anthony Gilchrist** waits at the end. A **Nomad** V can earn a **Militech gear crate** here.
- **Path 4, fight Maelstrom:** any hostility (drawing a weapon, touching the cameras or turrets, not sitting, paying with the infected chip without a warning, or taking down or killing Royce in the meeting). You fight the gang and **Royce** in his exosuit. If you met Meredith, she waits at the gate (unless you take Royce down with the Body option): that is the road to **Venus in Furs**.

**By goal:**

- **Chaos (Royce's iconic pistol):** Royce must die. Path 4, or path 3 letting Royce fight Militech alone.
- **Maelstrom Weapon Vendor:** path 2 only.
- **Doom Doom (Dum Dum's iconic revolver, in Second Conflict):** Royce must stay alive. Path 2 (guaranteed) or path 3 if Royce survives the Militech assault. It clashes with Chaos: you can't have both.
- **Venus in Furs (Meredith):** take the chip and turn Maelstrom hostile after meeting Dum Dum in person, **without** using the Body takedown on Royce.
- **Brick:** paths 3 and 4 only, and he only pays off if Royce dies (see "Brick and Act 2").
- **Militech crate (Nomad only):** read Gilchrist's message inside the factory and bring it up in the conversation at the gate.

## Meredith Stout (optional)

1. **Call Meredith Stout** (Dex gave you her number) and **meet her** in the **storm channel under the overpass**, near All Foods. If you arrive first, wait for her.
2. As you approach, one of her bodyguards pins you and **jacks into your personal link** for a lie-detector interrogation. **Anthony Gilchrist** is there, her suspect for leaking the convoy.
3. **Talk to her.** You propose a deal: information in exchange for the Flathead. Meredith agrees if you pay Maelstrom with a **Militech credchip** (the **Militech Datashard**), which secretly carries a **daemon** (malware).
4. **Accept or refuse the chip.**

**Lifepath lines:**

- **Corpo:** you can tell her she is in no position to negotiate and that **you know the chip is spiked**. Say it **before taking it** and the optional objective **"Remove the virus from the chip"** appears, along with the option to **warn Royce** in the meeting.
- **Nomad:** you can offer to find the mole and patch up the convoys' security. Meredith isn't interested.

**Careful:** per the wiki, **just meeting Meredith** (deal or not) already rules out path 2: if you then pay with your own money, Militech storms the factory (path 3). PowerPyx says the opposite: refuse the chip and the ending is peaceful. Sources disagree; if you want the Maelstrom vendor, **don't call Meredith**.

## Hacking the Militech chip

If you took the chip, you can hack it from the **Shards** menu with **Breach Protocol**. **Save first.**

- **Copy the malware** → you get the **Synapse Burnout** quickhack.
- **Neutralize the malware** → the chip is clean and you collect **€$10,000** later: it shows up in the Backpack as the **Hacked Militech Datashard**; use it there to receive the money.
- **Both at once** is possible. IGN's sequence: solve the **bottom** code first (**BD BD FF 55**), then the **top** one (**55 1C E9**), that is **BD BD FF 55 1C E9** in a single attempt.

**Sources disagree on the details:** the wiki's chip page says the copy only works if you do it **before** neutralizing, and that the €$10,000 only pays out if you hack it **after** The Pickup is completed; IGN isn't sure you get to keep the money; and game8 says copying leaves the malware in and leads to a Maelstrom fight. Safe advice: save, do **the copy and the neutralize in the same Breach**, make sure the chip is clean before paying, and once the job is done, check the Backpack for the **Hacked Militech Datashard**.

## All Foods: the way in

1. **Meet Jackie** outside All Foods (if you arrive first, wait for him). A Nomad V can admire Jackie's ARCH Nazaré bike: dialogue only.
2. **Talk to Jackie**, **go to the All Foods gate** and **use the intercom**. **Talk to Dum Dum**: he lets you in when you say you're there for the Flathead.
3. **Get to the main production floor.**

**Don't touch the security.** On the way there are **Militech mines** on the stairs and in a doorway, plus **turrets and cameras**, all inactive. **Shooting or disabling them turns Maelstrom hostile** (path 4). The one exception: **turning off the cameras from the catwalk computer** on the main production floor doesn't alert them.

**Loot on the way in:** a crate with **uncommon components** and a **shard** behind the shelves on the right, right at the entrance. In the lobby, before the elevator, you can grab **grenades and ammo**.

If you start fighting before the elevator, the objectives switch to **defeat Maelstrom**, ride the elevator with Jackie and head to Royce's office (path 4).

## Dum Dum, the couch and Royce

1. **Get in the elevator** with Jackie, **wait** and **talk to the Maelstromers.**
2. **Sit on the couch.** Dum Dum demands Jackie sit too, and Jackie refuses: **tell Jackie to sit**. If you don't, or if you don't sit yourself, Maelstrom turns hostile (path 4).
3. Dum Dum offers you an **inhaler**: it's **Black Lace**. Taking it or refusing it doesn't matter. A Streetkid V recognizes it.
4. **Talk to the Maelstromers**, **take the Flathead** when they show it, and talk again.
5. **Royce comes out.** Per PowerPyx, there is an **autosave** just before.

**The conversation with Royce is timed.**

- When he asks **how you're going to pay**, **don't let the timer run out**: Royce shoots you in the head and you die ("path 1"). Reload and answer.
- A key line, also timed, is **"Fuck Brick. Let's cut a new deal"**.
- Then come the payment options: **your €$10,000**, **the Militech chip** (and, if you're Corpo and knew about the virus, **pay with the chip while warning Royce**), or break the deal with **[Draw weapon]** or **[Take down Royce]**.
- **[Take down Royce]** is a **Body** check: PowerPyx and IGN list it as Body 6 and a game8 commenter reports 12; the exact value in 2.5 is not confirmed.
- If you pay with the infected chip without a warning, **Royce scans it and hides**: the fight begins.

## Path 2 — Peaceful deal

**Conditions (all):** you never met Meredith; you never pointed a weapon at or attacked anyone from Maelstrom; you told Jackie to sit; you paid **€$10,000 of your own**.

1. **Take the elevator down with Dum Dum**, **follow him** and **talk to him.**
2. **Leave the All Foods premises.**

**Result:** Dum Dum escorts you out. **A Streetkid V gets a Black Lace from Dum Dum** even if they refused earlier. It is the **only** way to unlock the **Maelstrom Weapon Vendor** at All Foods after the job, and it guarantees **Dum Dum stays alive**: in **Second Conflict** (Act 2) he carries his iconic gun **Doom Doom**, which you can take from him. IGN doesn't recommend it unless you want to stay friendly with everyone, because **you miss out on looting the factory**.

## Path 3 — Fight Militech (with Maelstrom)

**Conditions (all):** you met Meredith (chip or not); no hostility and Jackie seated; you paid with **the chip after removing the malware**, with **the chip while warning Royce** (Corpo only), or with **€$10,000 of your own**.

1. After the payment, **Militech storms the factory**. Dum Dum leads you.
2. **Pass through the maintenance tunnel** and **turn on the production line** to clear the way.
3. Optional: **free Brick** (see below). In this path you never see him again.
4. **Defeat the Militech agents.** In the last room you fight **alongside Royce** against **Militech mechs**: IGN says two and PowerPyx says three. Use **grenades, especially EMP**; the mechs can be **hacked**, and shooting the exposed parts helps. PowerPyx advises **letting Royce handle the mechs** while you clear the agents.
5. **Follow Dum Dum**, **escape from All Foods**, leave the premises and **talk to Anthony Gilchrist** at the gate.

**Chaos in path 3:** it only drops if **Royce dies in the attack**. Let him fight Militech alone and loot his body if he falls.

**Gilchrist:** he tells you you're even for saving each other's lives. Later he **has Meredith killed**; her body turns up off a pier in north Watson, between the Ebunike Docks and Oil Fields fast travel points.

**Nomad only — the Militech crate:** in the **red-lit side room** of the **first big hall** after the negotiation room there is a computer with the message **"Transports - LOA"**, from Gilchrist to Maelstrom. Anyone can read it, but **only a Nomad V understands what it means**. Confront Gilchrist with it at the gate and he promises an incentive to keep quiet: **a crate of Militech gear in your apartment in Act 2**.

**Loot in this path:** a box with **€$1,000** in the room after the ladder (IGN) and the **Defender** LMG on a crate in the last room.

## Path 4 — Fight Maelstrom (with Militech)

**Triggered by any of these** (and what happens with Royce at the end):

- **Pointing a weapon at or attacking Maelstrom** → Royce is a strong boss; killing him pays **€$3,000**.
- **Tampering with cameras or turrets** (except via the catwalk computer) → strong boss, **€$3,000**.
- **Not sitting, or not telling Jackie to sit** → strong boss, **€$3,000**.
- **Paying with the infected chip without a warning** → Royce shows up **weakened**; killing him pays **€$3,000**.
- **Taking down or killing Royce during the conversation** → no boss fight and no extra money. Per PowerPyx, if you kill him there, only **three regular Maelstromers** wait in the last room.

**The route:**

1. **Defeat the Maelstromers** and **talk to Jackie.**
2. If the fight started **before Dum Dum showed you the Flathead**, **find the Flathead's case** and **open it**.
3. **Take the Flathead**, **pass through the maintenance tunnel** and **turn on the production line**.
4. Optional: **free Brick** (see below). Worth it if Royce is going to die.
5. **Royce:** you can **sneak past him** (optional objective) or, if spotted, **defeat Royce and his crew** (see "Boss — Royce").
6. If you met Meredith: **leave the premises** and **talk to Meredith Stout** (or Gilchrist, see below).

**Chaos** drops from Royce in the negotiation room (if you kill him there) or after the boss fight. After this path, **Maelstrom ambushes you while you drive around Watson**.

## Brick and Act 2 (paths 3 and 4)

**Brick**, Maelstrom's former leader, is locked in a cell in the **second big combat room**, between two raised sub-rooms with glass windows.

1. **The door:** use the **Remote Deactivation** quickhack on the terminal, or read the email **"only you guys know"** on the laptop in the room to the right: the code is **9691**.
2. **The trap:** there is a **laser mine** inside. Disarm it with the **detonator** on the table or box by the door (the **second option**), or disarm it by hand inside the cell.
3. **Careful:** **using the detonator to set it off kills Brick** and pays **€$5,000** (his NCPD bounty).
4. If you free him, tell him **"You owe us"**.

**Consequences in Second Conflict (Kerry's chain, Act 2):**

- **Royce alive:** Dum Dum greets you at the Totentanz; you meet Royce and steal the data.
- **Royce dead and Brick freed:** Patricia leads you to Brick, who lets you leave peacefully with Bes Isis.
- **Royce dead and Brick not freed:** Patricia leads, Bes is locked up and you have to fight your way out.
- Brick's page says that, if freed, he retakes Maelstrom even if Royce survived; Second Conflict's conditions say otherwise. Sources disagree on that detail.
- Unless The Pickup was peaceful, the Maelstrom leader orders you attacked at the Totentanz.
- **Doom Doom** only comes from Dum Dum in Second Conflict: if you side with Militech, or side with Maelstrom but let Royce die, **Dum Dum won't appear**.

## At the gate: Meredith or Gilchrist?

Someone from Militech only waits at the gate **if you met Meredith**.

- **Meredith waits** if you met her (deal or not) **and** Maelstrom turned hostile **before you got the Flathead**.
  - If you **took the deal** and Maelstrom turned hostile **after meeting Dum Dum in person** (not sitting, infected chip, shooting Royce), Meredith is **impressed** and suggests working together again. It is the **only** way to unlock **Venus in Furs** (Act 2, No-Tell Motel; reward: Sir John Phallustiff).
  - If you **refused the deal** and fought anyway, or fought **before meeting Dum Dum**, Meredith is **colder**. The wiki doesn't confirm whether Venus in Furs still unlocks that way.
  - In the conversation, PowerPyx recommends answering **"Yeah, maybe."** so she contacts you later. With the Nomad intel, **"I know the mole's identity"** gets you the **Militech crate**.
  - Either way, Meredith ends up having Gilchrist killed.
- **Gilchrist waits** in path 3 and whenever Meredith's conditions aren't met. This **closes off Venus in Furs**.
- **The Body takedown is a trap:** per the wiki (probably a bug), **knocking Royce out with the Body option makes Gilchrist appear and Meredith die**; shooting him through the dialogue option doesn't. A game8 commenter confirms it, but PowerPyx calls [Take down Royce] "the best option" for the Meredith romance. Sources disagree: **if you want Meredith, shoot Royce, don't use the takedown**.
- PowerPyx sums it up as: if you met Meredith, anything other than handing Royce the untouched chip ends with Gilchrist; the wiki, on the other hand, says not sitting or shooting Royce also brings Meredith.
- Per IGN, if you run too far from the objective, Gilchrist calls instead of appearing (unconfirmed).

## Boss — Royce (Militech Centaur exosuit)

Path 4 only, if you didn't kill or take him down in the meeting. Royce is in the **last room** with one more Maelstromer, with a **shield** and a **cannon**.

1. **You enter in stealth:** from the raised platform, **tag both**. You can **sneak straight to the exit** and skip the fight, but **you get no Chaos**. If you're spotted, **the doors lock**.
2. **Weak point: the battery on his back, left side.** Shoot it or grenade it, or destroy it with a **stealth takedown**, which also deals **25%** damage. A second stealth takedown can finish him.
3. **Once the battery is gone he stops raising the shield.**
4. **Use the crates as cover:** his attacks come at waist height and hit the cover.
5. **Useful quickhacks:** **Reboot Optics** or **Disable Optics** to break his tracking, **Overheat**, **Weapon Glitch** (staggers him) and **Short Circuit**.
6. If you paid with the infected chip, he arrives **weakened**.

Some guides mention the "Bladerunner" perk and Technical Ability 9: that is a pre-2.0 perk and doesn't exist in that form in 2.5.

**Loot:** **Chaos** (Iconic Kenshin tech pistol: each reload randomizes its damage type, crit chance and status effect), the **Spiked Maelstrom Short-Sleeve Jacket** (Epic per IGN) and **€$3,000** for the kill.

## Factory loot

- **Safe with a blue Smart sniper rifle:** go to the far-left room and through the second door into **Royce's office**, before the ladder to the maintenance tunnel (PowerPyx).
- **Uncommon components and a shard** behind the shelves at the entrance; **grenades and ammo** in the lobby.
- **€$1,000** in a box in the room after the ladder (path 3, IGN).
- The **Defender** LMG on a crate in the last room (IGN).
- A **Drop Point** around the corner as you leave.

**A known bug (game8, unconfirmed):** if Jackie stays inside, an operative can block the "Talk to Jackie" step. Wait for Jackie to follow you out.

## Choices

- **Calling Meredith** → opens the chip (Synapse Burnout, €$10,000), Venus in Furs and the Nomad crate; per the wiki it **rules out the peaceful deal** (PowerPyx disagrees).
- **Taking the chip** → required for Venus in Furs. **Paying with it uncleaned and unannounced** → path 4 with a weakened Royce.
- **Hacking the chip** → **copy** = Synapse Burnout; **neutralize** = €$10,000 later; you can do both (order and payout disputed, see above).
- **Not sitting, not getting Jackie to sit, drawing a weapon or touching cameras/turrets** → path 4 with a strong Royce.
- **Taking too long to answer Royce** → you die. Reload.
- **Paying €$10,000 of your own without having met Meredith** → path 2: Maelstrom Weapon Vendor, Dum Dum alive (Doom Doom in Second Conflict), an extra Black Lace for Streetkid. **Permanent:** this is the only chance.
- **Paying clean after meeting Meredith** → path 3: Militech assault, Gilchrist at the gate, Meredith dead later.
- **[Take down Royce] (Body)** → no boss and no extra money, but Gilchrist at the gate and Meredith dead (probable bug). **[Draw weapon]/shooting him** → path 4 while keeping Meredith.
- **Letting Royce die or killing him** → **Chaos**. If you miss it, **Herold Lowe** (Phantom Liberty, EBM Petrochem Stadium) sells it later, as long as its acquisition conditions were met.
- **Brick: free him or kill him with the detonator** → €$5,000 now, or a Second Conflict without a fight (if Royce died). **Permanent.**
- **Path 4** → Maelstrom ambushes you while driving in Watson.

> **Don't leave without:** **Chaos** from Royce's body (if he died), the **Spiked Maelstrom Short-Sleeve Jacket**, the **safe with the Smart rifle** in Royce's office, the **Defender** in the last room, the **€$1,000** box (path 3), the shard and components at the entrance, **freeing Brick** if Royce fell, reading **"Transports - LOA"** if you're Nomad and, if you have the chip, the **Hacked Militech Datashard** in your Backpack. And don't let the timer run out with Royce.

## Rewards

- **1,547 XP** and **1,727 Street Cred**, per the wiki.
- **€$3,000 to €$13,000** depending on the path (Royce killed €$3,000, chip neutralized €$10,000, Brick killed €$5,000).
- **Chaos** (if Royce dies), **Black Lace** (Streetkid), **Spiked Maelstrom Short-Sleeve Jacket**, **Synapse Burnout** (if you copy the chip).
- When you call Dex: if you haven't done **The Information** yet, he sends you to **Lizzie's**; if you have, to the **Afterlife** for **The Heist**.
