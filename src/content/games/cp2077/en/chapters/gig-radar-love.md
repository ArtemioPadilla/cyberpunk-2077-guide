As you approach the **Sunshine Motel** (the gig calls it the Lone Star Motel), by Interstate 9 in **Rocky Ridge**, **Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, calls you. It's from her last set, with **Goodbye, Night City**: it opens after **MIA** and **Sparring Partner**, in **Act 2**. **Militech** has turned the deserted motel into a heavily guarded outpost where it's testing a **mobile radar** that scans vehicles over a huge radius. Dakota wants the prototype so the **Aldecaldos** can reverse-engineer a jammer and their transports can cross unseen again.

> **Order:** get inside the Militech base → find the radar van P9D/2.161 → steal it → deliver it to Dakota's garage → leave the garage.

## How to do it

The wiki has no detailed walkthrough beyond the opening call. Expect a heavily guarded **Militech** outpost: go in quietly, find the **P9D/2.161** van, get in and drive out. Take it to Dakota's **DaKota** garage and get out to close the gig.

## Rewards

€$21,780, 1,000 XP, 1,710 Street Cred and, optionally, the **Militech heavy tactical vest**. With **Goodbye, Night City**, it completes Dakota's gigs and opens **Every Grain of Sand**: the **Thorton Mackinaw "Saguaro"** pickup waits for you in the Megabuilding H10 garage.
