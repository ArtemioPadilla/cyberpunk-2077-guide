**Sinnerman** opens the **Joshua Stephenson** chain, one of the strangest stories in Night City. The fixer **Wakako Okada** gives it to you; she calls some time after you capture Anders Hellman in **Life During Wartime**, so it's an **Act 2** job. Her client, **Bill Jablonsky**, wants to avenge the murder of his wife, Caroline, and will pay well as long as everything goes exactly to plan; he also wants to be there when the killer is taken out. It doesn't matter whether you accept the gig on the call: the objective to meet Bill shows up either way. The meeting is at the **Dewdrop Inn**, on the corner of Sixth and Brandon in **Arroyo** (Santo Domingo). When you get there, **Gig: Serious Side Effects** is added automatically if you hadn't started it.

> **Order:** talk to Wakako → sit on the bench and wait for Bill → talk to him → get behind the wheel of his truck → tail the NCPD SUV → stop at the underpass → follow Bill → talk to Joshua or kill him.

## The tail

Sit on the bench and wait for Bill. He tells you the target is **Joshua Stephenson**, a murderer who killed his wife and who, despite his conviction, is getting out of Night City Prison thanks to a corporation's involvement. Get in the **driver's seat** of his truck. When the **NCPD SUV** carrying Joshua drives past, **follow it** without losing it. You can't shoot it: the game treats it as a friendly vehicle.

If you lose it, Bill calls off the deal, kicks you out of the truck and later texts you, furious, that **you won't be paid anything**. The job ends there.

If you follow it to an **underpass**, the SUV slams into a parked semi-truck. Bill gets out, draws his pistol and rushes at Joshua, but NCPD Lieutenant **Vasquez** shoots him first.

## Choices

- **Talk to Joshua** → Joshua orders Vasquez not to shoot you (he threatens to kill his deal with the corporation) and invites you to come along.
  - **Go with him** → you get in the NCPD SUV and the job rolls straight into **There Is A Light That Never Goes Out**.
  - **Don't go** → they leave without you and the job ends.
- **Kill Joshua** → call Wakako and she pays **€$9,300**. Only Joshua has to die; you don't need to kill Rachel Casich or Vasquez. **It's permanent:** you lose **There Is A Light That Never Goes Out** and **They Won't Go When I Go**. Careful: depending on difficulty and your level, Vasquez can be a very tough opponent, and since Update 2.0 fighting him gives you a **one-star NCPD wanted level**.

## Rewards

- **€$9,300** if you kill Joshua and call Wakako.
- **Nothing** if you hear him out (the pay comes in the next job) or if you lose the SUV.
