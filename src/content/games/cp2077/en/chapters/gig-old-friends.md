**Old Friends** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at **Jacked and Coke**, a bar on the corner of Congress and Pigeon Streets in **Vista del Rey**. It belongs to Padre's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the four gigs of the first. It is personal: **Karubo Bairei**, an old **solo**, killed Padre's friends years ago, fled to the East Coast and came back to open a bar. The **Valentinos** hang out there and protect him, so Padre asks you not to make a scene.

> **Order:** enter the bar → neutralize Karubo (without raising the alarm) → carry his body outside → carry him to the fixer's transport → collect your reward from the Drop Point.

## The bar

Karubo moves around the **kitchen** between four spots. One or two Valentinos are usually watching the kitchen, another watches the front door and four more are in the main room.

## Getting in unseen

- **Back door:** with high **Technical Ability** it opens into a storage room next to the kitchen, skipping most of them. From there you only have three witnesses to worry about: a drunk Valentino wandering the main room, another by the kitchen window who sometimes turns away, and Karubo himself.
- **Roof hatch:** leads to a second storage room where one Valentino is taking inventory.

## At the end

Once Karubo is dealt with, Padre calls. If you **knocked him out without alerting anyone** and left him in the car he sent, Padre is especially pleased.

Known bug: according to the wiki, Karubo's body can reappear after the gig ends and pay out twice.
