**Takemura** gives it. During the parade for Saburo Arasaka's death in **Japantown**, Takemura is going to jump onto **Hanako's** float to kidnap her. Your job: cover him by taking out **three Arasaka snipers** and disconnecting the **netrunner** watching the area. **Sandayu Oda**, Hanako's bodyguard, waits there for you in the act's most technical boss fight. Per the wiki, Takemura only calls once you've finished **Gimme Danger and Life During Wartime**.

> **Order:** go to the Japantown bazaar → talk to Takemura (take his shard) → neutralize the 3 snipers → talk to Takemura → reach the netrunner → disconnect him → defeat Oda → decide his fate → talk to Takemura → connect to the access point → deactivate the turret → wait for Takemura's move → escape the parade grounds.

> **Get ready:** Oda uses optical camo, Mantis Blades and a smart SMG. Bring **stun grenades**, a fast melee weapon (a katana), an accurate weapon for headshots and quickhacks like **Weapon Glitch** and **Cyberware Malfunction**. If you want the **Genjiroh** pistol, note the code **2906**. **Save before disconnecting the netrunner**: the Oda fight starts on its own.

## The bazaar and the snipers

Go to the **Japantown bazaar**, talk to Takemura and take his **shard**. Then **neutralize the three snipers** (game8):

**Sniper 1.** Behind a door with a **mine just inside**: disarm it or step around it.

**Sniper 2.**

1. Take the **elevator to floor 21**.
2. Right after leaving the elevator, **before the ladder**, there's a locked door: inside is the iconic **Genjiroh** pistol. **Code 2906**, or open it with **Technical Ability** (IGN gives Technical 11, probably from 1.x).
3. Climb the **ladder** and **jump the gap**. Watch for **drones**.
4. You can talk to **Johnny** (optional).

**Sniper 3.**

1. There's a **mine at the door** and **Arasaka agents on the stairs**.
2. Cross the **bridge only after the burning floats have passed**.
3. Take the **elevator to floor 24**, where there are **more mines**.

## The netrunner

**Talk to Takemura**, **reach the netrunner** and **disconnect him from the net**. As soon as you do, **Oda appears and the fight starts** (see "Boss — Sandayu Oda"). Afterwards, decide what to do with him.

## After Oda

1. **Talk to Takemura.**
2. **Connect to the access point.**
3. **Deactivate the security turret.**
4. **Wait for Takemura to make his move.**
5. **Escape the parade grounds.**

If you call **Judy** afterwards, she asks about the parade incident.

## Choices

- **Spare Oda** (Takemura asks you to) → Takemura is grateful. Oda appears in **Totalimmortal** (the Arasaka route, **The Devil** ending) and fights alongside you against Yorinobu's forces, **but only if Takemura also survives Search and Destroy**. In the endings, Adam Smasher tells you: "You spared Oda... Mercy is disgusting."
- **Kill Oda** → Takemura is angry and says he "would remember", but per IGN it doesn't affect the story. Smasher approves: "You killed Oda? Good! He was useless."
- If you want the fullest version of The Devil ending, **spare him**.

## Boss — Sandayu Oda

An Arasaka cyberninja trained by Takemura. He has **Mantis Blades**, a **TKI-20 Shingen** (smart SMG), **optical camo**, flashbangs, Combat Stim and light subdermal armor.

**His moves:**

- **Fast Mantis Blade dashes:** keep moving and dodge. Shoot him in the head as he dashes in.
- **Smart SMG with homing bullets:** use corner cover to break their path.
- **Retreats under optical camo and heals** (IGN says up to all his health). Track him and keep damaging him while he's invisible.

**The Oni Mask:** it disrupts smart-weapon bullets. Destroy it by attacking it, with **Cyberware Malfunction** (instant), or with a stealth takedown after losing his attention.

**Useful quickhacks:**

- **Weapon Glitch:** shuts down the homing SMG.
- **Cyberware Malfunction:** removes the mask.
- **Detonate Grenade:** he carries one.
- **Friendly Mode** on cameras: lets you track him while he's invisible.

**Other keys:** **stun grenades** slow him down, and fast melee weapons like **katanas** punish his approaches well.

## What opens here

- **Search and Destroy:** Takemura and Hanako's safe house in Vista del Rey. **Make a manual save at the start**: saving Takemura decides **The Devil** achievement.
- **Totalimmortal** (Act 3, Arasaka route): Oda appears if you spared him and Takemura lives.
- It doesn't open or close any side chain.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Genjiroh** (code 2906, on the way to the second sniper; there's another copy at the Arasaka Estate during Last Caress) and the **Jinchu-Maru**, Oda's katana, looted when you defeat him. If you miss the Jinchu-Maru, Herold Lowe sells it in Phantom Liberty.
