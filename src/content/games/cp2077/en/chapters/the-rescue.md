**The Rescue** is V's first job in Night City, six months after the prologue. The fixer **Wakako Okada** hires you and **Jackie** to find **Sandra Dorsett**, a client whose locator implant (biomonitor) went dark. Everything points to the **Scavengers**, a gang that kidnaps people to rip out their implants and sell them. The netrunner **T-Bug** supports you remotely. The den is in **room 1237** of a Japantown block (Westbrook); the job ends at your apartment in **Megabuilding H10**, in Watson. At the start, Jackie and T-Bug offer you the optional tutorial **Practice Makes Perfect**.

> **Order:** talk to Jackie → follow him and take the elevator → talk to T-Bug → head to the apartment and hack the door → examine the woman's body → find Sandra (optional stealth) → defeat the Scavenger leader → examine Sandra and save her with the AirHypo → carry her to the terrace → wait for Trauma Team and place her on the stretcher → take the elevator and call Wakako → ride back with Jackie and defeat the Scavengers in the van → go home and go to bed.

> **Get ready:** save before entering the den. It is the first real fight and you can shoot your way through or do most of it in stealth. In the **garage**, as you leave the elevator, there is a **MaxDoc Mk.1** on the right and a **suitcase** with armor between two leather chairs (it needs **level 2**: come back for it if you're not there yet).

## Arrival: the garage and the elevator

You start in the car with Jackie, talking about the job.

1. **Talk to Jackie** and **follow him.**
2. Grab the **MaxDoc Mk.1** to the right of the garage elevator exit and look for the **suitcase** between the two leather chairs (armor, level 2).
3. **Take the elevator with Jackie** and **talk to T-Bug.** This is where they offer **Practice Makes Perfect**: you can do it now or skip it (since 2.3 you can replay it any time from Settings > Controls).
4. Stepping out of the elevator earns your **first 2 Street Cred**, per IGN.
5. On the way to the apartment, **T-Bug calls you**: that starts **The Gift** (a free Ping quickhack, see below).

## The Scavenger den (room 1237)

1. **Head to the apartment** and **hack the door**: T-Bug opens the entrance and disables the security.
2. **Follow Jackie.** You cross two rooms; the second holds the corpse of a woman on a table, packed with implants. **Examine the body.** Easing her back or not makes no difference: no consequences.
3. **Move to the next room.** Your first enemy is here.

**The first Scavenger (guided stealth).** The optional objectives teach you stealth:

1. **Sneak up** and **neutralize him** (you can kill him or knock him out).
2. **Hide the body in the freezer** right in front of you.
3. **Hide from the Scavengers** and **wait while staying unnoticed.**
4. **Neutralize the next one** and **use the path T-Bug marks.**

**The main room.** There are **five Scavengers** in the big room, plus the **leader** in the next room. Two options:

- **Stealth (IGN):** let the **two door guards** walk up to your doorway and take them down together with Jackie. Then **hug the right wall** and slip into the **side bathroom** to bypass the others.
- **Guns out:** take cover behind the furniture and let Jackie draw fire. Heal with the MaxDoc when needed.

## Boss — Scavenger leader

The leader is in the next room, which opens onto a terrace. The optional objectives suggest **going onto the terrace** and **distracting him**. Ways to get the upper hand (IGN and game8):

- **Hack the lamp** and wait until he stares into it: he is exposed for a takedown or a shot.
- Use **Reboot Optics**, then **go for headshots**.
- **Climb through the left-side window** to flank him.

His computer has a message hinting the gang had a run-in with Viktor, the ripperdoc you meet in the next job.

## Sandra Dorsett

1. **Talk to T-Bug** and go into the bathroom on the other side of the apartment: Sandra is in an **ice-filled bathtub** next to a dead man.
2. **Examine the body** and **check for vital signs.** Scanning her shows she is missing critical cyberware.
3. **Save Sandra with the AirHypo** when she starts flatlining.
4. V removes the **GPS jammer** that was keeping her Platinum **Trauma Team** alert from going off.
5. **Carry her to the terrace.**
6. **Wait for Trauma Team by the terrace door.** **If you stand too close, the AV won't land:** step back to the door.
7. **Place Sandra on the stretcher** and wait for Trauma Team to take her.
8. **Talk to Jackie**, **take the other elevator** and **call Wakako** to confirm the job. The elevator ride down advances the clock to 10 PM.

## The ride back: the Scavenger van

1. **Get into the passenger seat** and **ride back with Jackie.**
2. **Defeat the Scavengers** in the van that attacks you. There are **four**: two shoot from the **rear doors**, one later opens the **side door**, and the fourth is the **driver**. **Shoot the driver** to end it fast. Even if you don't kill them all, the van eventually crashes and they die anyway.
3. Jackie talks your way through an NCPD checkpoint at the Watson border and, shortly after, you watch a **MaxTac** team wipe out some gangoons.
4. **Exit the car** when Jackie drops you off at Megabuilding H10.

## Home: V's apartment

1. **Go home.** Optional: **check your weapons stash**.
2. **Go to bed** to finish the job. Per IGN, you reach **level 2** on arriving home.

**What your apartment has:** a **stash**, a **wardrobe** to change your look without changing stats (up to **6 outfits**; clothing unlocks its appearance when you pick it up, so you can sell it afterwards), and the **iconic weapon wall**, which since 2.0 displays every base-game iconic. The **bed** gives the **Rested** status (XP bonus), the **shower** gives **Refreshed** (Health regen) and the **coffee** gives **Energized** (Stamina); each lasts 60 minutes.

## Choices

- **Stealth or combat in the den** → same result; only your risk changes.
- **Killing or knocking out the first Scavenger** → no documented consequences.
- **Easing the woman's body back into the apartment** → changes nothing.
- **Practice Makes Perfect now or later** → no story effect; no XP.

## What unlocks

- **The Gift** (T-Bug calls on the way up to the apartment): a **free Ping quickhack** at **Yoko Tsuru**'s netrunner shop at Kabuki Roundabout. It is close by when **The Ride** ends.
- **The Gun**: **Robert Wilson**, at the **2nd Amendment** gun shop in Megabuilding H10 itself, gives you the Iconic **Dying Night** pistol (it got a new iconic modifier in 2.0). A Streetkid V gets special "rite of passage" dialogue. If the job **Shoot To Thrill** is active, you can't get Dying Night until you finish it.
- **The Gig** (Wakako): the pay for this job is collected once the Watson lockdown lifts, that is, in **Act 2**. Wakako pays **€$3,380** and then gives you a free tattoo, the **Tyger Claws Dermal Imprint**, at **Cassius Ryder**'s clinic.

> **Don't leave without:** the **MaxDoc** and the **armor suitcase** in the garage (level 2), and a stop at **2nd Amendment** in H10 for **Dying Night** before heading out to The Ripperdoc.

## Rewards

- **1,460 XP** and **1,167 Street Cred**, per the wiki infobox.
- V's apartment in Megabuilding H10, free.
- Later: **€$3,380** and the Tyger Claws tattoo (The Gig, Act 2).
