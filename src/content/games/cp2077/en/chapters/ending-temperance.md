**Temperance** is the ending where **Johnny keeps V's body**. It is not chosen on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop: it is decided inside Mikoshi, in **Changes**, at the end of the Panam route ([The Star](../ending-the-star/)), the Rogue route ([The Sun](../ending-the-sun/)) or the secret one ([(Don't Fear) The Reaper](../ending-the-reaper/)). Everything before that (including **Adam Smasher**) is played on those pages. Then comes a single job, **New Dawn Fades**: Johnny, in V's body, says goodbye to Night City in Pacifica, The Glen and North Oak. The rewards are those of the route you came from.

> **Order:** Changes: choose to let Johnny keep the body → West Wind Apartments: notification, ticket, bag → Steve and his dad → Time Machine: pick and try a guitar, pay **€$5000** → North Oak Columbarium: pay **€$4000**, leave the mementos → talk to Steve → bus out of Night City.

> **Get ready:** save **right after defeating Smasher** on the route you're playing: that way you do this ending and the other one (The Star or The Sun) without replaying the assault. New Dawn Fades has no combat. Johnny pays €$5000 for the guitar and €$4000 for the niches.

## How to get here: the choice in Changes

Changes starts the same on every route: you relive the climb to Misty's rooftop, talk to Jackie (if his body went to Viktor in The Heist) or pick up the necklace, and Alt takes you into cyberspace. The talk reveals V's body is damaged beyond repair: whoever keeps it has about **six months**. For Temperance:

- **Panam route** (Belly of the Beast): as V, pick "Body's Johnny's. I'll go" and **cross the bridge** with Alt.
- **Rogue route** (Knockin' on Heaven's Door): here you speak as Johnny; **enter the well** to return to V's body.
- **Secret route** ((Don't Fear) The Reaper): as V, **cross the bridge**. The wiki is clear that on this route the well returns the body to V (The Sun) and the bridge gives Temperance; PowerPyx and Game8 word it the other way round. Follow the wiki: **the well returns the body to whoever is speaking**.

If you finished Phantom Liberty, Changes adds dialogue about Cynosure or Songbird.

## New Dawn Fades: the West Wind apartment

Johnny wakes up in an apartment at the **West Wind Apartments** (West Wind Estate, Pacifica).

1. **Read the notification from the automated delivery system:** the **North Oak Columbarium** asks you to pay for the order within 24 hours or it will be cancelled.
2. Optional: read the texts from V's friends (Viktor, Mama Welles, Misty, Judy), worried because V hasn't answered since the raid.
3. **If you came from the Rogue route**, Johnny takes **Pride** off a shelf and apologises to Rogue for getting her killed.
4. **Pick up the bus ticket** and **your bag**, and leave the apartment.

## Steve and his dad

1. **Call Steve** on the intercom. He is a kid from the building Johnny befriended.
2. **Talk to Steve.** You can ask about the bruise (his dad hit him for not waking him in time) and offer comfort or your alarm clock; and about his progress on guitar.
3. Ask him for a ride. Outside, his dad, **Henry Sanchez**, gets angry and attacks him for taking the car keys.
4. **Talk to Steve's dad:** tell him to leave the kid alone, calm him down by saying you'll be back in two hours, or answer aggressively. Either way he lets you go. If you stay close, you'll hear him complain about your "terrible influence".
5. **Get in the car** and go to **Time Machine**. On the way, Steve talks about Kerry and his dream of being a musician: advise him as you like (dialogue only).

## Time Machine: the guitar

1. **Enter the store** and ask the owner for a six-string electric. On the wall you can scan three guitars (Anima Rebel, Ardor Session and Noise Contagion); Johnny picks them apart.
2. **Go to the back room** and **choose a guitar** from the "vintage" ones: **Deluze Orphean** or **Tenzor Paradize**.
3. **Try it out.** Steve is surprised you play like Silverhand: you can tell him you're Johnny reborn or that he should practise until he finds his own style.
4. Talk to Steve, who tries the guitar, and **pay €$5000**. Go back to the car.

## North Oak Columbarium

1. **Go to the columbarium.** On the radio, Yorinobu walks out of peace talks with Militech; Steve asks you whether there'll be a war (dialogue only).
2. Johnny tells Steve to wait in the car and **pays €$4000**.
3. **Check the columbarium layout** and **find the niche** you ordered.
4. **Leave your memento:**
   - **V's niche**, epitaph **"Dreamer"**: Johnny leaves V's **bullet necklace** inside. The rental runs to 21 July 2177, which places this scene on **21 July 2077**.
   - **Rogue's niche**, epitaph **"Queen of the Afterlife"** (if you came from the Rogue route): Johnny leaves **Pride**.
5. **Leave the columbarium.** Talk to Steve: you can take the cigarette out of his hand ("Gave all that up. So should you.").

**Known bug** (wiki, version 2.31 on PC): Steve may fail to stop at the columbarium and keep driving in circles around Jackson Plains. If it happens, skip the ride or load an earlier save.

## The bus

1. **Get on the bus**, **use your ticket**, put your bag away and sit down.
2. Steve runs after it: you left the guitar. Johnny tells him to keep it.
3. Johnny takes off his sunglasses and the bus leaves Night City.

## Choices

- **Giving the body to Johnny in Changes** → this ending. **Final for the playthrough** until you reload: save first.
- **Answers to Steve and his dad** → dialogue only; Henry always lets you go.
- **Which guitar you pick** → only changes the scene; Johnny gives it to Steve at the end.
- **Route of origin** → decides the deaths, the rewards and whether there is a niche for Rogue.

## Who lives and who dies

The deaths are **the same as on the route you came from**: on Panam's, Bob, Teddy, Saul and several Aldecaldos; on Rogue's, Rogue and Weyland (depending on events); on the secret one, only Arasaka staff (Rogue lives). On all of them, most of the Arasaka board, **Smasher** (your call) and **Hanako** (per Yorinobu). V, as a person, is gone: Johnny lives on in their body. Final reputation: **at most 90% (Edgerunner)**.

## Credits calls

- **Viktor:** V missed the last checkup; he asks at least to know they're alive.
- **Misty:** draws The Hanged Man, The Magician and The Star ("something like death, but not quite").
- **Mama Welles** (with Heroes): hasn't seen you in a while and invites you on Jackie's birthday.
- **Judy** (if she is your partner): worried at being ghosted. **Panam** (if she is your partner): furious, swears to rip V out of Johnny. **Kerry** (if he is your partner): calls you a coward for ghosting him. **River** (if he is your partner): has searched everywhere for you.
- **Rogue** (with her chain done): furious with Johnny for stealing V's body ("don't ever come back to Night City").
- **Mitch** (with Panam's chain): talks about storming Arasaka.
- **Takemura** (if you saved him): "rot in hell".
- **Reed** (Phantom Liberty): berates you for refusing the cure or calls from Dayton, depending on your expansion ending.

## Rewards and where you return

After the credits you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1**. The rewards are **identical to those of the route of origin** (The Star, The Sun or the secret ending): Legendary **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk**, the route iconics you picked up, Smasher's **Access Token** (secret armory on the **DV Ebunike**, **Ba Xing Chong** spec), the **Judgement** card if you took it and some shards. **Temperance** achievement.

> **Don't leave without:** a **save after Smasher** on the route of origin, so you can go back and also play The Star or The Sun without replaying the assault.
