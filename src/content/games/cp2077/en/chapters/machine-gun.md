**Machine Gun** is about **Skippy**, an iconic smart pistol (an Arasaka HJKE-11 Yukimura) with a talking AI. It lies **next to a body in an alley in Vista del Rey**, Heywood, so you get it in **Act 2**, once you can leave Watson. You don't need Smart Link cyberware for it to lock on.

> **Order:** pick up Skippy and choose a mode → rack up neutralizations → Skippy changes or locks its mode → keep using it until it asks to go home → take it to Regina Jones (Yaiba Tower, Kabuki) → set it down → talk to Regina → later, get it back.

## Skippy's modes

When you pick it up you choose: **Puppy-Loving Pacifist** (limbs only) or **Stone Cold Killer** (headshots only). Skippy tallies how many you kill and how many you knock out. When either count reaches **50**, it talks to you:

- If you hit **50 kills** first → it locks into **Puppy-Loving Pacifist** for good.
- If you hit **50 incapacitations** first → it asks you: "Killing is wrong" keeps it in Pacifist; "I don't know" or "I'm not having a conversation with my gun" switch it to Stone Cold Killer.

Keep neutralizing enemies with it and after a while it says it has found data on its previous owner, **Regina Jones**, and asks you to return it. If you refuse, it only screams "no" and "bad user" until you hand it over.

## With Regina

Go to Regina at **Yaiba Tower** in **Kabuki** (Watson) and **set Skippy down** so she can run a diagnostic. If it's in Stone Cold Killer, she says it needs a deeper test; if it's in Pacifist, she factory-resets it and renames it "Daisy". Ask where she got it or about your compensation, and get paid.

Some time later Regina texts you: you can have Skippy back, now **without its AI** and, since 2.0, **with no mod slots**. Pick it up at Yaiba Tower.

Per the wiki (unconfirmed), the job can fail during missions that strip your weapons, such as **Automatic Love**.

## Rewards

- 570 XP and **€$12,420**.
- **Skippy** (no-AI version), if you take it back.
