**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, gives you this rescue in **Rocky Ridge**, at a makeshift **Wraiths** outpost inside the **municipal landfill** (Edgewood Ln.). It's from her second set, with **Big Pete's Got Big Problems** and **Trevor's Last Ride**: it opens after **Dancing on a Minefield** and **Flying Drugs**, in **Act 2**. **Iris Tanner**, the best techie in the Badlands, did business with the Wraiths and, instead of paying her, they kidnapped her. She managed to send an SOS on an old radio.

> **Order:** infiltrate Wraiths territory → find Iris → talk to her → (optional) retrieve her van and park it in the garage → take Iris to Dakota → talk to Dakota.

## How to do it

The front entrance has **turrets** and sentries; the side routes are mined. Most of the gang is gathered by the building at the back, where they're trying to force Iris to work for them. Once freed, she asks you to find her **van** (a Villefort Columbus) in the nearby garage.

## Choices

- **Drive her out in her van** → on top of Dakota's payment, Iris gives you **crafting components**.
- **Refuse** → Dakota and Iris are both displeased, but Dakota still pays in full.

Either way, delivering Iris to the **DaKota** autoshop closes the gig.

## Rewards

€$13,500, 1,000 XP, 1,710 Street Cred and the **DR-12 Quasar** revolver.
