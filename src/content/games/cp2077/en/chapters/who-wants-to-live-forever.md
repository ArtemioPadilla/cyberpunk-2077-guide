**Who Wants to Live Forever** and **Things Done Changed** make up **The Tower** ending, the one Phantom Liberty adds to the base game. It's only available if you closed Phantom Liberty with **King of Swords** (you handed Songbird to Reed in The Killing Moon) or **King of Pentacles** (you spared Songbird in Somewhat Damaged). Reed keeps his promise: NUSA will remove the Relic. You say goodbye to Night City from the rooftop above **Misty's Esoterica**, leave with the FIA and wake up **two years later**, in **2079**. V lives, but has lost almost everything: the cyberware, Johnny and a big part of their life.

> **Order:** Who Wants to Live Forever: wait a day for Reed's call → accept → Misty's Esoterica rooftop → sit down → talk to Johnny → text your friends (optional) → wait for the FIA → get in the AV → Things Done Changed: wake at the Edward Kernaghan Military Medical Center → talk to Reed → call your friends → leave the clinic → Delamain cab → Viktor's clinic → the back-alley thugs → Misty.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save before accepting.** This ending works as **a second point of no return**: once you accept the offer, V's story ends. Finish everything you want to do first (gigs, Dogtown jobs, romances, main story). The sources don't say explicitly which save the game returns you to after The Tower credits, so **don't rely on it**: keep your own backup save.

## How it opens

- **Through Who Wants to Live Forever:** about **a day** after Through Pain to Heaven (King of Swords) or Four Score and Seven (King of Pentacles), Reed **calls or texts** you.
- **From Nocturne Op55N1:** if you reach the base game's finale without having accepted, the rooftop offers the option to **call Reed** alongside the others.
- **Declining** Reed's offer ends this job and you go back to the main story. On King of Swords, the wiki says the cure is offered **no matter what you say** after surrendering Songbird; game8 warns that The Killing Moon line "Don't want nothin' in return" would close it. That's why the earlier pages recommend asking for help in return.

## Who Wants to Live Forever

1. **Wait a day** for Reed to contact you and **talk to him**. Accept his help.
2. **Head to the rooftop above Misty's Esoterica** (Little China, Watson).
3. **Sit in the chair** and **talk to Johnny**. It's his farewell: he dies so you can live.
4. **Text your friends** (optional) to say goodbye.
5. **Wait for the FIA agents**, talk to them and **get in the AV**. Inside, Johnny confronts you and comes to terms with his end.

## Things Done Changed

**2079.** V wakes from a **two-year medically induced coma** at the **Edward Kernaghan Military Medical Center**, with Reed at their side.

1. **Talk to Reed** (you can go to the window). The operation to remove the Relic succeeded, but it left your brain **unable to handle cyberware**. Reed offers you **a job with NUSA** so you can at least move on.
2. **Call your friends** and decide who to visit:
   - **Viktor:** happy to hear from you, says the doctors are wrong; come see him.
   - **Judy:** relieved you're alive, but she now lives in **Pittsburgh** and is **married to Bianca**. If she was your partner, she apologizes and asks you not to try to ruin her marriage: **the romance ends**.
   - **Panam:** **doesn't answer**; you leave a message. Later **Mitch** sends you a video: she doesn't want to talk to you and it's best not to call again. If she was your partner, **the romance ends**.
   - **River:** angry at first, but lets you explain. He tells you he sold everything he knew about the NCPD to **Trauma Team** to pay for Randy's rehab and is now a wanted man. Ashamed to face you, he hangs up. If he was your partner, **the romance ends**.
   - **Kerry:** happy but swamped: he's on tour at the **Crystal Palace** and has no time right now. If he was your partner, he **promises to reunite with you in a few months**, once the tour is over.
3. **Use the intercom** to leave the clinic (you can call other friends, optional) and wait for the **medical orderly**.
4. You return to Night City in a **Delamain cab**. Talk to Delamain on the way about what has changed, then get out.
5. **Go to Viktor's clinic**, which is now a **Zetatech franchise**. Talk to the receptionist and to Viktor; **sit in the ripper's chair**. Viktor agrees with the NUSA doctors: your motor cortex has "turned deaf to implant signals".
6. **Leave the clinic** and head back to Delamain. Some **back-alley thugs** beat you up: that's how you learn your new frailty.
7. **Wait for help.** **Misty** arrives, helps you up and tells you she's **moving to Poland**. She gives you advice on living as an ordinary person; you can tell her how you see your life and goals. **Walk with Misty.** She leaves and V fades into the crowd.

**2.5 note:** Update 2.5 fixed a bug where the **Delamain cab got stuck** after a crash in Things Done Changed, blocking the ending.

## How the world ends up

- **Deaths:** Johnny (removed with the Relic). Hanako, according to Takemura.
- **Reputation:** at most **90%**.
- V's **necklace** stays on the chair in the medical center and is lost on returning to Night City.
- **If the NCX attack happened** (King of Swords), the epilogue mentions tensions between Orbital Air and Night Corp and a possible Fifth Corporate War or Second Unification War.

**Credits voicemails:**

- **Viktor:** always calls; in this ending, he's being sent to Frisco while Zetatech renovates the clinic.
- **Judy and Misty:** **don't call** in this ending (the only one where they don't). Neither does **Panam**.
- **Rogue:** her message mentions **Nibbles** and the **iguana** if you adopted them.
- **Takemura:** in hiding.
- **Reed:** after **King of Swords**, he shares his doubts about Songbird; after **King of Pentacles**, he just checks in on you.
- **Mama Welles**, **Saul and Mitch**, **Peralez:** depend on their jobs (Heroes, Panam's line, Dream On).

## Choices

- **Accepting Reed's offer** → **The Tower** ending: V survives without the Relic, but with no cyberware, no Johnny and almost every bond broken. **The Tower** achievement. **It's an ending:** save first.
- **Declining the offer** → you go back to the main story and the Nocturne Op55N1 endings (where the call to Reed is still available).
- **Who you call in Things Done Changed** → only changes the scenes and how your romances end (Judy, Panam and River end; Kerry promises to come back).

> **Don't leave without:** a save from before accepting Reed. At the end you receive the **Access Token**, the **Gorilla Arms** and the **Militech Berserk**.

## Rewards

- **Access Token**, **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk** (at the end).
- **The Tower** achievement.
