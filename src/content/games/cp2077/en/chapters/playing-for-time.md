It starts on its own as soon as **Love Like Fire** ends. The job is "given" by **Johnny Silverhand**, though **Goro Takemura** is the one pulling the strings. You pass through the **Municipal Landfill** (Red Peaks, Badlands), **Viktor's Clinic** (Little China), your **apartment** in Megabuilding H10 and **Tom's Diner**. Although the game lists it as the first Act 2 job, everything until V stands up in the shower is still part of the interlude: **the map and the inventory are locked** until then. It has no important choices, but finishing it opens the whole city and the act's three main lines.

> **Order:** approach the stranger (cyberspace) → crawl out of the landfill → use the air hypo → take down the motorcyclists → call Delamain → talk to the stranger → Viktor's diagnosis → Misty's pills → Johnny in your apartment → crawl to the pills and take them → stand → leave the apartment (optional: eat, get dressed, ammo, email) → Takemura's call → Tom's Diner → talk to Johnny.

> **Get ready:** you can't prepare: the inventory doesn't open until the shower. What you can do is **save as soon as you regain control in your apartment**, before leaving: from there Act 2 is open-ended and a clean save point pays off. The car fight is almost entirely scripted, so don't worry about your gear.

## Cyberspace and the landfill

V appears in cyberspace and sees a **red figure**. Follow it and approach: interacting with it pulls you out of cyberspace and you wake up in the landfill's garbage. **Crawl to safety.** Drifting in and out, you see **Dexter DeShawn** and **Takemura**; Takemura kills Dex and puts you in his car to take you to **Hanako Arasaka**.

## The escape in Takemura's car

On the way, Arasaka agents attack the car. Takemura, wounded, offers you an **air hypo**.

- **Use it in time.** If you don't use it when he offers it, V succumbs to their injuries and you go back to the last checkpoint. It's the job's only way to fail.
- Takemura hands you a **Kenshin** (JKE-X2 Kenshin). The fight against the motorcyclists is largely scripted: each enemy goes down at a fixed point, whatever damage you take. **Aim low and take the right-hand rider first.**
- At the end, an assassin with **Mantis Blades** jumps onto the car. Takemura pins him and you finish him. The car ends up crashed into a billboard.

Takemura asks whether you know a ripperdoc you trust: you name **Viktor**. **Call Delamain** to pick you up and **talk to the stranger** (Takemura). With Delamain's help he stabilises you on the way, and you barely make it to Viktor's clinic: Takemura himself nearly doesn't.

## Viktor and Misty

Several weeks pass with moments of consciousness. When you finally wake up fully, **Viktor** explains what's happening: the hallucinations are Johnny Silverhand's memories. Dex's shot triggered the **Relic**, whose nanites revived your brain, but they are also erasing it to make room for Johnny's engram. **You have weeks left** and Viktor can't reverse it.

**Misty** takes you to your apartment and leaves you two medicines you'll see all game:

- **Pseudoendotrizine:** speeds up the engram and lets Johnny take control for a while.
- **Omega blockers:** slow it down and suppress Johnny.

You can talk with her a little before she leaves. Then V goes to sleep.

## Johnny in your apartment

When you wake, Johnny is standing next to you, hostile. As you try to leave he pushes you: you both realise your movements are connected, and he tries to disconnect the Relic, which knocks you out again. When you come to and go for the Omega blockers, Johnny slaps them out of your hand.

1. **Crawl towards the pills.**
2. **Take them.**
3. **Stand.**

V passes out again and wakes up in the **shower**. From here you have full control: map, inventory and menu.

## Your apartment: optional objectives

Before leaving, the game suggests four optional things. None is required, but all four help:

- **Find something to eat.**
- **Put on some clothes** from the inventory (your apartment's closet is also the wardrobe for changing looks without changing stats).
- **Stock up on ammo.**
- **Check your email** on your computer.

If you played Nomad in **The Pickup** and told Meredith or Gilchrist about the "Transports - LOA" message, the **Militech gear crate** waits for you in the apartment. On your way out you can read the message from the **Megabuilding H10 Administration** (optional).

**Takemura calls** to meet you at **Tom's Diner**.

## Tom's Diner

You find Takemura drinking coffee. He explains why he saved you: he wants to prove that **Yorinobu killed his father**, with V as the witness, and promises Arasaka can help with the biochip. If you suggest other ways out, he dismisses them and leaves. There are lifepath lines:

- **Corpo:** V has seen the "exterminators" before, through Troy, and speculates about Hellman's defection.
- **Streetkid:** V remarks that Tom is a good guy.

Once you're alone, **Johnny** projects himself in front of you. He's been thinking: you need to work together. He suggests talking to **Rogue**, whom he knew long ago and who can help. From here V can talk to Johnny in their head; you'll notice V's voice changes slightly when they do.

## Choices

- **The air hypo** → if you don't use it in time, V dies and you replay from the checkpoint. No consequence beyond that.
- **The talk with Takemura** → you can raise other options, but he dismisses them; nothing changes.
- **Lifepath lines at the diner** → dialogue only.
- PowerPyx confirms it: **this job has no important choices.**

## What opens here

Leaving the apartment unlocks **all of Night City and the Badlands**, with gigs and NCPD Scanner Hustles; some content waits until you finish talking to Takemura. The three main lines start at once and you can do them in any order:

- **Evelyn Parker:** Automatic Love → The Space in Between → Disasterpiece → Double Life. Judy's chain, which ends in **Pyramid Song**, grows out of it.
- **Anders Hellman:** Ghost Town → Lightning Breaks → Life During Wartime. It leads to **Riders on the Storm** (Panam) and **I Fought the Law** (River, which leads to **The Hunt**).
- **Takemura and Hanako:** Down on the Street → Gimme Danger → Play It Safe → Search and Destroy.
- **Voodoo Boys / Alt:** opens after Double Life (M'ap Tann Pèlen → I Walk the Line → Transmission).
- **Tapeworm** runs underneath them all: it triggers on its own after several jobs and, once complete, opens Act 3 and **Chippin' In** (Johnny and Rogue).

Side jobs that appear now or soon after:

- **Heroes:** Mama Welles texts you when you leave the building. What happens depends on what you did with Jackie's body in The Heist.
- **Human Nature:** first of the Delamain chain. It starts in the H10 garage when you get in your car; the car is repaired about 6 in-game hours after Playing for Time and skipping time doesn't speed that up.
- **The Gig:** Wakako can pay you now, because the lockdown has been lifted.
- **Paid in Full,** if you still owe Viktor money.
- **Venus in Furs** (Meredith), if you unlocked it in The Pickup: it arrives some time after you meet Takemura.
- **Clampdown** (Update 2.5): needs The Heist and two of Wakako's gigs; then wait for her message.
- **Welcome to Paradise** (Update 2.5): needs The Heist and Street Cred 15.
- **Phantom Liberty** doesn't start yet: Songbird calls after **Transmission**.

## Act 2 map: what you must not lose

What decides endings and what closes for good is spread across the whole act. Keep it in mind from now on:

- **Search and Destroy:** save Takemura. If he dies, you lose **The Devil** achievement. Make a manual save at the start.
- **Queen of the Highway** (Panam's chain) unlocks **The Star** ending. **Riders on the Storm** fails if you wander too far or skip time: Saul dies and you lose the whole chain.
- **Chippin' In** (after Tapeworm) decides **The Sun** and the secret ending through your answers at the grave. Save before it.
- **Love Rollercoaster** disappears once you finish **Double Life**.
- **I'll Fly Away** is lost if you abandon Panam in Life During Wartime or don't do it right after Riders on the Storm.
- **Ex-Factor, Pisces, The Hunt, Rebel! Rebel!** and **Epistrophy** have choices that cut their chain.
- The **point of no return** comes in **Nocturne Op55N1**, when you approach the Embers entrance. Everything above must be done before (or recovered from the earlier save the game offers after the credits).

> **Don't leave without:** going back to the **Municipal Landfill** whenever you like for **Plan B**, Dex's Iconic Liberty: it's on his body any time after Act 1. It spends **€$1 per shot** instead of bullets, holds 20 rounds per magazine and has a higher Bleeding chance. If you miss it, **Herold Lowe** sells it in Phantom Liberty. Also collect the Nomad crate if you earned it, and get paid by Wakako.
