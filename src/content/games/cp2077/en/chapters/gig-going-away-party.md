**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, sends you to a hideout on Mallagra St., **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his last set, with **Desperate Measures** and **For My Son**: it opens after **Cuckoo's Nest** and **Severance Package**, in **Act 2**. After **Will Gunner**'s coup inside **6th Street**, **Flavio dos Santos** is on the hit list. El Capitán has already arranged for an **Aldecaldo** smuggler to get him out of town; you just have to deliver him. The catch: instead of lying low, Flavio threw a huge party.

> **Order:** enter the hideout → look for Flavio → scan the bodies to find him → talk to him → escort him to the car → get in with him → bring him to the nomad contact → get out and talk to the nomad.

## How to do it

When you arrive, **6th Street** has already surrounded the house and massacred the party. The first three entrances are in sight of enemies; there are four ways in and out in all: front door, garage, back door and a **crawlspace under the house** leading to a hole in the floor. You can use the **deactivated cameras** to scout part of the interior.

Flavio is on the **second floor**, behind the door furthest from the stairs; he's the only survivor. Wake him and lead him down to the **car in the garage**. **You have to use that car**: if you leave it for another vehicle, El Capitán sends you an angry message and the gig can fail. Drive to the parking lot where the smuggler is waiting.

## Rewards

€$23,300, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred. It counts toward **Ride Captain Ride** (the iconic **Bloody Maria** shotgun) and, with **For My Son**, opens Rogue's **The Union Strikes Back**.
