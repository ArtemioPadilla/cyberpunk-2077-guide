**Love Rollercoaster** comes from **Jean Rochelin**, one of a group of kids hanging out at the **Eden Beach Mega Ride** rollercoaster behind the **Grand Imperial Mall**, in **Coastview**, **Pacifica**. The wiki classes it as a side job. It opens after **Love Like Fire**, at the start of Act 2. **Do it early:** the job and its characters disappear once you finish **Double Life**. After that you can still repair the rollercoaster and ride it, but without the job.

> **Order:** talk to Jean → scan the car to find the control panel → use it → tell Jean → get on → enjoy the ride.

## How to do it

Offer to help. **Scan the rollercoaster car** to locate the nearby **control panel** and use it to switch the ride on. Tell Jean: the group gets cold feet about riding something that old, but Johnny is all for it. If you agree to do the first test run yourself, don't worry: it goes fine, and there are a few interactions during the ride.

There is no combat and no attribute check.

## Rewards

The wiki infobox lists no XP, eddies or items: it is an atmosphere job.
