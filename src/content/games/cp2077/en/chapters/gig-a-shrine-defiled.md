**A Shrine Defiled** is the last gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place at the **Shinto shrine** (Chram Denya Jinja) on Milagro Terrace, in the hills of **North Oak**, an area with a strong **Tyger Claws** presence. It belongs to the **fourth set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Getting Warmer...** and **Wakako's Favorite**. **Taki Kazo**, a Tyger Claws boss, thinks he can rule Japantown without Wakako and uses the shrine's honden (main hall) for secret meetings. Wakako gives you a wiretap to place by the shintai, in the most sacred spot. Her advice: don't make a noise.

> **Order:** (optional) remain undetected → get to the honden → place the wiretap inside the main hall → get out of the shrine complex → collect your bonus from the Drop Point.

## The shrine

You enter through a torii at the top of the outer compound's stairs; behind it is the purification pavilion and, further in, a second torii in front of the main hall. There is a stage, a gift shop and priests tending the grounds, as well as the Tyger Claws. The optional objective is to reach the honden and leave without being detected: the last step is "collect your bonus from the Drop Point", so the bonus depends on that stealth part.

## What it unlocks

Finishing Wakako's gigs unlocks **Professional Widow** and Rogue's gig **Family Heirloom**.
