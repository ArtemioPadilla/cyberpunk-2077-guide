The **Rogue** route. It opens on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop if you finished **Blistering Love**: pick "Think you and Rogue should go" and take the **pseudoendotrizine**. Johnny takes control of V's body and **you play as Johnny** for most of the route. It is four jobs: **For Whom the Bell Tolls** (convince Rogue at the **Afterlife**), **Knockin' on Heaven's Door** (the assault on **Arasaka Tower** with Rogue and Weyland, and **Adam Smasher**), **Changes** (Mikoshi) and **Path of Glory** (the epilogue, a few months later). If Johnny keeps the body in Mikoshi, it ends in [Temperance](../ending-temperance/). There is a variant of this ending with Rogue alive: [(Don't Fear) The Reaper](../ending-the-reaper/).

> **Order:** Afterlife: Rogue → Weyland → **Retrothrusters** → gear up (**Prejudice**) → netrunner suit and Alt → AV from the roof → tower: guard (optional) → **commanding officer** (elevator token, **HJKE-11 Yukimura**) → jungle (**Caretaker's Spade**) → 68th-floor atrium → security room → servers and Alt → netrunner's nest → Mikoshi → **Adam Smasher** → **Pride** and **Judgement** card → **save** → Changes: **the bridge** → Path of Glory.

> **Get ready:** Johnny uses V's gear and level. Bring healing, grenades, electrical or EMP quickhacks and several ammo types. The **Retrothrusters** you get here are for dropping over the atrium edges to the security floor. Many items on this route are lost if you don't grab them on the spot: check the "Don't leave without" list.

## For Whom the Bell Tolls: the Afterlife

Johnny, in V's body, goes to the **Afterlife** to ask Rogue for one last job.

1. **Meet with Rogue** and talk to her.
2. **Follow Weyland** and talk to Rogue again.
3. **Take the Retrothrusters.**
4. **Gear up (optional).** This is where **Prejudice**, Rogue's iconic assault rifle, is: don't leave the Afterlife without it. It pairs with **Pride**, which you'll get in the tower.
5. Go to the netrunner's room, **put on the suit**, sit in the chair and **talk to Alt**. Stand.
6. Go back to Rogue, talk to her and follow her. **Take the elevator to the roof** and get in the **AV**.

Job shards: "Banking details" and "Greetings from nowhere".

## Knockin' on Heaven's Door: the tower

1. **Follow Rogue (optional):** take down one of the guards and talk to Rogue and Weyland.
2. **Find the commanding officer**, at the far left of the floor; use the jungle for cover. **Caretaker's Spade:** in the central jungle area, near the conference table, leaning against some rocks or a tree.
3. **Defeat the officer and search his body:** he carries the **elevator access token**. Per Game8, the **HJKE-11 Yukimura** smart pistol is on his table.
4. Get in the elevator, wait for Rogue and Weyland and **ride up to the atrium** (68th floor).
5. **Follow Rogue.** There are drones. Drop over the edges with the **Retrothrusters** to the security floor. **Watch the camera** above the security-room door (Game8).
6. **Get to the security room**, **open the server room doors** and **connect Alt** to the system.
7. Go to the elevator, **ride up to the netrunner's nest** and get to **Mikoshi**. **Open the gate.**
8. **Cutscene:** Smasher impales Rogue and lifts her into the air; she plants a grenade in his chest before she dies. Smasher starts the fight wounded (about **80% health**) against Johnny and Weyland.
9. **Defeat Adam Smasher** (below).
10. Optional: **pick up Rogue's gun, Pride**, by her body, near the entrance gate; **decide Smasher's fate**; loot the **Access Token**; find the **Judgement tarot card** on a wall of the room.
11. **Save.** Go to the Mikoshi access point (talk to Weyland, optional), connect and talk to Alt.

Job shards: "The Myth of Er", "The Iliad", "The Valar Mission, Back to Square One", "'Secure Your Soul' – VIP Clients", "BREAKING: SABURO ARASAKA DEAD" and "The Relic - why all the fuss?". Knockin' on Heaven's Door gives 501 XP (Game8).

## Boss — Adam Smasher

On this route he starts 20% down thanks to Rogue's grenade. Since 2.1 he uses a **Sandevistan** and adapts his tactics to your build.

- **Three phases:** close combat, long range (once he loses his right half) and weakened.
- **Weak point:** the right side of his chest, behind plating you can shoot off. Shooting off his **right arm** (on your left, facing him) ends his melee; then wreck the **back rocket launcher** (PowerPyx tip from before 2.0).
- **Electrical quickhacks** and **EMP** to block his attacks for a moment.
- **At about 50% health, on this route, snipers appear on the upper level.** Take them out or keep cover from them while you work on Smasher.
- **Rockets:** they target where you were; don't stand still. More aggressive the less health he has.
- **Fate (optional):** the sources don't document what sparing him changes. To be sure of the Access Token, finish him and loot it.

## Changes: Mikoshi as Johnny

1. You relive the climb to Misty's 9th floor. **Go to the roof:** you talk to Jackie (if his body went to Viktor in The Heist) or pick up the bullet necklace.
2. Alt recites the end of "Sailing to Byzantium". This time it is Johnny who **talks to V**.
3. V's body is damaged beyond repair: about **six months** for whoever keeps it. If you finished Phantom Liberty, there is extra dialogue about Cynosure or Songbird.

## Choices

- **"Goin' with Alt, you live on" and cross the bridge** (as Johnny) → Johnny leaves with Alt and V gets their body back → **Path of Glory (The Sun)**.
- **Enter the well** (as Johnny) → Johnny keeps V's body → **New Dawn Fades (Temperance)**: see [3.5 · Temperance](../ending-temperance/). Game8's table for this job gives the bridge as The Star ending: that's an error; the wiki and PowerPyx agree the bridge is The Sun.
- **Picking up Pride** → you keep it; if you choose Temperance, Johnny leaves it in Rogue's niche.
- **Rooftop call** (decided in Nocturne) → who wakes up with you in the penthouse.
- **Smasher's fate** → no documented effect.

## Path of Glory: the legend of the Afterlife

**A few months** have passed.

1. You wake in **V's penthouse**. Turn around: the partner you called on the rooftop is there (**Panam** is already in the shower). With no call, you're alone.
2. **Take a shower** (Judy, Kerry or River may join) and get dressed (optional). In the kitchen your partner voices doubts; join them on the balcony. **The outcome is fixed:** Kerry and River promise to wait for you, things with Panam stay open but hopeful, and **Judy ends the relationship**.
3. Before leaving, check the penthouse: **V's computer** has messages about the coming job, and **Johnny's Samurai jacket** hangs on the wall: **take your jacket** (you can wear it). The bullet **necklace** is on the nightstand (optional).
4. Get in the **AV**. **Delamain** flies it depending on what you decided in Don't Lose Your Mind.
5. **Enter the Afterlife.** Optional: talk to **Claire**, who offers a special drink for the operation.
6. **Meet with the client** in Rogue's old booth: **Mr. Blue Eyes** hires you for a job on the **Crystal Palace** casino (data on its clients).
7. On the ship, engage the autopilot, **leave the cockpit**, get armed, **put on the helmet**, listen to the client, **enter the airlock**, decompress it and **exit and soar into the stars**. The scene stands for **The Sun** card and unlocks the achievement.

## Who lives and who dies

**Rogue**, **Weyland** (depending on events), many Arasaka employees, most of the board, **Adam Smasher** (your call) and **Hanako** (per Yorinobu) die. V is Night City's new legend: reputation **100% (Legend)**.

## Credits calls

- **Viktor:** hasn't seen you in a while ("found you a new, major-league ripper, huh?") and asks you to stop by.
- **Misty:** draws Strength, The Emperor and The World ("the world's gonna hear about you").
- **Mama Welles** (with Heroes): amused by your new fame.
- **Judy:** if she is your partner, she dumps you and leaves Night City; if you did her chain without romance, she thanks you.
- **Panam:** if she is your partner, news from the Badlands and the promise of a long ride; if you did her chain without romance, she thanks you.
- **Saul and Mitch:** with Panam's chain, Saul is still alive and says you're an Aldecaldo, and Mitch invites you to camp; without it, Saul thanks you for saving an Aldecaldo and Mitch for saving his ass.
- **Kerry and River** (if they are your partner): Kerry wishes you could hang out more; River invites you to Randy's birthday.
- **Takemura** (if you saved him): "rot in hell".
- **Reed** (Phantom Liberty): berates you for refusing the cure (King of Swords or Pentacles) or calls from Dayton (King of Cups).
- **Peralez** (with Dream On): depending on how it ended.

## Rewards and where you return

After the credits you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1** with:

- Legendary **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk** (if you didn't own them).
- **Prejudice**, **Caretaker's Spade** and **Pride**, if you picked them up.
- **Access Token** (if you looted it): opens Smasher's secret armory on the **DV Ebunike** (**Ba Xing Chong** spec).
- The **Judgement** card (if you took it) and some shards.
- **The Sun** achievement.

Epilogue clothing (for example, what you put on in the penthouse) does not come back with you.

> **Don't leave without:** **Prejudice** when gearing up at the Afterlife, **Caretaker's Spade** in the jungle, the **HJKE-11 Yukimura** on the officer's table, **Pride** by Rogue's body, Smasher's **Access Token**, the **Judgement** card on the wall and a **save after Smasher**.
