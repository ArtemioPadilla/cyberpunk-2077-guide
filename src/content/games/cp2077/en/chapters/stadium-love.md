**Stadium Love** starts on its own: near some rooftops in **Rancho Coronado**, **Santo Domingo**, you hear a **6th Street** party. Up top are their leader, **Captain Sherman**, on a couch, and **Walker** next to him. Santo Domingo opens in Act 2.

> **Order:** see what's going on → talk to Sherman → take the revolver → 4 stations (a shot and 12 seconds of shooting at each) → back to Sherman → ask about your results → collect the prize.

## How to do it

**Don't draw your weapon:** if you do, the job ends and the whole gang turns hostile. Stay polite and Sherman keeps things calm. Ask whether it is a shooting contest and join. The rules:

- Take a shot of alcohol before each round.
- Use only the **revolver** on the table next to Sherman.
- Don't cross the yellow line at each station or you are disqualified.

There are four stations with 12, 12, 11 and 11 targets (46 in total), 12 seconds each and 6 rounds in the revolver. You cannot save manually while it runs, but you can load an autosave. Let the alcohol wear off between stations so the penalties don't stack. To beat Walker you need **at least 44 points**.

The wiki reports (2.0, unconfirmed) that if you aim before you are told to start, you sometimes can't draw the revolver: wait for the signal.

## Choices

- **You win** → Sherman offers you a place in 6th Street; V turns it down and he warns you that next time will be hostile. Collect the prize.
- **Insult Sherman** → the gang attacks you.
- **If you did the Life's Work gig earlier**, per the wiki (unconfirmed), a gang member recognizes you and starts a fight at the end; survive it and you still collect the prize.

In **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** (Phantom Liberty), Will Gunner remembers how you handled this.

## Rewards

**Divided We Stand**, an iconic Smart assault rifle (Nokota D5 Sidewinder) that locks onto up to five enemies at once if you have smart-targeting cyberware.
