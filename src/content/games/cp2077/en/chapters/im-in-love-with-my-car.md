**I'm in Love with My Car** is a tiny job added in **Update 2.0**. It's in a **warehouse in northeastern Northside**, Watson, and it only works **after Playing for Time**, in **Act 2**. If you go earlier, the garage is empty and the hidden container holds no key. It's a tribute to rally driver Ken Block.

> **Order:** search the warehouse → find the key → get in the car.

## The warehouse

When you walk in, **Johnny Silverhand** appears next to a **Quadra Type-66 "Hoon"**, an armed car. Behind the car, a **collapsed metal shelf** covers a **small container**: the **key** is inside. Take it and **get in the car**: it's yours.

The Hoon carries **front-mounted machine guns** that aim where the car points. Ammo is infinite, but the guns need reloading.

If you have trouble finding the warehouse, the Delamain cab you chase in **Epistrophy (Northside)** drives right past it.

## Rewards

- **Quadra Type-66 "Hoon"**.
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
