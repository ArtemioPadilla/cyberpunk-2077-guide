**The Gift** starts near the end of **The Rescue**: **T-Bug** calls and asks you to stop by **Yoko Tsuru**'s netrunner shop in **Kabuki Roundabout** (the EdgeNet shop in Kabuki Market, Watson). A free **Ping** quickhack is waiting for you there. It is an Act 1 job with no combat and no time limit, and it works as a hacking tutorial: do it the first time you pass through Kabuki, for example after **The Ride**.

> **Order:** go to Yoko Tsuru → pick up Ping → equip it in your inventory → upload Ping to the camera to find the access point → connect to the access point with your personal link and hack it.

## At Yoko Tsuru's

1. Go to the marker in Kabuki and talk to **Yoko**. She is T-Bug's friend and kept the program for you; she says it works on any third-gen cyberdeck.
2. Even with a different cyberdeck, V can tell her it is a **Militech Paraline**: according to the wiki, the option shows up anyway.
3. Yoko sells quickhacks. Come back when Regina's gigs have earned you some eddies.

## Testing Ping

1. Open your inventory and **equip Ping** on your cyberdeck.
2. Scan the nearby **camera** and **upload Ping** to it. It reveals the enemies and devices connected to the local network, including the **access point**.
3. Walk up to the access point and connect with your **personal link**. **Breach Protocol** opens: solve it and the job ends.

## What Ping is good for

- It is **untraceable**: enemy netrunners can't trace you for using it. At tier 1 it lasts 8 seconds and costs 4 RAM.
- Use it when you reach any gig building: you see the guards and cameras on the network before you go in.
- Better versions exist: tier 3 adds +25% lock-on speed with Smart weapons against pinged targets, and tier 5 lets you quickhack devices through walls. They come from Breach Protocol or netrunner shops.

## Rewards

- **Ping** quickhack (tier 1).
