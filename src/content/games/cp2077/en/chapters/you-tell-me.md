**You Tell Me** arrived with **Update 2.5** and extends **Fool on the Hill**, the tarot graffiti job of **Misty Olszewski** in Little China (Watson). **The wiki has no page for this job yet**, so this covers only what the patch notes and the tarot cards page confirm. It starts as soon as you **scan any of the 12 new Minor Arcana cards**, and collecting them all earns a **special reward** (the wiki doesn't say what). The tarot menu now splits Major and Minor Arcana. It isn't known whether it needs Act 2 or an active romance: the safe bet is to hunt for them in **Act 2**.

> **Order:** scan a Minor Arcana card → find all 12 → get the reward.

## The cards

They are the **Queen**, **Knight** and **Page** of each suit (**Cups, Pentacles, Wands and Swords**). Per the wiki, they're spread around Night City and **tied to V's romances**. The four **Kings** belong to Phantom Liberty, sit in Dogtown and are part of a different job, **Tomorrow Never Knows**.

Of the 12, the wiki locates only one:

- **Queen of Cups:** on the outside back wall of **Kutcher's Home**, the home of River Ward's sister, in the **Red Peaks** trailer park (Badlands).

The wiki has no locations yet for the other 11.

## Rewards

- A special reward for all 12 cards (no details on the wiki).
