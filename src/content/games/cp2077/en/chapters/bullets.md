**Bullets** takes place in **Jinguji**, **Zane Jagger**'s clothing store in Downtown (City Center). City Center opens in **Act 2**. The job does not appear until **you have shopped at Jinguji at least once**: when you come back some time later, Zane greets you and it starts.

> **Order:** shop at Jinguji → come back later → defeat the cyberpsycho → talk to the MaxTac officer → sit next to Zane.

## The attack

While you browse, a **cyberpsycho** walks in and kills the store's guards. Zane hits the alarm and it is up to you to take him down inside the store. Come in geared up: the fight cannot be avoided.

## MaxTac

MaxTac lieutenant **Melissa Rory** arrives. Talk to her, then sit on the couch next to Zane. On your way out you can talk to her again about her **Higurashi 20-13 Mantis Blades** and the attack; she invites you to join MaxTac, and you can refuse or say you will think about it.

## Optional fight

Since 2.0, if you leave Jinguji, come back in and **attack** Melissa Rory and her squad, it turns into a boss-type fight. She **may** drop the **MaxTac Mantis Blades** (not guaranteed). You are taking on a full MaxTac squad, so save first. The same blades can also be had in **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos**, in Phantom Liberty.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
