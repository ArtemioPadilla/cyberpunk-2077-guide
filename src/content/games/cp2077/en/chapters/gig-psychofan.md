**Psychofan** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place in **apartment 1702**, on the 17th floor of a building on Los Lobos Street in **the Glen**. It belongs to Padre's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the first (**Fifth Column**, **Life's Work**, **Jeopardy** and **On a Tight Leash**). The client wants **Kerry Eurodyne's guitar**, from Samurai, now owned by **Gaston Slayton**, a collector obsessed with the band. You have access to the building's elevator.

> **Order:** get inside the building → go to apartment 1702 → find the guitar → get inside the shrine room (code 2065) → take the guitar → leave the building → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## Getting into the apartment

- **Front door:** forced open with high **Technical Ability**.
- **Balcony:** take the fire exit to the right of the door and climb to the roof. Walk straight to the edge: below you see the balcony. Disable or avoid the **camera** and drop down; the balcony door is unlocked.

Inside you can explore with no risk of being caught. There are Samurai concert posters and vintage guitars, which Johnny scorns.

## Upstairs

In the **bedroom** there is a suitcase with **Johnny's pants**. The **shrine room** holding the guitar has a keypad: the code **2065** appears on a photo of the guitar. You can also force the door with a **Body** check. The bedroom computer unlocks when you enter the code at the door; it shows that Gaston wanted surgery to look like Kerry.

> **Don't leave without:** the iconic **Johnny's pants**, required for the **Breathtaking** achievement. The wiki's infobox marks them as missable, although the item page says you can return to the apartment for them. Take them during the gig and don't risk it.
