**Big in Japan** is offered by **Dennis Cranmer**, who approaches you at the **Afterlife** (Little China) after you meet Takemura in **Playing for Time**, so it's an **Act 2** job. Dennis wants to hire you directly, without going through a fixer: collect the contents of a marked fridge in the **Kabuki favela** and call him.

> **Order:** go to the Kabuki favela → open the container with the "No Future" graffiti → talk to Dennis → pick up the body → carry it to Dennis's car → drive to Northside → get out of the car → talk to Dennis.

## The favela

The fridge is in the **last shack out on the pier** and has **"No Future"** painted on it. When you open it, an exhausted man falls out. Dennis tells you to drive him to **Northside** and that **Tyger Claws** nearby want him dead, and you too.

- While carrying the man you **can't draw weapons**. If you want to fight, **set him down** first.
- If anything happens to him while he's on the ground, **the job fails**. Pick your spot carefully, or sneak past the Tyger Claws.

Put him in the **trunk** of Dennis's car and drive to the Northside coordinates.

## The delivery

Dennis reveals the man's name: **Haruyoshi Nishikata**. Talk to him: he claims to be a world-class surgeon who had a Tyger Claws boss die on his operating table, and Dennis helped him escape. What the gang doesn't know is that he killed the boss on purpose.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Scalpel**, an iconic katana you can pick up when you get paid. The wiki flags it as missable. With Sandevistan active it gives +50% Crit Chance and its hits apply Bleeding.

## Rewards

- 398 XP, 596 Street Cred and **€$1,350**.
- **Scalpel** (iconic katana).
