**Every Grain of Sand** is the final reward from fixer **Dakota Smith**. When you finish **all** her gigs in the **Badlands**, she texts you one last time to thank you: the gift is already waiting in the garage. Go to the garage area of **Megabuilding H10** in Watson, where your apartment is. The Badlands open in Act 2. It was added in Patch 1.5.

> **Order:** finish Dakota's 9 gigs → read her message → collect the pickup in the Megabuilding H10 garage.

## The gigs that count

- **Tier 1:** Dancing on a Minefield, Flying Drugs.
- **Tier 2:** No Fixers, Big Pete's Got Big Problems, Trevor's Last Ride.
- **Tier 3:** MIA, Sparring Partner.
- **Tier 4:** Goodbye, Night City, Radar Love.

## Rewards

The **Thorton Mackinaw "Saguaro"**, the nomad version of the Mackinaw pickup: all-wheel drive, a cage and retractable storage on the bed, and racing seats. The infobox gives no XP or eddies. Per the wiki, your Saguaro wrongly shows the Thorton Mackinaw "Beast" description.
