**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, hires you for this rescue at the **Militech detention center** on Coachwhip Ln., in **Jackson Plains**, by the SoCal border. It's from her last set, with **Radar Love**: it opens after **MIA** and **Sparring Partner**, in **Act 2**. Smuggler **Bruce Welby** has been held for days by **Militech** border security. His friend **Archibald Crane**, a Militech employee, asked Dakota for help. And Militech mustn't notice a thing.

> **Order:** reach the Militech compound → (optional) don't kill any Militech personnel → find Bruce → talk to him → escort him outside → take him to the extraction point → wait for Archie and talk to him → (if nobody died) collect your bonus from the Drop Point.

## How to do it

The bonus objective is to **kill no Militech personnel**. According to the wiki, **destroying drones** and neutralizing staff by **non-lethal** means doesn't fail it. Check the center's computer and shards if you want the background.

## Rewards

€$8,580 (the wiki lists no XP), plus the Drop Point **bonus** if no Militech staff die. With **Radar Love**, it completes Dakota's gigs and opens **Every Grain of Sand**: the **Thorton Mackinaw "Saguaro"** pickup waits for you in the Megabuilding H10 garage.
