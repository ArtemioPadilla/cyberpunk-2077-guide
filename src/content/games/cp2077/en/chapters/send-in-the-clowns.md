**Send in the Clowns** is given by **Ozob Bozo**, a Brazilian merc who wears a live grenade in place of a nose. Shortly after you finish **Transmission** (Act 2), Ozob texts you: he got your number from a friend and has a job for you.

> **Order:** read the text → call Ozob → park at Cherry Blossom Market (Japantown) and honk → wait for Ozob → drive him to Little China (Watson) → wait for him → defeat the Tyger Claws → talk to Ozob.

## The drive

Pick Ozob up at **Cherry Blossom Market** in Japantown (Westbrook). He asks you to drive to **Urmland Street** in Little China (Watson). While Ozob is in your car, the vehicle **takes no damage**, not even from NetWatch breaches at a 5-star wanted level.

According to the wiki (a bug reported on Xbox, version 2.31, unconfirmed): if **Johnny** spawns in the passenger seat while you drive to pick Ozob up, the job gets stuck because he is using Ozob's seat. Get out and back in so Johnny despawns.

## The restaurant

Ozob steps out to pick up "takeout" at a Chinese restaurant next to **Misty's Esoterica**. An explosion goes off and Ozob runs out with several **Tyger Claws** on his tail. Help him take them all down and talk to him to get paid.

## What comes next

Ozob comes back later as an opponent in the **Beat on the Brat** fights (the Pacifica one).

## Rewards

- €$3,920.
- The **Crafting Spec** for **Ozob's Nose**, a Tier 5 iconic grenade. It works like the F-GX Frag Grenade, with slightly less damage, a 15% higher chance to knock enemies down and a burst of confetti. Crafting it costs 10 Legendary components.
