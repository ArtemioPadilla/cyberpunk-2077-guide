**The Heist** is Act 1's final job. With the **Flathead** from The Pickup and the intel from The Information, Dex calls you and **Jackie** to the **Afterlife** to go over the plan: get into **Konpeki Plaza** (Arasaka Waterfront) as guests, sneak the Flathead up to **Yorinobu Arasaka**'s penthouse with **T-Bug**'s help, and steal the **Relic**, an experimental biochip. Nothing goes to plan. The job ends at the **No-Tell Motel**, in Kabuki. **You need to have finished The Pickup and The Information.** The penthouse holds several of the game's easiest-to-miss iconic weapons.

> **Order:** Afterlife: talk to Jackie → bouncer → sit with Jackie → follow Oleg to Dex's booth → put the Flathead in the briefcase and go over the plan → put on the suit and get in the Delamain → Konpeki: stash your weapons → reception → (optional) bar → floor 42 → drive the Flathead to the netrunner → time skip → penthouse: hidden switch and safe → hide behind the screen → escape via the balcony (optional: Saburo's AV) → jump → the officer's token → lobby and garage → drones → Jackie's body → No-Tell Motel, room 204.

> **Get ready:** **point of no return:** as soon as you get into the **Delamain** at the Afterlife, **you can't go back to exploring Night City or finish other jobs until Act 2**. Wrap up whatever you want in Watson first (for example **The Gun** or **The Gift**) and **save manually** before telling Jackie you're ready. At Konpeki you **hand over your weapons**: the escape starts unarmed and you arm yourself with what the guards drop. Your gear waits for you at the No-Tell Motel. No required gear or build: stealth helps, but everything can be shot through.

## Afterlife

1. **Head to the Afterlife** and **talk to Jackie**, who is on a call with **Mama Welles**.
2. **Talk to the bouncer**: any answer works, he lets you in.
3. You can look around the club while Dex is busy (Panam and Nash can be seen arguing). Then **sit with Jackie** at the bar and **talk** to him and to the bartender, **Claire**.
4. **Follow Oleg**, Dex's bodyguard, and **join Dex's booth**, where Dex and T-Bug are waiting.

## The plan and the cut

1. You can talk to T-Bug. **Put the Flathead in the briefcase**, sit and answer Dex: he mostly asks about Evelyn.
2. **Slot the shard** on the table to **go over the plan**, and ask whatever you like.
3. Jackie brings up the pay. Dex offers you **30%** as fresh talent:
   - **35%:** tell him you deserve more, or that it's about more than eddies (being remembered, the challenge). A Corpo V can say it's revenge on Arasaka ("Arasaka is overdue for payback").
   - **40%:** tell him **Evelyn wanted to cut him out** of the deal (what she offered you in The Information).
   - If you side with Dex or with Jackie over the 30%, Jackie forgives you.
4. PowerPyx shows a **Reflexes** line ("Something's off about her") about Evelyn; the exact value it needs in 2.5 is not confirmed.
5. Dex and T-Bug leave. Jackie asks if you're ready:
   - **"Something I gotta take care of first"** → you can leave to wrap things up before the point of no return.
   - **"Let's go"** → you commit. If you put it off, when you come back **take the Militech suit**, **put it on** and **get in the Delamain**.

**The cut is never paid:** Dex betrays you at the end, so the percentage **has no mechanical consequence**.

## Konpeki Plaza: arrival

1. **Drive to Konpeki** (talk to Jackie on the way if you like), **stash your weapons** and **get out**.
2. **Wait for Jackie to take out the Flathead** and follow him: **guard**, **scanner**, **reception desk** and **hand panel**.
3. At reception you have to **stall the receptionist**. If you say nothing, **T-Bug covers for you** by posing as a receptionist. A Corpo V can bully her.
4. **Talk to Jackie.**

**Optional: the hotel bar.** If you detour, Jackie goes up alone, grumbling. There is lifepath dialogue with the bartender: a Corpo V gossips about Hanako (and gets a "kiddo" or "baby" exchange); with a Nomad V the bartender spins a made-up story. In the lounge to the right of the bar there is a **Hideo Kojima** cameo (as Hideyoshi Oshima).

Then **take the elevator to floor 42**.

## The room and the Flathead

1. **Head to your room**, **talk to T-Bug** and **scan for the Flathead's entry point**: it's in the **corner between the terrarium and the window**.
2. **Take the control shard** and guide the Flathead. Jackie drives it at first, but the bot gets stuck and it's your turn. The Flathead navigates itself; you tell it where to go and interact with some objects:
   - **The vents** and the **terrarium distraction** for housekeeping.
   - The **CCTV access point**: **switch cams** to see into the hotel netrunner's room and find a way in.
   - The **floor grate** and **hacking the netrunner** (the _dweller_). PowerPyx shows an optional **Intelligence** line here ("Lemme neutralize him"); the value it needs in 2.5 is not confirmed.
3. T-Bug needs a few hours: **sit** (time skip to night) and **talk** to Jackie. He muses about Yorinobu, with lifepath variants (Corpo: "kick the rat outta corp"; Streetkid: "Rich asshole's whim"; Nomad: "ditch the system").
4. **Head to the penthouse** by elevator.

## Yorinobu's penthouse

1. **Go to the safe**, **scan for the hidden switch** (by the **bed**) and **use it**.
2. **Use your personal link on the safe.** Unexpected AVs arrive and **Jackie takes the Relic**.
3. T-Bug warns that Yorinobu is coming up too fast: **hide in the maintenance shaft**, behind the **central TV screen**.
4. **Observe the meeting**: Yorinobu arrives with **Adam Smasher**, then his father **Saburo Arasaka** shows up with his bodyguard **Goro Takemura**. Yorinobu kills Saburo and triggers the hotel lockdown.
5. **Leave the hiding spot.**

**Don't linger in the penthouse.** If you take too long, **Adam Smasher comes back and kills you instantly**, both before you hide and during the escape. Grab what you want quickly (see "Penthouse loot").

## Penthouse loot (missable)

All of this can be taken **before or after hiding**, always without lingering:

- **Kongou:** Yorinobu's Iconic Liberty pistol, with ricochets and no Ballistic Coprocessor needed. Sources disagree on the exact spot: the weapon's page says "on a table at the foot of the bed", the job notes say "on Yorinobu's bedside" and IGN says "the bedside table to the right of his bed". Search around the bed. game8 adds that it only shows up if you scanned it in The Information; no other source confirms that.
- **Yorinobu's formal shirt and pants:** in a suitcase up the **small staircase to the left of the entrance** (IGN).
- **Kotetsu no Ryu coat:** by the seat next to the **bathroom door** (IGN).
- **Iguana Egg:** **behind the tree trunk**, near the **adult iguana**. After **Playing for Time** a bowl appears in your apartment: place the egg there and, after **90 in-game days**, **V's Iguana** hatches. The bowl doesn't exist on PS4 or Xbox One; Switch 2 isn't listed (it should work as on current-gen consoles).
- **Nehan:** Iconic kaiken (throwing knife), added in 2.0. **Loot it from Saburo's body** after the murder, along with his **dog tag**.
- **Satori:** Saburo's Iconic katana, in his AV (see the escape).

## The escape: the balcony and Saburo's AV

1. **Go to the balcony door**: T-Bug manages to open it, but Arasaka detects her and kills her in the process.
2. **Optional (Satori):** by Saburo's body, Jackie suggests the AV is still on the roof. **Take the detour to the landing pad**, **get rid of the two AV guards** and **examine the AV**: **Satori** is **leaning against a seat inside the AV**. It can only be reached **after T-Bug opens the window**. The AV detects intruders, locks down and alerts more security: **head back** quickly.
3. **Escape through the window**, cross the ledge to the **ladder** and **jump**: halfway across you are spotted and there is no other way.
4. **Talk to Jackie** and **call Evelyn**. Jackie plugs the Relic into himself to keep it safe.
5. **Behind Jackie**, while he patches up, there is a **shard** and the **Oxy Booster crafting spec** (IGN). Don't miss it.

**Satori and Nehan together:** combined, they turn bleeding into hemorrhage, and hemorrhage heals you. Satori got a new iconic modifier in 2.0.

## Corridors and elevators

You start **unarmed**.

1. **The first two guards are mandatory takedowns** (Jackie handles one). **Loot their SMGs and silencers.**
2. IGN says **four points in Intelligence** let you connect to the Konpeki terminal near the first guard (unconfirmed for 2.5).
3. The corridors have **a camera on the left** and **a netrunner**. **Distract with the TVs**, use **Breach Protocol** on the access points for the cameras, and **hide the bodies**.
4. **The first elevator is locked down.** At the **second elevator** there is an **armored Arasaka officer** carrying the **Personnel Access Token**. **Take him out** (lethal or not) and **search him**.
5. **Take the elevator.**

## Lobby, garage and drones

1. **Head to the lobby**:
   - **Stealth:** **hug the left wall** to the garage elevator (PowerPyx).
   - **Loud:** four guards, then **an Arasaka mech drops in**. Use grenades and target the already damaged limbs, or **just run for the elevator**.
2. **Save manually** if you're not in combat: the choice about Jackie's body comes right after the chase. **Take the garage elevator** and **sprint to the Delamain**; it's under fire, ignore the enemies and **get in**.
3. **Get rid of the drones:** **one Octant and two Griffins**, sent along with Adam Smasher. **Take down the small ones first** (about two shots each), then the big one.
4. **Talk to Jackie** and **escape.**

## Jackie's body

Jackie doesn't make it. Before dying he hands you the Relic. **Delamain** asks where to take his body. This is the job's important choice (see **Choices**):

- **"Take him home, to his family"**.
- **"Take him to Vik Vektor's clinic"**.
- **"Nowhere. Wait for me here."**

## No-Tell Motel

1. **Get out**: your gear is returned and there is a **Drop Point** by the motel door.
2. **Head to room 204** and **talk to Dex**. What you tell him doesn't matter.
3. **Go to the bathroom**, **wash your face** (or smash the mirror), **leave the bathroom** and **talk to Dex**. His bodyguard knocks you down and Dex shoots you. The story continues in **Love Like Fire**.

## Choices

- **The cut (30 / 35 / 40%)** → only changes the dialogue: Dex never pays. No consequences.
- **"Something I gotta take care of first" or "Let's go"** → the first lets you leave before the point of no return. **Once you get in the Delamain there is no going back until Act 2.**
- **Reception:** staying silent (T-Bug covers) or, as Corpo, bullying → same result.
- **Visiting the bar** → dialogue only; Jackie goes up alone.
- **The AV detour** → **Satori**; skip it and your only later option is buying it from Herold Lowe (Phantom Liberty).
- **Jackie's body — to his family or "wait for me here":** per the wiki, the body goes to **Mama Welles** and the full version of **Heroes** opens up (Act 2): the ofrenda at El Coyote Cojo, the **Key to Jackie's Stash** (it opens **Jackie's Garage** in The Glen, near El Coyote Cojo), Misty, the **Jackie's ARCH** motorcycle, the **La Chingona Dorada** pistols (looted from the ofrenda altar **24 hours after** leaving El Coyote Cojo; 2.5 changed their iconic modifier to Crit Chance) and Jackie's pin-up posters as memorabilia if you scan them. IGN agrees that "wait for me here" gives the full version. Sources don't fully agree: game8 says leaving him in the car means missing Heroes and the ARCH; the wiki and IGN outweigh it. **The safest option is "Take him home, to his family".**
- **Jackie's body — to Viktor's clinic:** short version of **Heroes**: Mama Welles mails the ARCH key to your apartment. Arasaka takes the body and makes an engram of Jackie (Secure Your Soul), which shows up in some endings (IGN says Totalimmortal, PowerPyx The Devil ending, the wiki "several endings").
- **Dex in room 204** → nothing changes.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Kongou** (by the bed), **Nehan** and the dog tag from Saburo's body, the **Iguana Egg** behind the trunk, **Yorinobu's clothes** (suitcase, small staircase on the left) and the **Kotetsu no Ryu coat** (by the bathroom door), **Satori** from the rooftop AV, and the **shard** and **Oxy Booster spec** behind Jackie after the jump. If you miss an iconic, **Herold Lowe** (Phantom Liberty) later sells the ones you didn't get, subject to his conditions.

## Rewards

- The **Relic**.
- Missables: **Kongou**, **Satori**, **Nehan**, **Iguana Egg**, Yorinobu's clothes, the Kotetsu no Ryu coat, the Oxy Booster spec.
- The cut of the job (30–40%) **is never paid**.
- **Next:** **Love Like Fire** (interlude as Johnny) and Act 2 with **Playing for Time**.
