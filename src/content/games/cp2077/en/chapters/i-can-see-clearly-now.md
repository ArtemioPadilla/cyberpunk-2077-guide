**I Can See Clearly Now** is the first job from **Brendan**, a SoftSys vending machine (S.C.S.M.) running an experimental AI, installed outside **Megabuilding H8** in **Japantown** (Westbrook). Westbrook does not open until **Act 2**: if you are still in Watson, leave it for later. According to Brendan's wiki page, after **Playing for Time** you can see the techie configuring him behind the dumpster; later, when you walk past the machine, Brendan calls you over and the chain starts.

> **Order:** walk past Megabuilding H8 → Brendan calls you → move the dumpster → talk to Brendan.

## The dumpster

Brendan complains that a dumpster is blocking his view and asks you to move it. Moving it takes a **Body check**, and the value needed rises with your level. If you cannot pass it yet, come back when you have more Body.

## What comes next

As thanks, Brendan gives you a **NiCola** and considers you a friend. The chain continues with **Spray Paint** (the marker returns a day or more later) and ends with **Coin Operated Boy**.

## Rewards

- A **NiCola**.
- The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
