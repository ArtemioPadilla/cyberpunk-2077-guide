**Somewhat Damaged** follows Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos. The MaxTac truck has crashed and Songbird has fled into **Cynosure**, a secret Militech facility under Dogtown. In there the Blackwall is consuming her, and what's left of her lets you in alone… only to set a **Cerberus** on you, a maintenance robot you **can't damage or hack**. It's a **stealth and survival** job, Alien: Isolation style. At the end, in the core, Songbird makes a request, and your answer decides the ending: **King of Cups** or **King of Pentacles**.

> **Order:** MaxTac truck → follow the Blackwall traces (2 sealed Body 20 gates or floor-exit panels into the water) → deep shaft → terminal: talk to Reed → elevator (Johnny is cut off) → memory of Songbird and Hansen → lockdown: terminals **Alpha** and **Bravo** → the Cerberus hunts you → Living Quarters → terminal **Sierra** (Engineering) → terminal **Victor** (Security) → Sector 3 gate → outrun it over the bridge → airlocks → Core Control → shut down 3 subsystems → back to Core Control → the core → **choice**.

> **Get ready:** have **Body 20** if you can (gates and the Crowbar) and some **Technical Ability** and **Intelligence** for extras. Bring **grenades** as decoys. **Don't use the Sandevistan**: it malfunctions here, gives no slow-mo and drains about 5 HP every 2 seconds. The **Dash**, **Lynx Paws**, crouch-sprinting and **Optical Camo** all help. The game **autosaves every time the Cerberus enters the vents**, but make your own save before the end too.

## Searching for Songbird

1. From the hole in the wall, head down to the crashed **MaxTac truck** and search it: there are traces of the **Blackwall**, the thread that leads to Songbird.
2. To follow the traces you have two options:
   - **Force open two sealed gates** (**Body 20**).
   - **Bypass them** through a **floor-exit panel** and swim through the flooded passages.
3. **Iconic Crowbar:** after forcing **the first sealed gate** (Body 20), turn **right**: it's **on top of some crates in the corner**. You only need to open one of the two gates.
4. You can chat briefly with Johnny before plunging down a **deep shaft**. Get out of the water and follow the traces to a room with a terminal and the Cynosure logo.
5. **Jack in** to talk to Reed. Johnny comments depending on whether you did **Shot by Both Sides** (he says Bree's videos probably came from here). Songbird cuts the line before talking to you.

## Deeper into Cynosure

1. Take the **elevator**. Songbird severs the link with **Johnny**: from here on you're alone.
2. Go on until a **"memory"** of Songbird talking with Hansen and talk briefly with her. Next to that terminal there's a facility **Site Map** you can check like the Firestarter dossier: take it.
3. **Force open a door** (**Body** check).
4. In the office at the bottom of the elevator area, a computer shows strange messages from a possible AI: read the first and look at the screen for a few seconds for the other two to appear.

## The lockdown: Alpha and Bravo

You see Songbird just past the gateway to a new area. If you approach, she closes the gate and starts a **lockdown**. Disable it by shutting down two terminals:

- **Alpha:** enter the **Server Room** by forcing the shutters (**Body** check) and jack into terminal Alpha with your personal link: **Breach Protocol**.
- **Bravo:** shutting down Alpha opens the floor exit in that room. Drop down, continue to a ladder and climb up to Bravo. Songbird blocks your hack: **scan the terminal** to see its power cables and **crawl underneath** to unplug it.

## Escaping the Cerberus

As you crawl out from under Bravo, **a ceiling grate falls on you** and the **Cerberus** appears, controlled by Songbird.

1. **Remove the grate** (QTE): fail and it's game over.
2. After the scene, **run** into the only open room. On the left there's an open **floor-exit panel**: drop through it to hide. (Before dropping, another map lies by the computers.)
3. Continue to a ladder up to the **Living Quarters**. There's another map on a bed and, in the next room, another memory: Songbird being operated on by the FIA.

**From here on the Cerberus hunts you throughout Cynosure.**

## Cerberus rules

- **It can't be damaged or hacked.** Outside scripted moments, **if it detects you, you die**.
- **The HUD glitches** when it's near.
- Unless provoked, it follows a **fixed patrol route** through the hallways. You can follow it until it enters the **ceiling vents**: that's when you have the most time before it comes back. **The game autosaves every time it enters the vents.**
- **Firing a weapon or throwing a grenade lures it instantly.** Use that: **throw a grenade far away** so it goes to investigate there.
- **Completing objectives draws it** into the room through the door or the ceiling: hide or leave before it arrives.
- **Safe rooms** it won't enter (unless chasing you or reacting to noise): the **closet outside the Engineering entrance**, **Data Analysis Research**, **Experimental Prototyping**, **Neural Networking** and **Datafort Central Command**.
- **Mobility:** **Dash** to dart between cover; **Lynx Paws** and crouch-sprinting to move fast without noise; **Optical Camo** hides you at long range or while dashing (at medium range, with Dash, the Cerberus comments as if it detected you but doesn't pursue).
- **The Sandevistan is useless:** it malfunctions, slows nothing and keeps draining your health.

## Sierra, Victor and the Erebus

**Terminal Sierra (Engineering).** Leave through the door directly across from Engineering (**Body** check) or through the doors across from Administration: right, then right again at the intersection. In Engineering, on your right is Sierra's room: jack in and do the **Breach Protocol**. That **draws the Cerberus**; wait until it's safe and leave.

**Terminal Victor (Security).** Leave Engineering, go right to the intersection and right again toward the Sector 3 gate. **Security** is the **second door on the left**. The first room has a map; the second has a hiding spot on the left. The last one holds Victor: **Breach Protocol**. As you leave you see another memory of Songbird after her surgery.

**Erebus crafting spec (optional, missable).** Across from the Engineering entrance is **Maintenance**, with its door panel unpowered.

1. Turn left, enter the **Storage Area** and go to the back room.
2. On the wall is a **distribution box**: **rewire** it with **Technical Ability** to power the panel.
3. Go back and enter Maintenance: the **Erebus** spec is straight ahead.
4. You can leave through the doors or climb the ladders into the ceiling vents, which drop you into **Sector 3**, past the gate. **Careful:** leaving through the vents **locks you out of the previous area** (and skips Sierra and Victor if you hadn't done them).

## Sector 3 and the airlocks

1. Use the terminal and wait for the **Sector 3 gate** to unseal.
2. Soon you'll have to **run from the Cerberus**: **flee down the only path** and **cross the bridge**.
3. Reach an **airlock**: enter, **close the door** behind you and wait for it to cycle. The next airlock has another memory (Myers and Songbird taking the oath).
4. Follow the Blackwall traces to **Core Control**.

## Shutting down the core: three subsystems

Talk to Songbird and use the terminal to start the shutdown. **Three subsystems** stay online and must be shut down by hand. Turning around, another memory (Reed and Songbird). Head into **Labs**:

- **Neural Network:** on your left in Labs, across from another memory. **Unplug 3 cables** from the subsystem. The Cerberus arrives and resumes the hunt.
- **Datafort Central Command:** go straight into the **Observation Room**; on the left is room 1, then rooms 2 and 3. **Destroy 6 firewall devices: 2 in room 1 and 4 in room 3.** Each time you destroy one, the **Cerberus drops from one of three vents** (in room 1, in room 3 and outside the entrance): hide as soon as you break each one.
- **Thermic Control:** from the Observation Room, go straight ahead. Hack the terminal with **high Intelligence**, or **scan it** to trace the cables to an **outside electrical panel**: open the shutters, climb out, override the panel and climb back in (another memory).
- **Experimental Prototyping (optional, missable):** near Neural Network and the Observation Room. Get in with **Intelligence** or code **714212**: inside is the spec for the **Militech Canto (Mk.6)** cyberdeck. It's also a safe room.

## The core and the choice

1. Evade the Cerberus back to **Core Control** (there's one last memory in the Observation Room). Use the terminal: a scripted sequence and the **Cerberus falls**.
2. **Pick up the Behavioral system component** from the fallen Cerberus (missable).
3. **Enter the core** and **check Songbird's vitals**. Talk to her: she asks you to kill her.

**The choice:**

- **"Kill So Mi: Fine."** (PowerPyx: "KILL SO MI | OK I'LL DO IT") → you grant her request: **King of Cups**. **Song So Mi dies. No cure and no Tower.**
- **"Spare So Mi: Won't do it."** or "…need the FIA's help. So I need you alive." (PowerPyx: "AIN'T OVER YET", "THAT'S NOT A SOLUTION", "SPARE SO MI | WON'T DO IT") → you refuse: **King of Pentacles**. Songbird survives (as Myers' Blackwall puppet) and **The Tower becomes possible**.

## Choices

- **Body 20 at the gates or swimming** → with Body 20 you get the **Crowbar**; swimming skips it.
- **Rewiring the distribution box (Technical Ability)** → the **Erebus** spec. Leaving through the vents locks the area.
- **Intelligence or code 714212** → the **Militech Canto Mk.6** spec.
- **Taking the Cerberus component** → with at least one spec, it opens **This Corrosion** about 24 hours later: Yoko decrypts it in Kabuki and you craft **only one**: Erebus **or** Canto Mk.6. Later, NetWatch agents ambush you to confiscate it.
- **Killing Songbird** → **King of Cups**. In Leave in Silence, Myers offers no thanks (just €$5000); in Four Score and Seven, Reed bitterly concedes you were right and the story ends. **No cure.**
- **Sparing Songbird** → **King of Pentacles**. In Leave in Silence, Myers offers the **NUSA distinction** and €$30,000; in Four Score and Seven, Reed promises the cure and **Who Wants to Live Forever** (The Tower) opens.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Crowbar**, the **Erebus** and **Militech Canto** specs, and the **Behavioral system component** from the fallen Cerberus. All four are lost if you leave without them.

## Rewards

- Crowbar (iconic, missable).
- Erebus and Militech Canto Mk.6 crafting specs (missable).
- Behavioral system component (missable).
