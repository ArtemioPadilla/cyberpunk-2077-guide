**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, sends you to **Vasquez Pass**, by the nearest road off **Interstate 9**. Her gigs open in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. It's one of the two in her first set, with **Flying Drugs**. The Badlands are full of "disarmed" minefields from 2071 that smugglers use as routes. One of them got stranded in the middle of one, and Dakota wants his vehicle: some nomads pay well for that GPS data.

> **Order:** find the vehicle → approach it → get in → (optional) examine the body → get rid of the pursuers → deliver the vehicle to the garage → park inside → get out.

## How to do it

The hazards are **mines** and **drones**. The wiki has no detailed walkthrough, so move carefully toward the vehicle and watch your step. If you like, examine the smuggler's body before you drive off. Since **Update 2.0** the gig includes a **car chase**: lose the pursuers before you take the vehicle to Dakota's garage.
