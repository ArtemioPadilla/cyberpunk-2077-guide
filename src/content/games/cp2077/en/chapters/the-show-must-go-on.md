Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. This job is the epilogue to Mr. Hands's gig **Spy in the Jungle**. It only appears if, in the last talk with the agents, you **left the biomonitor at the Drop Point or destroyed it**. A few days later **Steven Santos** texts you to meet. The wiki puts the meeting at the **Wellsprings docks** (Heywood), on Gold Beach; the gig's page says instead that he asks to meet in Downtown, so follow the map marker.

> **Order:** Steven's message → Wellsprings docks → talk to Steven.

## The meeting

There is no fight and no choice. Go to the marker and talk to Steven. He hands you **€$3,000** and catches you up: his career is on the rise, **Ana** has been fired and the **SovOil** deal is moving along the way Brazil wanted. Johnny, in the journal, has no respect for him, but the money is yours.

If you gave the biomonitor to Ana in Spy in the Jungle, this job doesn't appear: instead you find Steven at the EBM Petrochem Stadium, now with BARGHEST and bitter about it.

## Rewards

- €$3,000
- 1,832 XP
- 167 Street Cred
