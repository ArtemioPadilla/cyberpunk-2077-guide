**Johnny Silverhand**'s chain with **Rogue** is two side jobs: **Chippin' In** and **Blistering Love**. Chippin' In unlocks when you finish **Tapeworm**, after you wake at the **Hotel Pistis Sophia** in Pacifica and talk to Johnny. game8 places it in Act 3, but it is still **before the point of no return** (the Embers entrance in Nocturne Op55N1). Johnny wants back what he lost in 2023: his jacket, his gun, his Porsche and, above all, a reckoning with Rogue. The final conversation, at a grave in the **Northern Oil Fields**, decides whether you carry on with Rogue (Blistering Love) or Johnny sends you to Kerry.

> **For the ending:** this chain is the key to **The Sun** (with Rogue) and to the **secret ending** ((Don't Fear) The Reaper), and one of the two routes that open **Temperance**. The Sun needs the right answers at the Chippin' In grave **and** finishing Blistering Love. The secret ending depends only on **three answers** at the grave (below); later, on Misty's rooftop in Nocturne Op55N1, you must **wait about 5 minutes** without picking anything. **It does not depend on Johnny affinity**: the famous "70% affinity" is a myth. Fail the grave and the Rogue route is gone for good (Temperance stays open if you do Panam's chain). Johnny has a one-off romance with Rogue in Blistering Love: **V's body type and voice do not matter**.

> **Order:** Afterlife (pills: A Cool Metal Fire) → wait for Rogue's call → Afterlife (jacket in the trunk) → Ebunike Docks → Grayson's ship → Grayson fight → Porsche in the container → grave at the Northern Oil Fields → **Blistering Love:** call Rogue → pick her up at the Afterlife in the evening → Silver Pixel Cloud drive-in → projection booth → Johnny and Rogue.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save before the grave** at the Northern Oil Fields; it is the choice that opens or closes The Sun and the secret ending. At the Ebunike Docks, **Technical Ability 9** or **Body 6** help (game8's numbers; guides don't confirm the exact 2.x thresholds). The ship fight is against Maelstrom and ends with **Grayson**, who does not die in the fight: you decide afterwards. You can get the Porsche whether you spare Grayson or kill him, but if you kill him the game moves you off the ship and it is easy to forget.

## Chippin' In

### A Cool Metal Fire (Johnny's memory)

Go to the **Afterlife** and take the **pseudoendotrizine** when Johnny asks. You relive a night in 2023 as Johnny:

1. Johnny drinks at the bar.
2. At **Cassius Ryder**'s studio, **choose a tattoo**: "Johnny's Special" or "Together Forever". Neither changes the story; the reward is **Johnny's aviators**.
3. The empathy club and **Ruby**.
4. The car crash; Ruby writes "**Ebunike**", the clue to where the Porsche ended up.
5. A Northside motel.

This memory raises Johnny affinity to 30%.

### Meeting Rogue

Wait **a day** for Rogue's call and return to the **Afterlife**. After the chat with **Weyland**, go out to the car with her and **take the replica of Johnny's Samurai jacket from the trunk**. The next stop is the **Ebunike Docks**, Maelstrom turf: that is where **Grayson**, Johnny's gun and his Porsche are.

### Ebunike Docks

The docks are full of Maelstrom. Find the **dataterm** to get into the area. game8 marks a short route:

- **Technical Ability 9:** opens the east gate.
- **Technical Ability 9:** the storehouse side door.
- **Body 6:** the storehouse back window.

Without those attributes, go in by stealth or guns blazing; the route only saves a fight.

### The ship

Board the ship. Before combat starts you can **scan** optional clues about **Adam Smasher**:

- The **Kang Tao crates** (Smasher was behind the Kang Tao raid).
- A **woman's body** (Smasher's signature).
- The **audio recordings** (the Gilchrist blackmail).
- The **"Tri-phet" packages**. **Known bug since Patch 2.2:** combat can start before you can scan them.

**Grayson** waits at the end. The fight ends with him **defeated, not dead**. Interrogate him: Smasher is in **Japan**. Take back the **Malorian Arms 3516**, Johnny's gun.

### The Porsche (easy to miss)

Johnny's **Porsche 911 Turbo (930)** is in a container on the ship. Get it **before you leave**:

1. Get the **access key**: Grayson hands it over if you spare him, or you loot it from his body if you kill him.
2. Climb the **crane ladder** on the ship's right side and press the button to lower the container.
3. Go back down, open the container with the key and **remove the canvas**.

If you kill Grayson, the game steers you off the ship right away: don't let it take you away without the car.

### The grave at the Northern Oil Fields

Off the ship, Johnny takes you to a grave in the **Northern Oil Fields** to talk. This is the choice of the whole chain. **Save before talking.**

**The three answers that open the secret ending and Blistering Love** (Fandom and game8 agree):

1. Johnny: "Not to fuck this up, what we have." → **"Nah, fucked that up too."**
2. Johnny: "Is it too late to ask for a second chance?" → **"What do you want from me?"**
3. Johnny: "At least, so it seemed... till now." → **"OK. But as second chances go, this is your last."**

**The safest full sequence** (PowerPyx; it includes the three above, and one player reports that a single early line alone didn't get them the ending):

1. "[Inscribe Johnny's Initials] Let's do something about that."
2. "The Guy who Saved My Life."
3. "Nah, fucked that up too."
4. "What do you want from me?"
5. "OK. But as second chances go, this is your last."
6. "You were a real dick in the beginning."
7. "When you said you let down your friends…"
8. "Smasher biz really got to her."
9. "Yeah, I'll call Rogue."

**If you answer anything else** (for example, you say "Nah, fucked that up too" and then refuse the second chance), Johnny sends you to Kerry: **Holdin' On** starts and **Blistering Love never appears**. You lose The Sun with Rogue and the secret ending.

Fandom lists another combination that opens Blistering Love **without** the secret ending ("A hard road, but looks like we made it." → "Johnny, no need to bare your soul to me."); only that source gives it, so don't rely on it if you want the secret ending.

## Blistering Love

If the grave went well, Johnny wants a date with Rogue. It is short and cheap, and **it is required for The Sun with Rogue**.

1. **Call Rogue** and ask her out. She picks the **Silver Pixel Cloud** drive-in in North Oak.
2. **Pick her up at the Afterlife in the evening.** **Emmerick Bronson** is there. If you own the Porsche, it is called automatically and there is extra dialogue.
3. At the drive-in, the door code is **0000**. Climb to the **projection booth**, start the film (_Bushidō X: Fade to Black_) and **take the pills**.
4. As Johnny, choose: **kiss Rogue** (she stops it) or **say he has little to offer**. Both end the same way: Rogue leaves and Johnny takes an Omega blocker.

Afterwards, Johnny asks you to go and see **Kerry**: **Holdin' On** starts (see Kerry's page).

**Is Blistering Love needed for the secret ending?** Sources disagree: PowerPyx includes it in the steps, game8 says it isn't needed and Fandom's endings table only asks for the grave dialogue. Do it anyway: The Sun with Rogue needs it and it costs nothing.

## Johnny affinity: the 70% myth

Johnny has an affinity meter that rises with Tapeworm, A Cool Metal Fire (30%), certain Chippin' In lines (40%), Blistering Love (50%), Holdin' On (60%) and A Like Supreme (70%). Fandom says affinity **does not seem to have any major impact** on the game. **The secret ending does not depend on affinity**: it depends only on your answers at the Chippin' In grave. Don't chase the percentage.

## Choices

- **Tattoo at Cassius Ryder's** ("Johnny's Special" or "Together Forever") → cosmetic only; the aviators come either way.
- **Spare Grayson** → he gives you the container key; for the Porsche, sparing or killing him makes no difference.
- **Kill Grayson** → you loot the key from his body; the game pushes you off the ship and the **Porsche** is easy to forget.
- **Grave: the three right answers** → **Blistering Love** and the **secret ending** stay open; afterwards Johnny sends you to Kerry anyway. **Permanent.**
- **Grave: anything else** → straight to **Holdin' On**; **you lose Blistering Love, The Sun with Rogue and the secret ending**. Temperance stays open only if you do Panam's chain. **Permanent.**
- **Blistering Love: kiss Rogue or say he has little to offer** → same result; the Johnny–Rogue romance continues briefly in For Whom the Bell Tolls (The Sun route).
- **Secret ending** → on Misty's rooftop (Nocturne Op55N1), wait about **5 minutes** (5:08) for Johnny to suggest the plan; pick "Let's hear this plan" → "[Attack Arasaka Tower] Means goin' out with a bang – I like it". In that version of The Sun, **Rogue survives**. You cannot save during that ending; sources disagree on whether there are checkpoints, so keep a rooftop save.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Samurai jacket** (Rogue's trunk), the **Malorian Arms 3516** (Grayson), the **Porsche 911 Turbo (930)** (ship container, via the crane on the right side) and the **aviators** (A Cool Metal Fire). If you want the Smasher lore, scan the Kang Tao crates and the recordings before the fight.

## Rewards

- **Chippin' In:** replica of Johnny's Samurai jacket, **Malorian Arms 3516**, **Porsche 911 Turbo (930)** and Johnny's aviators (from A Cool Metal Fire).
- **Blistering Love:** 290 XP and 433 Street Cred; opens the **The Sun** route with Rogue.
