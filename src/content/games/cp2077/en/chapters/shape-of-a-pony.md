**Shape of a Pony** was added in **Update 2.1**. It starts when you buy the **Porsche 911 Turbo Cabriolet (930)** on the **AUTOFIXER** netpage for **€$73,000** (this guide's vehicle notes list it for sale at Street Cred 26). **Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes** texts you: his service normally delivers the cars, but this time the seller vanished. It is Act 2 content: it takes you to **Santo Domingo** and the **Badlands**.

> **Order:** read and answer the message → abandoned parking lot on the outskirts of Arroyo → inspect the body → makeshift garage south of the EBM Petrochem Stadium → take out the Wraiths and grab the key → get in the car → text El Capitán.

## How to do it

Reply to Muamar to get the car's last known location: an **abandoned parking lot** on the outskirts of **Arroyo**. The car is gone; there is only a body. Johnny shows up and mocks it: it is **Johnny Silverman**, an impersonator of his. Inspect the body: it carries two **shards**, a conversation with Muamar and a set of **GPS coordinates** that load into your navigation.

Follow them to a makeshift garage south of the **EBM Petrochem Stadium**, in **Biotechnica Flats**. Several **Wraiths** surround the car: take them out and loot the **car key** from one of them. Get in the Porsche and text Muamar that you have it.

## Rewards

The **Porsche 911 Turbo Cabriolet (930)**, and Muamar refunds half the price: **€$37,000** per the wiki infobox.
