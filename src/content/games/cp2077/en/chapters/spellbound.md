**Spellbound** comes from **Nix**, the **Afterlife** netrunner (Little China) and an expert in antique tech. He texts you when you approach the Afterlife after Rogue sends you to Panam in **Ghost Town**, or after you finish **KOLD MIRAGE** (Act 2). He wants you to buy **The Book of Spells** on his behalf, a rare piece the netrunner **R3n0** is selling, in case the deal goes wrong; he'll reimburse you and pay for the errand. Spellbound and KOLD MIRAGE can't be active at the same time: whichever appears first must be finished before the other shows up.

> **Order:** talk to Nix → call R3n0 → go to Au Cabanon → get the book → open the stash → take the book → (optional) decrypt it → return to Nix.

## With R3n0

Go to the **Au Cabanon** restaurant in **Charter Hill** (Westbrook), next to the Lele Park fast travel point. R3n0 waits at the bar with a laptop.

- **Pay €$5,500** → a clean deal.
- **Corpo lifepath** → you work out she's stealing from her employer and blackmail her down to **€$1,800**.
- **Attack her** (scanning her laptop also turns her hostile) → defeat her and jack into the laptop for the coordinates. Once you've paid her, the laptop can no longer be accessed.

The book is in a **briefcase next to a vending machine** in the shopping plaza across the street: a Zetatech XPR tablet. You can **decrypt it yourself** from the Shards menu: it gives eddies and some quickhacks, and makes **KOLD MIRAGE** start as soon as you finish Spellbound (otherwise it takes several days).

## Choices

- **With Nix:** he pays **€$15,000**. With enough **Intelligence** you point out the book is worth much more and he raises it to **€$22,000**.
- **If you saw R3n0's post on the Smuggler's Cache netpage first**, Nix pretends he isn't interested and won't reimburse the purchase, but you can use **Cool** options to make him pay double or triple. Per the wiki, unconfirmed.

Doing Spellbound and KOLD MIRAGE with Nix alive gets you his help in **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** (Phantom Liberty).

## Rewards

- 206 XP, 308 Street Cred and **€$15,000** (up to €$22,000).
