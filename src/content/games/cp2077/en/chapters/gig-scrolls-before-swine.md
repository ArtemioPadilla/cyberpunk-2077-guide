NCPD sergeant **Aaron McCarlson**, a former consultant on one of **Regina Jones**'s shows, says he found the warehouse where **Maelstrom** tortures people, on the corner of Industrial and Eisenhower St (Northside, Watson), and wants the security cam footage for his case. It is in **set 6 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 5 (Last Login and Small Man, Big Evil). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** get inside the warehouse → find the computer with the footage → retrieve the footage → bring it to Aaron (optional: tell him you know what he did) → leave Aaron's building.

## The warehouse

- There are **six** Maelstromers: three outside and three inside.
- The computer is in a room on the **second floor**: go in through an open hatch on the roof, or through any door and up the stairs.
- **Before copying it**, watch the video in the "FILES" tab; stealing it deletes it. It shows **Aaron murdering a woman** in the alley behind the warehouse, and it opens the optional objective.

## With Aaron

His apartment is on the second floor of a small building just north of the Longshore North fast travel point. Use the call button to the left of the door. If you got the footage without alerting anyone, he compliments you.

## Choices

- **Hand over the footage and say nothing** → normal pay; Regina says winning Aaron's trust gives her NCPD connections.
- **Tell him you know and extort him** → he pays double.
- **Demand he goes to jail** → he turns hostile (and can't be targeted by quickhacks). **If you kill him**, there's no payout, though there is money in the apartment, and Regina criticizes you. **If you knock him out**, Regina is pleased: she'll use him as her asset in the NCPD.
- If you do it in Act 2, Johnny reacts to the video and to your choice.

## Rewards

- €$4,800 (depends on your choice), 1,000 XP and 906 Street Cred.
