**Birds with Broken Wings** sets up the heist. Reed and Alex want to intercept the Cassel twins, take their identities, meet Hansen, grab both the matrix and Songbird, and kill Hansen for the attempt on the President's life. That means hacking **Charon Exotics** (the company that rented the twins their car) from its tracking stations and getting an implant that copies the netrunners' biometrics. Along the way, Songbird asks to meet you alone and Alex invites you for a drink. In this job Songbird gives you **3 more Relic points**.

> **Order:** wait a day for Reed's call → Alex's safehouse: briefing → leave → 1st tracking station (Songbird contacts you) → optional meeting with Songbird at Tranquil Terrace (11 PM to 1 AM) → neutralize the enemies and access the transceiver → 2nd transceiver (remote with high Intelligence, or restart it) → wait a few hours → The Moth with Alex (optional) → Farida's clinic → implant.

> **Get ready:** the Songbird meeting can only happen **between 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM**; if you want to see her (and her confession), adjust the time before moving on with the transceivers. You lose nothing material if you skip it, but it's the most important talk with her before Firestarter. With high **Intelligence**, you skip the second station.

## The safehouse briefing

1. **Wait a day** for Reed to call and talk to him.
2. Go to **Alex's safehouse** and talk to Reed and Alex. Take part in the briefings.
3. In the basement there's a **board** with BARGHEST's top brass: Kurt Hansen, Jago Szabó, Albert Murphy, Chester Bennett, an unidentified member, Yuri Bychkov, Carl Robinson and Leon Rinder. The last three can be killed before this job; if you did, their photo is **crossed out with a red X**.
4. Follow Alex and talk to her. **Before leaving, check the safehouse armory:** a weapon case holds the **Nokota NDI Osprey**, an iconic sniper rifle that fires explosive rounds. If you miss it, you can come back later through the safehouse's back entrance (a camera scans you and opens the door), and failing that you can buy it from Herold Lowe at the EBM Petrochem Stadium. Then **leave the safehouse**. The plan: install an encryption key at the **Charon Exotics tracking stations** to take control of the twins' car.

When you slot the shard you're given at the start, the codename "Phantom Liberty" briefly appears while it boots.

## The meeting with Songbird (optional)

As you leave The Moth, Songbird asks to see you in private.

- **Go to Tranquil Terrace between 11:00 PM and 1:00 AM.** Talk to her, follow her and talk again.
- Songbird reminisces about old times and, when you confront her, **confesses that she was the one who told Hansen when and where Space Force One would be**. Her plan was to run away with you and the matrix and save the President, but her capture changed everything. Now she wants to escape Hansen and take the matrix to a ripperdoc who can cure you both.
- Afterwards you **call Reed** and talk to him.

This talk doesn't change the story on its own, but it gives you the full picture for the **Firestarter** decision. Tranquil Terrace also comes back on Songbird's path.

## The tracking stations

**First station:**

1. **Neutralize all enemies** near the transceiver.
2. **Access the tracking station's transceiver** and talk to Alex.

**Second station:**

- **With high Intelligence:** you access the second transceiver **remotely** and don't have to go there.
- **Without it:** the transceiver is broken and you have to **restart it**:
  - A kid, **Luka**, offers hints for **€$1000** in a hot-or-cold game. Refuse once and he drops to **€$10**; refuse again and he goes no lower.
  - **Or go straight there:** the panel is **at the end of the roof, to your left when facing away from the station**. The button can always be pressed.
  - **Use the control panel** to restart the power, **wait** for it to come back and **download the data** from the transceiver. You can talk to Johnny (optional).

## A night with Alex (optional)

After the data, you **wait a few hours** for Reed's call. Meanwhile Alex messages you: **reply to her message**.

- If you accept, go to **The Moth**, talk to Alex and sit down. You talk about who you'd want to be if you weren't who you are. If you like, **you can dance**; Alex picks "Hardest to Be" on the jukebox.
- Leave the bar when you're done.

## Farida's clinic

1. Reed calls: he sends you to a ripperdoc for an implant that uses the netrunners' biometrics. He also wants to take Songbird with him and thinks she has to be put on ICE first, so the implant includes a **module to ICE Songbird**.
2. Go to **Farida's clinic**, use the intercom and talk to Farida and Reed.
3. **Sit in the chair** and proceed with the operation. You get the **Behavioral Imprint-synced Faceplate** (the faceplate that will copy a Cassel's behavior).
4. Talk to Johnny. He sums up the dilemma: on one side, a woman trying to survive as a living nuke who might drag you into the blast; on the other, a man of principle trying to do right who might wreck the whole thing.
5. Get up and **exit the clinic**. The job ends and I've Seen That Face Before begins.

## Choices

- **Going to the Songbird meeting or not** → if you go, you hear her confession (she leaked Space Force One's route) and her plan. It locks no path, but it's the basis for deciding in Firestarter.
- **Paying Luka** → €$1000, or €$10 if you refuse once; or skip him by going to the roof panel.
- **Going to The Moth with Alex** → an optional scene with her (and a dance).

> **Don't leave without:** the **Osprey** from Alex's armory and spending Songbird's **3 new Relic points**. If you still have Dogtown gigs or side jobs to do (above all **Balls to the Wall**), do them before finishing the next job: Firestarter follows without a break.

## Rewards

- **Nokota NDI Osprey** (iconic sniper rifle).
- **Behavioral Imprint-synced Faceplate**.
- 3 Relic points.
