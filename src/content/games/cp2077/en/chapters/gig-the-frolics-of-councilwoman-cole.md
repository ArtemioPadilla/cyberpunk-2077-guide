**The Frolics of Councilwoman Cole** is a gig from **Dino Dinovic**, the **City Center** fixer. It takes place at the **Gold Beach Marina**, a private yacht harbor in **Downtown**, on Gold Beach street (Dino says "Cannery Row", but the actual place is in Downtown). It belongs to Dino's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Serial Suicide** and **An Inconvenient Killer**. **Eva Cole** is a council member who goes after corporate corruption by day and parties on a yacht at the marina by night. A corp she hurt wants the recordings to pressure her. Dino says there are more eddies if you do it quietly.

> **Order:** go to the docks → (optional) remain undetected → get inside the docks' security room → download the recordings and send them to the fixer → escape the docks → collect your bonus from the Drop Point.

## The marina

The marina has its own security (in the gig, **Militech** guards) and had just installed new systems. You do not need to board the yachts: the recordings are downloaded from the docks' **security room**. Sneak in, download them and leave without setting off the alarm to collect the bonus at the Drop Point.

## Afterwards

According to the wiki, if you hand over the recordings, radio news later reports that Cole's political career tanked over leaked footage.

## Rewards

- €$7,270 (plus the bonus for staying undetected).
