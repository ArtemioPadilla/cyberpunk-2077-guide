**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, puts you in touch with **Pedro Aimar** at the **Las Palapas Motel** on Wollesen St., in **Arroyo (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his last set, with **For My Son** and **Going-away Party**: it opens after **Cuckoo's Nest** and **Severance Package**, in **Act 2**. According to the wiki, this gig becomes available during **Ghost Town**. Pedro, a **Zetatech** coder, has ALS and needs money for a full-body conversion his company refused to pay for, so he's stealing one of its trucks loaded with AV thruster parts.

> **Order:** meet Pedro at the motel → take his shard → be at the Kenmore Diner at 5 PM → take a seat and wait for his call → take the passenger seat → slot in the shard → wait for the hack → lose the pursuers → drive to Tirempire → get out and talk to Pedro.

## Step by step

Take the shard before you leave the room. At the **Kenmore Diner**, sit down and wait until **5 PM**: Pedro stops the truck with traffic barriers. **Get in the passenger seat quickly**; if you take too long, Zetatech guards arrive (killing them is optional). Slot in the shard and Pedro takes control.

On the way, Zetatech security catches up and there's a shootout. Once the pursuers are down, you have to **drive** the rest of the way to the **Tirempire** garage near Megabuilding H4. Park and get out to close the gig.

## Afterwards

It's required for **Ride Captain Ride**, El Capitán's reward for finishing all his Santo Domingo gigs: the iconic **Bloody Maria** shotgun, which turns up in your apartment's stash.
