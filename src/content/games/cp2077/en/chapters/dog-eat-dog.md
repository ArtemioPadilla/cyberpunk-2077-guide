**Dog Eat Dog** is the first main job of **Phantom Liberty**. A stranger who calls herself **Songbird** contacts V from a plane, **Space Force One**, which is about to come down in **Dogtown**, the walled Pacifica district Kurt Hansen runs with his **BARGHEST** militia. On board is the President of the New United States, **Rosalind Myers**, and Songbird wants V to get her out before BARGHEST captures her. In return she promises a **cure** for the Relic. To prove she means it, she installs Militech software that opens the sixth attribute tree, **Relic**. Reward: 3050 XP and 430 Street Cred.

> **Order:** Songbird's call → Dogtown's northern gate → talk to Songbird → EBM Petrochem Stadium underground parking (stealth or fight) → generator and access point → elevator platforms → catwalk → black market → construction site → roof → jump down the chute toward the crash site.

> **Get ready:** this job runs straight into Hole in the Sky, which can fail and block the whole expansion, so arrive with ammo, healing and a build you can use on foot. Make a manual save before entering the parking garage: the **smuggler caches** are lost for good once you leave that area. Phantom Liberty takes place **before** the Nocturne Op55N1 point of no return, so you don't need to finish the main story first.

## How and when Phantom Liberty starts

**Normal playthrough.** The journal shows a tracker quest called "Phantom Liberty". It completes when you have finished all of these: The Heist, Playing for Time, Automatic Love, The Space in Between, Disasterpiece, Double Life, M'ap Tann Pèlen, I Walk the Line, Transmission and Never Fade Away. In other words, the call comes after the meeting with the Voodoo Boys and Alt is resolved; game8 says it comes after Transmission **no matter how you handled the Voodoo Boys**. Songbird calls shortly after and Dog Eat Dog opens.

- If you turn her down at first ("Nah, not convinced"), you are **not locked out**: you can pick it up later.
- Your save is still in Act 2: you can play Phantom Liberty, go back to Night City and finish the game later. Phantom Liberty also adds an ending of its own (The Tower) that depends on what you choose here.

**Skipping straight to Phantom Liberty.** When you start a new game you get the option "Skip ahead to Phantom Liberty".

- It auto-completes your lifepath prologue and every main job up to Transmission, plus the Delamain jobs up to Don't Lose Your Mind (with Delamain's core reset). Down on the Street and Ghost Town are still available.
- You start reading a book at a stand in Coastview, outside the Voodoo Boys' chapel in Pacifica, with a starter kit. **You cannot edit that character's attributes.**
- According to game8, attribute checks are lower on a skip-ahead save. Per rpgsite, you start at **level 20** with uncommon (green) gear; it is the only source with a number, so treat it as approximate.

**Level.** No reliable source gives a recommended level; since 2.0 enemies scale with your level. Phantom Liberty raises the level cap from 50 to **60**.

## The call and the Dogtown gate

1. Answer the call from the unknown number and talk to the mystery caller.
2. Go to the Dogtown border, at the **northern gate**. Look for Songbird and talk to her.
3. Songbird explains the plan: Space Force One is going to land (crash) somewhere in Dogtown, and you have to reach Myers before BARGHEST does.

**The Relic tree.** During this talk Songbird gives you the Militech software: the **Relic** tree unlocks automatically and gives you **3 Relic points**. There are **15 Relic points** in total:

- **3** from Songbird here, in Dog Eat Dog.
- **3** more from Songbird in Birds with Broken Wings.
- **9** at the **Operational Data Terminals** spread across Dogtown: EBM Petrochem Stadium (behind scaffolding at the northern end of the market), Brave Atlas (2nd floor of the parking garage), Elizabeth Kress Street (ground floor by the elevator), Montezuma's Throne (northeastern ruined tower), Eventide Resort & Spa (below the elevated platform at the entrance), Luxor High Wellness Spa (south section, at the Voodoo Boys Increased Criminal Activity), ESC Explorer (inside, at the Scavengers Increased Criminal Activity), The Tunnel (at the BARGHEST Increased Criminal Activity) and George Washington Street (balcony up a staircase north of Heavy Hearts).

The tree's perks are Data Tunneling, Emergency Cloaking, Jailbreak (it gives the Monowire a Control quickhack slot), Launch Capacity Override, Limiter Removal, Machine Learning, Sensory Protocol, Spatial Mapping and Vulnerability Analytics. You can spend the 3 points right away; you'll come across the terminals as you move around Dogtown in the next jobs.

## The stadium's underground parking

Songbird guides you to the entrance of the **old parking garage** under the **EBM Petrochem Stadium**.

1. Find the entrance and cross the **restricted area**. There are BARGHEST guards: get through **by stealth or by shooting**. It's your first taste of how BARGHEST fights, a militia with military gear; if you sneak, use the cover and the cars.
2. Go down to the garage and then to the **underground garage**.
3. Find an **access point**. It has no power: find a way to **restore power**, **start the generator** and go back to **connect to the access point**.
4. Step onto the **elevator platform** and ride it. Then reach the **car elevator platform**, get on and reach the **catwalk**.
5. Talk to Songbird and keep going through the garage toward Dogtown until you enter the **elevator**.

## The smuggler caches (lost once you leave)

Before riding down the garage elevator you can **scan a map** of the Stadium Parking: it marks three sectors (**3A, 3B and 6B**) and has a drawing of an insect. Each sector hides a cache of smuggled goods, and there's a fourth in sector 1A. **There's no way back to this area** once you leave, so grab them all now:

- **Sector 1A:** a derelict Villefort Alvarado holds a **Perk Shard** in its trunk. The map doesn't mark this sector; according to the wiki, it was likely meant to be "3A".
- **Sector 3A:** next to a wrecked blue Villefort Columbus there's a makeshift wound dressing kit and minor gear.
- **Sector 3B:** inside a wrecked Villefort Columbus, with signs of a firefight, there's a unique **BARGHEST helmet** and a shard with an archived conversation (Foz Dawkins and Dylan Rea).
- **Sector 6B:** inside a **locked** Villefort Columbus there's a **Cyberware Capacity Shard**. Its quality depends on your level: it's **Tier IV from level 25**.

The Cyberware Capacity Shard is the one that matters most: it raises your cyberware capacity permanently.

## Black market, construction site and chute

1. Leave the elevator and go to the stadium's **black market**.
2. Go to the **construction site**. Songbird talks to you again on the way.
3. **Climb onto the roof** of the site and talk to Songbird again.
4. Head for the crash site: **jump down the chute**. Dog Eat Dog ends here and Hole in the Sky starts immediately.

## Choices

- **Accept or turn down Songbird** → turning her down ("Nah, not convinced") does no real harm: you are not locked out of Phantom Liberty. Accepting starts the job and the promise of the cure, which weighs on every choice in the expansion (Firestarter, The Killing Moon, Somewhat Damaged).
- **Stealth or fight in the garage** → doesn't change the story; it only decides how much combat and loot you get.
- **New game with the skip** → completes the base game up to Transmission without playing it and won't let you spend attributes: if you want to make the Act 1 and Act 2 choices yourself, play from the start.

> **Don't leave without:** the four garage caches (Perk Shard in 1A, dressing kit in 3A, **BARGHEST helmet** and shard in 3B, **Cyberware Capacity Shard** in 6B) and spending your 3 Relic points. Save before the chute: the next job can fail.

## Rewards

- 3050 XP and 430 Street Cred.
- 3 Relic points and the Relic tree.
- Perk Shard, BARGHEST helmet and Cyberware Capacity Shard (all missable).
