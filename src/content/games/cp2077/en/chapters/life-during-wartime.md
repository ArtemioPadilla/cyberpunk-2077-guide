It starts on its own when **Lightning Breaks** ends: the **Kang Tao AV** loses altitude and **Panam Palmer** and V chase it into the **Jackson Plains** in the southern Badlands, near the **regional airport**. **Mitch** and **Scorpion** have reached the crashed AV first. It's the longest job in the Hellman line: a turret chase, the choice to **help Panam or abandon her**, a gas station full of Kang Tao and, finally, **the talk with Hellman at the Sunset Motel**. Finishing it opens several important chains.

> **Order:** chase the AV → take out the drones → reach the AV → wait for Panam to tend her wound → check out the crash site → scan with the drone → disconnect → clear the site (turret and operatives) → get in the AV → talk to the pilot → talk to Mitch and Panam → (if needed) follow the tracks by bike → gas station → upper floor → knock Hellman out → call Takemura → carry Hellman out → talk to Panam → Sunset Motel: talk to Hellman.

> **Get ready:** **save when you reach the crash**: that's where you decide whether to help or abandon Panam, and abandoning her **closes I'll Fly Away** for good. Kang Tao operatives and **robots** await (weak to electrical damage, per game8), plus a **gas station with mines** around it. To get in quietly, have **Technical Ability** (roof) or **Body** (garage doors).

## The chase

Over the radio you hear **Mitch** and **Scorpion** approaching the AV. Panam tries to warn them, but the EMP interferes. **Three Kang Tao Griffin drones** attack you.

1. **Jack into the truck's machine gun** and **take out the drones**.
2. Mid-fight the gun jams; Panam puts the truck on autodrive to fix it and **a ricochet hits her in the back**. Keep firing until the drones are down.

**Reach the AV** and **wait for Panam to tend to her wound** (she uses an expired injector). She stays behind to cover you with Mitch's rifle while you head to the AV.

## The crash site: help or abandon

This is the main choice (see "Choices"). If you **head straight for the gas station**, you abandon Panam. If you **help**:

1. **Use the surveillance drone** and **scan the AV** and **Mitch**. Optional: scan the signs of the Aldecaldos, the **Kang Tao operatives** and the **combat drones**.
2. **Disconnect from the drone.**
3. **Check out the crash site:** **take out the turret** and **defeat the Kang Tao operatives**. Panam provides overwatch.
4. **Wait for Panam** and **get in the AV**. The last operative has locked himself inside **with Mitch as a hostage**. **Talk to the pilot:**
   - **Intimidate him** with "only looking for Hellman" → "I'm losing my patience": he tells you **where Hellman is** and you head **straight for the gas station** to the west.
   - **Or shoot him** (if you wait too long, Panam does). Mitch tells you **Scorpion died** in the assault and Kang Tao escaped in Aldecaldo vehicles. You then have to **scan the tracks**, **get on the motorcycle** (optional) and **follow Kang Tao's tracks** (optional: defeat the operatives on the airstrip). This detour means **more Kang Tao reinforcements at the gas station**.
5. **Talk to Mitch** and **Panam**.

## The gas station

**Go to the gas station with Panam** (the SovOil on Highway 101) and **get inside**:

- There are **mines around the perimeter**: scan before you approach.
- **Stealth route:** the **roof**, via a **Technical Ability** check.
- **Direct route:** force the **front garage doors** with **Body**.
- The **owner** is locked in a room behind the counter: he's unarmed and tells you **Hellman is upstairs**.

**Go to the upper floor**, where Hellman is with a **Kang Tao medic**. **Confront Hellman** (he panics, thinking you're from Arasaka) and **knock him out**. **Call Takemura** to meet at the Sunset Motel. **Pick Hellman up**, **carry him outside** and **put him down**, where the Aldecaldos arrive. **Talk to Panam**: she lets you take **Scorpion's Apollo**.

If you've already finished **Phantom Liberty**, you get a new dialogue option with Hellman.

## The Sunset Motel

In a motel room you wake Hellman and **talk to him**. You explain the biochip; he scans it:

- The biochip was experimental, still in trials. **V can no longer function without it**: their neural network has deteriorated too much.
- All he can offer is a **clinic in Sweden** and help managing the pain. He may also ask how you're coping with Johnny's engram (you can decline to talk about it).
- If you threaten him, he offers help if **you come with him to Kang Tao**: V refuses.

**Takemura** walks in and takes over Hellman. Before that, Hellman gives you a **chip with the blueprint of your brain and the biochip**. As you leave, the Relic malfunctions and you talk with **Johnny**. That ends the job.

## Choices

- **Help Panam at the crash site** → you rescue Mitch together. **I'll Fly Away opens** (after Riders on the Storm).
  - **Intimidate the operative** → straight to the gas station, no extra reinforcements.
  - **Shoot him** → you learn Scorpion died, follow the tracks and face **more Kang Tao at the gas station**.
- **Abandon Panam and go straight for Hellman** → you get Hellman alone. Panam turns up later with reinforcements, saying she rescued Mitch herself and that "unlike V" she doesn't bail. Her later texts are bitter, Mitch buries Scorpion alone and **I'll Fly Away is closed for good**.
- **Panam romance (masculine V):** **defend her when Saul berates her**, and later, when she texts, **send the long reply**, not "OK", or she snaps at you (game8).
- **The talk with Hellman** → V always refuses to go to Kang Tao; the options only change the dialogue.

## What opens here

- **Riders on the Storm:** Panam's chain (→ With a Little Help from My Friends → **Queen of the Highway**, which gives **The Star** ending). Careful: it fails if you wander too far or skip time once it's started.
- **I'll Fly Away** (Mitch's minor job): only if you **didn't abandon** Panam, and it has to be done **right after Riders on the Storm**.
- **I Fought the Law:** River Ward's chain with Elizabeth Peralez, leading to **The Hunt** and Following the River.
- **Play It Safe:** the wiki lists Life During Wartime as a prerequisite for Takemura's parade.
- **Tapeworm:** finishing Life During Wartime triggers a stage.

> **Don't leave without:** **Scorpion's Apollo**: you get it whether or not you used the bikes. And save before the crash-site choice: it's the only way not to lose **I'll Fly Away** by mistake.
