**Welcome to Paradise** arrived with **Update 2.5**. It opens after **The Heist**, in **Act 2**, once you reach **Street Cred 15**. **Aera** calls you: her sister **Haze**, an activist hacker, stole a shard from powerful people, hid it, and Aera fears they want her dead.

> **Order:** answer Aera → find the shard at Creek Loop (Kabuki) → slot it → call Aera → follow the tour stops → find Haze at the bridge → talk to Aera at the basketball court.

## The shard

In the dry creek at **Creek Loop**, **Kabuki**, move the suspicious metal sheets: the shard is underneath. Slotting it starts the **Night Corporation Guided Tour**, with a hologram of **Richard Night** who takes you for someone called **Chiang**. You can decrypt the shard: it gives a clue that pays off later.

## The stops

Each stop has a terminal; start it and listen.

1. **Night Corp Tower**, Crunch Plaza (Downtown): connect to the drone.
2. **Memorial Park**, Corpo Plaza.
3. **Arasaka Memorial**.
4. **Night City Hall**: connect to the drone; you see a Tyger Claws–Valentinos shootout.
5. **Biotechnica Flats** (Pacifica): people are blocking the way.
6. **Megabuilding H7 NCART station** (Rancho Coronado): decrypt the tour shard.
7. **Coronado Dam viewpoint**: defeat the **6th Street** crew before starting the terminal.
8. **Night City Medical Center** (Little China): Haze warns she's being tailed by a **red Caliburn**.
9. **Bellevue Overwalk** (Kabuki): Haze recalls an old bridge.

Search the nearby bridge on Bellevue Street. On the beach below, **Militech** soldiers stand around a body: defeat them. It's **Haze**: Chiang's guards killed her (the shard by the body tells you). Update Aera and meet her at the **basketball court on Forbes Street**, Kabuki.

## Choices

- **Give her the shards** → Aera thanks you for the truth, though she regrets she can't take revenge.
- **Keep them** → you tell her holding evidence against Night Corp is dangerous. Aera is furious and cuts you off.

## Rewards

- 950 XP, 2,200 Street Cred and **€$1,050**.
- **Yasha** (per the wiki, it depends on the outcome). With Phantom Liberty, Herold Lowe sells it at the Dogtown stadium once the job is done.
