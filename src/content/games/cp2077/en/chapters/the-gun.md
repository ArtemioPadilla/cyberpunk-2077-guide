**The Gun** is the shortest job in the game. **Robert Wilson**, the gunsmith at **2nd Amendment** in **Megabuilding H10** (Little China, Watson), calls out when you walk past his shop, which faces the elevator in V's building: your "sweet little forty-five" is waiting for you. It opens after **The Rescue**, so it is available from the morning of **The Ripperdoc**, and it has no time limit.

> **Order:** walk past 2nd Amendment → talk to Wilson → collect your gun.

## Collecting the gun

1. Leave the apartment and walk toward the elevator; Wilson speaks to you as you pass.
2. Go to the counter and **collect Dying Night**. It is free.
3. If your lifepath is **Streetkid**, Wilson tells you that getting your own gun is a rite of passage in Night City and that he is making up for the one you never had.

Wilson calls it a "forty-five", but it is a **9mm pistol**: a Militech M-10AF Lexington he modified ("9mm" is written on the slide).

Wiki note: if Wilson's job **Shoot To Thrill** is active, you can't get Dying Night until you finish it.

## Why it's worth it

- **Iconic:** it deals more damage while you move and guarantees **Shock** with rapid hits. 20-round magazine, 25 armor penetration; takes a scope and a muzzle attachment.
- **Against Lt. Mower**, the cyberpsycho in Kabuki's flooded plaza: this guide's Lt. Mower entry recommends **quick melee hits with Dying Night**, because its bayonet gives them +150% damage and a chance to shock, and chained together they interrupt her. Lure her to the arena entrance, out of the water, and keep hitting.
- Wilson also sells guns, including the standard Lexington. Check his shop whenever you pass through H10.

## Rewards

- **Dying Night** (iconic pistol).
