**Beat on the Brat** comes from **Coach Fred**, a retired boxer who hangs around **Megabuilding H10** (Little China, Watson), after **The Rescue**. First you fight a training robot; then the job tracks four district fights. In Act 1 you can only do the robot and the **Kabuki** fight; Arroyo, Rancho Coronado and The Glen open in Act 2, and the final against **Razor Hugh** needs all four plus the main job **Transmission**. They are fist fights, but recovery cyberware and **Gorilla Arms** are allowed.

> **Order:** talk to Fred → beat Punchin' Bag → Kabuki (Act 1) → Arroyo, Rancho Coronado and The Glen (Act 2) → final against Razor Hugh → optional: Ozob Bozo in Pacifica.

## Punchin' Bag (H10)

Step into the ring and knock down the training robot. If you lose, talk to Fred and try again.

## Kabuki: Esquerdo and Certo (Act 1)

On a Kabuki rooftop at Bellevue and Allen. You fight **Esquerdo and Certo**, two bodies with one mind. With enough **Cool** you get an extra question about their love life, and there's a shard in the arena with a conversation between them. Pays €$6,000–12,000, 602 XP and 901 Street Cred.

## Arroyo: Buck Arnold (Act 2)

Buck hits very hard: 2 or 3 punches put you down, so eat or use boosters first. The bet is €$6,000. With **Body 8** (per the wiki) you can get him to put up his iconic rifle **O'Five** for €$18,000: if you win, he and his 6th Street crew attack you; beat them and take the rifle and the money from his body. With the normal bet, provoking him at the end also turns him hostile and leaves O'Five within reach. If you leave without killing him, he despawns.

## Rancho Coronado: Rhino (Act 2)

In an Animals club, with no bet. Rhino also knocks you out in 2 or 3 punches: dodge a lot. Winning unlocks the **Animals melee weapon vendor**. If the gig **For My Son** is active and you start a firefight in the gym, this job completes on its own, but you lose that vendor for good.

## The Glen: César Diego Ruiz (Act 2)

You bet €$20,000 against his car, the **Villefort Alvarado "Vato"**. His heavy punch knocks you out in two, but it is telegraphed: back off, leaving room on your right, and when he blurs and steps in, circle to his right; punch his head and back off to recover stamina. After you win you choose: car and money, just the car, just the money, or neither. If you don't keep both, he later sends you a photo of his baby.

## Final: Razor Hugh (Act 2)

At the **Grand Imperial Mall** in Pacifica, at 12:01 AM (if you arrive early, wait on a bench). Viktor is in the audience and gives you the key: Razor has new abdominal muscles, so **body shots stagger him**. Block on time to counter in slow motion and, with Gorilla Arms, break his guard with charged punches.

## Pacifica: Ozob Bozo (optional, Act 2)

Opens if you helped Ozob in **Send in the Clowns** (after Transmission). His nose is a grenade: after about 75% of his health in head damage it explodes and you win by technical knockout; collect from the referee. Pays €$64,000.

## Choices

- **Fred offers you money to lose.** If you lose, you get €$50,000 plus an extra €$20,000 (if you hadn't accepted, he offers it again); Viktor criticizes you for it.
- **Win**, whether you accepted or not → €$50,000; Viktor and your past opponents congratulate you and a **trophy** appears in your apartment.
- **The girl in the audience** asks you to make Razor pay. If you win, you overhear her on the phone: she lied to win her bets.

> **Don't leave without:** **O'Five** in Arroyo, if you take the big bet or provoke Buck.

## Rewards

- Final: €$50,000 (€$70,000 if you take the dive).
- Per fight: Kabuki €$6,000–12,000; O'Five in Arroyo; the Vato or €$20,000 in The Glen; 1,919 XP, 2,878 Street Cred and the Animals vendor in Rancho Coronado; €$64,000 from Ozob.
