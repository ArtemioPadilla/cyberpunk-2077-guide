**We Have Your Wife** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place at the **Monokatana Workshop**, a **Tyger Claws** workshop under an overpass on Raymond Street in **Japantown**. It belongs to Wakako's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**; in Act 1, in Watson, it does not exist yet. As you approach the workshop, Wakako calls with the job. The client is **Bradley Costigan**: in prison, the Tyger Claws asked him to shiv some snitches and he refused, so they kidnapped his wife **Lauren** to make him comply.

> **Order:** get into the workshop → find Lauren → free her → escort her out of the building → talk to her.

## The workshop

The lot is walled off from the street. The ground floor holds the workshop floor and a small lobby; the **basement** is used as storage and as a cell, and that is where Lauren is. Plenty of Tyger Claws stand guard. There are two computers: one in a small room linking the lobby and the garage, and another in the basement. They show how long Lauren has been held.

You can clear the place before going down or sneak to the basement. Keep in mind that once she is free you have to **escort Lauren** outside, so it helps to have the way out already cleared.

## At the end

Outside, talk to Lauren. V advises her to lie low with someone she trusts in case the Tyger Claws come looking; according to the wiki, she goes to her mother's.
