**Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place in **Gustavo Orta's apartment**, on the 32nd floor of a building on Petrel Street in **Vista del Rey**. It belongs to Padre's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the four gigs of the first. Gustavo, a high-ranking **Valentino** (you saw him at Jackie's funeral in **Heroes**), was dating **Martha Frakes**, daughter of **Nolan Frakes** of **6th Street**. Martha was shot and left in a coma, and Nolan blames Gustavo. Padre's netrunner has already sorted your access.

> **Order:** go to the building → go to the 32nd floor → go to the apartment → find Gustavo → neutralize him (or talk to him) → leave the building.

## The 32nd-floor hallway

- A neutral **camera** linked to two cameras inside the apartment: use it to scout the interior.
- A **utility closet** with loot (**Technical Ability 9**).
- The apartment **door**: **Body 17**, or quickhack the keypad to its left. Forcing it alerts everyone inside.
- A **shutter** that opens onto an outside catwalk leading to the lower and upper balconies.

Gustavo is in his **office on the second floor**.

## Choices

- **Kill him** → leave the building.
- **Knock him out** → carry him to the car waiting outside and put him in the trunk.
- **Reach him without violence** (via the upper balcony, or by ringing the doorbell and answering with the **Streetkid** option) → Gustavo tells you the ambush was 6th Street's. You can convince him to **leave Night City**: he lets you walk out in peace. Do not read computers or hack anything while they watch you, or they turn hostile. Once you leave, Padre calls: Gustavo took his own life. The rewards are the same.

## Rewards

- €$8,300, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
