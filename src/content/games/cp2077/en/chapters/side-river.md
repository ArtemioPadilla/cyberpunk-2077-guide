**River Ward** is the NCPD detective you meet in **I Fought the Law**, the job from **Elizabeth Peralez** that opens when you finish **Life During Wartime**. Two branches grow from it: the **Peralez** one (**Dream On**, a conspiracy around their campaign) and **River**'s (**The Hunt**, the search for his nephew Randy, and **Following the River**, the family dinner and the romance). The Hunt does not depend on Dream On; do them in any order.

> **For the ending:** River **opens and closes no ending**. His romance needs a **feminine body, any voice** (Fandom's Following the River page says "feminine voice and body", but the Romance page and game8 say voice doesn't matter). A feminine V can hold River and **Judy** at the same time with no penalty, but on the rooftop you call only one partner. River waits for V in **The Sun** and **The Devil** (if V refuses the contract and goes back to Earth); The Tower ends the romance. What ruins it all: **picking Poppy Farm** in The Hunt, **leaving Edgewood Farm** during the rescue, or saying **"count me out"** at the end of The Hunt.

> **Order:** **I Fought the Law:** City Hall braindance → River at Chubby Buffalo's → Neil White (Max-imum Pleasure) and Christine Markov (Cherry Blossom Market) → Tyger Claws at River's car → Red Queen's Race → Han → the Peralezes → **Dream On:** Peralez apartment → rooftop → van chase → Elizabeth → Jefferson → **The Hunt:** news clip → NCPD lab → Joss's trailer → three braindances → **Edgewood Farm** → **Following the River:** dinner with the family → water tower.

> **Get ready:** The Hunt's braindances ask for a lot of clues; if you have visited **Edgewood Farm** before, V recognises the skyline when scanning it. At Joss's trailer, **Intelligence** helps (hacking the computer and tracing Harris's IP). **Save before picking the farm.** Edgewood has mines, four turrets and cameras: go in carefully. For the romance, back River in the timed lines and act quietly or non-lethally when he is around.

## I Fought the Law

**Elizabeth Peralez**, wife of **Jefferson Peralez**, gives you an investigation.

1. **City Hall braindance.** **Péter Horváth**, detective **River Ward** and the **Red Queen's Race** appear.
2. **River at Chubby Buffalo's.** You meet him.
3. **Two leads:**
   - **Neil White** at **Max-imum Pleasure**: use the "Igor" line.
   - **Christine Markov** at the **Cherry Blossom Market**.
4. **Tyger Claws at River's car.** Say nothing, or "jump" when asked, and they leave. River likes it when you handle things non-lethally or quietly.
5. **The Red Queen's Race** (Charter Hill, Animals turf). The entrance is in a **shipping crate**. Upstairs, the boss can be choked out and interrogated. The computer reveals **Holt** and **Han**. **Interact with the braindance wreath** in the booth.
6. **Confront Han.**
7. **Visit the Peralezes.** Johnny advises you to withhold; you can tell them what you know or keep quiet. They pay either way.

Finishing it opens **Dream On**, **The Hunt** and _Sweet Dreams_.

## Dream On

The Peralez branch. Something is wrong in their home: Elizabeth's memories don't add up.

1. **Investigate the Peralez apartment.** There are three ways into the **hidden room**: **Body 9**, the code **6709** at the control-room computer, or the **roof hatch**.
2. **On the roof**, find the Militech-style **transmitter** and the line of sight to a van.
3. **Chase the van** (Emperor 620 Ragnar) from Japantown to the Maelstrom compound near All Foods. **If you lose it**, the job ends early with a smaller payout. Agents you incapacitate have their heads explode (Project Condin).
4. **Elizabeth**, at the ramen stand in the **Yagami Market**, asks you not to tell Jefferson.
5. **Jefferson** waits for you at **Reconciliation Park**. Afterwards, an unknown caller (Mr. Blue Eyes) warns you.

## The Hunt

River calls and picks you up in his truck. Watch the **news clip** about **Peter Pan** (Anthony Harris): the victim in the video is wearing the shoes of **Randy**, River's missing nephew (the son of his sister **Joss**). Harris is in a coma, so River wants to view his dreams as a braindance.

**NCPD lab.** Go in through the **Technical Ability** door on the left, the **Body** door on the right, or over the wall from a container. Find Harris's file: **Yawen Packard** is being blackmailed.

- **Romance (game8):** back River in the timed lines ("Hasn't let me down yet.", "I wanna see for myself.") and tell Johnny "River's all right.".

**Joss's trailer (Randy's room).**

1. Take the **key from the shelf** to open the cabinet.
2. Get the computer password from the **record player**, or hack it with **Intelligence**.
3. On the **Net** tab, open the red-people image → "Join Us" → Files, and replay the video. River sends it to Yawen.
4. Optional: with high **Intelligence**, find Harris's IP.
5. Sleep. **Romance (game8):** in the car, "Sign me up for that beer.".

**Three braindances.** You need enough clues in each:

- **The school:** 3 visual clues.
- **The childhood barn:** 7 visual clues.
- **The adulthood barn:** 13 visual clues, including the "**towers of light**", plus the **AV sounds** on the audio layer.

If you've visited **Edgewood Farm** before, scanning the skyline or the towers lets V recognise it instantly. Careful: a HUD bug can show "Entering a new area: Edgewood Farm" during the braindances; it is not a reliable clue.

**The farm: pick EDGEWOOD FARM.** Save first. If you pick **Poppy Farm**, you find Wraiths and Randy dead; River blames you, kills Harris alone and texts a goodbye (in the credits he ends up in jail). **Following the River is locked and there is no romance.**

**Edgewood Farm.**

1. There are **mines**, **four turrets** and **cameras**.
2. In the house, the **switch under the desk** opens a **hidden room**; its computer shuts down the turrets and cameras. The **Tinker Bell** is nearby (Fandom's weapon page puts it in the farm's "control room": look around that area).
3. Climb the **ladder on the left side of the barn** to the roof opening and **switch off the braindance machine**.
4. Help the other victims: two survive.
5. **Don't leave the area**: if you do, **River and Randy die**.

**The final talk.** If you want the romance, **don't pick "count me out"**; game8 recommends "I'll help you.".

## Following the River

Reply to River's text (game8 suggests "How about a new record?") and accept his dinner invite at Joss's, in the evening.

1. River is making **jambalaya**. He sends you for the **rice**: Joss is inside, and you can talk to her.
2. Have a drink on the patio. If you ask about his past, he tells you about his parents' deaths; you can stop the conversation and he is grateful.
3. His niece and nephew, **Monique** and **Dorian**, want to play an AR game (_Big Trouble in Heywood_): you can **let them win**.
4. Dinner with the family. With a feminine V, Joss makes it clear River likes you.
5. **The water tower.** River gives you **Crash**, his revolver. A feminine V chooses **romance or friendship**: with romance there is a scene and a talk the next morning; otherwise you drink late and wake up hungover in the trailer.

Later, Joss texts you a photo of the family at the hospital with Randy.

## Choices

- **I Fought the Law — Tyger Claws at the car** → keeping quiet or "jumping" sends them off without a fight; River appreciates non-lethal or quiet handling.
- **I Fought the Law — tell the Peralezes or not** → they pay either way; Johnny prefers you keep quiet.
- **Dream On — losing the van** → the job ends early and you get paid less.
- **Dream On — tell Jefferson the truth** → he plays along with his manipulators while trying to expose them. **Lie (blame Holt)** → he focuses on winning first. This choice also changes Peralez's credits call: paranoid about his wife if you told him the truth, asking you for a job if you hid it.
- **The Hunt — Poppy Farm** → Randy dies, River goes after Harris alone and ends up in jail; **Following the River locked, no romance. Permanent.**
- **The Hunt — leaving Edgewood Farm** → **River and Randy die. Permanent.**
- **The Hunt — "count me out"** → you lose the romance.
- **Following the River — romance or friendship** (feminine V) → with romance, River waits for you in The Sun and in The Devil (if you go back to Earth) and gives you his tank top; either way, the **Kutcher home** unlocks as an apartment.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Tinker Bell** at Edgewood Farm (by the hidden room; if you miss it, Herold Lowe sells it in Phantom Liberty), switching off the barn machine, helping the victims and, in Following the River, **Crash** on the water tower.

- **Dream On:** 2475 XP, 3711 Street Cred (less if you lose the van).
- **The Hunt:** 2598 XP, 3896 Street Cred and **Tinker Bell** (missable).
- **Following the River:** **Crash**, the _Big Trouble in Heywood_ AR box (memorabilia), River's tank top (if romanced) and the **Kutcher home** as an apartment.
