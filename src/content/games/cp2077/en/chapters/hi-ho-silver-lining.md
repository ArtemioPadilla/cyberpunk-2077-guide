Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is **Mr. Hands**'s reward for finishing **all his Pacifica and Dogtown gigs**: the base-game Pacifica one (Two Wrongs Makes Us Right) and the nine in Dogtown, which come in three sets. The last set (Heaviest of Hearts and Roads to Redemption) only opens some time after **Firestarter**, so this job is among the last things you'll do in Dogtown.

> **Order:** finish every gig → Mr. Hands's message → wait for Heavy Hearts to open → talk to Mr. Hands → leave the club → get in the car.

## The reward

Once you finish the last gig, Mr. Hands asks you to come back to the **Heavy Hearts** club in Dogtown. If the club is closed, wait for it to open. Go up to see him and hear him out. When you leave the club, the **Quadra Sport R-7 "Sterling"**, which used to belong to Mr. Hands, is parked outside: get in and it's yours.

There are no fights and no choices. Johnny, in the journal, calls him a megalomaniacal buffoon, which is saying something coming from him.

## Rewards

- Quadra Sport R-7 "Sterling" (car)
