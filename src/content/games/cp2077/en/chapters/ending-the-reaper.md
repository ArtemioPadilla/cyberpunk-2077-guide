The **secret ending**: V and Johnny storm **Arasaka Tower** with no allies, "like it's 2023". It unlocks with three specific answers at Johnny's grave, at the end of **Chippin' In**, and by waiting on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop. It is the hardest job in the game: **you can't save manually**, V's max health drops throughout the assault and dying rolls the credits of the suicide ending. If you connect Alt to Mikoshi, it ends in a version of [The Sun](../ending-the-sun/) with **Rogue alive** or in [Temperance](../ending-temperance/). It has no achievement of its own.

> **Order:** rooftop: pick nothing for ~5 min → "Let's hear this plan" → "[Attack Arasaka Tower] Means goin' out with a bang - I like it" → lobby: 2 Samurai + 3 Special Agents, 2 mechs → more guards, **access token** → hold out until the elevator arrives (Juggernauts) → elevator to the Netrunner floor (a mech) → control room → mainframe room → expose the mainframe and connect Alt → **Adam Smasher** (6 snipers, 3 Juggernauts) → Changes: **the well** (The Sun) or the bridge (Temperance).

> **Get ready:** before waiting on the rooftop, get to the **highest level and best gear** you can: the lobby enemies are at max level. Carry **lots of healing**, grenades, spare ammo and electrical or EMP quickhacks for Smasher. Game8 recommends **playing the Rogue route first** ([The Sun](../ending-the-sun/)) to learn the tower. Keep a **manual save on the rooftop** before sitting next to Misty: if you die with no useful autosave, you go back there.

## Requirements

- **At Johnny's grave (Chippin' In, Northern Oil Fields)**, answer:
  1. Johnny: "Not to fuck this up, what we have." → **"Nah, fucked that up too."**
  2. Johnny: "Is it too late to ask for a second chance?" → **"What do you want from me?"**
  3. Johnny: "At least, so it seemed... till now." → **"OK. But as second chances go, this is your last."**
- PowerPyx's full recommended path: "[Inscribe Johnny's Initials] Let's do something about that." → "The guy who saved my life" → the three above → "You were a real dick in the beginning" → "When you said you let down your friends..." → "Smasher biz really got to her." → "Yeah, I'll call Rogue.".
- **Johnny affinity doesn't matter.** The wiki and Game8 confirm it: the famous 70% is a myth; only the answers count. (Game8's Nocturne page talks about a "high gauge", but its own guides and the wiki contradict it.)
- **Blistering Love:** PowerPyx lists it as a step; Game8 says it isn't needed, and the wiki only asks for the answers. Do it anyway: those answers unlock it and it's the key to the Rogue route.
- **You don't need** Queen of the Highway (per Game8).

## On the rooftop: waiting

Play Nocturne Op55N1 up to the rooftop, **save before sitting next to Misty**, sit and talk to Johnny (the goodbye call is optional). When the options to decide what comes next appear, **pick none of them**. Wait about **5 minutes** (5 min 8 s, the length of the song). PowerPyx advises moving your view between your gun and Johnny about once a minute. One Game8 page says to wait after selecting one of the first three options, but its secret-ending guide and PowerPyx say to select nothing: select nothing.

Johnny remarks how hard it is to decide who "will die for you" and proposes going alone. Pick **"Let's hear this plan"** and then **"[Attack Arasaka Tower] Means goin' out with a bang - I like it"**. The scene cuts straight to the front entrance of Arasaka Tower.

## The rules of the assault

- **No manual saves** for the whole job. There are **three autosaves**: at the start, after descending to the mainframe level and right before Smasher.
- **V's max health drops 25%** at the start and then **1% more** at intervals ("Relic Malfunction Detected" notice). **The longer you take, the worse it gets:** don't explore, push on.
- **If you die**, the credits roll with the suicide-ending messages. There is no "load last save" screen, but you can load those autosaves from the menu. Game8 and PowerPyx frame it as "one try only"; with the wiki's autosaves or the rooftop save, you can retry.

## The lobby

1. **Find a way to the Mikoshi level.** At the entrance you face **2 Arasaka Samurai** and **3 Special Agents**, all at max level. Use cover and drop the ones hurting you most first.
2. After them come **2 Arasaka mechs**. Electrical quickhacks and grenades.
3. Johnny leads you to the elevators: **you need an access token**. More guards spawn: **defeat them and loot the token** from one of them.
4. **Call the elevator.** It takes a long time: hold the lobby while waves come in, **Juggernauts** included. Stay close to the doors.
5. **As soon as they open, get in** and pick the **Netrunner floor**. A **mech** spawns in front of the elevator before the doors close.

## The Mikoshi level

1. **Find the Mikoshi Access Point.**
2. **Defeat all enemies in the control room.**
3. Find a way to connect **Alt** to the mainframe. **Defeat all enemies in the mainframe room.** (Autosave on descending to this level.)
4. **Expose the mainframe** and **connect Alt**.
5. Autosave before the fight: **Adam Smasher**.

## Boss — Adam Smasher

You arrive with less max health than on any other route, and Smasher brings reinforcements: **6 snipers** on the balcony around the room and **3 Juggernauts** on the ground floor, who come in at set points of the fight.

- **Three phases:** close combat, long range (once he loses his right half) and weakened. Since 2.1 he uses a **Sandevistan** and adapts to your build.
- **Weak point:** the right side of his chest, behind plating you can shoot off. Shooting off his **right arm** (on your left, facing him) ends his melee; then the **back rocket launcher** (PowerPyx tip from before 2.0).
- **EMP and electrical quickhacks** to cut his attacks for a moment.
- **Snipers:** when they appear, find cover from the balcony and take them out before they wear you down; with your health cut, every shot counts.
- **Rockets:** they go where you were; keep moving.
- **Fate (optional):** the sources don't document what sparing him changes. To be sure of the **Access Token**, finish him and loot it. Look for the **Judgement card** on a wall of the room.

## Changes and the choices

After connecting to Mikoshi, **Changes** starts: you relive the climb to Misty's rooftop, talk to Jackie (if his body went to Viktor) or pick up the necklace, and talk to Johnny. V's body has about **six months**.

- **Enter the well** ("Enter the well to return your body") → V gets their body back → **Path of Glory (The Sun)**, the version with **Rogue alive**: at the Afterlife she sits by V and suggests a tequila before the meeting with Mr. Blue Eyes. It is the only way to see Rogue in Path of Glory. The rest of the epilogue is as in [The Sun](../ending-the-sun/).
- **Cross the bridge** → Johnny keeps the body → **New Dawn Fades (Temperance)**: see [Temperance](../ending-temperance/). Rogue lives here too.
- PowerPyx and Game8 word these two options the other way round; the wiki (and the job's own objective) says **the well returns your body**. Follow the wiki.

## Who lives and who dies

Many Arasaka employees, most of the board, **Smasher** (your call) and **Hanako** (per Yorinobu) die. **Rogue lives**, and none of your other allies risk their lives. Final reputation: **100% (Legend)** in The Sun; the necklace ends up as in The Sun (on the penthouse nightstand).

## Credits calls

- **If you win:** the calls from [The Sun](../ending-the-sun/) or [Temperance](../ending-temperance/), depending on your choice.
- **If you die:** the grief messages from the suicide ending: Viktor, urged by Misty ("I'll miss you, kid"); Misty crying; Mama Welles praying for you (with Heroes); Judy crying (with her chain); Panam, devastated and furious (with her chain); Kerry, furious; River, grieving. See [Path of Least Resistance](../ending-least-resistance/).

## Rewards and where you return

After the credits you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1** with:

- Legendary **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk** (if you didn't own them).
- **Access Token** (if you looted it): Smasher's secret armory on the **DV Ebunike** (**Ba Xing Chong** spec).
- The **Judgement** card (if you took it).
- **The Sun** or **Temperance** achievement, depending on the ending. This ending has no achievement of its own.

> **Don't leave without:** Smasher's **Access Token** and the **Judgement** card from his room's wall. And remember: don't waste time exploring, every minute costs you max health.
