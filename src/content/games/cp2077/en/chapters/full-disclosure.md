**Full Disclosure** comes from **Sandra Dorsett**, the netrunner you saved in **The Rescue**. She calls after the gig **Gig: Last Login** becomes active (whether or not you've finished it): her missing **databank** is in the **same building** as that gig, Charles Bucks' clinic, so you can do both at once. If you already have the databank, she just asks you to return it. (The wiki lists Double Life and two gigs as previous quests; its text ties it to Last Login, which can activate in Act 1. The safe bet is to wait for **Act 2**.)

> **Order:** find the databank → (optional) decrypt it → call Sandra → meet her at her apartment (Little China) → decide what you tell her.

## At Sandra's apartment

Sandra nervously lets you in. Her apartment holds three shards (**Entry 36**, **37** and **38**) that tell how she uncovered **Operation Carpe Noctem** and that she's on the run from **Night Corp**.

## Choices

- **Don't decrypt it** → she thanks you for your discretion and pays the agreed **€$830**.
- **Decrypt it and deny it** → she's upset and pays **less**.
- **Admit you read it:**
  - **Say you'll do nothing** → she pays the agreed sum (**€$830**).
  - **Congratulate her** (hacking Night Corp deserves respect) → she pays **€$1,900** for your honesty.
  - **Congratulate her and pass an Intelligence check** → she tells you what she's after and what she fears, and pays **€$3,800**.
  - **Blackmail her** → she turns hostile and you have to neutralize her.

In Phantom Liberty, Sandra is one of the netrunners for **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos**, but only if you resolved this job well.

## Rewards

- 346 XP, 778 Street Cred and **€$830** (up to **€$3,800** depending on the talk).
