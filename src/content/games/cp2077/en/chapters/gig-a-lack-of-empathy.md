**A Lack of Empathy** is the first gig from **Dino Dinovic**, the **City Center** fixer. It takes place at **Empathy**, a braindance and striptease club in **Corpo Plaza**, on Republic Way. It belongs to Dino's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. The club belonged to two partners, **Adam Ibrahimovic** and **Larry Fanghorn**. Adam took it over, changed the codes and hired the **Animals** as security. Larry, the client, wants you to upload a virus to the club's network to shut it down until Adam lets him back in. Dino asks for no witnesses.

> **Order:** enter the club → (optional) deposit your weapons → (optional) avoid combat → find the subnet's main computer → upload the virus → leave the club → retrieve your weapons → collect your reward from the Drop Point.

## Getting in

Through the main entrance you have to **hand over your weapons**. The alternative is the door to the right of the reception: it leads into the staff area, with several hackable computers and access points, and from there into the club **without passing the weapons check**.

## Reaching the computer

Inside, look for the area marked **"Private"**: it leads up to the computer room. You will have to get past one Animals guard; in the room there is another with his back turned. Sneak up and upload the virus.

## Getting out

You can go back the way you came or leave through the computer room's back door and drop into the lot below. That counts as "leave the club". Then circle the building and go back to the entrance for your weapons. If you were not detected, go to the Drop Point to finish the gig.

## Rewards

- €$3,410 (plus the no-combat bonus).
