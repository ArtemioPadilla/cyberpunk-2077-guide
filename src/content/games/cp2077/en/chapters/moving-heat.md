Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. Some time after **Firestarter** (and Run This Town, according to the wiki) you get a text from **Ashlay**, a netrunner hired by **Charon Exotics** for its net security. According to his page, it depends on V having hacked Charon Exotics' transceivers and taken control of the **Quadra Sport R-7 "Charon"**. Another guide places it later, near the end of the story; follow the message when it comes.

> **Order:** elevator near the EBM Petrochem Stadium entrance → reply to Ashlay → go down to the garage → approach the car → get in → wipe the on-board computer → leave with the car.

## The stadium garage

Ashlay wants the stolen car out of the company's network: if you wipe the Charon Exotics software, you keep it. Go to the elevator near the main entrance of the **EBM Petrochem Stadium**, reply to his message and wait: he hacks the cameras to keep an eye on you and unlocks the elevator to the underground garage.

Down there, move in stealth; Ashlay gives you tips to stay unseen. If there are enemies by the car, neutralize them. Get in, reply to Ashlay and access the on-board computer to wipe its memory. On the way out, don't provoke the guards.

- **No alarm:** you get out clean. Ashlay asks you never to put him in that spot again and deletes his contact.
- **Alarm raised:** furious, Ashlay reports your intrusion to the whole BARGHEST security force, and its response unit chases you as you leave.

According to the wiki (patch 2.1, PC), the objective to neutralize the enemies by the car can get stuck and keep you from getting in; save before going down.

## Rewards

- Quadra Sport R-7 "Charon" (car)
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
