Someone is smuggling syn-leather jackets to get around a corporate embargo; underground ripperdocs use the leather in their implants. **Regina Jones**'s client tracked down the shipment: you have to **steal the van** from the **Revere Courier Services** warehouse on Martin St (Northside, Watson). The **Tyger Claws** control the place and launder cash there. It is in Regina's **set 1 of 8**, the first she offers: her gigs come in sets that unlock one after another, and the next one appears once you finish every gig in the current set. According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the Revere warehouse → gain access to the van using the main computer → get in → drive to the indicated location → get out.

## Getting in

- There are **eight cameras** and plenty of Tyger Claws.
- Enter through one of the two gates or, quietly, through the gaps in the fence (there is one near each gate).
- You can enter the main building through the ground-floor doors or by climbing to the roof, where there is a stairwell door and an open window into a small hallway watched by a camera.

## The van

The van won't roll until it validates the driver. Go into the **office** and use the **computer** to authorize yourself as a driver. Then head downstairs, get in and drive it out of the facility to the marked spot. Get out there and the gig is done.

## Rewards

- €$3,000, 1,000 XP and 707 Street Cred.
