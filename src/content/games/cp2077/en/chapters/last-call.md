**Last Call** (added in 1.5) wraps up **Regina Jones**'s gigs in Watson. When you finish her **23 gigs** (all eight sets, **Concrete Cage Trap** included), Regina calls to thank you and leaves a gift in your apartment **stash**. According to players' reports, all those gigs can be finished before **The Heist**, so Last Call can arrive in Act 1.

> **Order:** finish the 23 Watson gigs → take Regina's call → go to your apartment stash → collect the reward.

## The reward: Axolotl

- An **iconic** **Frontal Cortex** implant, costing 48 capacity.
- When you **neutralize** an enemy, it instantly cuts the cooldown of all your cyberware by 5% to 7.5%, depending on its tier. Since 2.11, non-lethal takedowns count too.
- Since 2.0 the reward is the Axolotl (in 1.5 it was a different item).

## When to close it

The wiki says the Axolotl **only comes at tier 5 if you are level 33 or higher**. If you finish the gigs early, in Act 1, you get a lower tier. If that matters to you, leave one gig open until level 33; if not, finish them now for the money and XP. With Phantom Liberty it can also be bought from Dogtown ripperdocs.

The Watson achievement **It's Elementary** ("Complete all gigs and NCPD Scanner Hustles in Watson") doesn't need Concrete Cage Trap, but Last Call does.

## Rewards

- **Axolotl** (iconic), in your apartment stash.
