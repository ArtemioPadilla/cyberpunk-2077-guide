**Through Pain to Heaven** is the epilogue of the **King of Swords** ending ("take Songbird home"): you helped Songbird in Firestarter, but on the platform in The Killing Moon you **handed her over to Reed** (or called him from the train). Songbird goes back to NUSA and, in return, Reed keeps his end: he has a way to get the Relic out of you. It's a short, single-conversation job, but it opens the door to **The Tower** ending. Johnny notices it too: since that first encounter with Songbird, what he feels now is silence.

> **Order:** wait for Reed to contact you (after the Phantom Liberty credits) → talk to Reed → go to the CHOOH2 station → talk to Reed → talk to Johnny → _(a day later)_ Who Wants to Live Forever.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save** when this job is done. The next one, **Who Wants to Live Forever**, leads to **The Tower** ending, which works as **a second point of no return**: if you accept, V's story ends there. If you also want to see the base game's endings, keep an earlier save.

## Waiting for Reed

1. After the **Phantom Liberty credits**, **find something to do** until Reed contacts you.
2. **If he doesn't call:** skip time **24 hours** a few times and drive around the city; according to PowerPyx, that eventually triggers it.
3. Talk to Reed.

## The CHOOH2 station

1. Go to the **CHOOH2 station** in **Rancho Coronado** (Santo Domingo) and **talk to Reed**. He offers you the **neural matrix**: the cure NUSA can give you.
2. **Talk to Johnny.** The job ends.

The real offer comes in the next job: **Who Wants to Live Forever**. Reed contacts you again about **a day later**, and that's where you decide whether to accept.

## What to do with the offer

- **Accept (Who Wants to Live Forever)** → you go to the rooftop above Misty's Esoterica, say your goodbyes and leave with the FIA: **The Tower** ending (Things Done Changed). See the Who Wants to Live Forever page.
- **Decline it** → if you decline on Reed's call, the job ends and you go back to the main story. Even so, the **call to Reed** also appears as an option on the **Nocturne Op55N1** rooftop, so you can leave the decision for the end of the game.
- **If you refuse the cure and finish the game with The Sun, The Star or Temperance**, Reed calls you during the credits to berate you.

## Where your save stands

- **Who dies in King of Swords:** Kurt Hansen, many BARGHEST soldiers and stadium civilians, **Albert Murphy** (if you killed him) and numerous NCX casualties (Orbital Air and Corp-Bud employees, civilians and NUSA black ops). **Reed and Alex live.**
- **The Tower epilogue:** because the NCX attack happened, the epilogue mentions tensions between Orbital Air and Night Corp and a possible Fifth Corporate War or Second Unification War. In The Tower credits, Reed calls you with doubts about Songbird.
- **Achievement:** **King of Swords**.

## Choices

- **Accepting or refusing Reed's cure** (in Who Wants to Live Forever or on the Nocturne rooftop) → accepting leads to **The Tower**: V survives without the Relic but loses almost everything else. Refusing takes you back to the base game's endings. See Who Wants to Live Forever.

> **Don't leave without:** saving after the station scene. If you still have Mr. Hands gigs, Dogtown jobs or main-story business left, do it **before** accepting Reed's offer.

## Rewards

- **Neural matrix** (the cure; opens The Tower).
- From the path: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog and Murphy's Law (if you picked them up).
