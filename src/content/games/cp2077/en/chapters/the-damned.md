**The Damned** starts with Reed's call after the two-day wait in Lucretia My Reflection. At **The Moth** bar you meet **Alex** (Alena Xenakis), an old colleague of Reed's, and together you chase a lead on Songbird: **Slider**, a **Voodoo Boys** netrunner hiding at the **Eventide Resort & Spa**. By the end of the job the target of the next few jobs appears: the **Black Sapphire**, Kurt Hansen's hotel-casino.

> **Order:** go to The Moth → sit at the bar → Reed's call → talk to Reed and Alex → meet Reed near Slider's hideout → talk to the guards **or** go in through the vents (code **230598**) → destroy 4 server cores and take out the guards (infiltration route) → reach Slider → jack into his device → talk to Songbird → leave with Reed → look at the Black Sapphire.

> **Get ready:** if in the base game's Voodoo Boys storyline (Transmission and Never Fade Away) you **spared Placide and Brigitte**, you can talk your way in. If not, or if you prefer the other route, bring a build for stealth or close-quarters combat.

## The Moth

1. **Go to The Moth** and **sit at the bar**.
2. **Answer Reed's call** and talk to him.
3. Talk to **Alex** and Reed, then to both together. They explain that Songbird's trail runs through **Slider**.

## Slider's hideout

1. **Meet Reed** near Slider's hideout, at the **Eventide Resort & Spa**. You can talk to Johnny on the way.
2. Talk to Reed and follow him. Now you choose how to get in:

**Option A: talk to the guards.** If you suggest talking to the Voodoo Boys guards, you walk up, talk to them and follow Reed. **If you spared Placide and Brigitte** in the base game, a dialogue option mentions it and **the guards let you in peacefully**.

**Option B: another way in.** If you suggest another route or walk away from the guards peacefully:

- Find a way into the **ventilation shafts**.
- Use code **230598** to open the **side door**.
- Inside, find Slider: **destroy the 4 server cores** to sever the Voodoo Boys' connection and **take out the guards**. The cores are the main objective; you can drop the guards with stealth.

3. Reach Slider. Follow Reed and talk to Slider.

**The cameras.** If you use the security cameras to **spy on Slider**, he spots you, calls you out and **permanently disables the camera** in his room. You lose nothing important, but you gain nothing either.

## Jacking into Slider's device

1. **Jack into Slider's device.** **Songbird** is on the other side: talk to her.
2. Talk to Reed and **leave the hideout** with him.
3. Follow him and talk to him. At the end he points out the **Black Sapphire**: **look at it**. That's where Hansen is holding Songbird, and getting in there is the plan for the next jobs.

## Gris-Gris, Slider's revolver

The iconic revolver **Gris-Gris** is in the **locked room of Slider's hideout**. To open it you need **Slider's hideout storage room key**, which you get in Mr. Hands' gig **Treating Symptoms**. If you do that gig, come back here to pick it up.

## Choices

- **Talking to the guards** → peaceful entry if you spared Placide and Brigitte (it has its own dialogue option). Without that option, the safe route is B.
- **Vents and side door (230598)** → the infiltration route: you destroy 4 server cores and take out guards before reaching Slider.
- **Spying on Slider through the cameras** → Slider spots you and disables his room's camera for good.

> **Don't leave without:** knowing where the hideout's locked room is: with the key from **Treating Symptoms** it gives you the **Gris-Gris**. Keep doing Mr. Hands' gigs: Get It Together asks for at least 3.

## Rewards

- Gris-Gris (depends on the Treating Symptoms gig, missable).
