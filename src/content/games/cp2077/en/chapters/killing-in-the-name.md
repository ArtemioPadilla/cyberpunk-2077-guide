**Killing In The Name** starts some time after **Transmission** (Act 2): a message arrives on the **computer in your apartment** (Megabuilding H10, Little China) about the **Bartmoss Collective**. Read **Nancy Hartley**'s (Bes Isis) post about a certain **Swedenborg-Riviera**, who writes nonsense everyone quotes. To call Nancy you first have to advance **Kerry Eurodyne**'s chain to **Second Conflict** and **A Like Supreme**: the journal tells you to "complete other jobs" until she's available.

> **Order:** read the message on your computer → call Nancy → Rancho Coronado → Jackson Plains → Biotechnica Flats → West Wind Estate → decide what to do with the fortune-teller machine → call Nancy.

## The routers

At each spot: **scan the area**, **hack the router**, read the message you receive and talk to Johnny.

1. **Rancho Coronado** (Santo Domingo): an apartment building; the router is on the wall next to the fire escape.
2. **Jackson Plains** (Badlands): on top of a trailer, **guarded by Wraiths**.
3. **Biotechnica Flats**: at the top of one of the large Biotechnica installations.
4. **West Wind Estate** (Pacifica): next to a hacked **fortune-teller machine**, the original source of the signal. You can pay it **€$1** per fortune.

## Choices

- **Leave it alone** → the machine keeps spouting lines and misleading the net; Johnny is pleased.
- **Shut it down** → the site goes dark, to Johnny's dismay, and a niche commemorating Swedenborg appears at the North Oak columbarium.
- **Hack it with Intelligence to spout even more nonsense** → Johnny is pleased too.

Then call Nancy to report and close the job.

## Rewards

- 1,152 XP, 1,727 Street Cred and **€$6,220**.
