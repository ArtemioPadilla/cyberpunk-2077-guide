Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his first Dogtown set, which opens during **Lucretia My Reflection**. The Scavengers raided a **Zetatech** convoy and took a prototype implant and its documentation. The prototype's signal leads to **the Scraper**, an unfinished skyscraper in **Longshore Stacks**.

> **Order:** Scraper construction site → enter the den → follow the signal → talk to the prisoner → open his cell (2753) → take the tablet → lower the drawbridge → decide → Drop Point.

## The den

Get into the Scavenger den inside the Scraper (stealth or combat) and follow the signal to the top. The prototype is in the ocular system of **Hasan Demir**, an ex-Zetatech techie, locked in a cell: open it with the code **2753**. Follow him, take the **tablet with the schematics**, lower the **drawbridge** to escape and get him out of the base.

## Choices

- **Call Mr. Hands** → you decide together:
  - **Send the coordinates** → you turn Hasan over to Zetatech.
  - **Ask him to let Hasan go** → Mr. Hands agrees once the prototype is safely removed. A few days later Hasan opens a shop in Longshore Stacks: this opens **Go Your Own Way**.
- **Let him go yourself** → you keep the schematics for Mr. Hands. Days later Mr. Hands tells you a vendor is selling the same implant: a ripperdoc scammed Hasan out of it. You find him at the Kress Street Hideout and he no longer wants to talk to you.

At the end, leave the schematics at the **Drop Point**.
