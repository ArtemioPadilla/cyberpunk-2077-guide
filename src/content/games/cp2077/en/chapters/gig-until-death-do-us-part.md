**Until Death Do Us Part** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place in the **Gutierrez penthouse**, on the top floor of a building on Palm Street in **Charter Hill**. It belongs to Wakako's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Tyger and Vulture** and **We Have Your Wife**. The client, **Emilio Gutierrez**, lost almost everything in his divorce and left his depository receipts in the apartment, where his ex-wife **Nina** has already changed the locks. Wakako gives you elevator access and offers a **bonus if no one raises the alarm**.

> **Order:** go to the Palm Street building → go to the top floor → find Gutierrez's office → (optional) remain undetected → steal the shard → escape the building → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## Security

Take the main elevator up. Inside you will find:

- Two Militech **Wyvern** drones: one inside and one patrolling outside.
- A **robot** in a room on the second floor.
- Three security cameras.
- Two turrets in the main room, which only activate if the alarm goes off.
- **Red security gates** that trigger the alarm if you walk through them while active.

You can destroy the drones and the robot without an alarm as long as you stay undetected.

## The shard

It is on the second floor, in the room immediately to the left at the top of the stairs. The computer in that office can run an **emergency reset**: it permanently shuts down the drones, turrets and robot, and turns off the red gates for one minute. An open window in the dining room lets you onto the outside porch even while the gates are active.

> **Don't leave without:** the second-floor bathroom opens with a Technical Ability check and holds a loot container. The computers around the house tell how Nina ruined her ex-husband's life.

## Rewards

- €$4,200, 1,000 XP and 1,136 Street Cred (plus the no-alarm bonus).
