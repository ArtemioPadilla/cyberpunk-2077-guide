**Where Eagles Dare** arrived with **Update 2.3** and starts after **Motorbreath** (Act 2). The **Yaiba Semimaru** you took from the cyberpsychos has a broken **CrystalCoat**, the system that changes the car's color. The trail leads to **Mint**, the racer who built it years ago, and for that you need **Claire Russell**, the Afterlife bartender and ex-racer from **The Beast In Me**. If in that chain you chose to win the Big Race instead of helping her get revenge, V's journal doubts she'll be happy to see you. (The wiki page is a stub: it only lists the objectives.)

> **Order:** contact Claire about the CrystalCoat → share the Semimaru data with her → go to the place she points you to (Mint's garage, in Arroyo) → find info on the car → hack Mint's terminal → extract the CrystalCoat data → connect to the Semimaru and upload it → try the CrystalCoat.

## Mint's garage

Claire sends you to **Mint's garage** in **Arroyo** (Santo Domingo). There are **6th Street** members around: go in quietly or guns out. Look for info on the Semimaru, **hack Mint's terminal** and extract the CrystalCoat data. The wiki doesn't say which attribute the terminal needs.

Then **connect to the Semimaru**, upload the data and try the CrystalCoat feature to close the job.

## Rewards

- A working **CrystalCoat** for the Yaiba ARV-Q340 Semimaru.
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
