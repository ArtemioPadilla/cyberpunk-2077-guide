**New Person, Same Old Mistakes** is a short **Phantom Liberty** job that follows the gig **Gig: Waiting for Dodger**, from **Mr. Hands** in Dogtown. In that gig you rescue two NCPD officers, **Bill Mitchel** and **Charles Wilson**, held in an old police station in **Coastview** that is now the base of **Carl "Dodger" Robinson**, one of Hansen's men. This job **only appears** if, in the gig, you **save Bill and Charles and kill Dodger**. Some time later, Bill texts you. (The wiki marks the page as a stub.)

> **Order:** read Bill's message → go to Coyoacan in The Glen → talk to Bill → take or refuse the payment.

## Setting it up in Waiting for Dodger

If you haven't done the gig yet, keep this in mind:

- There are three ways into the station: the **front door** with the key Stella (Bill's partner) gives you, with Barghest guards inside; a **side door** on the right (**Body 15**) leading to a room with a single unaware guard; or a **hatch** on that room's roof (**Technical Ability 10**), watched by one soldier.
- In the storage room before the garage, an airdrop container holds the iconic machete **Volkodav**.
- **Kill Dodger:** he drops the iconic revolver **Rosco**, and it's the condition for this job.
- If instead you walk away peacefully from Dodger (tell the whole truth and kill no Barghest personnel), Bill and Charles live, but Bill stays in the NCPD under Dodger's blackmail. You find him by a patrol car outside Coyoacan, wishing you had killed him, and there is no job.

## Bill's stand

Bill has finally quit the force and opened his **scopdog** stand, **Breakout Hotdogs**, at **Coyoacan** in **The Glen** (Heywood). He dreams of saving enough eddies to open a restaurant. Talk to him: he offers you **€$2,000** as thanks for saving him.

## Choices

- **Take the money** → you get €$2,000.
- **Turn it down** → he gives you access to his wares. Among them are the **Military-Grade Lactic Acid Recyclers** (cyberware).

## Rewards

- 1,832 XP and 167 Street Cred.
- **€$2,000** if you take the payment.
