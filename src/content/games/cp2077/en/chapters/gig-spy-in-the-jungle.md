Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his second set, which opens during **Get It Together**. Brazilian agents **Steven Santos** and **Ana Friedman** wait in room 203 of the **Paradise Hotel**, in Longshore Stacks: the biomonitor of their mentor, **Mark Bana**, presumed dead, has switched back on at **Organitopia** (Terra Cognita).

> **Order:** Paradise Hotel → Organitopia → talk to "Bana" → main hall → fight Ribakov → secret room → decide on Katya → deliver the biomonitor.

## Organitopia

Go in through the smashed gate, open a shuttered window (**Body 3**) and jack into an access point. The indoor jungle holds Scavengers, Spider netrunners, and deactivated mines and turrets; if you fight, "Bana" won't open the door until no one is left.

**Boris Ribakov** drops from the ceiling. First he snipes with a Nekomata from the catwalks and uses optical camo, Sandevistan, smoke and flashbangs. After losing 30% of his health he vanishes and Griffin drones come in. Then he fights on the ground with the **Pizdets** smart SMG and holographic decoys that have little health and deal no damage. At 5% he adds more decoys and can use System Collapse. Loot the Pizdets and his contract shard.

## Choices

"Bana" is **Katya Karelina**, a **SovOil** spy. Ana orders you to kill her.

- **Kill her**, or **spare her** in exchange for her stash (opens **Money For Nothing**; you can still kill her afterwards).
- Steven asks you to leave the biomonitor at the **Drop Point**: doing so closes the gig.
- **Go back to the agents** → **give it to Ana** (the SovOil deal collapses, Ana ends up in prison and Steven hides out with BARGHEST) or **destroy it**. Destroying it or the Drop Point opens **The Show Must Go On**.

> **Don't leave without:** after the gig, the **Kappa x-MOD2** smart pistol, hidden in the abandoned diner behind the basketball court where you met Reed.
