**Fool on the Hill** comes from **Johnny Silverhand** and activates after **Playing for Time**, in **Act 2**. Johnny suggests asking **Viktor** or **Misty** about the tarot murals. The task is to **scan or interact with the 20 Major Arcana tarot graffiti** in Night City and the Badlands. Finishing it gives the **The Wandering Fool** achievement.

> **Order:** talk to Misty → talk to Viktor → find the 20 graffiti → talk to Misty.

**The Devil** and **Judgement** don't count (they only appear in certain endings), and neither do the four Phantom Liberty **Kings** (the job **Tomorrow Never Knows**, in Dogtown).

## Where they are

- **The Fool:** outside your apartment (Megabuilding H10, Little China).
- **The Magician:** outside Lizzie's Bar (Kabuki).
- **The High Priestess:** at Takemura's hideout (Vista del Rey).
- **The Empress:** down the stairs inside the Afterlife.
- **The Emperor:** left of the Konpeki Plaza gate (Arasaka Waterfront).
- **The Hierophant:** down at water level in a Japantown tunnel, near where you meet Takemura in **Down on the Street**.
- **The Lovers:** behind the screen at the Silver Pixel Cloud (North Oak).
- **Strength:** on a wall of the Rancho Coronado container freight station.
- **Wheel of Fortune:** at the Sunset Motel (Red Peaks).
- **The Hanged Man:** on a water tower near Johnny's grave (Oil Fields).
- **The Moon:** outside the Arasaka Estate (North Oak).
- **The World:** on the rooftop by Misty's Esoterica (Little China).

The wiki only gives the district for the rest: **The Chariot** and **The Sun** (Little China), **The Hermit** (Coastview), **Justice** (Arroyo), **Death** (The Glen), **Temperance** (North Oak), **The Tower** (Corpo Plaza) and **The Star** (Jackson Plains).

## With Misty

Once you have all 20, go back to Misty. She talks about the Fool's journey to The World and gives you her **dreamcatcher**, which shows up above your bed.

Update 2.5 added 12 Minor Arcana cards with their own job: **You Tell Me**.

## Rewards

- **Misty's Dreamcatcher** (decoration for your apartment).
- **The Wandering Fool** achievement.
