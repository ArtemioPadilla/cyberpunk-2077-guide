Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his first Dogtown set, which opens during **Lucretia My Reflection**. **Nika Yankovich** and a group of Scavengers have taken the **Haven Clinic**, a former church on Montaña de Oro Ave, because ripperdoc **Anthony Anderson** won't let her see her twin Gaspar and has barricaded himself in his office. The client is his assistant, **Odell Blanco**, who set up a temporary clinic across the road at **The Golden Fleece**.

> **Order:** talk to Odell → get into the clinic → go down to the basement → Anderson's office → talk to Nika and Anderson → leave.

## Getting in

- **Front door** with a **Body** check.
- On the **left side**: the scaffolding, watched by a sniper, or the **back door**, with a thug and a camera.

Inside, deal with the Scavengers (stealth or combat) and go down to Anderson's office. Nika holds him at gunpoint: Gaspar died of an overdose and Anderson harvested his implants to save other lives.

## Choices

- **Fight Nika** (you can do it at any point in the talk) → Anderson lives and keeps the clinic going; any Scavengers still conscious come for you.
- **Let Nika kill Anderson** → open the cold room across the hall with the laptop on the desk and tell Nika where her brother is. You convince Odell to keep the clinic running; Mr. Hands is disappointed, but closes the gig.
- **Calm everyone down** → pick "Everyone chill", "Just tell her, doc." and "Go help yourself", then "Least he didn't suffer", "Wasn't your choice to make, doc" and "Give Nika her brother.": Anderson gives up the body and nobody dies. Mr. Hands is pleased (if you killed Scavengers, he comments on the bodies).
