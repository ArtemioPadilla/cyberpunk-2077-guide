**The Nomad** is the Nomad lifepath prologue, and you only play it if you pick that lifepath when creating V. The other two lifepaths have their own prologue (The Streetkid and The Corpo-Rat); all three end by introducing you to **Jackie Welles** and lead into the same job, The Rescue. V has just left their nomad clan and, to get into Night City, takes a job from **Willie McCoy**: smuggle a package across the border together with a stranger from Heywood, Jackie. It all happens in Los Padres County, in the Badlands: the town of Yucca, Dry Creek's Farm, the SoCal Border Crossing and the Biotechnica fields. It is a short, linear job meant to teach you to drive and move around.

> **Order:** talk to the mechanic → fix the engine and start it → talk to the sheriff → leave the garage → climb the telecom tower and call McCoy → drive to Dry Creek's Farm and meet Jackie → open the trunk → drive to the border → deposit your weapons, go to room 2 and talk to the guard → collect your weapons and leave → fight off the pursuers → hide with Jackie and talk to him.

> **Get ready (creating V):**
>
> - **Difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard): change it whenever you like; since 2.1 it lives in the Accessibility tab.
> - **Attributes:** each starts at 3 and you spend **7 free points** (maximum 6 per attribute at creation; later they go up to 20). Each level gives +1 Attribute point and +1 Perk point. **Attribute points are permanent**; perks can be refunded one by one for free.
> - **Romances** (body and voice matter, lifepath doesn't): **Judy** needs a feminine body and voice, **River** a feminine body, **Panam** a masculine body and **Kerry** a masculine body and voice.
> - **Lifepath:** it only changes the prologue and some dialogue; **none is mechanically better**.
> - **Phantom Liberty:** creation offers to **skip ahead to Phantom Liberty**. That auto-completes the prologue and every main job through Transmission: you skip all of Act 1 and its choices.

## Yucca garage

V starts in a mechanic's garage in Yucca, removing their old clan patch in front of the mirror. V's car, a Thorton Galena "Rattler", needs a part the mechanic doesn't have.

1. **Talk to the mechanic**, then **fix the engine** yourself: it is a workaround that will hold until Night City.
2. **Get in the car** and **start it**.
3. Before leaving, search the garage: the game's **first shard**, "The Unification War... For Gonks!", is here, plus consumables. A crate by the **front-left tire** holds common components.

**The sheriff.** As you leave, the town sheriff stops you and asks pointed questions: nomads, with a clan or without one, are not welcome. **What you tell him has no consequences**, same as the chat with the mechanic. Talk to him and leave the garage.

**Town loot:**

- Enter the **mechanic's office through the front door**: a drawer behind his desk holds **€$100**.
- **Between the trailers**, near a man playing guitar, there is a **shard** and **€$61**.

## Telecom tower

Head south to the Yucca telecom tower. **The entrance gate opens with a kick.** A side platform has common crafting materials.

1. **Climb the tower** and find the control box.
2. **Connect your sat phone** to the tower.
3. **Talk to McCoy**: he gives you your contact's location, an abandoned farm near the border.
4. **Return to the car.**

## Dry Creek's Farm: Jackie

Drive to Dry Creek's Farm, get out and **meet Jackie Welles**. Talk to him, follow him and **open the trunk**: Jackie puts the package in.

Before leaving, go into **Jackie's trailer**: there is **€$45** on the middle shelf under the bed, plus junk and a **shard**.

Get in the car and drive to the border.

## The border

1. **Drive into the checkpoint** and talk to Jackie while the security check finishes.
2. **Get out** and enter the border security building.
3. **Deposit your weapons at the front desk**, go to **room 2** and **sit**.
4. **Talk to the border guard**: you hand over the cargo declaration ("lost on arrival") and can slip the bribe (a credit chip) in with it. The guard accepts, but V gets the feeling he knows more than he lets on.
5. **Collect your weapons** at the front desk, return to the car and **depart the border crossing**. Jackie takes the wheel.

## The chase

As you leave, Arasaka security cuts you off and demands the cargo: it was a setup. Jackie floors it and you shoot from the car.

- **Ammo is unlimited** during this chase, though you still have to reload.
- **Shoot the cars' grilles**, between the headlights, to blow them up.
- There are about **three** pursuing vehicles.

Jackie takes you through a Biotechnica protein farm to a garage where V's car dies for good. **Get out and talk to Jackie.** Opening the package reveals the cargo is a **live iguana**. Jackie offers you his friendship and the Night City introduction montage begins, leading into **The Rescue**.

## Choices

- **Mechanic and sheriff:** any answer → no consequences.
- **The bribe at the border:** part of the scene with the guard; no documented consequences.
- **The Nomad lifepath in the long run** → gives you unique dialogue all game (the "Stanislavski's Method" achievement asks for 10 lifepath lines) and the exclusive side job **These Boots Are Made for Walkin'**. In **The Pickup**, only a Nomad V understands a message that exposes Militech's mole, earning a **Militech gear crate** in Act 2.

> **Don't leave without:** the shard and components in the garage, the **€$100** in the office drawer, the shard and **€$61** between the trailers, the tower materials, and the **€$45** and shard in Jackie's trailer. Once you leave Yucca you don't come back to this area until Act 2.

## Rewards

- **Memorabilia:** a **car model** on the desk in V's apartment.
- **Lifepath starting gear:** the **DR5 Nova** pistol, V's vest (with or without the Bakkers patch), and nomad pants and boots.
