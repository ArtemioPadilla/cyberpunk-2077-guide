**Sex on Wheels** is a minor job in **Rancho Coronado**, **Santo Domingo**, in a garage at the container freight station. It depends on the gig **Life's Work**, from fixer **Padre** in The Glen (Heywood), where you have to return **Jake Estevez**'s pickup truck. It is Act 2 content: neither Heywood nor Santo Domingo opens while you are in Watson.

- **If you completed Life's Work:** about a day after returning the truck, **Jake** calls and sends you to the garage.
- **If you failed Life's Work:** some time later **Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes** texts you about a preem ride stashed in that garage. You can also start it just by walking up to the garage.

> **Order:** Jake's call or El Capitán's text → garage by the cargo station → (Scavengers, if you failed Life's Work) → get in the car → leave the area.

## How to do it

In El Capitán's version there are **Scavengers** at the garage: take them out to get in. Jake's version mentions no enemies. Inside is the **Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech**: get in and leave the area to finish.

The car you take here is a separate copy from the one you can later summon from your vehicle list. This copy stays in the world and does not despawn like other vehicles unless it is destroyed.

## Rewards

The **Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech**. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
