**Wakako's Favorite** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place in the basement of the **Ginger Panda**, a restaurant on Crescent Street in **Japantown**. It belongs to Wakako's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the second set (**Until Death Do Us Part**, **Greed Never Pays** and **Olive Branch**). **Chang-Hoon Nam** has worked with Wakako for years and never let her down, but he stopped answering her. He rents that basement as his netrunner den.

> **Order:** find a way to the hideout → find Chang-Hoon → find out what happened to him → follow the lights → find the shard that stabilizes him → take it → slot it into his neural port → talk to him → leave the area.

## The basement

Careful: the **stairwell down is mined**. Below you find Chang-Hoon in his netrunner chair, unresponsive but alive. Check his computer: the messages switch on some **red lights**.

## Following the lights

The lights lead to a dead-end hallway. Look down: there is a **grate** you can open. The tunnel has more mines before you reach the room holding the **shard** you need. Alternatively, force the doors open with **Body 20**. With the shard, leave the room through the door, slot it into Chang-Hoon's neural port and ask him how he ended up like this. Then leave the area to complete the gig.

## Afterwards

According to the wiki, if you save him, Chang-Hoon later becomes a **netrunner vendor**: he sells you quickhacks at a **20% discount**. In Phantom Liberty he can also appear in **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** if you ask him for help with the intel on the MaxTac convoy.
