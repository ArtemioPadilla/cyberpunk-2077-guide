**The Ride** is the meeting with the fixer **Dexter DeShawn** ("Dex"). As you leave Viktor's clinic, **Jackie** tells you he has set up a meeting with him. Dex waits in his limo (a Chevillon Thrax), a short walk from **Misty's Esoterica** in Little China (Watson); the talk happens during the ride and ends at **Kabuki Roundabout**, in Kabuki. It is a conversation job with no combat that opens Act 1's big gig: stealing an **experimental Arasaka biochip**.

> **Order:** talk to Jackie → meet Dex → get into the limo → talk to Dex → exit at Kabuki Roundabout → call Jackie → talk to Jackie (and choose where to start).

> **Get ready:** nothing special. If you want to go into the next jobs stronger, note that The Pickup and The Information recommend about **level 3** per IGN.

## Misty's Esoterica and the limo

1. **Talk to Jackie** at Misty's Esoterica.
2. **Meet Dex**: his limo is a short walk from the shop.
3. **Get into the limo** and **talk to Dex.**

**The gig.** Dex lays out the job: steal a biochip from Arasaka. It needs a **Flathead**, a **Militech** combat spider-bot that the **Maelstrom** gang took when it ambushed a convoy. **Meredith Stout**, a Militech agent, could help you get it back from them. Dex also suggests visiting the client, **Evelyn Parker**. A Corpo V gets a unique line deducing Meredith's corpo tactics.

## Kabuki Roundabout: the choice

1. **Exit the limo** at Kabuki Roundabout.
2. **Call Jackie** and **talk to him**: you fill him in on the meeting and he asks where to start.

The next two jobs can be done **in either order** and **both are needed** for **The Heist**.

## Choices

- **"Visit Maelstrom first"** → **The Pickup** becomes the active job.
- **"Gonna start with the client"** → **The Information** becomes the active job.
- **Consequence:** none. It only changes which of the two jobs is marked active in the journal; you must do both. game8 recommends starting with **The Information**: it has no combat and gives you time to level up and gear up before the Maelstrom factory.

## After the job

- **Fast travel:** fast travel terminals (_dataterms_) are added to the map as you pass near them and **can only be used from The Ride on**.
- **Regina Jones:** you can call her to ask about **Royce**, the Maelstrom leader you will deal with in The Pickup.
- **The Gift:** you are dropped off near **Kabuki Roundabout**, home to **Yoko Tsuru**'s netrunner shop: IGN suggests picking up your **free Ping quickhack** now.
- During Act 1 you can only explore **Watson**.

> **Don't leave without:** stopping at Yoko Tsuru's shop at Kabuki Roundabout for **The Gift** and, if you want more background for The Pickup, calling Regina about Royce.

## Rewards

- **1,516 XP** and **167 Street Cred**, per the wiki.
