**These Boots Are Made for Walkin'** only exists if you picked the **Nomad** lifepath. During **Ghost Town**, after you see **Rogue** at the Afterlife, you get a message from your old car, the **Thorton Galena "Rattler"** you lost in the prologue (The Nomad). It gives its location in the **Badlands**: the **Municipal Landfill** in **Red Peaks**, south of the Medeski Fuel Station. The wiki classes it as a side job. It is Act 2 content.

> **Order:** go to the location → scan the car (optional) → look under the hood → check the engine → talk to the stranger → get in the car → talk to Johnny.

## How to do it

The engine is still warm: someone drove it recently. As you finish checking it, **Lana Prince** walks up, surprised and annoyed that you are touching "her" car. She fixed it because she wants to leave the city and start over, and it is her only hope. Save before you answer.

## Choices

- **Let her keep it** → you give her repair tips. The car stays where it is, you can no longer use it and Lana disappears when you leave: **you don't get the Rattler back**.
- **Buy it from her** → you pay her a large sum so she can buy another car and leave. Since Patch 2.0 the price scales with your level. The Rattler is yours again.
- **Threaten her** (even at gunpoint) → she calls you names and leaves. The Rattler is yours again.

## Rewards

The **Thorton Galena "Rattler"**, if you buy it or threaten her. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
