**Spider and the Fly** follows straight on from Hole in the Sky. Myers survived, but Kurt Hansen is hunting her and you are cut off in Dogtown. You have to get her somewhere safe, remove the tracker she's carrying, cross the **Akebono Hotel** without BARGHEST's drone spotting you and get through the **Founding Our Future Expo**. There Songbird takes over a Militech robot tank, the **Chimera**, to help you… until she loses control and the Chimera turns on you. It's Phantom Liberty's first boss.

> **Order:** talk to Songbird → lead Myers to safety → open the door with Myers → remove her tracker → hide from the drone → cross the building (fusebox, elevator, access point) → optional BARGHEST car → reach the building avoiding patrols → leave the parking lot → the Expo → the elevator → fight until Songbird is ready → support the Chimera → flee the Chimera → elevator to the subway → chandelier → **boss: Chimera** → finish it off.

> **Get ready:** bring plenty of ammo and weapons that do damage at range: the Chimera has **yellow weak points** you shoot from a distance. Arm cyberware matters for the finisher (Gorilla Arms or Mantis Blades give their own animations, but don't change the outcome). Save before riding down the Expo elevator.

## Getting Myers away from the crash site

1. Talk to Songbird and **lead Myers to safety**.
2. **Help Myers open the door** and follow her.
3. Talk to her: she's carrying a **tracker**. **Help her remove it**.
4. A BARGHEST **drone** passes: **hide** until it's gone and talk to Myers again.

## Through the building

You have to **escort Myers through the building** without the drone spotting you:

1. Talk to Songbird.
2. Find the **fusebox** in the utility room and **reset it**.
3. Take the **elevator** to the upper floor.
4. **Connect to the access point** so Songbird can distract the drone.

**The BARGHEST car (optional).** You can **take the BARGHEST car** for the next stretch. If you do, there's a chase with vehicle combat; you can also get out and fight on foot. The goal is to **reach the building while avoiding the patrols** and **lose the tail** before you get there.

5. Take the elevator out of the parking lot, step out and talk to Myers.

## The Expo and the ambush

1. **Go through the Expo** (Founding Our Future Expo), **open the door** and enter the **elevator**.
2. **Get ready to fight:** Hansen's soldiers arrive. **Hold out until Songbird is ready**: she's getting into a BARGHEST Chimera.
3. Once the Chimera activates, **support it** against Hansen's people. Let the tank carry the fight and pick off soldiers from cover.
4. Talk to Songbird. Something goes wrong: she loses control of the robot (a rogue AI from beyond the Blackwall takes it) and the Chimera attacks V and Myers.

## Escape to the subway and the chandelier

1. **Flee the Chimera** and **take the elevator down to the subway**.
2. **Survive until the elevator arrives.** According to PowerPyx, the safest spot is **behind the pillar opposite Myers** until it comes.
3. A series of **chandelier QTEs** follows: **shoot the cables** holding it, jump, grab and dodge. **Miss the first one and you die**, so be ready for the on-screen prompt.

## Boss — Militech Chimera

The Chimera is a six-legged tank with a dome turret, a laser cannon, a flechette cannon and missile launchers. The fight takes place after its fall, already damaged.

- **Weak points:** shoot the **glowing yellow spots**. If you can't see them, **scan it** to find the ones left (there's an optional objective to destroy them all). The game's own database says it is vulnerable to attacks from above.
- **Front laser:** when it sweeps the floor side to side, **jump over it**.
- **Missiles:** dodge the barrages. **Change cover often**: the Chimera destroys cover.
- **At about 70% health** **repair drones** appear. **Destroy them** (optional objective) or the Chimera heals.
- **At about 40%** it floods the floor with **toxic gas**. Myers warns you: get to **high ground** until it clears.
- **At 2%** it stops. **Jump on it** and use "Finish it off".

**The finisher depends on your arms:**

- **Gorilla Arms:** V rips out the **core** and throws it away.
- **Mantis Blades:** V stabs the core and pulls it out.
- **Any other cyberware or none:** V uses a grenade Myers hands over.

In every case the core ends up on the ground; you pick it up at the start of the next job.

## Choices

- **Taking the BARGHEST car or not** → only changes the stretch: with the car there's a vehicle chase; without it, you go on foot avoiding patrols.
- **Destroying the repair drones** → optional, but if you don't, the Chimera regains health.
- **Arm cyberware** → only changes the finisher animation.

After this job the side job **Balls to the Wall** opens (Paco and Babs, at the BARGHEST garrison outside the Black Sapphire). **Do it before Firestarter**: once Firestarter is done it becomes unavailable and, if it's active, it fails.

> **Don't leave without:** noting where the **Chimera's core** ends up: it lies by Myers at the start of Lucretia My Reflection and is missable if you don't take it.

## Rewards

- No items of its own; the **Active Chimera Core** is taken in Lucretia My Reflection.
