**Placide** gives it at **Batty's Hotel** (Coastview, Pacifica). The **Animals** have dug into the **Grand Imperial Mall** (GIM) as private security for a **NetWatch** netrunner who is attacking the Voodoo Boys' network. Your job is to get in, jack into a van in the lobby and find the agent. On the way is **Sasquatch**, this Animals pack's leader, and at the end a choice that **completely changes how Transmission ends**.

> **Order:** leave Batty's Hotel → meet Placide's people → enter the mall through the garage → find the van in the lobby → connect to it → talk to Placide → find the agent in the cinema (Sasquatch, optional if sneaking) → confront the agent → decide → leave the GIM → head back to Batty's Hotel → talk to Placide and Brigitte.

> **Get ready:** decide whether you're going **stealth**: if nobody detects you in the whole GIM, the cinema gates stay open and **you can avoid Sasquatch**. If you plan to fight, bring **thermal** damage (her weakness) and quickhacks like **Short Circuit** and **Overheat**; Sasquatch has a **€$15,000** bounty (the wiki; game8 says "16k"). **Save before talking to the agent**: that choice is final.

## Arrival and the garage

**Placide's two sentries** wait by an old vehicle near the GIM. Talk to them: they tell you to go in **through the garage**.

The garage has about **three Animals** at the entrance and **five more** inside; two of them leave the complex and the other three group up in the left corner. It's a good spot to practise quiet takedowns before the atrium.

**game8 shortcut:** going through **Kabayan Foods** on the upper floor lets you skip both the van and Sasquatch.

**Skill-check doors:** in the corridor before the fitness centre there are **Body** or **Technical Ability** doors leading to the atrium's upper floor (game8).

## The atrium and the van

Placide warns you before you enter. The atrium is a large space on the lower level with a gallery around it on the upper level: about **six Animals on the gallery**, **12 to 18 below** and a **turret**.

- You enter at one corner of the gallery. Right in front of you is a pair of **escalators** going down.
- Downstairs you reach a side room. Leaving it, you find another: inside is a **server** linked by a thick cable to the **van**, parked by another entrance to that room.
- **Three alert Animals** stand by the van.

**Connect to the van.** You learn the NetWatch netrunner is in the **upstairs cinema**, but connecting also **tips him off that you're there**. Talk to Placide.

## The cinema

Head up to the cinema. If you fought in the atrium, **Placide opens a route** to it: one enemy at the top of the escalator and, round the corner, the cinema entrance with **Sasquatch** on guard.

- **If you were detected** at any point in the GIM, the netrunner has shut the gates and Sasquatch is waiting: fight (see "Boss — Sasquatch").
- **If nobody detected you**, the gates stay open and **you can sneak past**. If Sasquatch spots you, the gates close and the fight starts anyway.
- Sneaking, you can **creep up behind her and rip out the implant on her back** before the fight for heavy damage (game8).

## The agent: Bryce Mosley

In the projection room you find **Bryce Mosley**, the NetWatch agent (the screen is showing _One-Eyed Jacks_). He tells you the Voodoo Boys plan to double-cross you. **Save here.** You have to choose (see "Choices"):

- **Make a deal with him:** **take the shard**.
- **Knock him out:** **jack into the agent**, then talk to Johnny.

## Exit and Batty's Hotel

**Leave the Grand Imperial Mall** and talk to Placide. What happens with the Animals on the way out depends on what you did (see "Choices"). Outside, Placide's sentries are shocked to see you alive and offer to drive you back: you can **get in the back seat** or decline and talk to them.

At **Batty's Hotel**, **talk to Placide**, then **Brigitte**. Depending on your choice, Placide calls you "a normal, filthy ranyon", or Brigitte defends you as "no ranyon".

## Choices

- **Deal with Mosley (take the shard)** → the shard **removes Placide's malware from you**, but installs a **NetWatch tracker**. In **Transmission**, when they contact Alt, she takes the Voodoo Boys for NetWatch and **kills Brigitte and everyone connected**; you'll have to **fight Placide** to get out of the church. If you also **spared Sasquatch**, the Animals let you leave the GIM in peace; if you **killed her**, they hold a grudge and turn hostile.
- **Knock Mosley out (stay with the Voodoo Boys)** → Placide's virus **wipes out many NetWatch facilities** and nearly kills you too (the Relic saves you). In Transmission, Brigitte's netrunners cross the Blackwall with Alt's help and afterwards **you choose to leave peacefully or settle the score**. game8 says this option gives "less rewards" but lets you punch Placide (unconfirmed).
- **If Mosley dies** (either way) → the remaining Animals **fight you on the way out**.
- **Sasquatch alive or dead** → only changes how the Animals see you off (see above). If you defeat her, you can mention her in the side job **No Easy Way Out** to intimidate Angie.

## Boss — Sasquatch

**Matilda K. Rose**, aka Sasquatch, the pack leader. She fights in an arena-like open space with her hammer.

- **The clock is ticking:** while you fight, Mosley tries to **fry your brain** with hacks. Placide slows them, but not completely: **finish fast**.
- **Weak point:** the **glowing implant on her back** (game8 calls it a purple "Juicer"). Destroy it: she stops regenerating and slowly loses health.
- **Her hammer swings** are powerful but slow, with several seconds between them, and she "rests" after a full combo. Dodge the swing, circle her and punish her back during that pause.
- **Quickhacks** (game8): **Short Circuit** destroys the Juicer instantly; **Overheat** works very well (thermal is her weakness); **Cripple Movement** and **System Reset** help.
- **Her abilities:** Berserk, Sandevistan Mk.1, Kerenzikov, Frag Grenades and Juice Tank.
- **Stealth:** if you reach her unseen, ripping out the implant before the fight deals huge damage (game8).

## What opens here

- **Transmission:** the meeting with Alt. How it ends depends entirely on the Mosley choice.
- **No Easy Way Out** (side job): if you defeated Sasquatch, you get an extra intimidation option.
- No other chain changes here.

> **Don't leave without:** **Sasquatch's Hammer**. Loot it after the fight; sneaking, you can take it before she picks it up, and she can also drop it if she throws it or if you destroy her implant. A possible random drop from Sasquatch is the **Animals Power-Absorbent Jumpsuit** (game8).

## Rewards

- **1135 XP**, **3574 Street Cred** and **€$16,720** (game8).
- **Sasquatch's Hammer** and Sasquatch's bounty (€$15,000 per the wiki; game8 says 16k).
