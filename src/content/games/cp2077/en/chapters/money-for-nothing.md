Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It comes from **Katya Karelina**, the ex-SovOil agent who was posing as Mark Bana in the gig **Spy in the Jungle**. If you **spare her** in that gig in exchange for her stash's location, she sends you the coordinates. Even if you spare her, the Scavengers kill her later anyway; the stash is still yours.

> **Order:** spare Katya → go to the abandoned Longshore Stacks parking garage → move the dumpster → open the grate → take the stash.

## The stash

Go to the marker: an **abandoned parking garage in Longshore Stacks**, in Dogtown. The stash isn't in plain sight. Find a **dumpster** and move it; underneath is a **grate**. Open it and the hiding spot with the money and a shard is inside.

There are no enemies and no choices. Johnny, in the journal, doubts it's worth it, but it's easy eddies.

> **Don't leave without:** the shard **For Katya**, next to the money.

## Rewards

- €$17,500
- Shard For Katya
- The wiki gives no XP or Street Cred for this job.
