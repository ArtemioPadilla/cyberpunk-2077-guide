**Anna Hamill** is an honest NCPD cop digging into the smuggling racket in **Kabuki Market** (Watson), and her corrupt colleagues want her stopped. **Regina Jones** takes the job but asks you not to kill her: talk her into leaving or dropping the case. It is in **set 3 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the three gigs of set 2 (Troublesome Neighbors, Welcome to America, Comrade and Dirty Biz). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** find Anna (optional: ask around the market) → confront her → convince her to drop the case → leave the area.

## Asking around the market

- **Imad:** arguing with a food vendor. Bribe him, intimidate him as a **Streetkid** or win him over as a **Nomad**.
- **Eve:** bribe only; she gives you the exact room.
- **Robert Rainwater:** free with **Street Cred 16** or higher; he also gives you the room.

Anna is at **Motel Kabuki**, third floor, **room 303**. Get in by paying for a room (front door), through the back door with enough **Technical Ability**, or by climbing the building across the street. The room number is also on the terminal in the reception office.

## Choices

There are **three timed replies** in a row. Don't move within 7 meters of her, don't draw a weapon and answer in time, or she opens fire.

- "Here to warn you." then "Just wanna help you." → peaceful outcome.
- Third reply "It doesn't matter." as a **Nomad** → she leaves Night City. With the other option she drops the case but stays: a day later there's a dead cop in the market and, days after that, the radio reports Anna died in a shootout at a precinct.
- **If she attacks** → neutralize her; if you knock her out, carry her to the fixer's car.
- If you bribed or intimidated Imad, he turns up dead by the Drop Point a day later.

## Rewards

- €$7,800, 1,000 XP and 590 Street Cred.
