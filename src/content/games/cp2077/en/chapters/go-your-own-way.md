Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. **Mr. Hands** sets it off as a consequence of the gig **Prototype in the Scraper**. For it to appear, in that gig you must **call Mr. Hands** and ask him to let **Hasan Demir** go once the prototype is safely removed. If you let Hasan go without calling the fixer, or hand him to Zetatech, this job doesn't appear.

> **Order:** let Hasan go with Mr. Hands's approval → wait a few days → Hasan's message → his shop in Longshore Stacks → take the gun.

## The visit

A few days after the gig, Hasan texts you: he has set up shop in **Longshore Stacks**, in Dogtown. Go to the marker and talk to him. To thank you for your help, he gives you the **Ambition**, an iconic variant of the **Arasaka JKE-X2 Kenshin** pistol. Pick it up and you're done.

There are no fights and no choices. Johnny remarks that the guy has a head for business and wonders whether he misses his old corporate cage.

## Rewards

- Ambition (iconic Tech pistol, JKE-X2 Kenshin)
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
