Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his first Dogtown set, which opens during **Lucretia My Reflection**. **Milko Alexis**, a Voodoo Boys netrunner, threatens to hack **Indira Barazza**'s lungs. His base is the **Luxor High Wellness Spa**, in Dogtown.

> **Order:** get into the base → (optional) talk to the supplier → server room → emergency exit → talk to the netrunner → decide → leave.

## The base

Get into the Voodoo Boys base. On the way you may run into **Kyle Araujo**, an implant supplier: if you intimidate him (a **Body** check) or bribe him, he explains that leftover implants go into old combat robots and offers to sabotage the server room to make them explosive. You can also chew him out or neutralize him.

In the **server room** a group of **Robot R Mk.2**s attacks. Bring down the elite one: the rest self-destruct and it drops the **Ogou** smart pistol. Follow the emergency hallway to Milko, jacked in, next to **Alan Noël**, an undercover **NetWatch** agent who needs Milko alive to infiltrate the Voodoo Boys' network.

## Choices

- **Spare Milko and Alan** → Indira goes into NetWatch witness protection; Mr. Hands pays you an extra €$8,000 and Noël sends €$2,000 later. With the **Corpo** lifepath option, that message opens **Corpo of the Month**.
- **Refuse the offer** → you neutralize both and fulfill the original contract.
- **Shoot Milko before hearing Alan out** → Alan scolds you, but lets you go.

> **Don't leave without:** **Slider's hideout storage room key**. According to the wiki you get it during this gig (it doesn't say where) and it opens the locked room in Slider's hideout with the iconic Gris-Gris revolver, from The Damned. Search the base well before you leave.
