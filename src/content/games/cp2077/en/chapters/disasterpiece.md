**Judy Álvarez** gives it as you leave Fingers' clinic. You need to get a specific **XBD** (illegal braindance) on **Jig-Jig Street** (Japantown), analyse it with Judy to work out where it was recorded, then go to that **old power plant** (Electric Corporation Power Plant, between Charter Hill and Arroyo), taken over by the **Scavengers**. Evelyn is there. The job has no heavy choices, but it does have an easy-to-miss iconic katana and an infiltration you can do quietly or loud.

> **Order:** talk to Judy → find the XBD dealer (via the net, or Wakako → sex shop) → buy **XBD: Deep-Fried Death's-Head Moth** → Judy's van → analyse the BD → talk to Judy → ride with her to the plant → main building → sub-levels → level -1 → lead Judy → find Evelyn → grab the cable → talk to Judy → elevator → talk to Johnny.

> **Get ready:** have **eddies** on hand for the XBD (sources disagree on the price: game8 gives €$4,837 and IGN €$1,000, probably from 1.x; the price may depend on level). The plant is full of Scavengers: bring suppressed weapons or quickhacks if you want to go quietly, since Judy follows you and you can let her distract them. Save before going down to the sub-levels.

## Finding the XBD dealer

Talk to Judy and, if you like, **ask around about XBDs on Jig-Jig Street** (optional). The wiki's objectives give two routes, both leading to the same dealer:

- **Net route:** find a **terminal** and look up the **"Pleasures of NC"** domain. Get into its **underground twin** and find an XBD dealer there.
- **Wakako route:** **call Wakako** or visit her on Jig-Jig Street and ask about the XBDs with the "Death's-head" moth. She sends you to the **sex shop** on Jig-Jig Street: visit it.

Then **find the XBD dealer** on Jig-Jig Street. The keyword with him is **"Death's Head"**.

## Buying the XBD

Buy **XBD: Deep-Fried Death's-Head Moth**. If you don't want to pay, you can also **kill the dealer** and loot it (IGN). Then meet Judy at her **van**, get in and put on the braindance gear.

## Analysing the braindance

You're looking for where the BD was recorded. The clues (IGN):

**Visual layer:**

- The **netrunner** and the **scav**, from the start onwards.
- A **pizza box** and a **scav woman** at **00:11**.
- **Coveralls** and a **box** at **00:23**.
- A **coffee cup** at **00:38**.

**Audio layer:**

- The **radio** at **00:10** and **00:32**.

The clues point to an **EC power plant** near a **Buck-A-Slice**. Exit the BD once you have it and talk to Judy.

## The power plant

Ride with Judy to the old plant in Charter Hill. **Reach the main building**, find a way down to the **sub-levels** and get to **level -1**.

From there you have to **lead Judy** through the sub-levels. Two ways to clear the path:

- **Let Judy distract the Scavengers** (optional) and slip past while they look her way.
- **Neutralize the Scavengers** yourself (optional).

There's a **hackable access point** that can **blow up the furnace**: use it to take out several at once.

**Errata**, the iconic katana, is **at the forge** inside the plant. Look for it before you reach Evelyn.

IGN also mentions, in a guide written for 1.x, an **M-179e Achilles** in the crate in the weapons room, an **SPT32 Grad** behind a **Technical Ability** door in the hallway, and Mantis Blade mods. They may not be the same in 2.5: check if you pass by.

## Evelyn

**Find Evelyn**, go over to her, **grab the cable** and talk to Judy. Ride the elevator up and talk to **Johnny** to wrap up the job.

## Choices

- **Route to the dealer** (the net or Wakako) → only changes the path; the dealer is the same.
- **Paying for the XBD or killing the dealer** → you save eddies by killing him; sources list no story consequence.
- **Stealth with Judy as a distraction, or an open fight** → doesn't change how the job ends.

## What opens here

- **Double Life:** Evelyn goes to Judy's apartment, where you analyse her BDs. It's the last job in the Evelyn line.
- It doesn't open or close side chains on its own. Judy's chain (up to **Pyramid Song**) starts after Double Life.

> **Don't leave without:** **Errata** at the plant's forge. If you miss it, Herold Lowe sells it in Phantom Liberty. Also check the weapons room and the Technical Ability door in the hallway in case the old loot is still there.
