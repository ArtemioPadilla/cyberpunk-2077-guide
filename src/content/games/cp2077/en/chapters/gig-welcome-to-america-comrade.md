**Mikhail Akulov**, the Soviet Union's top fixer, arrived in Night City claiming he's in town for a small deal. Nobody buys it. **Regina Jones**'s client wants to follow his every step: you have to plant a **GPS tracker** on his car, which sits in a container at the **Kabuki docks** (Watson), and keep it quiet. It is in **set 2 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the five gigs of set 1 (Monster Hunt, Playing for Keeps, Hippocratic Oath, Many Ways to Skin a Cat and Rite of Passage). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the Kabuki docks → optional: remain undetected → find the container with the vehicle → plant the GPS tracker → leave the docks → if nobody saw you, collect the bonus from the Drop Point.

## How to do it

- The optional objective is to **stay undetected** for the whole gig: move crouched, scan to spot guards and cameras, and stay out of their sight cones.
- Find the container with the car (a Rayfield Aerondight "Guinevere"), plant the GPS and leave the area.
- If you stayed hidden, stop by the **Drop Point** for your bonus.
- This gig sets up **Fixer, Merc, Soldier, Spy** (set 8), where you run into Akulov again.

## Rewards

- €$2,600, 1,000 XP and 590 Street Cred, plus the Drop Point bonus if nobody spots you.
- Optional, per the wiki: an **M-10AF Lexington**.
