**KOLD MIRAGE** starts in one of three ways, all in **Act 2**: by reading the shard **Notice of Expiration**; some time after **Spellbound**, when **Nix** texts that he decrypted the book and sends coordinates; or just by reaching the spot after **Ghost Town**. It's deep inside the **Municipal Landfill**, down the road past the Medeski Fuel Station. Spellbound and KOLD MIRAGE can't be active at the same time.

> **Order:** go to the coordinates → open the freezer → talk to Johnny → scan the freezer → take the deck → go to Nix at the Afterlife → place it on his desk → wait for the decryption → save Nix → talk to Nix.

## The freezer

It's an old freezer from 2020. Open it: inside is the body of **Rache Bartmoss**. After talking to Johnny, scan the freezer and take **Bartmoss' Cyberdeck**.

> **Don't leave without:** **Plan B**, **Dexter DeShawn**'s iconic pistol, on his body at this same landfill. It fires eddies instead of bullets (€$1 per shot). If you skip it, with Phantom Liberty Herold Lowe sells it in Dogtown.

## With Nix

Take the deck to the **Afterlife** (Little China). Nix thinks **NetWatch** killed Bartmoss and offers to decrypt it now or later. Place it on his desk. At first it goes smoothly, but a hidden piece of malware, a "brain broiler", trips and Nix's cyberware starts to malfunction.

## Choices

- **Override the power panel** by the door → you save Nix.
- **Jack into the computer next to his desk** and breach the deck yourself → you save Nix and he gets full access to the data. Out of gratitude he gives you five quickhacks: **Memory Wipe**, **Reboot Optics**, **Short Circuit**, **Sonic Shock** and **System Collapse**. Per the wiki, only this option gives the quickhacks.

## Rewards

- Five quickhacks (if you breach the deck yourself).
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
