**Eye for an Eye** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at **Tucker Albach's condo**, on the corner of Scoffield and Sinkyone Streets in **the Glen**. It belongs to Padre's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the second (**Psychofan**, **Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta** and **Old Friends**). **Rosita**, a 17-year-old girl Padre knew, was run over at night; the driver fled. The NCPD found the culprit, **Tucker Albach**, a vice managing director at **Kiroshi**, but her insurance covered vehicular manslaughter and she walked. Padre wants an eye for an eye.

> **Order:** get inside Tucker's building → find her → neutralize her (optional: talk first) → carry her to the fixer's car or leave the building.

## Getting in

You have three routes: the **front door**, with a guard; the **back door** (Technical Ability); or an **upper window**, using an augmented jump to reach the fire escape. Inside there are **eight Militech guards** spread around. Avoid or neutralize them on your way to the **office on the top floor**.

## Choices

You can take her down right away or hear her out. Tucker thinks a competitor sent you and, on learning it is about the girl, says the case is closed because she already paid. Then she offers a deal: **the money in her safe** (more than you are being paid, she claims) in exchange for her life.

- **Neutralize her non-lethally** → carry her out of the building to the fixer's car. Padre asks whether you have turned pacifist.
- **Kill her** → leave the building. Her plaque later appears at the Columbarium in North Oak.
- **Take the deal** → Tucker stands down her security and you can empty the safe and enter the locked rooms. Padre complains that it is not up to you to decide the fate of sinners, but closes the contract by saying Tucker disappeared.
- **Take the deal, then neutralize her** → also possible; guards who see you carrying her turn hostile.

## Rewards

- €$7,120 (or the contents of the safe if you take the deal).
