**Ezekiel Saw the Wheel** starts at the **BuryGer** in Arroyo (Santo Domingo), a diner that opens along with Santo Domingo in **Act 2**. It has no prerequisites: talk to the owner and order a drink.

> **Order:** talk to the owner → pick a drink → the robbers walk in → pay, scare them off or fight → talk to the owner.

## The robbery

As you look around, **three gangers** come in to rob the place. You get a timed reply:

- **Pay** → you hand over between €$2,900 and €$8,400 (per the wiki).
- **Body** (between 7 and 10, per the wiki) → you scare them off.
- **Streetkid lifepath** → you warn them they are on someone else's turf and they leave.
- **Do not answer in time** → you have to fight all three.

## Choices

- **Scare them off**, or **beat them without killing them** (non-lethal weapons or quickhacks) → the owner is pleased and the drinks are on the house.
- **Kill them** → the owner throws you out for taking lives to save a few eddies.

According to the wiki (unconfirmed), if you scare them off or beat them non-lethally, from then on you can take anything the diner sells for free, but you can no longer sell to it, because its shop is left with €$0.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
