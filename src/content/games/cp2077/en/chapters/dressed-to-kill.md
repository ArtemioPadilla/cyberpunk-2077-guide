**Dressed to Kill** starts on its own when you reach the **Desert Film Set**, at **Jeff's Dirt Track**, east-northeast of **Rocky Ridge** town in the **Badlands**. Since Patch 2.0 it no longer shows on the map: it starts when you get close to the vehicle. The Badlands open in Act 2.

> **Order:** reach the set → search the area → read the shard → get in the car.

## How to do it

As you arrive, a **Trauma Team** AV flies off and leaves a scene of carnage behind: dead **Wraiths** and models in high-end clothing, next to a very nice **Thorton Colby CX410 "Gran Butte"**.

Search the area: one of the dead carries the shard **New Ad Shoot: Rural Beauty - "Inspire the city, enjoy the country"**. It explains that a clothing executive staged a photo shoot here, with the Colby as the centerpiece. The activity drew the Wraiths; the models had brought their own guns and a firefight broke out. The survivors fled in the AV and left the car. By the time you arrive the fight is over: get in the Colby to finish the job: better in your hands than the Raffen Shiv's.

## Rewards

The **Thorton Colby CX410 "Gran Butte"**. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
