**Serial Suicide** is a gig from **Dino Dinovic**, the **City Center** fixer. It takes place at NCART's **Memorial Park station**, under the park of the same name in **Corpo Plaza**. It belongs to Dino's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **A Lack of Empathy**. The journalist **Zoli Barnes** "fell" onto the tracks at that station and her death was ruled a suicide. Her parents do not buy it: she had gone to meet an informant. Every bit of CCTV footage was wiped and **Militech** took over the station's security. Barnes was investigating **Brad Noorwood**, a council member close to Militech. Dino wants the files so his people can try to recover what was deleted.

> **Order:** sneak into the station → reach the main server room → steal the CCTV footage → leave the station.

## The station

Dino transfers you a **Militech authorization** to get in. Inside, the enemies are Militech guards; the objective asks you to **sneak in**, so move quietly to the main server room. Download the CCTV files there, then leave the station.

## What to read

Around the station there are shards and messages that explain the cover-up, including Militech's orders about the station and a conversation between Case Mod and Dino. According to the wiki, they point to Noorwood himself pushing Barnes.
