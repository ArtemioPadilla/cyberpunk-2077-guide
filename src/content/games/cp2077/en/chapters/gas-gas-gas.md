**Gas Gas Gas** is the gift from **Dino Dinovic**, the City Center fixer, when you finish **all his gigs** in the district. It was added in Patch 1.5. His gigs open after **The Heist**, in **Act 2**.

> **Order:** finish Dino's 5 gigs → read his message → go to the Megabuilding H10 garage → collect the car.

## The gigs that count

- **Set 1:** A Lack of Empathy.
- **Set 2:** An Inconvenient Killer, Serial Suicide.
- **Set 3:** Guinea Pigs, The Frolics of Councilwoman Cole.

Each set opens when you finish the one before it.

## Collecting the car

Dino texts you, thrilled: you did so many gigs that he ran out of them, and he left you a gift in your garage. Go to the **Megabuilding H10 garage** and collect the **Quadra Type-66 "BULITT"**.

## Rewards

The **Quadra Type-66 "BULITT"**: a dark green two-seat sports car, rear-wheel drive, 555 horsepower. It is a version of the Type-66 640 TS that pays homage to the Mustang from the film Bullitt, and it accepts CrystalCoat.
