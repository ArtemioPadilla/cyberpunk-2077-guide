**Judy Álvarez** gives it right after Disasterpiece. Evelyn is recovering at **Judy's apartment** (Kabuki, Watson) and Judy has extracted **two braindances** from her. Analysing them reveals who hired Evelyn for the Konpeki heist and how to reach **Alt Cunningham**. It's a calm job with no combat, but **finishing it closes a side job** in Pacifica.

> **Order:** talk to Judy → (optional) sit with Evelyn → meet Judy in the editing room → BD 1: look for clues → exit the BD → watch the second BD → talk to Judy → switch to the audio layer → scan the netrunner's phone → talk to Judy about the translation chip → listen to the call → exit the BD → talk to Johnny → talk to Judy → leave the apartment.

> **Get ready:** if you want to do **Love Rollercoaster** (the Eden Beach rollercoaster in Coastview, Pacifica, with **Jean Rochelin**), **do it before finishing Double Life**: afterwards the job is no longer available (the ride itself keeps working). If you've already visited the **Pacifica Serenity Bible Church**, V recognises it in the first BD and you skip most of the analysis.

## At the apartment

Talk to Judy. She goes into her workspace to keep tweaking the BDs. Evelyn is on the bed: you can **sit beside her** (optional), but she doesn't respond.

Useful quirk: you can leave the area and roam before entering the apartment, but **the in-game clock freezes at 6:20 PM** until this or another job gets time moving again. You can skip time, but it stays frozen.

## The first BD (visual layer)

Sit with Judy in the editing room and **look for clues in the recording**. Evelyn is talking to a **mysterious netrunner**, the one who hired her to record the BD of Yorinobu's penthouse. Her face is heavily encrypted: neither V nor Judy can identify her (it's **Brigitte**). Scan:

- **The red hologram** on the Holoview: the **Voodoo Boys** symbol.
- **The flyers on the floor** near Evelyn: they point to **Pacifica**.
- **The vévé markings** around the altar: they also point to Pacifica.
- **The unknown netrunner.**

If you'd already visited the Pacifica Serenity Bible Church, V recognises the place and you only need to **scan the netrunner**.

## The second BD (audio layer)

Exit the BD and watch the second one. Evelyn is hiding at the top of a staircase in an alley, while the netrunner talks on the phone at the bottom.

1. **Switch to the audio layer.**
2. **Scan the netrunner's phone.**
3. **Talk to Judy about the translation chip:** she installs one so you can understand the conversation.
4. **Listen to the call.** She's talking to **Placide**. From what they say, you learn that **they need to reach Alt Cunningham in cyberspace through Johnny's engram**.

Exit the BD. Judy asks about the engram; **talk to Johnny** for the details, then **talk to Judy** and **leave the apartment**.

## Choices

- **There are no story-changing choices** in this job.
- **Evelyn's fate is fixed:** whatever you do, she takes her own life in Judy's bathtub. You find out in **Both Sides, Now**. **No choice saves her**, so don't go looking for one.
- **Finishing Double Life** → **Love Rollercoaster** disappears for good. It's this job's only real loss.

## What opens here

- **M'ap Tann Pèlen:** the Voodoo Boys line. Call **Mr. Hands**, Pacifica's fixer.
- **Both Sides, Now** (Judy's chain): opens **only after you call Mr. Hands** in M'ap Tann Pèlen. It continues with Ex-Factor → Talkin' 'bout a Revolution → Pisces → **Pyramid Song**. Romancing Judy needs a **feminine body and feminine voice**; the chain is worth doing even without the romance.
- **Phantom Liberty:** the expansion's tracker asks for the Evelyn line (Automatic Love → Double Life) as well as the Voodoo Boys line through Transmission.
- **Closes:** Love Rollercoaster.

> **Don't leave without:** finishing **Love Rollercoaster** if you care about it, before you wrap up this job, and talking to Johnny when asked: that's what triggers the rest of the line.
