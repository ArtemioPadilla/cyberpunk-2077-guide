**Regina Jones** wants footage of **Maelstrom**'s initiation rite: the surgery that splits recruits' optic nerves. It is recorded at the **HeavenMed** clinic on Daniels St, in the Northside docks (Watson), a former fish cannery (MeatyFish) where new recruits go under the knife. It is in Regina's **set 1 of 8**, the first she offers: her gigs come in sets that unlock one after another, and the next one appears once you finish every gig in the current set. According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the Maelstrom clinic → reach the server room → steal the surgery recording → escape from the clinic.

## The clinic

- It has **two entrances**: the front lobby and the garage.
- **Ground floor:** lobby, a makeshift operating room and offices. **Top floor:** locker rooms and the **server room**, where you download the footage.
- Both floors have **cameras** and Maelstromers, including **Brandon Frost** and **Othmar Brasi**: frag grenades, Charge Jump and Smokey Eye (Othmar also has Combat Stim).
- If you have already done **The Pickup**, the Maelstromers upstairs talk about what happened to Royce.

Take the footage and leave the clinic to close the gig.

## Rewards

- €$3,000, 1,000 XP and 707 Street Cred.
