**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, gives you this gig in **Red Peaks**. Her gigs open in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. It's one of the two in her first set, with **Dancing on a Minefield**. Her friend Mosquito sends chems to the city by drone, and someone is plucking them out of the sky. The last drone carried a GPS tag: the signal comes from **Union Railroad Station**, held by the **Wraiths**.

> **Order:** go to the signal source → find out how the drones are intercepted → destroy the antenna → leave the outpost.

## Finding out how

The station is heavily guarded: Wraiths inside and outside the building, **turrets** at the gateways and **cameras** over most of the grounds. You have two ways:

- **Ground-floor computer:** takes **Intelligence** (the wiki says 7, unconfirmed as the 2.x value). Its messages show that the rooftop antenna broadcasts a virus that kills the drones' propulsion.
- **Go straight to the roof:** examine the antenna and **Johnny** points out the obvious.

## The antenna

- **Shoot it** → alerts any remaining guards and security.
- **Cut its power** with **Technical Ability** (the wiki says 10, unconfirmed for 2.x) → you stay undetected.
- **Overload it with a quickhack** → destroys it, and Dakota has a unique line.

Then leave the area to close the gig.
