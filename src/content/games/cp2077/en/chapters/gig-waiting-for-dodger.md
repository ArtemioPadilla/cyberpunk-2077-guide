Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his first Dogtown set, which opens during **Lucretia My Reflection**. Two NCPD officers, **Bill Mitchel** and **Charles Wilson**, are held in their old precinct in **Coastview** (Pacifica), now the base of BARGHEST's **Carl "Dodger" Robinson**. The client is **Stella**, fiancée of one of them.

> **Order:** talk to Stella → enter Dodger's base → find Bill and Charles → enter and secure the garage → take them to the squad car.

## Getting in

- **Front door** with the key Stella gives you. BARGHEST guards patrol outside and more wait at the front desk.
- **Locked door on the right** of the building, with **15 Body**: it leads to a side room next to another with a single unaware guard.
- **Hatch** on top of that side room, with **10 Technical Ability**; a soldier watches it from outside.

In the storage room before the garage, an airdrop cargo container holds the iconic **Volkodav** machete.

## Choices

- **Walk away peacefully** → only possible if you tell Dodger **the whole truth** and **haven't killed any BARGHEST personnel**; otherwise he turns hostile anyway. Both cops live, but later you meet Bill in Coyoacan (The Glen), blackmailed by Dodger.
- **Fight and kill Dodger** → he drops the iconic **Rosco** revolver. If you save Bill and Charles, Bill later calls you for the job **New Person, Same Old Mistakes**.

## Rewards

- €$8,000
- 1,832 XP
- 167 Street Cred
- Volkodav; Rosco if you kill Dodger
