**Panam Palmer** gives it after Ghost Town. She asks to meet **at midnight** at the garage by the **Sunset Motel** (Red Peaks, Badlands) to explain the plan: use the **Satwave Power Plant** to fire an **EMP pulse** that brings down the **Kang Tao AV** carrying Hellman. It's a short job, mostly on wheels, with the turret on Panam's truck and a quick raid on the plant. **When it ends, Life During Wartime starts on its own**, so save first.

> **Order:** meet Panam at midnight → talk to her → take the passenger seat → talk to Panam → shoot 3 targets with the turret → go to the Satwave Power Plant → take out the power plant security (4) → get to the terminal → overheat the terminals → take the passenger seat → go to the hill with Panam → wait for the AV → fire the detonator.

> **Get ready:** you face **drones** and **Kang Tao robots**; game8 says **Kang Tao robots are weak to electrical damage**, so bring electrical weapons or quickhacks (Short Circuit). **Save before starting**: the job flows straight into **Life During Wartime**, which has important choices.

## The Sunset Motel garage

Go to the garage near the Sunset Motel **at midnight**. Panam is working on her truck (the **Thorton Mackinaw "Warhorse"**). She wraps up quickly and explains the plan: fire an EMP from a power station to knock out the Kang Tao AV and go in for Hellman.

## Calibrating the turret

**Take the passenger seat** and **talk to Panam**. On the way she stops and lets you **calibrate the machine gun mounted on the truck**: jack into it and **shoot the three targets** (some abandoned vehicles). Then she explains how to launch the EMP from the plant.

## The Satwave Power Plant

You wait a few hours for the AV to pass and head to the plant.

1. On arrival you jack into the machine gun again: **security drones** chase you. **Take out the power plant security** (4 targets).
2. Jack out. Panam drives the truck into the entrance of a control station.
3. **Get to the terminal** and **overheat the terminals**.
4. Run back to the truck and **take the passenger seat**.

## The hill and the AV

**Go to the hill with Panam**, overlooking the plant, so the pulse doesn't affect you. Panam hands you the **detonator**. **Wait for the AV**: it shows up coming from Pacifica.

**Fire the detonator.** It doesn't work right away and the EMP goes off too late: not enough to bring the AV down. Panam gets out of the truck and fires a **Techtronika T40 Uragan** missile launcher at it. That doesn't take it down completely either, but the AV **loses altitude**... and **Life During Wartime** begins.

## Choices

- **There are no story choices** in this job.
- **Panam romance (masculine V):** game8 places a romance step around here: when Panam talks about sleeping at the motel, **offer to share a room**; she turns you down for now. It's unclear whether it happens in this job or in Ghost Town.

## What opens here

- **Life During Wartime:** starts on its own as soon as the AV loses altitude.
- It doesn't open or close any side chain. Panam's chain (**Riders on the Storm** → … → **Queen of the Highway**) opens when Life During Wartime ends.

> **Don't leave without:** saving. The next job starts without a break, and in it you decide whether to abandon Panam, which closes **I'll Fly Away**.

## Rewards

- game8 lists no rewards for this job.
