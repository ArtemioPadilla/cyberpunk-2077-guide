**Life's Work** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at the **Wicked Tires Autoshop**, a **6th Street** garage on McBride Street in **the Glen**. It belongs to Padre's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. **Jake Estevez** is a brilliant mechanic with a terrible character. He spent two weeks working for 6th Street on a custom **Thorton Colby CX410 Butte**, his "life's work", with software he designed himself. He angered the wrong people and had to run. Now he wants his truck back.

> **Order:** go to the auto shop → upload the software to start the truck → get to the truck → deliver it to Jake → exit the vehicle → talk to Jake → leave the garage.

## The auto shop

It has a front door with two garage doors and another entrance at the back. Inside there are two car lifts and a staircase up to the office, where the computer is. Upload Padre's software, get in the truck and drive it to Jake's hideout.

## Keep in mind

- The gig says you get more if the truck arrives undamaged, but according to the wiki **the vehicle's condition does not change the pay**.
- If you shoot your way through before **Stadium Love**, a ganger in that job will recognize V.

## Afterwards

About a day later, Jake texts you with a gift. This gig unlocks the minor job **Sex on Wheels**.

## Rewards

- €$8,030, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
