Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is the repeatable job from **Muamar Reyes**, El Capitán, and opens once you finish **Push It to the Limit**. From then on, vehicles marked with a **blue steering-wheel icon** (on screen and on the minimap) appear at random all over **Night City**.

> **Order:** find a blue-wheel car → take it → drive it to the destination → get out → collect the prize from the marked container → leave the garage.

## Delivery types

The car may be parked and guarded, or on the move: then stop it or pull the occupants out before you take it. There are three variants:

1. **With pursuers:** enemy vehicles try to kill or block you. Take them out or leave the yellow area on the minimap without being followed.
2. **No pursuers:** an optional objective to deliver it **in good condition** (no broken or missing parts).
3. **The Blackbird:** the car is a **Mizutani Shion "Blackbird"** with hacking gear. Drive alongside the target, which is on the move, until the system stops it; then steal that car and take it to the destination.

Any of them can add an optional **time limit** (90 seconds to 5.5 minutes).

## Tips

The destination is set when you get in, but the prize is generated when the car appears on the map. If you save before getting in, you can reload to try for a different prize. After **7 deliveries**, El Capitán calls you for **Baby Let Me Take You**. According to the wiki (2.3), a route may fail for no reason while that job is active.

## Rewards

They depend on the optional objectives and the car's condition:

- An **AUTOFIXER'S DISCOUNT EXTRAVAGANZZZA** ticket; they stack (13% with 1, 50% with 6, 100% with 25: a free car).
- One or two weapons and one or two weapon attachments.
- A money shard, a random Skill Shard and crafting components.
- A new car for sale at AUTOFIXER with each delivery; 14 unlock them all.
