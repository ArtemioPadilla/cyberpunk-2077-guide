**Lucretia My Reflection** starts beside the defeated Chimera. Songbird has gone silent and V is alone in Dogtown with President Myers. You need to get her into a hideout, restore the power, decide what to do with two strangers already living there and contact Myers' trusted man in the city: **Solomon Reed**, an FIA agent. By the end, the **Kress Street** hideout becomes one of your apartments and Dogtown opens up: Mr. Hands' gigs, airdrops and the west gate.

> **Order:** talk to Myers (optional Chimera core) → subway tunnel entrance → container → right tunnel (steam) or left (service tunnel) → elevator to the 8th floor → hideout → power (drone battery) → the strangers: kill them or make a deal → Capitán Caliente → phone **0931** → talk to Reed → basketball court → Black Thorton → back to the hideout with Reed → wait two days.

> **Get ready:** in the tunnels **avoid the cameras** or wipe the recordings; otherwise there's an ambush later. Save before talking to Taylor and Jacob. And **don't walk away from the deal with Reed**: that's the only way to fail the job, and with it you lose the rest of Phantom Liberty.

## The Chimera core and the tunnels

1. Talk to Myers. Before you leave, **take the Chimera's core** (optional): it's on the ground by the robot, where Myers stands; **scan** if you can't see it. It's the **Active Chimera Core**, the ingredient for crafting **one** of four iconic weapon mods: **Firecracker**, **Wallpuncher**, **Hackatomy** or **Severance**. Crafting one leaves you an inactive core. You can also display it in your apartment.
2. **Find an entrance to the subway tunnels**, **open the container** and lead Myers through the tunnels.
3. Leave the container through the **roof hatch**, talk to Myers (and to Johnny, optional) and find a way out.

**Pick a tunnel:**

- **Right tunnel:** there's **steam**; you can **shut it off** (optional) to get through. At the end of this route, in a room, there's a **laptop** you can use to **wipe the camera recordings**.
- **Left tunnel:** clear a way to the **service tunnel** and cross it.

**Watch the cameras.** If any camera sees you with Myers and you **don't wipe the recordings** at the laptop on the right route, a BARGHEST group later ambushes you and Reed on the drive back to the hideout, with more soldiers in the building's lobby. And if Taylor and Jacob stayed with you, **you'll find them dead**.

## The Kress Street hideout

1. Take the **elevator to the 8th floor** with Myers and walk with her to the hideout. Open the door and go in.
2. There's no power: **find a way to restore it**. Talk to Johnny, **scan** your surroundings to find a working battery, **remove the drone's battery** and **connect it**.
3. Restore power to the hideout and talk to Myers.

## The strangers: Taylor Gibbs and Jacob Long

Two people are in the hideout: **Taylor Gibbs** and **Jacob Long**. Talk to them and decide:

- **Neutralize them (kill them):** you fight both, talk to Myers and **pick up one of the bodies**; there's a short time skip while they're disposed of. The next morning it's **Johnny** who points out the first airdrop.
- **Make a deal:** they stay. You can talk to each of them (optional) and sit next to Myers. The next morning it's **Jacob** who points out the first airdrop. Careful: if you didn't wipe the camera recordings, you'll later find them dead.

## Capitán Caliente and Solomon Reed

1. Talk to Myers. She sends you to the **Capitán Caliente** restaurant.
2. Find a way in. Optional: **follow the cables to the fusebox** and override it to open the door.
3. Inside, **scan** to find the **old telephone** and **dial 0931**. Other numbers play different sounds and even get comments from Johnny, so experiment if you're curious.
4. **Talk to Reed** and go to the **basketball court**. Sit and wait for the meeting, then talk to the stranger.
5. Go to Reed's **Black Thorton**, get in and talk to him. Ride with Reed to the hideout (if the cameras caught you, this is where the **BARGHEST ambush** hits, on the way and in the lobby).
6. Enter the building with Reed, ride the elevator to the 8th floor, lead him to the hideout and **talk to Myers and Reed**.

**The deal.** Over the course of the job V commits to carrying on with the operation. **If you walk away from the deal, the job fails and Phantom Liberty ends**: Songbird later tells you Myers' and Reed's bodies were delivered to Hansen (BARGHEST found the hideout). When the dialogue offers you a way out, don't take it.

7. **Leave the building** and wait until Myers and Reed leave.
8. **Wait two days** for Reed to call. Meanwhile, do gigs in Dogtown (optional). Then talk to Reed and the job ends.

## What opens when it's done

- **Kress Street Hideout:** the Rindo Hotel hideout (Elizabeth Kress St., 8th floor) becomes a V **apartment**, with a bed, bathroom, wardrobe and **stash**.
- **Hawk:** Myers leaves her iconic assault rifle behind when she departs the hideout. Pick it up: it's missable.
- **Mr. Hands:** Dogtown's fixer registers you for the **west gate** and gifts you the **Makigai Tanishi T400**. His Tier 2 gigs open: Dogtown Saints, Prototype in the Scraper, Treating Symptoms and Waiting for Dodger. You'll need **at least 3 gigs** for Get It Together.
- **Airdrops:** they unlock here. The first is at the **Wild Blue Hotel**, behind the Akebono, and holds a **Carrying Capacity Shard**. An AV roars past and drops an orange cache with red smoke that beeps; hack it with Breach Protocol and fight off whoever contests it. Only one is active at a time and it despawns if you leave. **Unique ("metaquest") airdrops happen only once and their loot is lost if you skip them.**
- **Coded message:** a couple of days later a message arrives from the "American Animal Defenders' Association" with a bonus.

## Choices

- **Taking the Active Chimera Core** → lets you craft an iconic weapon mod. If you don't take it, it's lost.
- **Right or left tunnel** → the right one has the laptop to wipe the cameras; the left one avoids the steam.
- **Wiping the recordings or not** (if you were seen) → if you don't: a BARGHEST ambush on the way to the hideout with Reed and in the lobby, and **Taylor and Jacob dead** if you let them stay.
- **Killing Taylor and Jacob** → a time skip to dispose of the bodies; Johnny points out the airdrop.
- **Making a deal with Taylor and Jacob** → they stay in the hideout; Jacob points out the airdrop.
- **Walking away from the deal with Reed and Myers** → **the job fails and Phantom Liberty is over** (Myers and Reed die). Permanent.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Active Chimera Core**, the **Hawk** rifle Myers leaves behind and the first airdrop at the Wild Blue Hotel. Start Mr. Hands' gigs during the two-day wait, and do **Balls to the Wall** before Firestarter.

## Rewards

- Active Chimera Core and Hawk (missable).
- Kress Street Hideout as an apartment.
- Makigai Tanishi T400 (gift from Mr. Hands).
