Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It starts after **Spider and the Fly**: **Coach Fred** texts you to help a boxer, **Aaron Waines**.

> **Order:** Coach Fred's message → ring by Montezuma's Throne → Digimmortal (Terra Cognita) at 9 PM → Scavenger hideout → Damir's clinic → talk to Angie.

## Aaron and the clinic

Find Aaron at the ring near **Montezuma's Throne**. He asks to meet at **9 PM** at the **Digimmortal** exhibition in Terra Cognita. **If you leave instead of going in, the job fails.** There he tells you the Animals saddled him with a chip that knocks him out by remote to fix his fights, and that he needs ripperdoc **Damir Kovac** to block it. Fight through the Scavengers to the clinic and wait in a chair until the procedure ends. Then **Angie**, an Animals pack leader, shows up.

## Choices

- **Take Angie's deal** → days later she pays you **€$22,000** at the ring (the **Corpo** lifepath squeezes €$4,400 more). Coach Fred laments you couldn't help Aaron.
- **Refuse** → you fight Angie and two Animals; she drops the **Cheetah**, an iconic Unity pistol. Days later Aaron asks to meet at the **Dream Gig** bar: you find him dead in the alley out back.
- **Threaten her** (requires neutralizing Sasquatch in I Walk the Line; not available if you used the Phantom Liberty skip) → Angie flees. Then, if you tell Aaron to throw the fight, he stays in Dogtown coaching kids; if you convince him to fight, he loses and leaves for Costa Rica to try again.

On the way out the Scavengers aren't friendly: stay on the path to the exit or they turn hostile. According to the wiki (Xbox, 2.31, unconfirmed), if they spot you partway through, the objective to defeat them can get stuck.

## Rewards

- €$5,000 in every case
- Depending on the choice: €$22,000 from Angie or the Cheetah
