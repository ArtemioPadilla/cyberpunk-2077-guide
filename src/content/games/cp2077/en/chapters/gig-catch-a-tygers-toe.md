The netrunner **b@d** wants to hit an Arasaka subnet and use **Megabuilding H11** (Watson; the wiki places it in Northside) as a smokescreen: you upload their malware to the building's server, and every trace of the breach will lead back to H11. **Regina Jones**'s netrunner has already hacked the elevator. It is in **set 3 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the three gigs of set 2 (Troublesome Neighbors, Welcome to America, Comrade and Dirty Biz). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** get to the main server → upload b@d's malware to the system → leave the server room.

## The route

1. The main room holds **five enemies**, one of them a netrunner, and **three cameras**.
2. To the right, a stairwell (with one camera) leads down toward the server room.
3. At the bottom, the double door on the right opens with enough **Body**. Inside is a laptop with network access that **shuts down the cameras**.
4. In the server room, climb onto the **rafters**: they lead to a vent that opens with enough **Technical Ability** and drops you into the inner server room.
5. Use the computer in the inner room to upload the malware, then leave the area.
