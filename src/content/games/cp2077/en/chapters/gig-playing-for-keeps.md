**Jacob Lamb**, an old friend of **Regina Jones** and a former film director, gambled away one of his eyes at an illegal **Tyger Claws** casino hidden behind the **Kashuu Hanten** eatery on Clarendon St (Little China, Watson). He wants his implant back. It is in Regina's **set 1 of 8**, the first she offers: her gigs come in sets that unlock one after another, and the next one appears once you finish every gig in the current set. According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to Kashuu Hanten → find Jacob Lamb's implant → steal it or blackmail the bartender → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## Through the back door

1. Take the alley to the right of the eatery; a Racketeer guards the back door.
2. Inside, around the corner to the left, there is a Kunoichi. The implant is in the **locked storage room**.
3. Force the door with a **Body** check, or scan it for the optional objective to find the **bar staff key**.
4. Take the eye, leave through the back and deposit it in the Drop Point.

## Blackmail

Read the file on the **computer**: the Tyger Claws use implants to rig the games. This adds the optional objective to blackmail the bartender (Keisuke Kagawa). Leave through the alley, come in the front door and confront him with the evidence: he hands over the implant, but tells you neither you nor Jacob are welcome anymore.

## Choices

- **Steal the eye** → deposit it and get paid.
- **Blackmail the bartender** → same pay, but he stops serving you and may turn hostile if you come back to the bar.

## Rewards

- €$2,230, 1,000 XP and 484 Street Cred.
