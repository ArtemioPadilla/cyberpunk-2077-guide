**Firestarter** is the heart of Phantom Liberty. V and Alex, disguised as the Cassel twins, arrive at the **EBM Petrochem Stadium** for the meeting with **Kurt Hansen**. First you have to survive his questioning; then Songbird takes you to the **neural matrix** (the cure) and there you make **the choice that splits the expansion in two**: **help Songbird escape** (you betray Reed) or **help Reed capture Songbird** (you betray her). Each side has its own jobs, bosses, loot and two endings of its own; the other half is closed off on that save.

> **Order:** drive to the stadium → vehicle ID check → park → follow Alex → talk to Murphy → elevator → deposit your weapons in the locker → **interview with Hansen** → follow Songbird → connect to the mainframe → **choice** → escape (with Songbird or alone) → retrieve your gear from the locker → market exit → **Songbird:** sewer and camp, drive off with her / **Reed:** ambush and **Hansen boss**, garage, escape with Reed.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save before you start** (or right before the choice) and keep it: according to PowerPyx, from here you can reach **all four** Phantom Liberty endings. Have the **roulette result** from You Know My Name at hand (did the twins win or lose). Do **Balls to the Wall** first: once Firestarter is done it becomes unavailable and, if it's active, it **fails**. You play the twin of your gender: **female V is Aurore**, **male V is Aymeric**.

## Arriving at the stadium

1. **Drive to the stadium**, wait for the **vehicle ID verification**, park and get out.
2. Follow Alex and talk to **Albert Murphy**, a BARGHEST soldier. Murphy escorts you and warns that BARGHEST suspects an FIA operation.
3. Take the elevator with Murphy and **deposit your weapons in the locker**. Talk to Murphy again.

**Remember the locker.** Later, on both paths, there's an objective to **retrieve your gear from the locker**. If you leave the stadium without it, Mr. Hands messages you later: one of Hansen's men sold him your things, and he'll return them for **€$20,000** in the side job **Dirty Second Hands** (he leaves them at the Kress Street hideout). It's cheaper not to forget them.

## The interview with Hansen

Hansen tests V's and Alex's identities. **Two wrong answers is an instant game over.** Some wrong answers can be saved with a good follow-up answer or with **20 Cool**. You can check the **dossier** during the conversation; keep it open.

**In the car, before going in,** Alex runs through the details with you. Answer "A tough bastard. A hard man" or "A hard man. Just how I like them", depending on the twin.

**If you're Aurore (female V):**

- **Take the drink** when it's offered.
- **About gambling at the Black Sapphire:** answer according to what happened on the last roulette bet (whether they lost everything or won).
- **About "Raquel":** say you don't know any Raquel. Aurore was in the prison's **VIP wing** and wouldn't know prisoners from other cell blocks.
- **Fashion contact:** **Kerry Eurodyne**'s tailor; Aurore is a fan of his style.
- **Working with Le Collectif:** impossible (or only for a huge payout for the risk): **France**'s underworld has marked her for death.

**If you're Aymeric (male V):**

- **About gambling at the Black Sapphire:** answer according to the last bet's result.
- **The classic car:** the **Porsche 911**.
- **The drink:** **don't take it**, or give it to Aurore.
- **His contract at Petrochem:** answer that you **don't remember**; his stored memories from that time were stolen.

## The neural matrix: the choice

After the interview Songbird arrives and leads you to the **neural matrix**. **Talk to Songbird** and **connect to the mainframe**. Here you choose:

- **Top option, "Help Songbird escape":** you upload the access codes. **You betray Reed.**
- **Bottom option, "Help Reed capture Songbird":** you arm the **ICEbreaker** Farida implanted. **You betray Songbird.**

**This choice is permanent** and locks half of Phantom Liberty on that save. Read both paths before deciding.

## Path A — Help Songbird escape

**What happens in Firestarter:**

1. With the matrix in hand, you give the signal and **Alex kills Hansen** by surprise. **There's no Hansen fight.** Go back to his body and loot two iconic weapons: the **Bald Eagle** revolver and **Wild Dog**.
2. Follow Songbird's instructions and escape the secured area with her. **Retrieve your gear from the locker.**
3. Songbird hacks the defences for you: at each event you pick **one of two hacks**. Wait for her to open the doors.
4. **Murphy** shows up in the market area: **fight him or sneak past**. If you defeat him, loot his iconic club **Murphy's Law**. (If you don't get it, Herold Lowe sells it at the stadium later, still on this path only.)
5. Reach the market exit, escape the stadium (move the crates to open a path; avoid or destroy a few drones), open the **sewer gate** and jump down to the ground.
6. Talk to Songbird, **lead her out of the homeless camp** and drive off with her. **The Killing Moon** begins.

**What you keep:** **Alex** alive; Bald Eagle, Wild Dog and Murphy's Law; the **NCX spaceport** run with Songbird.

**What you lose:** Reed's whole line: the MaxTac convoy rescue, **Cynosure** and the **Cerberus**, the iconic **Crowbar**, the **Erebus** and **Militech Canto Mk.6** crafting specs, the side job **This Corrosion**, the **MaxTac Mantis Blades**, the **NUSA distinction** medal and Hansen's **Fang** knife.

**Pages you'll visit:**

- **The Killing Moon** (PL 11), the escape with Songbird through NCX. At the end, on the platform, you choose:
  - **Kill Reed** → **Unfinished Sympathy** and **From Her to Eternity** (PL 12): the **King of Wands** ending.
  - **Hand Songbird over to Reed** → **Through Pain to Heaven** (PL 13) and then **Who Wants to Live Forever** (PL 17): the **King of Swords** ending, with The Tower possible.

## Path B — Help Reed capture Songbird

**What happens in Firestarter:**

1. With the matrix in hand, Songbird senses the betrayal and **warns Hansen**. **Hansen kills Alex** on the spot. Your ICEbreaker neutralizes Songbird's attack, and she escapes in the following cutscene.
2. Talk to Johnny and **escape the secured area**. **Retrieve your gear from the locker.**
3. At the market entrance **Hansen and several BARGHEST soldiers** ambush you: **Kurt Hansen boss** (see below). Afterwards, **search Hansen**: **Bald Eagle**, **Fang** and **Wild Dog**, plus a stadium security shard.
4. Take the elevator to the garage, meet Reed, get in the car and talk to him. You watch **MaxTac capture Songbird**. **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** begins.

**If Hansen doesn't die by your hand:** if you **spare** him, Mr. Hands later tells you someone assassinated him in the hospital. If you **slip out of the stadium unnoticed** and skip the fight, Mr. Hands later tells you Hansen "caught the proverbial stray bullet". Either way you miss his loot.

**What you keep:** Bald Eagle, Wild Dog and **Fang**; Reed's whole line (MaxTac convoy, Cynosure, Crowbar, Erebus and Canto specs, This Corrosion, MaxTac Mantis Blades if you send Mr. Hands the coordinates, NUSA distinction if you spare Songbird).

**What you lose:** **Alex** (she dies here), **Murphy's Law** (you never run into Murphy), the **NCX spaceport**, Reed's **Pariah** pistol, the postcard, the **Quantum Tuner** and the **Metal pin**.

**Pages you'll visit:**

- **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** (PL 14), the MaxTac convoy assault.
- **Somewhat Damaged** (PL 15), Cynosure and the Cerberus. At the end, Songbird asks something of you:
  - **Fulfill her request (kill her)** → **Leave in Silence** and **Four Score and Seven** (PL 16): the **King of Cups** ending. No cure and no Tower.
  - **Refuse (spare her)** → **Leave in Silence** and **Four Score and Seven** (PL 16), then **Who Wants to Live Forever** (PL 17): the **King of Pentacles** ending, with The Tower possible.

## The four endings at a glance

- **King of Wands** ("take Songbird to the stars"): Songbird path and you **kill Reed** in The Killing Moon. Hansen, Reed, Murphy (if you kill him), NCX staff and civilians and NUSA black ops die. Loot: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog, Murphy's Law, **Pariah**, the postcard, **Quantum Tuner** and **Metal pin**. **No cure and no Tower.**
- **King of Swords** ("take Songbird home"): Songbird path and you **hand Songbird to Reed**. Hansen, Murphy (if you kill him) and NCX casualties die. Loot: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog, Murphy's Law and the **neural matrix (the cure)**. **The Tower available.**
- **King of Cups** ("fulfill Songbird's request"): Reed path and you **kill Songbird** in Somewhat Damaged. Alex, Hansen, a few NCPD officers and one MaxTac unit, and **Song So Mi** die. Loot: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog, Fang, Crowbar, Erebus and Canto specs, the Cerberus component, MaxTac Mantis Blades (if you qualify). **No cure and no Tower.**
- **King of Pentacles** ("refuse Songbird's request"): Reed path and you **spare Songbird**. Alex, Hansen and the same NCPD and MaxTac die. Loot: the same as Cups, plus the **NUSA distinction** and the **neural matrix**. **The Tower available.**

Each ending gives its **achievement** (King of Wands, Swords, Cups and Pentacles). **The Tower** is an extra ending of the base game: it only opens after **Swords** or **Pentacles**, by accepting Reed's offer (in Who Wants to Live Forever or with the call to Reed from the Nocturne Op55N1 rooftop).

## Boss — Kurt Hansen (Reed path only)

- **First, the two soldiers** who come with him.
- **Phase 1, ranged:** Hansen uses a light machine gun and his revolver. The arena has **little cover**: stack quickhacks or close the distance.
- **Switch to melee:** he draws his knife and charges you with dash-ins and a slam; he throws knives at your legs and grabs you if you get too far away. Sources disagree on when: game8 says at **75%** health (he grabs you as he switches) and the wiki says **50%**. Expect it from 75%.
- **Tactic:** learn the layout and **backpedal while shooting** once he goes into melee.
- **Loot:** Fang, Bald Eagle, Wild Dog and the stadium security shard.

## What happens on both paths

- **Balls to the Wall** becomes unavailable (or fails if active).
- Mr. Hands offers you **Run This Town**. While it's pending, no new Dogtown gigs open; if you refuse it twice, it fails (he doubles the pay once first).
- The side job **Moving Heat** opens.
- When you return to Dogtown some time later, Mr. Hands' Tier 4 gigs open: **Heaviest of Hearts** and **Roads to Redemption**.

## Choices

- **Interview answers** → two mistakes = game over. A wrong answer can be rescued with a good follow-up or 20 Cool.
- **Help Songbird escape** → Alex kills Hansen and lives; Murphy shows up; you go on to **The Killing Moon**. You lose Reed's whole line (Cynosure, Cerberus, Crowbar, Erebus, Canto, This Corrosion, MaxTac Mantis Blades, NUSA distinction, Fang). Possible endings: **King of Wands** or **King of Swords** (Tower). **Permanent.**
- **Help Reed capture Songbird** → Hansen kills Alex; Hansen boss; you go on to **Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos**. You lose Songbird's line (NCX, Pariah, Murphy's Law, Quantum Tuner, Metal pin, postcard). Possible endings: **King of Cups** or **King of Pentacles** (Tower). **Permanent.**
- **Sparing Hansen or slipping away** (Reed path) → Hansen dies later anyway, and you miss his loot.
- **Forgetting your gear in the locker** → **Dirty Second Hands**: you pay Mr. Hands €$20,000 to get it back.

> **Don't leave without:** Hansen's weapons (**Bald Eagle**, **Wild Dog** and, on Reed's path, **Fang**), **Murphy's Law** if you side with Songbird, and **your gear from the locker**. And keep the save from before the choice.

## Rewards

- Bald Eagle and Wild Dog (both paths, missable).
- Fang (Reed path, missable).
- Murphy's Law (Songbird path, missable; Herold Lowe sells it if you miss it).
