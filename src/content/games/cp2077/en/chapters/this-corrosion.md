**This Corrosion** is a **Phantom Liberty** job that only exists on **Reed's path**. To get it you need two things from **Somewhat Damaged**: at least one of the **crafting specs** found in the **Cynosure** facility (the **Erebus** SMG or the **Militech Canto** cyberdeck) and the **behavioral system component** looted from the **Cerberus**. If you didn't pick up the component, this job never appears. About **24 hours after** Somewhat Damaged, an **unknown number** messages you, interested in what you brought out of Cynosure. (The wiki infobox lists **Leave in Silence** as the previous job; its walkthrough counts the 24 hours from Somewhat Damaged.)

> **Order:** read the message → meet the netrunner at Kabuki Market → talk to Yoko → wait a few hours while she decodes the component → collect it → craft one item: Erebus or Militech Canto Mk.6.

## With Yoko at Kabuki Market

The message sends you to **Yoko Tsuru**, the netrunning vendor at **Kabuki Market** in **Kabuki** (Watson). Talk to her and hand over the component: she needs **a few hours** to decrypt its algorithm. Skip time or do something else, come back and pick up the **Cerberus' decoded behavioral system component**. Yoko hands it over with a word of warning.

## What to craft

The decoded component is enough for **one** item only, and crafting it completes the job. **It's permanent:** the other one is gone.

- **Erebus** → iconic Power SMG. Its rounds carry energy from beyond the Blackwall and are very effective against low-Health enemies; it can inflict Blackwall Gateway on weakened non-boss enemies. Good if you play with guns.
- **Militech Canto Mk.6** → iconic cyberdeck with **10 RAM**, four slots and the Cerberus AI, which talks. It comes with the **Blackwall Gateway** quickhack preinstalled, which jumps to up to three nearby enemies. Ideal for an Intelligence netrunner.

If you're unsure, save before opening the crafting menu and see which one suits you.

## Afterwards

Some time after you finish the job, a team of **NetWatch** agents ambushes you at random to confiscate what you crafted. Be ready for a fight outside any mission. If you have the Cerberus chip installed (Canto or Erebus), its AI comments on your choice at the end of the Delamain chain.

## Rewards

- **Erebus** or **Militech Canto Mk.6** (one of the two).
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
