The chain of **Delamain**, the AI that runs the self-driving cab company, has four jobs: **Human Nature**, **Tune Up**, **Epistrophy** and **Don't Lose Your Mind**. It starts as Act 2 opens, during **Playing for Time**, while you recover in your apartment after **The Heist**: a "divergent" Delamain cab rams your **Archer Hella** in the **Megabuilding H10** parking lot. It is a relaxed chain, spread across the whole map, that you can do alongside Act 2.

> **For the ending:** Delamain **opens and closes no ending** and has no romance. The only choice that matters is the **core** in Don't Lose Your Mind (which cab you keep and what Johnny thinks). If you started with the skip to Phantom Liberty, this chain comes already completed with the core **reset**.

> **Order:** **Human Nature:** H10 parking lot → crash → talk to Johnny → Delamain's message → **Tune Up:** Delamain HQ (Vista del Rey) → receptionist → €$4,900 → **Epistrophy:** follow the drone through the factory → scanner → 7 cabs (Wellsprings, North Oak, Coastview, Rancho Coronado, Northside, Badlands, the Glen) → return the scanner → €$4,900 → **Don't Lose Your Mind:** Corpo Plaza traffic jam or a call after 48 h → in through the side door or the roof hatch → terminal (1234) → workshop with drones → control room → core.

> **Get ready:** in Epistrophy you **can damage** the cabs, but **destroying one** fails the job and **locks the rest of the chain**. Use light weapons on the hostile Wellsprings cab and drive the North Oak one slowly. **Hacking the core** in Don't Lose Your Mind needs **Intelligence 20**; without it you can only reset or destroy it. **Don't harm the clients** in the HQ reception: the job fails.

## Human Nature

During **Playing for Time**, the H10 Administration tells you your car, the **Archer Hella EC-D i360**, is back in the parking lot (reading that message is an optional objective).

1. Go to the parking lot and get in the car. A divergent Delamain cab **rams** it and wrecks it.
2. Johnny appears and you argue. If you do this before finishing Playing for Time, Johnny is more hostile.
3. Get out of the car and read **Delamain**'s message: he wants you at his HQ.
4. Wait for the repair. The car is ready **about 6 in-game hours after finishing Playing for Time**; **skipping time does not speed it up**.

## Tune Up

Go to **Delamain HQ** (Pajaro street, **Vista del Rey**, Heywood). The reception is full of clients who also suffered rogue cabs.

1. You cut the queue; a client calls you out. With enough **Body**, an extra reply appears to talk back.
2. Talk to the **receptionist** (a Delamain subroutine). Once she confirms who you are, Delamain himself apologises: he is dealing with an internal crisis.
3. He transfers **€$4,900** in compensation. Epistrophy starts.

## Epistrophy

Delamain asks you to come through to the back. **Don't harm the clients in reception**: the job fails.

1. **Follow the Bombus drone** through the factory. Delamain suspects a virus in his network and fears **NetWatch** will start poking around.
2. In the **control room**, agree to help: he gives you the locations of **seven missing cabs** and **Delamain's Scanner**, which reconnects the cabs when you are close.
3. **Take the scanner** and leave the garage.

**Golden rule:** if you damage a cab, Delamain sends you a warning; **if you destroy one**, he ends the deal, the job fails and **the rest of the chain is locked**. Each recovered cab pays a share (Fandom gives **€$1,640** for several of them).

**The seven cabs** (any order):

- **Wellsprings (Heywood):** this is the cab that wrecked your car ("Beep-beep, motherfucker!"). It turns **hostile**: **damage it until it surrenders** to deactivate it, without destroying it. Use low-damage weapons and stop as soon as it gives up.
- **North Oak (Westbrook):** at the roundabout by the **North Oak Sign**. This cab is overwhelmed by the city: **drive it back to HQ yourself**. **Auto Drive is disabled**; if you go too fast it asks you to slow down, and if you damage it too much **this part and Epistrophy fail**. Drive slowly on wide streets.
- **Coastview (Pacifica):** patrols the road near the **Grand Imperial Mall** and the **Pacifica Serenity Bible Church**. It announces a "surprise": at the underpass near the Dogtown border, **Maelstrom** ambush you. Stay within signal range, beat the gang and talk to the cab; it goes back to HQ on its own.
- **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo):** in the residential area. It wants you to call it "**Clarice**" and asks you to **destroy 8 flamingos** in the neighbourhood. Stay within signal range while you smash them.
- **Northside (Watson):** the location is corrupted (Eisenhower street). It is hiding in a small **storage facility near the Eisenhower / Longshore North intersection**. When it sees you, it flees in fright: **follow it** until it crashes into a shack.
- **The Glen (Heywood):** at the intersection of **Hanford and McBride**. It thinks you have come to finish it off and tries to **drive off the viewpoint**. Convince it you are there to help. Pushing it over the edge with your own car also counts as a success (it says it is still conscious but can't move).
- **Badlands:** at the **Municipal Landfill** in **Red Peaks**. Get in the cab and listen: it only wants to live without restrictions. It opens the door and warns you to avoid it in the future.

With all seven, Delamain calls: go back to HQ, **put the scanner in the box** and talk to him. He pays you another **€$4,900**.

## Don't Lose Your Mind

It starts one of two ways: you run into a **traffic jam** on the **Corpo Plaza** ring road (City Center), with three Delamain cabs blocking a Kaukaz truck (the radio can tip you off too), or Delamain calls you about **48 hours** after Epistrophy. The virus has reached the **core**.

1. At HQ, Delamain can't open the doors for you. Go outside and find another way in: the **side door** (**Technical Ability**) or the **roof hatch**.
2. Inside, search the office for a way to open the door: the **terminal** opens with **Intelligence** or the code **1234**, found on another terminal nearby.
3. Follow the hallway to the **workshop**: **drones** patrol it and **attack each other**. Find the stairs, go down and cross the room to the **control room**.
4. Each divergent cab begs you to free it; the original Delamain asks you to **reset the core**.
5. At the core, Johnny asks you not to reset it. Choose (see "Choices").

## Choices

- **Epistrophy — destroy a cab** → the job fails and **the rest of the chain is locked** (no Don't Lose Your Mind). **Permanent.**
- **Epistrophy — push the Glen cab over the edge** → counts as a success, same as talking it down.
- **Core — reset** → the original Delamain returns, **with no memory** of you (he identifies you from his logs) and gives you a cab from his fleet. Johnny **dislikes it**. It is the automatic outcome if you skipped to Phantom Liberty.
- **Core — hack** (**Intelligence 20**) → the cabs merge into a single entity that **leaves Night City** and leaves you its "one true son", **Junior**. Johnny **approves**.
- **Core — destroy** → the divergent cabs are **freed** and flee; one stays behind in the hangar, **Excelsior**, and offers you its services. Johnny **strongly approves**.

> **Don't leave without:** returning the scanner and collecting Epistrophy's final payment, and getting into the cab you keep after the core (Delamain, Junior or Excelsior). If you have the Cerberus chip installed (Militech Canto or Erebus), its AI comments on your choice.

## Rewards

- **Human Nature:** the repaired **Archer Hella**.
- **Tune Up:** €$4,900.
- **Epistrophy:** a payment per cab (€$1,640 for several) and €$4,900 when you return the scanner.
- **Don't Lose Your Mind:** the **Delamain No. 21** and, from Patch 2.3, an **autonomous Delamain cab** you can call any time.
