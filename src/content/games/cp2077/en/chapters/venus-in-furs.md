**Venus in Furs** is your reunion with **Meredith Stout**, the Militech agent from **The Pickup**. It only exists if, in that mission, you **met her, accepted her deal** (without insulting her) and **resolved the job by fighting Maelstrom**. It comes in **Act 2**, some time after you meet Takemura in **Playing for Time**: Meredith texts you that everything worked out after the raid on the All Foods plant.

> **Order:** read Meredith's message → reply flirtatiously → go to the first floor of the No-Tell Motel → talk to Meredith → spend the night → pick up the gift.

## At the No-Tell Motel

Reply to Meredith with a flirt and she invites you to the **No-Tell Motel** on Titan Street in **Kabuki** (Watson). Go up to the **first floor** and into her room: she's dressed for the occasion. Ask what happens next and an intimate scene plays. It's a one-time encounter, not a romance.

Afterwards Meredith says goodbye, leaves and sends you a text. **Before you go, check the bed**: she leaves your reward there.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Sir John Phallustiff**, an iconic one-handed club. With the nudity censorship setting on, an **Electric Baton Alpha** appears instead. If you miss it, with Phantom Liberty Herold Lowe sells it at the Dogtown stadium.

## Rewards

- **Sir John Phallustiff** (iconic blunt weapon).
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
