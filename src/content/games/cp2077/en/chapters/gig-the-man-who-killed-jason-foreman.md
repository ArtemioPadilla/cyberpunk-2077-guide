Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his second set, which opens during **Get It Together**. **Leon Rinder**, an ex-BARGHEST soldier, killed eight civilians in **Longshore Stacks**, among them Jason Foreman. The client is **Briana Dolson**, the Stacks' unofficial representative.

> **Order:** vigil with Briana (10 PM-6 AM) → crack the shard → (optional) investigate the area → Los Osos motel → third floor → fight Yasha → confront Rinder → dog tag → deliver it to Briana.

## The investigation

Go to the night vigil and follow Briana. She gives you an **encrypted shard**: crack it. The optional objective to **investigate the area** asks you to scan the bloodied bullet holes, the large footprints in the mud, a box of spent cartridges and a fallen failsafe on top of a container. Do it: it opens a key dialogue option later.

The shard reveals that Jason was a go-between for a supplier of Militech chrome and the Scavengers, and leads you to the old **Los Osos** motel, in Pacifica, now a Scavenger hideout. On the third floor **Yasha Ivanov**, a huge Scavenger, ambushes you. Afterwards, Rinder is bedridden, so weak he knocks his own pistol out of reach.

## Choices

- **Kill him** or **spare him** → take his dog tag and bring it to Briana.
- **Spare him and take his deal** → he gives you his stash's coordinates (opens **Addicted To Chaos**). You can kill him right after to take the tag.
- **Force him to call Regina** → with the area investigated, pick "'Whatever I did'? Let me enlighten you." and then "Put gun to Rinder's head". Regina later confirms he had cyberpsychosis. Later Rinder wants to compensate Briana: if you encourage him, she berates you and bans you from her part of the Stacks.
