**Coin Operated Boy** closes the **Brendan** chain. It is given by **Theo Price**, whom you find at Brendan's usual spot outside **Megabuilding H8** (Japantown, Westbrook) after **Spray Paint**. It is an Act 2 job and also takes you to Heywood.

> **Order:** talk to Theo → go to the SoftSys service point → talk to the clerk (optional) → find Brendan → talk to him → return to Theo.

## Theo

Theo is upset: Brendan's owners took him away to "re-evaluate" his code, even though she insisted he was a real friend and not a simple S.C.S.M.

## SoftSys

The SoftSys shop is in Heywood (in The Glen, according to the wiki's minor jobs list). To get into the room where Brendan's firmware is being updated, **bribe** the clerk or **convince** him with a **Technical Ability** check.

## Brendan

Brendan tells you he is being updated because he mentioned someone's marital troubles, and that person did not like it; the computer next to him confirms it. With enough **Technical Ability**, V works out that Brendan is not a true AI but a very good conversation-emulating algorithm. He asks you to pass a message to Theo and, if you want, tells you one last joke.

## Wrap-up

Return to Theo and give her the message to finish. There is no way to save Brendan: his program is overwritten no matter what. According to the wiki, a niche for him later appears in the Columbarium ("The nicest guy in Night City"), although a bug can stop it from appearing.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
