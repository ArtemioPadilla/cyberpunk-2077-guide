Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It opens during **I've Seen That Face Before**: hear the **whole** conversation between **Aurore Cassel** and **Aymeric Cassel**. Then, when it's time to hack Aurore for her codes, an option to download a file called **Blind_n_Dead** appears, which gives you the code **941229**. If you don't hear the talk out or don't download the file, the job doesn't appear.

> **Order:** hear the Cassels out → download Blind_n_Dead → go to The Needle → enter 941229 → take the stash.

## Slider's stash

Go to the marked area by **The Needle**, in Dogtown (George Washington street). Look for a **teal and grey machine with a keypad**. Enter **941229** and a small door opens with a duffel bag: inside are the **Rook** pistol, a text log and **€$8,000**. It's **Slider**'s hidden cash, and, as Johnny says, he won't miss it.

If you already know the code, you can open the stash earlier and skip the job entirely.

> **Don't leave without:** the text log in the bag, next to the gun and the money.

## Rewards

- Rook (iconic M-10AF Lexington pistol)
- €$8,000
- The wiki gives no XP or Street Cred for this job.
