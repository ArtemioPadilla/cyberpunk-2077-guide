**Rogue Amendiares** offers this gig in **Wellsprings (Heywood)** after **Going Up or Down?**, the last gig from Padre (Sebastian Ibarra). It is **Act 2** content: you won't see it while you're in Watson. Rogue wants **Rebeca Price** gone. Rebeca sells jailbroken, untraceable **Militech** weapons from the **Data Inc.** store, and Rogue wants it stopped before Militech uses it as an excuse to put the army on the streets. The "how" is your call.

> **Order:** enter the store → reach the back of the store → get into the basement → find Rebeca → neutralize her → (if knocked out) carry her to the fixer's car.

## Getting in

- **Through the storefront:** walk in as a customer; you can even shop (it's an overpriced junk vendor) without raising suspicion. If you try to get behind the counter or into the staff area, the **Animals** posing as businessmen attack.
- **Around the back:** industrial shutters in the alley to the south, or circle west: the rear door takes a **Technical Ability** check and the garage door a **Body** check. If you open the garage, Rebeca starts tracing you as a netrunner, and the Animal in the back room may spot you at once.

Rebeca and a last guard are in the basement, in a netrunning den. She turns hostile as soon as she sees you. The quickest way to the fixer's car is the one-way door at the south end of that room.

## Choices

- **Kill Rebeca** → Rogue shows no remorse.
- **Knock her out and deliver her** → Rogue is a little more congratulatory. Either way she never mentions Rebeca's reasons.

> **Don't leave without:** the data shard Rebeca carries: it shows she works under duress, not by choice as Rogue assumed. Check her computer and the Data Inc. one too.
