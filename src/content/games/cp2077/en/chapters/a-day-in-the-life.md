**A Day In The Life** is given by **Darrell Zhou**, who runs a drink stand at **The Manufactory** on Brandon Street in Arroyo (Santo Domingo). It appears after **Playing for Time**, in Act 2.

> **Order:** sit at the stand and talk to Darrell → approach the kids with him → talk to them → defeat them if they attack → talk to Darrell.

## The problem

Two kids from the block, **Brad Stacy** and **Vance Noren**, are messing with Darrell's bike, and he asks for your help.

## Choices

- **Help Darrell** → you approach them together. You learn that Darrell's sister, Shenna, used to run with them until he convinced her to work for **Zetatech**, where she earns more. Brad and Vance see it as a betrayal and want to steal his bike as revenge.
  - **Interfere** (intimidate them): if you have done **several gigs for Muamar Reyes**, Vance recognizes you and both run off; if not, they attack you and you have to defeat them.
  - **Do not interfere** → they kill Darrell and the job **fails**.
- **Refuse to help** → Darrell goes alone, they kill him and the job **fails**.

## Rewards

2,157 XP and 3,235 Street Cred. The wiki lists no eddies.
