**I Really Want to Stay at Your House** is a **repeatable** minor job added in **Update 2.1**. Some time after you establish a romance (with **Judy**, **Panam**, **River** or **Kerry**), your partner texts you to meet up. You agree to meet at one of your **apartments**, except the **Kress Street Hideout** in Dogtown, for safety reasons. Romances wrap up in Act 2, so this comes late.

> **Order:** accept the date → go to the apartment (or sit and wait there) → activities → the date ends when either of you leaves.

## What to do on the date

- **Talk:** a set of short conversations.
- **Cuddle:** on the sofas (and, in some apartments, by a window or other spots); from there you can kiss your partner and open more dialogue.
- **Shower:** your partner joins you.
- **Bathroom mirror:** sometimes they join you there for a moment.
- **Dance:** in apartments with a dance spot, they dance with you.
- **Sleep:** they join you in bed. They may leave before you wake up.

## Notes

- If you are already at the apartment when you accept, you can sit and wait; if not, your partner is waiting there when you arrive.
- After the first date, your partner asks to meet regularly, and you can ask them out too.
- It can be repeated after waiting **48 hours**.
