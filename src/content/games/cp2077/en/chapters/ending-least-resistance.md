The **suicide ending**, which the wiki and Game8 call **Path of Least Resistance** ("the easy way out"). It is not a job: it is a dialogue option on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop, **always available**, that ends the playthrough within minutes. V refuses to ask anyone for help so as not to drag more people to their deaths, throws away the pills and shoots themselves. It is here so you know which option to avoid if you don't want it, and what to expect if you choose it on purpose.

> **Order:** rooftop → talk to Johnny → "Could also just put all this to rest" → "[Toss pills] I know exactly what we're gonna do" → last talk with Johnny → gunshot → credits with grief messages.

> **Get ready:** **save before sitting next to Misty.** This ending gives nothing: unlike the others, it adds no rewards to your pre-Nocturne save. If you play it just to see it, do it from that rooftop save and then pick another route.

## How it triggers

1. On the rooftop, after sitting by Misty and talking to Johnny, pick the **first option** the game offers to decide what to do.
2. When Johnny asks if you're sure, pick **"Could also just put all this to rest"**.
3. Confirm with **"[Toss pills] I know exactly what we're gonna do"**. V throws the pills away: **from here there is no going back** in this playthrough.

## The scene

V and Johnny have one last talk. V explains that the only way to avoid more deaths among the people they love is to end it now, before the chip drives them both mad. Johnny says it's not what he would do, but he understands. The view shifts to Night City and a gunshot is heard. **V and Johnny die.**

## Choices

- **Tossing the pills** → the suicide ending. **Final**: no more jobs.
- **Any other rooftop option** → the other endings. See [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) for the full list.

## Credits calls

The credits show the grief messages from the people close to you (the same as if you die in [(Don't Fear) The Reaper](../ending-the-reaper/)):

- **Viktor**, urged by Misty to leave a message, is crushed: "I'll miss you, kid".
- **Misty** cries: she doesn't think you knew how many friends you really had.
- **Mama Welles** (with Heroes) prays for your soul.
- **Judy** (with her chain) cries in bed: "I can't do this".
- **Panam** (with her chain), devastated and furious: if there's a hell, she hopes you're rotting in it.
- **Kerry** (with his chain), furious: you don't give a damn about your friends.
- **River** (with his chain), grieving: his first partner also took his own life.

## Rewards and where you return

- **No rewards.** The wiki's intro says every ending gives the Legendary Gorilla Arms, but its own table gives this ending "No rewards": trust the table.
- **No achievement.** None of the ending achievements unlocks with this one. A reader comment on PowerPyx says it doesn't count for The World (main-story completion) either; that is unconfirmed.
- **Afterwards:** load your rooftop save (before sitting next to Misty) or the one before Embers, and pick another route.

> **Don't leave without:** a **save on the rooftop** before choosing this option. Without it you'll have to replay Nocturne Op55N1 from Embers.
