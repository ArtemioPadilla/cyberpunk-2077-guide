Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. After **Firestarter**, **Mr. Hands** calls you to his suite at **Heavy Hearts**. While it's pending you can finish gigs already started, but **any new Dogtown gig is locked**.

> **Order:** Mr. Hands's suite → Jago's meeting with the Voodoo Boys → (optional) Bennett's car → Hansen's wake at the Black Sapphire → call Mr. Hands.

## The job

If you say you don't want to play politics, Mr. Hands locks the door and doubles your rate; if you refuse again, **the job fails**. The candidates are **Chester Bennett**, Hansen's former aide and his favorite, and **Jago Szabó**, the accountant. You go disguised as **Aguilar Nubiola**, a Cuban cartel merc: take the shard, the behavioral imprint, the **Mancinella** (a replica of Aguilar's revolver) and the suit.

## Jago and Bennett

Activate the imprint and get the Voodoo Boys out of the way (by fighting or not). Let Jago explain: Bennett is negotiating with an **Arasaka** front, and the optional meeting opens. If you go, take out and hide the driver and wait for Bennett in his car. **Threatening** him starts a fight with only the Mancinella (no scanner, other weapons, implants or grenades); **convincing** him to cut ties with Arasaka ends peacefully.

## Choices (the wake)

The wiki describes these branches confusingly; in short:

- **Bennett not provoked:** Bennett takes charge. Jago dies, unless you convince Bennett not to take his contacts (you draw your weapon), and then he lives. With **Reflexes 10** and Jago's contacts you can stop Bennett: Jago leads and Bennett dies.
- **Bennett provoked:** Jago leads if you convince Bennett to give up on **Terra Cognita** or Jago to hand it to him; otherwise Bennett wins and Jago dies.

Mr. Hands is pleased if Bennett wins with no deaths; otherwise he calls you unprofessional. If Jago wins without giving up Terra Cognita and nobody dies, Mr. Hands texts days later: Jago turned out to be a better partner.

## Rewards

- €$8,000
- Mancinella (iconic revolver)
