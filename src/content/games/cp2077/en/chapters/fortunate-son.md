**Fortunate Son** starts at the **Aldecaldos** camp (Badlands) after **Queen of the Highway**, in **Act 2**. Find **Bob Sagan** checking on a wounded nomad: **Jake Scooter** took a Raffen Shiv bullet in the attack on the camp and, because of his sickle cell anemia, needs a **special kidney implant**. Dakota Smith set up the pickup at the **Night City Medical Center** in **Little China** (Watson). **Don't postpone it too long**: if you wait too much, Jake dies and the job fails.

> **Order:** go to the hospital → meet the employee → take the briefcase with the implant → defeat the police → confront the employee → return to the camp → talk to Bob → wait eight hours → read his message → talk to Bob.

## The ambush

Go to the hospital's **back cargo area**. An employee is waiting with the implant; as soon as you take the briefcase, he runs and the **NCPD** attacks. **You have to defeat them all**: running away won't advance the job. Then confront the employee: the police learned of the deal, thought Dakota was helping the Raffen Shiv and came to take you out.

## Choices

- **Pass the Intelligence check** → you ask whether anyone else touched the implant: he admits the police put a **virus on the firmware** and gives you the original to swap in. Then you can let him go or kill him.
- **No check, let him go** → as he leaves he calls out to you, explains the malware and gives you a shard to remove it. **Don't kill him** if you failed the check.
- **Hand over the implant knowing about the virus** (V tells Bob) → Jake accepts the organ and recovers. If you later side with the Aldecaldos in **Nocturne Op55N1**, you can find him playing guitar in **We Gotta Live Together**.
- **Hand it over without knowing** → Jake rejects the implant and dies. Bob doesn't blame you.
