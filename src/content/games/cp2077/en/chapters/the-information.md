**The Information** is the visit to the heist's client, **Evelyn Parker**. Dex sends you to **Lizzie's Bar**, on the corner of Kennedy and Sutter (Little China, Watson), the **Mox** club. There Evelyn introduces you to **Judy Álvarez**, a braindance tech who teaches you to analyze memory recordings. The key piece is a braindance of **Yorinobu Arasaka** in his penthouse at **Konpeki Plaza**: the heist's target. **There is no combat**. IGN recommends **level 3**. You can do it before or after **The Pickup**; both are needed for **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to Lizzie's and talk to the bouncers → sit at the bar and ask about Evelyn → talk to her → follow her to the VIP room and talk → go downstairs with her and meet Judy → calibration braindance → talk to Judy and Evelyn and call T-Bug → Konpeki braindance → talk to Judy and Evelyn → leave the workshop and call Dex.

> **Get ready:** **Lizzie's is only open between 6 PM and 6 AM.** If you arrive during the day, use **Skip Time**. **Accessibility warning:** when the braindance headset comes on there is a light pattern that can trigger epileptic seizures; look away at that moment. Save before the Konpeki braindance if you want to complete all its optional objectives (see below).

## Lizzie's Bar and Evelyn

1. **Go to Lizzie's** and **talk to the bouncers**. A Corpo V can tell the Mox they've been to Lizzie's before.
2. **Sit at the bar** and **ask about Evelyn.** **Talk to her.**
3. **Follow her to the VIP room**, **sit** and **talk.** Evelyn makes you an offer (see **Choices**). A Corpo V can say they know the Konpeki franchise and have heard things about Yorinobu.
4. **Follow her downstairs**, **talk to Judy**, **sit**, talk and **look at Judy** to begin.

**Judy.** High **Technical Ability** unlocks a short chat about Judy's tech setup. You can also thank her or express interest in seeing her again.

## Calibration braindance

Judy plays you a practice recording: a Scavenger robbing a Cali Express. It teaches you the **braindance editing** controls (pause, rewind, fast-forward, reset) and the **layers** (visual, audio and thermal).

1. **Unpause**, **rewind**, **fast-forward** and **reset** when asked.
2. **Play until you spot the gun** (at **00:22**, per IGN) and **scan it**.
3. **Switch to the audio layer** and **scan the sound source**.
4. **Go back to the visual layer**: **fast-forward to the customer being hit and scan her**, then to **the robber being shot** (**00:51**).
5. **Rewind to the CCTV screen** (**00:48**) and **scan it**.
6. **Exit**, **talk to Judy and Evelyn** and **call T-Bug**.

## Konpeki braindance

Evelyn's recording in Yorinobu's penthouse. Objectives:

1. **Optional: watch the whole recording** from Evelyn's point of view.
2. **Optional: scan the security systems.** Per IGN: at **00:13**, Smasher, an alarm and a motion-sensor camera; at **00:16**, an alarm on Evelyn's right; another motion sensor by the central screen; at **02:09**, a ceiling turret and another camera.
3. **Find the Relic** in **Analysis Mode**.
4. **Audio layer:** **scan Yorinobu's phone** (**00:32**).
5. **Visual layer:** **scan his datapad**. At **02:44** there is the **gun**: it's the **Kongou** T-Bug tells you to remember. **Scan it.**
6. **Thermal layer:** **find the Relic**: the **hidden safe on the right** (**00:47**).
7. **Exit**, **talk to Judy and Evelyn**, **talk to Evelyn**, **leave the workshop** and **call Dex.**

**The two optionals clash:** switching layers to listen in breaks the "watch the whole recording" objective. If you want both, watch it all the way through first without touching anything, or reload the autosave (IGN).

**The Kongou.** game8 claims you only get the Kongou in The Heist if you scanned it here; no other source says so. Scan it anyway: it costs nothing.

## Choices

- **Evelyn's offer to cut Dex out:** "Think about it" or "Keep Dex in the deal" → **same outcome** in this job.
- **What you tell Dex later** → only changes your **cut** in **The Heist**: telling him Evelyn wanted to cut him out raises your share to **40%**. That cut never actually gets paid.
- **Judy** (thanking her, expressing interest, the tech chat) → no documented consequences in this job.
- PowerPyx: no important choices.

> **Don't leave without:** scanning the **Kongou** in the visual layer (02:44) and the **safe** in the thermal layer, and completing the braindance optionals if you want it all.

## Rewards

- **Braindance Wreath**, **1,532 XP** and **130 Street Cred**, per the wiki.
- Calling Dex moves Act 1 along: with The Pickup and The Information both done, **The Heist** starts at the **Afterlife**.
