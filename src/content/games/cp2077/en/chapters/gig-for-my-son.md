**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, sends you to the boxing club in the **old paint factory** (Tripple Extreme Gym) on Baptiste St., in **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his last set, with **Desperate Measures** and **Going-away Party**: it opens after **Cuckoo's Nest** and **Severance Package**, in **Act 2**. **Logan Garcia**, of the **Animals**, beat his sparring partner Lenny Steiner to death and had the body dumped in the bay. Lenny's mother, **Monica Steiner**, is paying for justice.

> **Order:** get inside the boxing club → find Logan → neutralize him → leave the club (if you take the body: carry it out, to the fixer's transport, and put it in the trunk).

## How to do it

Getting too close to **Logan's office** turns the whole club hostile, even if nobody has seen you, including the Animals' **melee weapon vendor**. If that vendor dies during the gig, **he's gone for good**. Entering the office always counts as a hostile act.

If **Beat on the Brat: Rancho Coronado** is active, you can still fight **Rhino** as long as you get in without attacking anyone. Do that fight before you go after Logan, or stay away for 48 hours so the club turns neutral again.

## Choices

- **Kill Logan** → leave the club and you're done.
- **Knock him out and take him** → carry him to the fixer's transport and put him in the trunk.

## Rewards

€$14,030 and 760 XP. It's one of the three requirements for **Ride Captain Ride** (the iconic **Bloody Maria** shotgun) and, with **Going-away Party**, it opens Rogue's **The Union Strikes Back**.
