**Greed Never Pays** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer, in **Japantown**. It belongs to Wakako's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Tyger and Vulture** and **We Have Your Wife**. **Leah Gladen**, a fence on Wakako's turf, got hold of a **CorpCracker**, a device that jailbreaks corporate cyberware. She was haggling over the price with Wakako when she went silent. Your job is to find out what happened to her and, above all, bring back the device.

> **Order:** go to Leah's building → enter her apartment → search it → search the hidden room → go to the Wired Head braindance club → search it → take the CorpCracker → get out of Wired Head → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## The apartment

The building is on the corner of Holly and Floyd; the door code is **2137**. In the bedroom there is a **hidden room** behind a vending machine: it opens with a button in the kitchen. The computer inside shows that Leah was negotiating the CorpCracker with Wakako and with the **Tyger Claws** at the same time. That sends you to Wired Head.

## Wired Head

It is a Tyger Claws braindance club on the corner of Crescent and Broad in Japantown, which the gang uses as a hideout. Leah took the Tyger Claws' offer and they double-crossed her: her body is in the club's restrooms. The **CorpCracker** is on the desk in the server room. Take it, leave the club and bring it to the Drop Point.

## Known bugs

- According to the wiki, if you open the secret doors before taking the gig, Johnny's and V's lines play all at once when it starts.
- In some cases Leah's body does not appear (unconfirmed).
