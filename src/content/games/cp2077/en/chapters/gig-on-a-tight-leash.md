**On a Tight Leash** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at an abandoned **bus depot** on Benko Street in **Wellsprings**, which the **Valentinos** use as a base. It belongs to Padre's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. **José Luis**, a Valentino, shot a police officer dead and the investigation was dropped. One officer wants justice, and Padre pays extra if you find out who is protecting José.

> **Order:** go to the depot → find José Luis → (optional) find out why the NCPD dropped the investigation → neutralize him → leave (or, if you knocked him out, carry his body out) → call Padre and talk to him → (if knocked out) carry him to the fixer's transport → collect your reward.

## The depot

There are plenty of Valentinos, including a **sniper on the roof** of the main building. You reach the second floor by a ladder on the side or the stairs inside. José is on the second floor; you can shoot him through the window, but that makes the optional objective harder. Careful: José uses **Sandevistan** and **Kerenzikov**. Inside there are **Militech** vehicles, uniforms and ammo, and Johnny comments on them.

## The optional objective

If you get close undetected, you overhear José talking to an **Arasaka** agent: the plan is for his people to attack an Arasaka facility dressed as Militech. That is **not enough**: examine the **computer** near José and, when Padre calls, **tell him the truth**.

## Kill or knock out

If you knock him out, you have to carry him out of the depot and put him in the trunk of a car by the street. The pay is no higher for it.

## Rewards

- €$8,570, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
