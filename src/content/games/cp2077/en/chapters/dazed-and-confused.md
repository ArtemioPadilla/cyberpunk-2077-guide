Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It requires **Get It Together**; once **You Know My Name** starts, Mr. Hands texts you: **Lina Malina**, a braindance star, claims she is being held at the **Brainporium**, on top of Jason Exiled in Dogtown. There **Shank** pitches you the real job.

> **Order:** Brainporium → talk to Shank → Lina's place → deal with the Scavengers → wait for Lina → convince her → the next day, direct the shoot.

## The Brainporium

Locked inside is **Edgar Tool**, who after a power cut while watching one of Lina's braindances believes he is her. If you talk Shank into letting him go or use the code **1111** on the door, the job ends there, with nothing but a kiss. To go on, talk to Shank and go upstairs with him. Optional: text Viktor or Judy (they suggest an emotional jolt) and scan **Tool's letter** in the basement.

## Lina's place

Scavengers wait outside. Kill them (and hide the bodies), talk them away with the **Streetkid** lifepath or, with **20 Technical Ability**, break into the apartment and read her schedule so they leave without bloodshed. If you killed them without hiding them and Lina asks, admitting it makes her flee: the job fails.

## Choices

- **Tell her everything** → she leaves; the job fails.
- **Convince her** with the photo of Kerry and Us Cracks (I Don't Wanna Hear It), with The Passion (They Won't Go When I Go), with the **Corpo** lifepath, by adding the shoot to her schedule (Intelligence), or by paying her **€$10,000**.
- At the shoot: **"Tell him that he's your sister"** → a hit; Lina leaves you the **Baby Boomer** bat and later the **Malina-mobile**. **"He's an FIA clone"** → mediocre; Baby Boomer and her T-shirt. **Read Tool's letter** → Tool recovers, no gifts.

The Brainporium closes for good once it's done.

## Rewards

- €$8,000 (Shank's pay)
- Depending on the scene: Baby Boomer, Malina-mobile or Lina Malina's T-shirt
