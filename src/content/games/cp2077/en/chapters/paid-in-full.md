**Paid in Full** only appears if, in **The Ripperdoc**, you didn't have the money or chose to pay **Viktor Vektor** later. You owe him **€$21,000** for the cyberware he installed: Ballistic Coprocessor, Basic Kiroshi Optics and Subdermal Armor. His clinic is behind Misty's Esoterica, on Urmland, Little China (Watson). **There is no deadline**, but **Viktor's services stay locked** until you pay: you can't buy cyberware from him or use his clinic.

> **Order:** raise €$21,000 → go to Viktor → pay off your debt.

## How to pay

1. Raise the money: Regina's gigs, the Cyberpsycho Sightings in **Psycho Killer** and selling junk at the H10 Drop Point are the fastest options in Watson.
2. Go to the clinic and talk to Viktor. He tells you to keep your eddies, but V insists and transfers the debt.
3. That gives you his services back.

## In the meantime

Viktor is the ripperdoc closest to your apartment, but not the only one in Watson: Charles Bucks, Dr. Chrome and Instant Implants are in Kabuki, and Cassius Ryder is in Northside. If you need cyberware before you have the money, buy it there.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no reward: what you get is access to Viktor again.
