Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It follows the gig **Roads to Redemption**, which only opens some time after **Firestarter**. It appears if in that gig you **saved Nele Springer** from Biotechnica and parted on good terms. A few days later a message from Nele's number asks you to meet at a spot in **Dogtown**.

> **Order:** Nele's message → meeting point → open the container → defeat the mercs.

## The meeting

The marker leads to a **closed shipping container**. When you arrive, another message comes in: Nele says she's inside. Before opening it, top up your health and have a weapon ready: **opening the container sets off a flashbang** and a small group of **mercs on Biotechnica's payroll** attacks while you're blinded. Back into cover while the effect wears off and take them all out to complete the job.

Johnny warns you in the journal: nobody comes back to Dogtown just to see you.

The wiki links two archived conversations to this job: Linda Sherman and Miriam Levy, and Miriam Levy and Gabe Fuller.

## Rewards

- The wiki gives no eddies, XP, Street Cred or items for this job; you keep the mercs' loot.
