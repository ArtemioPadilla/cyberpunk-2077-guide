Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. Nobody gives it to you: it starts on its own as soon as you discover **any of the four King tarot murals** in **Dogtown**. It is Phantom Liberty's version of Fool on the Hill: you scan graffiti and then **Misty Olszewski**, at Misty's Esoterica (Watson), reads them for you.

> **Order:** find one mural → find the other three (in any order) → talk to Misty → final reading.

## Where the murals are

- **King of Cups:** by Dogtown's main entrance, in the parking lot of northern Coastview, where you meet Songbird in Dog Eat Dog.
- **King of Pentacles:** on a wall on the upper floors of the **Kress Street Hideout** building, in Longshore Stacks, on the same level as the hideout from Lucretia My Reflection.
- **King of Wands:** on the basketball court at **Tranquil Terrace**, in Luxor Heights, where you meet Solomon Reed.
- **King of Swords:** on an outside wall on the northeast side of **The Moth**, in Longshore Stacks, next to the alternative entrance, where you meet Alex in The Damned.

Several are in places the story takes you anyway; check the walls as you pass. While you search you can tell Misty about the ones you've found. With all four, go back to her for the full reading.

## Rewards

- Misty's tarot reading.
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP, Street Cred or items for this job.
