**Murk Man Returns Again Once More Forever** is in the **Corp-Bud Mine**, in **Rocky Ridge Plains**, **Badlands**: the same tunnels that held **Nash**'s **Raffen Shiv** hideout in **Ghost Town**. It opens after that job, in Act 2. The wiki also asks for **Street Cred 40**; it is not confirmed that this requirement still holds in 2.x. Since Patch 2.0 it no longer shows on the map: it starts on its own when you get close to the vehicle.

> **Order:** enter the mine → search the container → read the shard → get in the car.

## How to do it

In the dark of the mine, inside an old shipping container, stands a jet-black **Rayfield Caliburn** surrounded by crates and furniture, as if someone had set up a base here. Search the nearby container: the shard **"Read this every day. Never give up. Remember your purpose."** tells the story of a young man orphaned when his parents were murdered, who decided to become a vigilante of the night: **Murk Man** (a Batman parody). He seems to have abandoned the hideout, or been killed by the criminals he meant to fight. Johnny mocks his manifestos but approves of his taste in cars.

Get in the car to finish.

## Rewards

The **Rayfield Caliburn "Murkmobile"** (renamed in Patch 2.1). The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
