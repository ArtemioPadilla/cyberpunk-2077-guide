**Ride Captain Ride** is the final reward from fixer **Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes**. When you finish **all** his gigs in **Santo Domingo**, he texts to thank you and leaves a gift in the **stash** at your apartment. Santo Domingo opens in Act 2, so this comes late in a playthrough. It was added in Patch 1.5.

> **Order:** finish El Capitán's 11 gigs → read his message → open the stash at your apartment.

## The gigs that count

- **Tier 1:** Error 404, Race to the Top, Serious Side Effects.
- **Tier 2:** Breaking News, Family Matters, Hacking the Hacker.
- **Tier 3:** Cuckoo's Nest, Severance Package.
- **Tier 4:** Desperate Measures, For My Son, Going-away Party.

## Rewards

**Bloody Maria**, the iconic variant of the **M2038 Tactician** shotgun (Constitutional Arms, Power tech). Its effect: much higher dismemberment chance (+600%), bleed chance (+20%) and knockdown chance (+10%); each time you dismember an enemy you gain +10% Crit Chance and reload 6% faster for 10 seconds, stacking up to five times.
