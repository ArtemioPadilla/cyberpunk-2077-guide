**Space Oddity** has no quest giver: in the south of **Santo Domingo** (**Rancho Coronado**) you come across some homeless men crowded around a corpo briefcase they cannot open. The wiki classes it as a side job. Santo Domingo opens in Act 2, and the ending takes you to **Jackson Plains** in the **Badlands**.

> **Order:** talk to the homeless men → convince them (Body) or pay them → follow the blood trail → take the card from the corpse (or hack the briefcase with Intelligence) → open launch.exe → drop point, about 2 km south → take out the 7 Militech soldiers → open the pod → talk to Johnny.

## How to do it

Convince the men to hand over the briefcase before they get hurt: **threaten** them (**Body** check) or **pay** them. The wiki gives neither the check value nor the amount.

Scan the **blood trail** to the nearby dumpsters: the corpo who owned the briefcase is there, with an **access card**. Use it on the briefcase or **hack** it open (**Intelligence** check). On the computer, open **launch.exe**: it sends a signal that makes something drop from orbit.

Head to the new marker, about two kilometers south, already in the Badlands. Come ready to fight: a **Militech** response team is after the same thing, and you have to take out all **seven** soldiers before you can open the pod. Inside is a valuable painting; Johnny reckons it belonged to some rich highrider on the Moon.

## Rewards

Per the wiki infobox: 768 XP and 1,727 Street Cred, plus the painting **Untitled 18 – Brancesi, 2021**, which then hangs in your apartment.
