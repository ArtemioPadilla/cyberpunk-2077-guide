**Fifth Column** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at **El Pinche Pollo**, a **Valentinos** restaurant in the backstreets of **the Glen**, near Los Lobos Street. It belongs to Padre's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. When you reach the restaurant, Padre calls: a self-styled journalist pays well for proof that **Javier Alvarado**, the interim mayor's advisor, is funneling public money to the Valentinos.

> **Order:** enter the restaurant → get inside the office → (optional) drink to Jackie with the bouncer → find the evidence on the mayor's advisor → leave the restaurant.

## The restaurant

The ground floor is an ordinary restaurant: bar, restroom and kitchen. The **second floor** is for Valentinos only and both of its staircases are guarded. Upstairs there is a lounge with pool tables, a closet with a readable shard and the **office** with the computer holding the evidence.

## Getting upstairs without a fight

If you have finished **Heroes**, the guard on one of the staircases is **Ernesto Muñoz**, a friend of **Jackie's**. Mention Jackie: he takes you up for a drink in his memory and lets you through, with a warning to keep the peace. Otherwise you will have to sneak or fight your way to the office.

However you get the evidence, leave the restaurant: Padre calls and the gig ends.

## Rewards

- €$8,300, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
