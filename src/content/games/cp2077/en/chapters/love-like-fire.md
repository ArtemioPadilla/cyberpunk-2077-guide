**Love Like Fire** is the interlude between Act 1 and Act 2. After Dex's shot in The Heist, the Relic's nanites repair V's brain and make them relive the memories stored on the chip: those of **August 20, 2023**, through the eyes of **Johnny Silverhand**. Johnny walks out of a gig at **The Hammer** and, with **Rogue**, **Spider Murphy** and Shaitan, raids **Arasaka Tower** (Corpo Plaza) by helicopter to plant a bomb and upload a virus to free **Alt Cunningham**. It is a linear combat job with **no rewards and no level**. Once it ends, Act 2 begins with **Playing for Time**.

> **Order:** go on stage → grab the mic → go outside (optional: talk to Kerry) → get in the helicopter → defeat Arasaka's forces → wait for the helicopter to descend and exit → follow Spider Murphy → wait for the virus upload → find the elevator and defeat the guards → deploy and arm the bomb → exit and shoot the elevator mechanism → tell Rogue your plan → find the access point, defeat the guards and connect → wait for the upload → return to the rooftop and get to the chopper.

> **Get ready:** nothing: you play with Johnny's gear and V's items don't count here. **Johnny's pistol** can **take off limbs with charged shots**, and **his melee hits burn**.

## The Hammer

1. **Go on stage** (Kerry Eurodyne is already playing because Johnny is late; the bouncer shoves you on the way in).
2. **Grab the microphone**: Johnny says goodbye to his fans.
3. **Go outside.** On the way **Kerry** stops you, trying to talk you out of it.
4. **Optional: talk to Kerry.** You can tell him to go solo or insult him (see **Choices**).

## The helicopter and the rooftop

1. **Rogue** is waiting outside. **Get in the helicopter** with her, Spider Murphy and Shaitan.
2. **Defeat Arasaka's forces** from the helicopter. **Shoot the rooftop turrets.** Shaitan is hit and Johnny takes over the gun.
3. **Wait for the helicopter to descend** and **exit.**
4. If the helicopter won't land after the roof is cleared, **reload the autosave** (IGN).

## Inside the tower: the bomb

1. **Follow Spider Murphy** and **wait for the virus to upload into the network.** The virus is called **Liberator** (IGN).
2. **Find the elevator** and **defeat the Arasaka guards**.
3. **Wait**, **deploy and arm the bomb in the elevator** (it's a miniature nuclear device) and **exit the elevator**.
4. **Wait** and **shoot the elevator mechanism** to send the bomb down.

## The virus for Alt

1. Rogue wants to head back to the roof. **Tell her your plan**: upload a virus into Arasaka's systems to free Alt. She gives you **four minutes**.
2. **Find an Access Point to the network**: it's in the CEO's office, through a garden area. **Defeat the Arasaka guards**.
3. **Connect to the Access Point** and **wait for the virus to upload**, guided by Spider.
4. **Return to the rooftop** and **get to the chopper**.

What follows is a cutscene: Adam Smasher ambushes Johnny, there is a gap in his memories, and Johnny makes it back to the roof just as the helicopter lifts off. After he goes down, Johnny wakes up in an Arasaka interrogation (see **Choices**), which **Saburo Arasaka** joins.

## Choices

- **Kerry:** tell him to go solo or insult him → dialogue only; no documented consequences in this job.
- **The interrogation:** stay silent or toy with the interrogators → dialogue only.
- PowerPyx: no important choices.

> **Don't leave without:** nothing; this job has no items or rewards. The memories you relive here are not entirely faithful to what happened in 2023, per the wiki.

## Rewards

- **None**: no XP, level or items (IGN).
