**Takemura** gives it a day after Down on the Street. With Wakako's information, his plan is to get into **Arasaka Industrial Park** (Arroyo) and **hack Hanako's float** for the parade. You start at **Redwood Market** in Japantown preparing the ground, then (if you want) scout with him from a rooftop, and finally get into the warehouse. It can be done entirely in stealth; shooting too early makes it loud for good.

> **Order:** meet Takemura → go over the plan → take the shard → go over the plan → break into the security room → infect the security system → go over the plan → sit beside Takemura → go to Arasaka Industrial Park → follow Takemura → get onto the roof → scan the area → talk to Takemura → return to street level → (optional) steal the truck → break into the industrial park → get inside the warehouse → hack the float → talk to Takemura → leave the park → talk to Takemura.

> **Get ready:** have **Body 6** if you want to force the market's security-room door (game8). For the iconic's container you need **Intelligence 8** to disarm a mine. If you're **Corpo**, pay attention to the rooftop scan: there's a guard you can fool. **Don't shoot from the roof.** Save before coming down to the street.

## Redwood Market

**Meet Takemura** and **go over the plan**. He asks you to prepare the market:

1. **Take the shard.**
2. **Break into the security room:** game8 says **Body 6** forces the door; otherwise find another way in.
3. **Infect the security system.**
4. **Go over the plan** and **sit beside Takemura**.

## Joining Takemura (or not)

Takemura offers to take you along to scout the industrial park. It's your first choice (see "Choices"):

- **Go with him:** **go to Arasaka Industrial Park**, **follow him** and **get onto the roof**. **Scan the area** (and keep scanning if you like, optional). This opens **extra infiltration and escape routes**, and Takemura tells you his backstory.
- **Don't go:** wait a day. You get fewer routes and miss those scenes.

**Corpo lifepath.** While scanning from the roof you can overhear a **guard near the front** talking about an **information leak**. Scan him: later you can **persuade him** to let you in and walk around freely without the guards turning hostile (even Takemura is amazed it works). The wiki says that scan has to be immediate ("upon accepting the quest") or the option is lost: scan him first thing when you reach the roof.

**Careful on the roof:** if you start shooting from up there and get spotted, **the job turns loud for good**, even if nobody is watching you when you approach later. Killing the mech at that point also plays Takemura's line as if the heist had already started.

## Arasaka Industrial Park

**Get up**, **return to street level** and go in:

- **Steal the truck** (optional) if you want to use it to get in.
- **Persuade the guard** (optional, Corpo only).
- game8 recommends entering the warehouse through the **side door**: the main one is guarded by a very tough **robot**.

**Get inside the warehouse** and **hack the float**. **Talk to Takemura**, **leave the park** and talk to him again.

**Prototype: Shingen Mark V**, the iconic SMG, is in a **shipping container numbered "667"**, at the end of the warehouse's **exterior loading dock**: **loading dock 4, west of the main entrance**. Several computers in the warehouse mention it. Inside is a **mine** you can disarm with **Intelligence 8**.

## Choices

- **Joining Takemura on the recon** → more infiltration and escape routes, and his personal story. game8 also says that if you sent Jackie's body to Viktor in The Heist, Takemura shares something about Jackie here (unconfirmed).
- **Not joining him** → you wait a day, get fewer routes and miss those scenes.
- **Corpo: fooling the guard** → you walk in and move freely without alerting anyone.
- **Shooting from the roof and getting spotted** → the job stays loud to the end.
- Per the sources, none of these choices affects later jobs.

## What opens here

- **Play It Safe:** the parade. Note: the wiki lists **Gimme Danger and Life During Wartime** as prerequisites, and game8 warns you may need to advance the other lines before Takemura calls. If he doesn't call, finish the Hellman line.
- It doesn't open or close any side chain.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Prototype: Shingen Mark V** in container 667 (loading dock 4). It's easy to leave without it because it's outside the warehouse. Check the warehouse computers too: they point you to it.
