**Over the Edge** came with the **Cyberpunk: Edgerunners** content (Patch 1.6); the wiki classes it as a side job. Nobody gives it to you: you find it when you reach **Megabuilding H4**, in the south of **Arroyo**, **Santo Domingo**. There is a green graffiti tag next to the entrance and, in a nearby trash can, a **braindance**. Santo Domingo opens in Act 2.

> **Order:** braindance in the trash → play it → text El Capitán about David Martinez → wait a day → reply to Falco → collect the gift at Dam Viewpoint.

## How to do it

Put on the Braindance Wreath and play the recording: you live through the death of cyberpsycho **James Norris** (the opening scene of the show's first episode) and at the end you get a warning about **David Martinez**. When it ends, V is on the ground.

Text **Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes** to ask about David. He tells you part of his story and promises to get in touch once he learns where David's crew went. The chat replies change with your lifepath.

About **24 in-game hours** later, **Falco**, who ran with David, texts you. Reply: he says he left a gift for you with Muamar.

## What to pick up

Go to **Dam Viewpoint** in **Rancho Coronado**, where Muamar is. There is a package on the balustrade: walk up to it and take the jacket. That ends the job.

## Rewards

**David's Jacket**, a REO Meatwagon outer-torso piece: it was David's and, before that, his mother Gloria's. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
