**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, sends you to an abandoned house on Clements St., in **Biotechnica Flats**. It's from her third set, with **Sparring Partner**: it opens after **No Fixers**, **Big Pete's Got Big Problems** and **Trevor's Last Ride**, in **Act 2**. Dakota promised **Benedict McAdams** safe passage out of the city after a job, but his driver, **Jason Wildriver**, went off route. Luckily, Ben had a GPS put in his biomonitor.

> **Order:** find Ben → talk to him → take him to the meeting point → get out of the car → wait for Dakota's guy → talk to him.

## How to do it

Wildriver decided to sell him to **Militech**, which wants to pin everything on Ben. He's the **only enemy**: he roams the yard, and you can kill him, knock him out or avoid him by sneaking into the house. There are **mines** behind the house and a **turret** out front. In the **basement**, where Ben is, there's a camera you can switch off from the computer in the house.

If you didn't bring a car, there's an **Archer Quartz "Sidewinder"** in the garage. Starting it alerts Wildriver if he's still up, but he's easy to escape.

At the meeting point, get out and wait: the new driver is **Starkey**, an old friend of Ben's. They drive off together and Dakota pays you.
