**War Pigs** only exists if V has the **Corpo** lifepath. It comes some time after you meet Alt Cunningham and deal with the Voodoo Boys in **Transmission**, so it's an **Act 2** job. **Frank Nostra**, your former colleague from Arasaka Counterintelligence, leaves you a recorded message: **Susan Abernathy** is purging anyone not fully loyal, he's on her list, and he has put together a file on her. If you're getting the message, he says, it's because he couldn't use it himself. Then he sends you the coordinates.

> **Order:** go to the location → (optional) talk to Johnny → move the dumpster → open the briefcase → talk to the stranger → confront Frank → talk to Johnny.

## The dumpster

The coordinates lead to a **dumpster** in an alley on Allen Street in **Kabuki** (Watson). Johnny shows up to mock your lingering interest in the corpo life. **Move the dumpster** and open the briefcase behind it: it's empty. Then **Frank appears, alive**, disheveled and bitter. He believes that handing you over to Abernathy will get him rehired by Arasaka.

## Choices

- **Tell him you're a nobody to Abernathy** → you explain you were just a tool of Jenkins and that both of you would be shot on arrival. Frank says he has nothing to lose and **fights**.
- **Tell him he's pathetic** → you tell him about Joey Livani, the most pathetic ex-colleague at Arasaka, and that Frank now holds the title. Then you can **tell him to open his eyes**: leave Arasaka behind and use what he has to start over in Night City. **This only avoids the fight if you exhausted every dialogue option with Frank in The Corpo-Rat**; otherwise he draws his gun anyway.
- **If you defeat him** → search his body and take the **Apparition**.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Apparition** from Frank's body, if you fought. If you don't have it (or aren't Corpo), with Phantom Liberty Herold Lowe sells it at the Dogtown stadium.

## Rewards

- 2,303 XP and 3,453 Street Cred.
- **Apparition** (iconic Tech pistol), if you fight Frank.
