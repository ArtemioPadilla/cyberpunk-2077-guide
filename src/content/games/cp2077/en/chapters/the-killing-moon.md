**The Killing Moon** is the central job of the **Songbird path**: it only exists if you chose to **help her escape** in Firestarter. Songbird wants to leave for the **Moon** on a shuttle from the **NCX spaceport** at Morro Rock, and she needs V to get her there. You go in under a false identity, with your **combat cyberware disabled**, cross the terminal while NUSA and Orbital Air clash, and at the end, on the shuttle platform, **Reed** is waiting. What you do there decides Phantom Liberty's ending: **King of Wands** or **King of Swords**.

> **Order:** wait for Songbird's call (about 3 days) → van → drive to NCX → security gate: obey or disobey the guard → reach the Tycho Terminal (suitcase **930604** and restroom vent, optional) → construction site → roof: pull Songbird up with the fire hose → vents and fans → main hall → Departures → Tycho Terminal under fire → maglev station: hold until the train arrives → Blackwall pulse → train → **choice** on the platform.

> **Get ready:** at the gate you're made to **power down your combat cyberware** (the **Sandevistan** still works). Bring weapons that don't rely on implants. **Save before boarding the train**: the final scene decides the ending and one answer **kills V**.

## Waiting for Songbird

1. **Wait for news from Songbird.** It takes about **3 in-game days**: you can skip time 24 hours at a time. According to game8, finishing **Run This Town** also triggers her call. While you wait, Reed calls you: talk to him.
2. Talk to Songbird and **read her message**. Meet her, **get in the van** and talk to her.
3. **Drive to NCX** and go through the **security gate**. Talk to the guard.

## The security gate

**If you obey the guard:**

- **Jack in** with your personal link. Your job is to **buy time** for Songbird to hack the biometric database: **stall the guard** until "Breaching Database" reaches **100%**, then **look at the camera**.

**If you disobey the guard:** there's an alternate route. Talk to Songbird, **jack into the intercom** and **escape the security room**.

## Toward the Tycho Terminal

You have to **find a way into the Tycho Terminal**. Several options are optional:

- **The suitcase:** at baggage reclaim there's a suitcase from Songbird's contact; open it with code **930604**. Inside is a **Corp-Bud uniform**. Find a discreet spot to change.
- **Round the back:** sneak behind the tarps and enter the **service shaft**.
- **The restroom:** enter the **ventilation shaft in the restroom, in the rightmost stall**, and reach the Tycho Terminal through the maglev train tunnel.

## The construction site and the roof

1. Talk to Songbird. Cross the **construction site** to the rooftop elevator (stealth is best) and ride it up.
2. On the roof, **search for a way to pull Songbird up**: use the **fire hose** and **pull her up**.
3. **Re-equip your weapons** and follow Songbird.

During the construction site, someone watches you from above: it's **Mr. Blue Eyes**, who appears again later with an umbrella as V and Songbird leave on the monorail.

## Vents, fans and Departures

1. Talk to Songbird and enter the **ventilation shaft**.
2. **Grip a fan blade** to stop it. **Hide from the guards.** Repeat with the second fan.
3. Take the elevator to the spaceport's **main hall**. NUSA and **Orbital Air** are clashing there.
4. Go back to the Tycho Terminal, reach the elevator and ride up to **Departures**. **Check the room** for hostiles, wait for Songbird and talk to her.

## The Tycho Terminal and the maglev station

1. **Proceed through the Tycho Terminal.** A **chopper** opens fire: **move while it reloads** and take cover when it shoots.
2. Reach the **maglev station** with Songbird and the **control tower**: **defeat all NUSA operatives** to get there.
3. Talk to Songbird and **survive until the train arrives**. Check on Songbird in the control tower.
4. A **Blackwall pulse** hits: it's a QTE; **press the button shown on screen**.
5. **Pick up Songbird** and **get on the train**.

**If the objective doesn't advance:** game8 warns of a bug where the job stalls; walk around the area so the remaining enemies spawn.

## The ending: the shuttle platform

This is where everything is decided. There are two moments.

**On the train.** You can choose **"Call Reed"** and tell him to take care of her ("Reed'll take care of you"). PowerPyx follows this sequence: Call Reed → "Wanna live on" → Deal → pick up Songbird → lay her down → "Take good care of her". This takes you straight to **King of Swords**, with **The Tower** possible.

**If you don't call and carry Songbird to the shuttle:** carry her; **Reed is waiting on the platform**.

1. **Lay Songbird down.** It's mandatory: **if you refuse ("Gonna have to kill me"), Reed kills V** and it's game over.
2. Talk to Reed and choose:
   - **"Reed, dammit!"** or **"Draw Weapon: Sorry, man."** → a slow-motion shootout and **V kills Reed**. **Reed can't be taken down non-lethally.** Then: talk to Johnny, pick up Songbird, carry her to the shuttle, **place her inside**, prepare takeoff and go to the **observation deck** with Johnny; sit down. The **King of Wands** ending. Reed drops his iconic pistol **Pariah**.
   - **"Holster Weapon: All yours, but you'll help me live on."** → you carry Songbird to the launchpad and hand her to Reed; follow him. The **King of Swords** ending with **The Tower** open.
   - **"Holster Weapon: Take her. Don't want nothin' in return."** → also **King of Swords**. Sources disagree: game8 says this line **locks The Tower**; the wiki says that if you surrender Songbird the cure is offered **no matter what you say afterwards**. **Pick "you'll help me live on" to be safe.**

## Choices

- **Obeying or disobeying the guard** → only changes the way in (stall him or escape through the intercom).
- **Calling Reed from the train** → **King of Swords** with no fight; The Tower possible.
- **Refusing to lay Songbird down** → **Reed kills V**. Game over.
- **Killing Reed** → **King of Wands**: Songbird leaves for the Moon; you get the **Pariah**. **No cure and no Tower**; Reed dies. Next page: **Unfinished Sympathy** (with From Her to Eternity).
- **Handing Songbird to Reed** → **King of Swords**: Songbird goes back to NUSA and Reed gets you the cure. Next page: **Through Pain to Heaven**, then **Who Wants to Live Forever** (The Tower). To be safe, use "you'll help me live on".

> **Don't leave without:** the **930604** suitcase if you take the baggage reclaim route and, if you kill Reed, his **Pariah** pistol (there's no other chance). Both choices are permanent for this save: save before the train.

## Rewards

- Pariah (King of Wands only, missable).
- Corp-Bud uniform (optional, in the suitcase).
- The **King of Wands** or **King of Swords** achievement when the path is done.
