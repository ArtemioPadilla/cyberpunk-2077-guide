**Hal Cantos**, a braindance editor, did a job for **the Mox** and something went wrong: one of them, **Anna Nox**, put on the braindance and snapped. Now she attacks anything that moves in a Mox warehouse on Longshore North (Northside, Watson), and Hal is hiding inside. **Regina Jones** only pays if **Hal makes it out alive**. It is in **set 8 of 8**, the last one: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 7 (Freedom of the Press and Lousy Kleppers). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** enter the Mox warehouse → neutralize the cyberpsycho (optional: hack into the Moxes' subnet to weaken her) → find Hal Cantos → talk to him.

## Before the fight

- Upstairs there's a **wounded Mox**: talk to her. She doesn't know where Hal hid, but explains that you can **weaken Anna** through the Moxes' subnet (they all share a localnet): linking into the dead ones disables part of her cyberware.
- Hal is in the **large blue shipping container**, in the same room as Anna. **Don't open it before she's down**: Hal won't come out and, if he peeks out, Anna goes for him first.

## Anna Nox

She has **Mantis Blades**, Sandevistan Mk.1, frag grenades, Charge Jump and Break Hold (you can't grab her). Weaken her through the subnet first. You can knock her out or kill her; you can tell the Mox upstairs you killed her either way, and Regina doesn't mention her at all. She **doesn't count** as a Cyberpsycho Sighting for **Psycho Killer**.
