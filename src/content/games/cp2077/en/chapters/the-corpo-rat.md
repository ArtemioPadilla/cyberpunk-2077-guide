**The Corpo-Rat** is the Corpo lifepath prologue, and you only play it if you pick that lifepath when creating V. The other two lifepaths have their own prologue (The Nomad and The Streetkid); all three end with **Jackie Welles** at your side and lead into the same job, The Rescue. Here V is a mid-tier agent in **Arasaka**'s counterintelligence division, and Jackie is already a friend of many years. The job comes from V's boss, **Arthur Jenkins**: kill **Susan Abernathy**, Jenkins' superior and rival. It starts in **Arasaka Tower** (Corpo Plaza) and ends at **Lizzie's Bar** in Watson. It is a short, linear job with almost no combat.

> **Order:** leave the bathroom → head to Jenkins' office (optional: talk to your old acquaintance) → sit and watch the vote → talk to Jenkins and take the datashard and the money → go to the AV garage and talk to Jackie (optional: read the report on your terminal) → fly Jenkins' AV to Lizzie's → deal with the locals → talk to the bouncer → sit with Jackie → talk to the agents and hand over the datashard → talk to Jackie.

> **Get ready (creating V):**
>
> - **Difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard): change it whenever you like; since 2.1 it lives in the Accessibility tab.
> - **Attributes:** each starts at 3 and you spend **7 free points** (maximum 6 per attribute at creation; later they go up to 20). Each level gives +1 Attribute point and +1 Perk point. **Attribute points are permanent**; perks can be refunded one by one for free.
> - **Romances** (body and voice matter, lifepath doesn't): **Judy** needs a feminine body and voice, **River** a feminine body, **Panam** a masculine body and **Kerry** a masculine body and voice.
> - **Lifepath:** it only changes the prologue and some dialogue; **none is mechanically better**.
> - **Phantom Liberty:** creation offers to **skip ahead to Phantom Liberty**. That auto-completes the prologue and every main job through Transmission: you skip all of Act 1 and its choices.

## Arasaka Tower

V starts in a tower bathroom, stress-vomiting in front of the mirror. After a short call with Jackie, Jenkins calls, furious about a data leak in Frankfurt, and orders you to his office.

1. **Leave the bathroom.**
2. **Head to Jenkins' office.** First you can step into your own office and read the emails on the desk.
3. **Optional: talk to your old acquaintance**, **Frank Nostra**. Per IGN, the chat has no repercussions.
4. **Optional: Harry's cubicle.** You can pull the spy exposed to BioTechnica or let him keep digging. Per IGN, it **has no impact on the story**.

**Loot:** there are lore shards in the lobby and the offices, booze, and a **roll of bills** on a table where people are gossiping.

## Jenkins' office

1. **Sit** and **watch the vote**: Jenkins launches a cyberattack on the European Space Agency council to delay its vote on Arasaka's lunar licenses. Abernathy calls to berate him.
2. **Talk to Jenkins.** He orders you to take out Abernathy using your underworld contacts.
3. **Take the datashard** (all of Abernathy's personal information) and **take the money**. IGN says Jenkins hands you **€$45,800** in cash.

## AV garage and the flight to Lizzie's

1. **Leave the office** and **go to the AV garage**.
2. **Talk to Jackie** over the holo.
3. **Optional: read the report** on your personal terminal.
4. **Get in Jenkins' AV**, **set course for Lizzie's** and fly there.
5. **Exit the AV.** You land on a rooftop that doubles as a basketball court.

## The basketball players

**Deal with the locals:** the players don't like a corporate AV landing on their court. You can **subdue them with a lightning attack** or simply **threaten them**; both options resolve the scene.

Before heading down, look for the **clothing container** behind the fallen hoop on the court.

## Lizzie's Bar

1. **Head to Lizzie's** and **talk to the Mox bouncer**, who lets you in.
2. **Meet Jackie** and **sit with him.** The DJ hallway has a **pink cabinet with clothing**.
3. **Talk to Jackie.** You explain the job and ask for help; Jackie sees the risk of working for Arasaka and refuses.
4. **Talk to the agents.** Arasaka agents arrive: Abernathy already knows about the hit and demands the datashard. They strip your corporate privileges and disable your cyberware.
5. **Hand over the datashard.** If instead you **wait out the agents' timer**, your cyberware really does shut off and the HUD disappears from the screen. The outcome doesn't change.
6. **Talk to Jackie.** The agents try to take you in, but Jackie convinces them to leave. V, woozy without their cyberware, falls to the floor and Jackie helps them up: the Night City introduction montage begins, leading into **The Rescue**.

## Choices

- **Frank and Harry's cubicle:** any option → no repercussions, per IGN.
- **The basketball players:** lightning attack or threat → same result.
- **Hand over the shard or wait out the timer** → same ending; waiting only shows you the cyberware shutting down.
- **Do you keep Jenkins' money?** Sources disagree: IGN says V keeps Jenkins' bundle of cash after the agents strip their corporate assets; the wiki only says they strip privileges and cyberware. Check your balance when The Rescue starts.
- **The Corpo lifepath in the long run** → gives you unique dialogue all game (the "Stanislavski's Method" achievement asks for 10 lifepath lines). In Act 1 the one that matters is in **The Pickup**: telling Meredith Stout you know her chip is spiked opens the objective to remove the virus and the option to **warn Royce**. In **The Heist** you can ask Dex for a bigger cut by saying Arasaka is overdue for payback. This lifepath's exclusive side job is **War Pigs**.

> **Don't leave without:** the lore shards and the roll of bills in the tower, the clothing container behind the fallen hoop on the court, and the pink cabinet in Lizzie's DJ hallway.

## Rewards

- **Memorabilia:** an **Arasaka clock** for V's apartment.
- **Lifepath starting gear:** the **Unity** pistol and office clothing.
