**Guinea Pigs** is a gig from **Dino Dinovic**, the **City Center** fixer. It takes place at the **Biotechnica corporate hotel** on Republic Way, **Downtown**. It belongs to Dino's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Serial Suicide** and **An Inconvenient Killer**. **Joanne Koch**, Biotechnica's regional director, tested a Project Nightingale virus that killed about 70 members of the **Red Ocher** nomad clan. The families hired Dino. Talk to the **hotel clerk** too: it unlocks extra Nomad dialogue in **Olive Branch**.

> **Order:** reach the 19th floor → get inside Koch's apartment → neutralize her → (if knocked out) neutralize the guards on the roof → wait for the AV → carry Koch to the AV → stay clear of the landing zone → escape the hotel.

## The lobby

It is watched by guards, cameras and a turret. Bypass them through the **unlocked side door** by the police cordon: it leads to an office with one worker and a computer that disables the lobby cameras. On the way out, turn right to the elevator.

## 19th floor and roof

There are guards, dormant robots and cameras. At the intersection, go left: that is where the apartments and the roof access door are. Koch's door (193) needs **Technical Ability 19**; otherwise go up to the roof, where four guards circle a skylight with an open window, and drop through it into the apartment.

## Choices

- **Kill her** on the spot and leave the way you came.
- **Knock her out** → Dino sends an AV to the roof; take out the four guards first, carry Koch up the stairs next to the apartment and put her in the AV.
- **Wake her and talk** → she asks to upload her data; if you agree, she calls security; if you refuse, she runs and calls them too. Either way there is a fight.

> **Don't leave without:** in apartment 193's bedroom there is a stash with the legendary **A-22B Chao** **Crafting Spec** and another with components and a **Skill Shard: Crafting**; in the rooftop storage room, another legendary Crafting Spec.
