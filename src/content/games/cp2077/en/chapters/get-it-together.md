**Get It Together** is the bridge job into the Black Sapphire. Getting into Kurt Hansen's hotel takes its plans, and the man who has them is Dogtown's fixer, **Mr. Hands**. First you have to earn his trust by doing gigs; then he receives you at his club, **Heavy Hearts**, tells you about his plan against Hansen and how Dogtown came to be, and hands over the plans. The job ends in Alex's hideout under **The Moth**, where Reed and Alex explain how you're getting in.

> **Order:** call Mr. Hands → do at least 3 of his gigs (or wait a few hours if you already have) → Mr. Hands' message → Heavy Hearts club → code **2589** at the terminal → private floor → talk to Mr. Hands → send the data to Reed → The Moth → V collapses → ride the elevator down with Reed → briefing with Alex and Reed → diving suit and box → talk to Reed.

> **Get ready:** if you didn't do any Mr. Hands gigs during the wait in Lucretia My Reflection, now's the time. The Tier 2 ones are **Dogtown Saints**, **Prototype in the Scraper**, **Treating Symptoms** and **Waiting for Dodger**: pick three that suit you by location. **Treating Symptoms** also gives you the key to the locked room in Slider's hideout (Gris-Gris revolver). If you haven't done **Balls to the Wall** yet, this is a good moment: it's lost once Firestarter is done.

## Winning over Mr. Hands

1. **Call Mr. Hands** and talk to him.
2. **If you haven't done three gigs for him yet**, the objective becomes **complete at least three gigs** for Mr. Hands. They're marked on the Dogtown map.
3. **If you've already done three or more**, just **wait a few hours** for him to message you.
4. Talk to Mr. Hands and **read his message**: he invites you to the club.

## Heavy Hearts

1. **Go to the Heavy Hearts club.**
2. **Enter code 2589 at the terminal** to go up.
3. **Reach the private floor** and talk to Mr. Hands. He tells you his plan to undermine Kurt Hansen and how Dogtown began, and at the end **gives you the Black Sapphire plans**.
4. **Leave the club.**

## The Moth and Alex's hideout

1. **Call Reed and send him the data.**
2. **Meet Reed at The Moth** and talk to him.
3. When you arrive, **V collapses**: the Relic malfunctions. Reed tells you his backstory and takes you downstairs to **Alex's hideout**. Follow Reed and **ride the elevator down**.
4. Talk to Reed: he tells you about his old crew, which included **Songbird**, until Alex shows up.
5. **Talk to Alex and Reed.** Alex lays out the plan for breaching the Black Sapphire.
6. **Grab the diving suit** and **examine the contents of the box**.
7. Talk to Reed to finish the job.

## The hideout loot

- **Her Majesty:** Alex's old service weapon, an iconic pistol. It's in the **small weapon case on the table, next to the diving suit**.
- **The safe and the cases:** after the briefing, the hideout's **safe** holds more cyberware and weapons, and there are several more **weapon cases**. Check everything before you leave.

## What opens when it's done

- **Mr. Hands' Tier 3 gigs:** The Man Who Killed Jason Foreman, Spy in the Jungle and Talent Academy. (In Spy in the Jungle, sparing Ana later opens the side jobs Money for Nothing and No Easy Way Out.)
- The next job, **You Know My Name**, takes you straight to the Black Sapphire.

## Choices

- **How many gigs you do** → with fewer than three, the job stalls until you complete them; there's no other way forward. Dogtown gigs also count toward the achievement for every Dogtown gig.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Her Majesty** pistol from the case by the diving suit and whatever is in the hideout's **safe** and cases. Before You Know My Name, if you want the **Amikiri Sound Cutter** at the party, you must have met Lizzy Wizzy in the minor job "Violence" (see the next page).

## Rewards

- Her Majesty (iconic pistol).
- Black Sapphire plans (quest item).
