**Heroes** is the farewell to **Jackie Welles**. It comes from his mother, **Mama Welles** (Guadalupe Alejandra Welles), who calls shortly after **The Heist**, while you recover in your apartment. What happens depends on a choice in The Heist: **where you sent Jackie's body**. If it went to his family (or you told Delamain to wait), there is an **ofrenda** at **El Coyote Cojo** (Heywood); if it went to Viktor, there is no ceremony and you only get a package.

> **For the ending:** Heroes **opens and closes no ending**. But finishing it adds **Mama Welles voicemails** in the epilogue that change with the ending. If you sent Jackie's body to **Viktor**, you later learn that Arasaka made an **engram of Jackie**, who appears in some endings. With the **Streetkid** lifepath, Heroes then opens _Small Man, Big Mouth_.

> **Order:** call Mama Welles → El Coyote Cojo (talk, beer) → Jackie's garage (the alley behind) → Misty → search the garage (room key, mandala, posters) → choose the offering → convince Misty → back to the bar → sit with Viktor → stories and your speech → toast → Mama Welles: the ARCH key → **24 h later:** La Chingona Dorada on the altar.

> **Get ready:** there is no combat. Take your time to talk to everyone. Before leaving the garage, **talk to Misty** to convince her to come to the ofrenda. And plan to come back to El Coyote Cojo **a day later**: Jackie's pistols wait on the altar.

## Mama Welles's call

Read Mama Welles's message (optional) and **call her**.

- **If you sent Jackie's body to his family, or told Delamain to wait:** she invites you to Jackie's **ofrenda** at El Coyote Cojo. Read on.
- **If you sent the body to Viktor:** there is no ofrenda. **Three days later**, Mama Welles tells you she left a **package** at your apartment: open it to get the **key to Jackie's ARCH**. If you ask **Viktor**, he tells you **Arasaka took the body**, and that's all he knows.

## El Coyote Cojo

Talk to Mama Welles: she offers you a beer and asks how you are. V wants to take part in the ofrenda properly but doesn't know what to place by the urn. Mama Welles gives you the **key to Jackie's garage** so you can find something from his past.

## Jackie's garage

The garage is in the **alley behind El Coyote Cojo**. **Misty**, Jackie's girlfriend, is sitting at the door: she is waiting for him even though she knows he won't come back. Talk to her and go in together.

1. **Scan and search the garage** (optional, but worth it).
2. **The posters:** scan the **pin-ups** on the wall to unlock those posters for your H10 apartment.
3. **Jackie's room:** the blue door is locked. The **key to Jackie's room** is **on the desk**. Open it: Jackie tried to make a **mandala**, as Misty had told him to, to clear out the "dark energy". Seeing it does Misty good.
4. **Choose the offering.** The options are **Jackie's Book**, a **basketball**, a **tequila bottle** or a **leather belt**. Any of them works.
5. **Before leaving, talk to Misty** and **convince her to come to the ofrenda**. If she comes, and you talk to Mama Welles afterwards, the relationship between the two noticeably improves.

## The ofrenda

Back in the bar, talk to Mama Welles and **sit next to Viktor** on the couch.

1. Several people tell a story about Jackie. Faces from gigs you've done may be there (for example **Gustavo Orta** and **Martha Young**, the **Fuscinos** or **Ernesto Muñoz**). If you already finished _Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta_, two Valentinos speak instead.
2. Your turn: **say a few words** about Jackie.
3. **Leave your offering** and **raise a toast to Jackie**.

After the ceremony you can talk (all optional) to the **bartender** (Pepe Najarro), **Viktor**, the **Valentinos** and **Padre**. If Misty came, Mama Welles mentions it and V asks her to be nicer to Misty and invite her to dinner.

Finally, Mama Welles gives you the **key to Jackie's ARCH**, his motorcycle. Leave the bar.

## Jackie's pistols (24 hours later)

**Twenty-four hours after leaving El Coyote Cojo**, return to the ofrenda altar: **La Chingona Dorada**, Jackie's twin iconic **Nue** pistols, are there. They are easy to forget. If you miss them, **Herold Lowe** sells them in Phantom Liberty.

## Choices

- **The Heist — where you sent Jackie's body** → family or "Delamain, wait": the full ofrenda, the ARCH and La Chingona Dorada; Viktor: only the package with the ARCH key, and Jackie's engram appears in some endings. Already decided when you arrive.
- **The offering: book, basketball, tequila or belt** → changes nothing beyond the scene.
- **Opening Jackie's room (mandala)** → lifts Misty's mood.
- **Convincing Misty** → she comes to the ofrenda and her relationship with Mama Welles improves.
- **Finishing Heroes** → Mama Welles voicemails in the epilogue, depending on the ending; with the Streetkid lifepath, it opens _Small Man, Big Mouth_.

> **Don't leave without:** the **posters** (scan the garage pin-ups), opening **Jackie's room** with the key on the desk, **convincing Misty** before leaving the garage, the **ARCH key** and, a day later, **La Chingona Dorada** on the altar.

## Rewards

- 300 XP and 446 Street Cred.
- **Jackie's ARCH** (Jackie's motorcycle).
- **La Chingona Dorada** (on the altar, 24 h later; ofrenda route only).
- Jackie's pin-up posters for your apartment (if you scan them).
