**Every Breath You Take** comes from **Blue Moon** of **Us Cracks**. It only arrives if, in **I Don't Wanna Hear It**, you settled the dispute between Kerry and Us Cracks amicably and also finished **Off the Leash**, so it's an **Act 2** job. Blue Moon calls you: someone is sending her death threats. **Ask her for clues**: the letters are signed **"GC"**. Her plan: stroll around **Kabuki Roundabout** as bait while you catch the stalker, without killing them. **Red Menace** keeps watch from a rooftop.

> **Order:** meeting spot → tail Blue Moon from a distance → find the stalker → defeat her → search for evidence → talk to Blue Moon → decide the stalker's fate.

## Finding her

- **Don't stick to Blue Moon:** if you follow too closely, she calls off the operation and the job fails. It also fails if you wander far from the roundabout or take down the wrong person.
- On the upper level a guy is taking photos of her. Confront him (optional objective) and ask if he's noticed anything odd: there's a **girl in green** in every one of his shots.
- Scan her: she's **Griselda Martinez**, brown ponytail, green tank top and a pink rabbit backpack. At one point she walks up to Blue Moon and says her name is **Green Cloud**.
- **Confront her early:** if you know about "GC", you can accuse her and she attacks. Without that clue she walks off; if you follow her, she goes into the gun shop, and if you ask what she's doing there, she attacks.
- **Wait:** halfway across the footbridge, Griselda steps in front of Blue Moon with a gun. Act fast: if she kills Blue Moon, the job fails.

If Griselda vanishes when you confront her in the gun shop (a bug reported on the wiki, unconfirmed), load a save or follow Blue Moon to the footbridge.

## Choices

Search Griselda's body and take the evidence (a disturbing diary and a still of Blue Moon with the eyes cut out). Then you can **kill her** or, if she's unconscious, tell Blue Moon to **leave her to the police**. Neither option has consequences. Blue Moon pays you shortly after.
