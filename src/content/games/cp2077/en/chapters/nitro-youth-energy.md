**Nitro (Youth Energy)** arrived with **Update 2.3**. To get **Yaiba**'s invitation you need to have bought **one Yaiba vehicle**, bought **at least three vehicles on AUTOFIXER** and finished the gig **Reported Crime: You Play with Fire...**. It starts when you read the invitation on the computer in one of your apartments or scan a Yaiba billboard. The event is at the **Kiroshi campus** on Grant Avenue, **Charter Hill** (Westbrook).

> **Order:** go to the Yaiba showroom at Kiroshi → wait until evening for the event → connect to the Kiroshi bot → leave the showroom area → place your Muramasa order → return the bot to its charging station and disconnect → wait for Yaiba's message → go to the Corpo Plaza docks → identify yourself → open the container → collect the bike.

## The showroom

You can connect to the showroom through **Yaiba's netpage** or go to the campus in person. The event starts **in the evening**. Connect to the **Kiroshi bot**: the showroom displays **seven sample configurations** of the new **Yaiba ASM-R250 Muramasa**. When you order, you choose from **four parts** you can add or leave off, so you can copy any of the seven. **You can only customize it once**, at purchase. It costs **€$95,000**.

**Don't fight at the Kiroshi campus**: the job fails. If that happens, you can still buy the Muramasa on AUTOFIXER.

## The delivery

The next day, Yaiba tells you the bike is ready at the **Corpo Plaza docks** (Republic Way). Go there, identify yourself, open the container and ride off.

## Rewards

- **Yaiba ASM-R250 Muramasa** (you buy it for €$95,000).
- The wiki gives no XP or Street Cred for this job.
