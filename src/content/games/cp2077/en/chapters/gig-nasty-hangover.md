**Rogue Amendiares** offers this gig on Hargreaves St., **Arroyo (Santo Domingo)**, in **Act 2**. It opens after **Desperate Measures**, **For My Son** and **Going-away Party**, El Capitán's last set. **Karl Ginsky**, a **Kang Tao** corpo, went drinking through Santo Domingo with a **shard** of secret data in his head, and **6th Street** ripped it out. Get it back before Kang Tao's top brass finds out.

> **Order:** enter the 6th Street warehouse → find and take the shard → leave the warehouse → call Karl to set up a meet → meet him and talk → watch the confrontation or protect Karl → talk to Karl or to the Kang Tao agents.

## The warehouse

The warehouse is held by **6th Street**, with **Jeff Miller** among the enemies. The wiki has no detailed walkthrough: find the shard, take it and get out.

## Choices

**Kang Tao** agents show up at the meet. They won't attack unless you start it.

- **Protect Karl** (shoot the agents before they kill him) → you fight the Kang Tao operatives. Karl gives you a **credit chip** and Rogue reacts better: it was close, but still a success.
- **Let them kill him** → talk to the agents to clear things up. If you **ask about the data**, they drop a **credit chip**; if you **tell them off** before that, they don't (after they drop it, you can tell them off with no fight). If you take their chip, **Johnny Silverhand** appears on the roof of the nearest shack and makes a disappointed remark.

## Rewards

€$17,610, plus Karl's or the agents' credit chip, depending on your choice.
