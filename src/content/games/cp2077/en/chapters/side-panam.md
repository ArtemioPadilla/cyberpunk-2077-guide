**Panam Palmer**'s chain follows the Hellman main line (**Ghost Town → Lightning Breaks → Life During Wartime**) and takes her back to the **Aldecaldos**: **Riders on the Storm**, Mitch's minor job **I'll Fly Away**, **With a Little Help from My Friends** and **Queen of the Highway**. Riders on the Storm unlocks when you finish **Life During Wartime**. It is the most trap-laden chain in the game: a hidden deadline, a job that expires and a hidden choice that costs you an ending.

> **For the ending:** **completing Queen of the Highway** is the requirement for **The Star** (leaving with the Aldecaldos) and for the Panam version of **Temperance**. What breaks it: letting Saul die in Riders on the Storm (deadline) and **telling Saul the plan** in With a Little Help from My Friends. **Romance:** **masculine body, any voice** (lifepath and genitalia don't matter). A masculine V can hold Panam and Kerry at the same time, but on the rooftop you call only one partner. **Panam stays with V only in The Star**; The Tower ends the romance.

> **Order:** **Riders on the Storm:** Aldecaldo camp → Mitch's drone (5 scans) → **SuperJet** → van with Panam → Corp-Bud factory → Saul in the basement → maintenance tunnel → storm: Ingalls Farm (power and outside panel) → morning: Overwatch → **I'll Fly Away** (Mitch, bridge) → **With a Little Help from My Friends:** control tower (Punchcard) → campfire → train raid and Basilisk → **Queen of the Highway:** leave the camp and let 24 h pass → Basilisk through the Wind Farms → Raffen Shiv ambush → camp attack → party → new camp.

> **Get ready:** earlier choices already count. In **Ghost Town**, helping Panam kill **Nash** gives you the **Widow Maker** there; if you refuse, Rogue gifts you the **Archer Quartz "Bandit"** and Nash shows up later in Queen of the Highway (Fandom says refusing doesn't change Panam's story; game8 says it locks you out of the romance: if you want it, help her). In **Life During Wartime**, **abandoning Panam** at the crash site loses you **I'll Fly Away**; for the romance, defend her when Saul berates her and answer her text with the long reply, not "OK". **Save before Riders on the Storm** and don't take other jobs while it runs.

## Riders on the Storm

At the **Aldecaldo camp**, Panam tells you the **Wraiths** have taken **Saul**.

**Hidden deadline.** If you start the job and then leave the area for too long, Panam texts that **Saul died**: the job fails and **you lose every follow-up, the romance and The Star**. PowerPyx is more specific: after taking the SuperJet, drive with Panam straight to the Wraith camp; drive the wrong way and you get kicked out of the car, and skipping time then fails the job. **Do the whole job in one go.**

1. **Recon.** Use Mitch's drone to scan the **gate**, the **tracks**, the **truck**, the **main building** and the **garage**.
2. **The SuperJet.** Take the **SuperJet** from Mitch.
3. **The van.** Ride with Panam in the van to the **Corp-Bud Cement Factory**, where the Wraiths hold him. A dust storm is coming.
4. **The factory.** Saul is in the **basement of the main building**. Near the main entrance there is a **Wraiths Boss Hunter**: kill him and loot the **Problem Solver** (iconic Saratoga, missable). Clearing every Wraith in the place gives unique dialogue.
5. **Way out.** Leave with Saul through the **maintenance tunnel**.
6. **The storm.** Shelter at the **Ingalls Farm**: restore power and use the outside panel.

**Romance (masculine body).** In Panam's argument with Saul about selling out, **side with Panam**. That night, on the couch, **touch her thigh**: she turns you down for now. The next morning (or when asking about the previous evening), a masculine V gets a **kiss**. game8 says riding with her to the camp also helps (only that source says so).

In the morning, Panam gives you **Overwatch**, Mitch's sniper rifle she promised you.

## I'll Fly Away

A minor job from **Mitch** that opens after Riders on the Storm, only if you **did not abandon Panam** in Life During Wartime. It is the farewell to **Scorpion**.

**It expires.** It stops being available once Mitch texts that the **Basilisk** is ready (Queen of the Highway), and also if you receive **With a Little Help from My Friends** first, because Mitch is then with Panam. The two Fandom pages word it differently; go with this: **do it right after Riders on the Storm, before you answer Panam's next call**.

1. Drive Mitch's car to Scorpion's favourite **collapsed bridge**.
2. Put the **CHOOH2 canister** in the passenger seat.
3. Light the rag and watch the car fly off the ramp.

## With a Little Help from My Friends

Panam has a plan: steal a **Basilisk** tank from a Militech train.

- **If you say no at first**, you can still join later at the **Corp-Bud Railroad Station**.
- **Don't tell Saul the plan.** It is a hidden choice: Saul pays you **€$42,940**, but **Panam cuts contact and you lose Queen of the Highway and The Star**. Later, Saul leaves you a red **Mizutani Shion "Bonewrecker"** at the Sunset Motel as a parting gift. **Don't do it on a Star or Panam-romance run.**

**The control tower.** Go in through the **Technical Ability** door, the **Body** rear door, or climb the generator to reach the balcony. Find the **Punchcard** in a cabinet and insert it.

**Romance (masculine body).** In the tower talk, **don't pick "Not doing it for free"**; **do pick "Next time, try following that impulse"**. game8 also suggests "I missed you" and touching her hand. At the **campfire**, "Gotten kinda cold" is **not** required: you can scoot closer and put an arm around her. You can toast Scorpion and Jackie.

**The morning raid.** Ride alongside the locomotive and seize the Militech **Basilisk**. Shooting the coupler changes nothing. Afterwards, Panam texts about your relationship.

## Queen of the Highway

It doesn't start on its own: **leave the camp and let 24 hours pass**, then return to Panam (PowerPyx).

1. **Basilisk test.** Panam pilots first, then you, through the **Wind Farms**. Shoot **five targets**.
2. **Jacking in.** You both jack into the Basilisk.
3. **Romance (masculine body):** say the Basilisk is **"cozy"** and let Panam touch your thigh. The romance scene starts.
4. **Ambush.** The **Raffen Shiv** attack you and then raid the camp. If you didn't kill **Nash** in Ghost Town, he shows up here and drops the **Widow Maker**.
5. **Party.** Saul asks Panam to co-lead the clan. Drinks follow, then **the Relic malfunctions**.
6. **New camp.** You wake in a tent at the new camp and explain your condition to Panam. If romanced, kiss her goodbye.

Once it's done, **Panam is your ally for The Star** (and Panam Temperance). The next Aldecaldo job is _Fortunate Son_.

## Choices

- **Ghost Town — help kill Nash** → Widow Maker in Ghost Town; game8 says it counts toward the romance. **Refuse** → Rogue's Archer Quartz "Bandit"; Nash returns in Queen of the Highway. Fandom and game8 disagree on whether refusing locks the romance: to be safe, help.
- **Life During Wartime — abandon Panam** → bitter texts from Panam and **you lose I'll Fly Away. Permanent.**
- **Riders on the Storm — leave or skip time** → **Saul dies: the job, the rest of the chain, the romance and The Star fail. Permanent.**
- **Riders on the Storm — back Panam against Saul and touch her thigh** → a kiss in the morning for a masculine V.
- **I'll Fly Away — leave it for later** → it expires as soon as you advance the chain. **Permanent.**
- **With a Little Help — tell Saul the plan** → €$42,940 and the Bonewrecker, but **Panam cuts you off and you lose Queen of the Highway and The Star. Permanent.**
- **With a Little Help — "Not doing it for free"** → closes the romance; "Next time, try following that impulse" keeps it open.
- **Queen of the Highway — "cozy" and the thigh** → romance scene (masculine V).
- **Finishing Queen of the Highway** → **opens The Star** and Panam Temperance on the rooftop in Nocturne Op55N1.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Problem Solver** from the Wraiths Boss Hunter (near the main entrance, in Riders on the Storm), **Overwatch** in the morning, **I'll Fly Away** before moving on with the chain and Nash's **Widow Maker** (Ghost Town or Queen of the Highway). If you miss the Problem Solver or the Widow Maker, Herold Lowe sells them in Phantom Liberty.

## Rewards

- **Riders on the Storm:** 1158 XP, 1737 Street Cred, **Overwatch** and **Problem Solver** (missable).
- **I'll Fly Away:** 2303 XP, 3453 Street Cred, **Stinger** and an Action Figure.
- **With a Little Help from My Friends:** 1322 XP, 1981 Street Cred (or €$42,940 from Saul, if you betray Panam).
- **Queen of the Highway:** 1322 XP, 1981 Street Cred, **Widow Maker** (if Nash survived Ghost Town) and the **The Star** route.
