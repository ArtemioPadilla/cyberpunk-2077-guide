**I've Seen That Face Before** starts as you leave Farida's clinic. The Charon Exotics link you made in Birds with Broken Wings has been severed, so Reed changes the plan: go to the **Dogtown Gate**, watch from a vantage point by the **Golden Pacific Data Term** and spot the rental car of **Aurore and Aymeric Cassel** yourself. Then you climb into their **trunk**, take over the car remotely and drive them straight into the trap Reed and Alex have set. At the end you put on their clothes and their face: Firestarter starts the moment this job ends.

> **Order:** call Reed → go to the Dogtown entrance → vantage point → wait for the car → scan vehicles at the intersection → intercept the car (lot below Eden Plaza) → open the trunk and get in → check the cameras → wait for the right moment → take control → drive to the meeting point → download the Cynosure codes from Aurore → put on the netrunner outfit → get in the car → activate the behavioral imprint.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save before you start** and don't overwrite it: Firestarter follows straight on and is Phantom Liberty's big branch point. If you still have things to do in Dogtown, above all the side job **Balls to the Wall**, do them **first**: you can't after Firestarter.

## Watching the Dogtown gate

1. **Call Reed** and talk to him.
2. **Go to the Dogtown entrance** and then to the **vantage point** near the **Golden Pacific** Data Term.
3. **Wait for the netrunners' car.** V waits several hours, until just before midnight.
4. Reed asks you to **scan the vehicles** coming from Night City into Dogtown at the intersection. The twins' car is a **Quadra Sport R-7 "Charon"**. Once tagged, it's tracked.

**If you don't tag it in time:** Reed moves to **plan C** and sends you coordinates for **three different locations** where you have to go and scan for the car. It takes longer, but the job doesn't fail.

## The trunk

1. **Intercept the car:** it's in a parking lot **below Eden Plaza**. Go to the car and talk to Reed.
2. **Open the trunk** and **get in**. V connects to the car's security system.
3. Talk to Reed and **check the cameras**.
4. The twins come back to the car to head for the **EBM Petrochem Stadium** and the meeting with Hansen and Songbird.

**Voodoo Treasure (missable).** While you wait in the trunk, the twins talk about **Slider's** death and a **hidden stash**. **Listen to the whole conversation.** If you do, you'll later be able to download the file **"Blind_n_Dead"** from Aurore's body, which unlocks the side job **Voodoo Treasure** (code **941229** for Slider's stash under The Needle, with the **Rook** pistol and €$8000). If you don't listen, the download option doesn't appear.

## Taking control of the car

1. **Wait for the right moment** and **take control of the car**.
2. **Drive to the meeting point**, the garage where Reed and Alex are waiting. The netrunners keep trying to take back control and drive back to the stadium: it's a **tug-of-war** where you have to win control back each time.

**What you can't do:** if you **never take control** and let the car reach the stadium, the guards scan it, find V in the trunk and **fry V's circuits**. **V dies: game over.**

## The identity swap

1. In the garage, Reed and Alex stun and execute the twins.
2. **Download the Cynosure mainframe access codes** from Aurore's body. If you heard the trunk conversation, the **"Blind_n_Dead"** download also appears here: take it.
3. **Put on the netrunner outfit**, get in the car and **activate the behavioral imprint** on the faceplate. A short test confirms it works and you and Alex set off for the meeting.

V impersonates the twin of V's own gender: **female V plays Aurore** and **male V plays Aymeric**; Alex plays the other. This decides the right answers in the Firestarter interview.

## Choices

- **Tagging the car in time or not** → if you miss it, plan C: three places to scan. No bigger penalty.
- **Listening to the twins' conversation** → with the "Blind_n_Dead" download, **Voodoo Treasure** opens (Rook and €$8000). If you don't listen or don't download, that route is lost (although code 941229 opens the stash if you know it).
- **Never taking control of the car** → the stadium guards find you and **V dies**.

> **Don't leave without:** listening to the twins and downloading **"Blind_n_Dead"**. And save: next comes Firestarter, the choice that splits Phantom Liberty in two.

## Rewards

- Netrunner suit.
- Access to **Voodoo Treasure** (missable).
