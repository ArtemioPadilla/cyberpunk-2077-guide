**Tiny Mike** stole some files from a **Militech** lab in the Badlands for **Regina Jones**; Militech traced the hack and has him surrounded in Regina's safehouse, apartment 63 of the **Cortes and Kennedy** residential block (north Kabuki, Watson), with armored vehicles and troops. You have to get him out alive. It is in **set 8 of 8**, the last one: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 7 (Freedom of the Press and Lousy Kleppers). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** get inside the lockdown area → optional: use the emergency stairs → go to the hideout → find Tiny Mike in the bathroom → talk to him → give him the inhaler → optional: take his gun → follow him → escort him to the fixer's transport (optional: through the restaurant) → get in the car with him.

## Getting in

1. Two locals tell you how to reach the fire escape: climb a vending machine and jump from some pipes.
2. On the fire escape, climb to the **second level** instead of going through the open window, and follow the catwalk. Watch out for a **Militech drone** on the ground to the left that can see part of the catwalk, and two soldiers guarding an open apartment.
3. At the end of the catwalk, drop down, keep going to a nearby ladder, climb it and enter the hideout's floor.

## With Tiny Mike

- He's in the **bathroom**. Take the **inhaler** from his backpack (or give him one of yours) so he can move; he won't help you fight.
- In return he lets you take his gun, **Hypercritical**, which sits on the sink.
- Downstairs he offers to open an **exit through the restaurant** next door: it's faster and avoids most guards. Three soldiers may patrol outside, but their routes leave a moment when nobody is watching the restaurant's entrance.
- Get into the car Regina sent with him.

> **Don't leave without:** **Hypercritical**, on the bathroom sink.

## Rewards

- €$9,600, 1,000 XP and 770 Street Cred.
- **Hypercritical** (iconic): shots have a chance to knock enemies down, the last round in the magazine always crits, and it deals +100% Crit Damage.
- Afterwards, Tiny Mike can be found at the Afterlife. This gig is required for **Last Call**, though not for the It's Elementary achievement.
