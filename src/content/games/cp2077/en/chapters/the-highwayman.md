**The Highwayman** is not marked on the map. It starts on **Woodhaven Street**, **Rancho Coronado** East (**Santo Domingo**, Act 2): behind the **Gun-O-Rama** gun shop there is an open garage with a ghost-horse decal to its left. You can also start by finding **Josie Alcazar**'s body first. The steps can be done out of order.

> **Order:** garage behind the Gun-O-Rama → terminal message and photo → James in Japantown → Josie's body in Heywood → confront James → ghost-horse garage in Watson (0214).

## How to do it

1. The garage holds a wrecked Brennan. Read the message on the terminal across the room and pick up the photo of **James Ito** and Josie.
2. **James** is in **Japantown** West: head south on the four-lane waterfront road; past Holly Street, in a plaza on the east side, he sits on the bench from the photo, in front of a 24/7 stand. Talk to him.
3. Go to **Ebunike** metro station in **Heywood**. Scan the trail of dried blood, go past the Drop Point and into the alley on the right: Josie is behind a dumpster. Read her shard **"Bad news"**: she stole a **Tyger Claws** boss's bike and hid it on Maelstrom turf near the All Foods factory in **Northside**, **Watson**.
4. Go back to James and confront him.
5. Across the street from the fast travel point is the ghost horse; at the garage next to it, enter **0214** (the day James and Josie met).

## Choices

- **The first dialogue option, "So you had a choice..."** → peaceful ending: James lives with his guilt.
- **The second option** → James attacks you and you avenge Josie.

You can skip almost everything and just open the garage with the code. If you do it in Act 1, the bike does not reach your garage until Act 2.

## Rewards

The **ARCH Nazaré "Itsumade"** motorcycle. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
