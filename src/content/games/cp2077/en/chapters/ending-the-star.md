The **Panam Palmer** and **Aldecaldos** route. It opens on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop if you finished **Queen of the Highway**: call Panam ("Gonna ask Panam for help" on PowerPyx's path) and take the **Omega Blockers**. It is five jobs: **We Gotta Live Together** (you join the clan), **Forward to Death** (the **Basilisk** panzer assault on Militech's Maglev tunnel construction site), **Belly of the Beast** (into Arasaka Tower and **Adam Smasher**), **Changes** (Mikoshi) and **All Along the Watchtower** (the Badlands epilogue). If you give Johnny the body in Mikoshi, the route ends in [Temperance](../ending-temperance/).

> **Order:** Panam picks you up → camp: Mitch, Saul, Dakota, the tub, nomads (optional, **Amnesty**) → Panam → drone over the site → Basilisk: repair, 11 kills, bridge ×2, gate → on foot: 8 kills, escort, 20 + 13 kills, strike team of 11 → tunnel → SERC → maintenance shaft → security room (shard for Alt) → netrunner's nest → Mikoshi → **Adam Smasher** → **save** → Changes: **the well** → All Along the Watchtower.

> **Get ready:** carry plenty of healing and ammo: Forward to Death chains many Militech waves and Belly of the Beast ends with Smasher. For Smasher, electrical or EMP quickhacks, grenades and several ammo types. **Save as soon as Smasher falls**: from there you can do The Star and Temperance without replaying the job.

## We Gotta Live Together: the camp

At Misty's shop, while you wait, Misty offers a tarot reading. Panam arrives and tells you everyone is waiting at camp.

1. **Follow Mitch** and **talk to Saul** (Mitch, optional).
2. **Talk to Dakota**, who is handing out free weapons in the centre of camp.
3. **Get in the tub**: it's the rite to join the clan.
4. **Talk to Saul** again. Now you can **talk to the nomads (optional)**; these count for the objective:
   - **Dakota Smith**, in the centre of camp.
   - **Saul Bright**, in front of the terminal truck.
   - **Bob, Carol and Teddy**, sitting at a table towards the west. When you get up, **stay until they finish talking among themselves** or it doesn't count; if you left, go back and the talk restarts as long as you haven't moved on to the next required objective.
   - **Cassidy Righter**, shooting bottles at the southeast end.
   - If you did their jobs: **Jake** (Fortunate Son), playing guitar by the big campfire (a unique acoustic take on the Pyramid Song music); **Benedict** (Gig: MIA), by the food truck stairs; **Bruce** (Gig: Goodbye, Night City), in a chair by the weapon dealer's tent; **Anna Hamill** (Gig: Woman of La Mancha), by the water truck at the west edge, only with the Nomad lifepath and if you convinced her to join a clan.
5. **Amnesty:** join Cassidy's target practice. Hit **at least 12 of the 16 bottles in 15 seconds** and he gives you his iconic revolver **Amnesty**. **Crouch and aim before he says go.** No second try is documented if you fail: save before the test.
6. Talk to Panam and join her when you're ready. Reward: the **Aldecaldos rally bolero jacket**.

## Forward to Death: the Maglev tunnel assault

1. Follow Panam, **take the shard from Saul** and use the **drone** to scan the construction site.
2. Talk to Saul and Panam, enter the panzer and follow Saul.
3. **Exit the Basilisk and protect it** during the repair. Get back in.
4. **Eliminate the 11 enemies in the area**, **ram your way through the bridge (2/2)** and **ram the gate**.
5. Exit the Basilisk: **neutralize 8 enemies**, wait for Mitch and Carol and **escort them**.
6. **Neutralize 20 Militech troops**, prepare for reinforcements and **neutralize 13 more**.
7. Wait for the Aldecaldos to regroup, exit the Basilisk and **take out Militech's strike team (11)**.
8. Return to the site and **drive into the tunnel** with the panzer.

## Belly of the Beast: inside Arasaka Tower

1. Talk to Panam, exit the Basilisk, join Mitch and Saul and talk to the Aldecaldos.
2. **Climb onto the SERC** and start it. Talk to Saul and Panam.
3. Get inside the Arasaka building and **look for a maintenance shaft**.
4. **Neutralize the guards** to reach the terminal, wait for the Aldecaldos to regroup and talk to Saul.
5. **Reach the security room** and **slot the shard into the terminal**. Wait for **Alt** and talk to the Aldecaldos.
6. Leave the manufacturing level, reach the **netrunner's nest** and then **Mikoshi**.
7. **Adam Smasher** appears and **crushes Saul's head** before the fight. Defeat him (below).
8. Optional: **decide Smasher's fate**, loot the **Access Token** and find the **Judgement tarot card**, on a wall of the room where you fought. Talk to Panam (optional).
9. **Save.** Go to the Mikoshi access point and connect.

## Boss — Adam Smasher

Since 2.1, Smasher uses a **Sandevistan** and adapts his tactics to your build.

- **Three phases:** close combat (melee and short-range projectiles), long range (with his right half destroyed he keeps his distance) and weakened.
- **Weak point:** the right side of his chest, behind plating you can shoot off. Shooting off his **right arm** (on your left, facing him) ends his melee; then the **back rocket launcher** (PowerPyx tip from before 2.0).
- **Electrical quickhacks**, above all **EMP**, which briefly blocks most of his attacks.
- **Rockets:** they go where you were; always keep moving. The less health he has, the more aggressive.
- Bring revolver, assault rifle and sniper ammo.
- **Fate (optional):** the sources don't document what sparing him changes. To be sure of the Access Token, finish him and loot it.

## Changes: Mikoshi

1. You relive the climb to Misty's 9th floor. **Go to the roof.** If in The Heist you sent Jackie's body to Viktor, you **talk to Jackie** one last time; if not, you **pick up the necklace** with the bullet.
2. Alt recites a poem as you walk towards the beam of light. **Talk to Johnny.**
3. The talk reveals that exposure to the engram has damaged your body beyond repair: Alt lets you go back with about **six months to live** or let Johnny keep the body. If you finished Phantom Liberty, there is extra dialogue about Cynosure or Songbird.

## Choices

- **"Wanna live out whatever life I got left" and enter the well** → V returns to their body → **All Along the Watchtower (The Star)**.
- **"Body's Johnny's. I'll go" and cross the bridge** → V leaves with Alt → **New Dawn Fades (Temperance)**: see [3.5 · Temperance](../ending-temperance/). If you saved after Smasher, reload and do the other one.
- **Rooftop call** (decided in Nocturne) → who waits for you at the epilogue camp.
- **Talking to every nomad** → dialogue only; the Bob, Carol and Teddy table doesn't count if you walk off early.
- **Smasher's fate** → no documented effect.

## All Along the Watchtower: heading south

**Several weeks** pass: Mitch has been in bed all that time after the attack.

1. You wake in Panam's Warhorse at the **Coronado Dam**, with Night City in the background. **Talk to Panam**: V coughs up blood. The clan is leaving for Arizona with the loot, and Panam will use her connections to try to save you.
2. Look at the city as long as you like and **get in the car**. Mitch joins on the way to the **Solar Arrays** (Jackson Plains).
3. **If you called your partner on the rooftop, find out who's waiting:**
   - **Panam:** nobody else; you're already together.
   - **Judy:** Carol brought her to camp and she **leaves with you** and the Aldecaldos.
   - **Kerry:** arrives in a Delamain cab, but **stays**: he can't bring himself to leave Night City. He asks you to give his album a listen before release.
   - **River:** comes on his own, but **stays** with his sister and her kids.
4. Talk to **Cassidy** and **Carol** and **discuss the plan with Mitch and Panam**: use an Aldecaldo smuggling tunnel to dodge Arasaka's checkpoints and the border patrol, then collapse it. With high enough **Body**, V asks about border security (extra dialogue).
5. **Get in the Basilisk** and talk to Panam. **Follow the nomads** through the sandstorm; Cassidy and Carol peel off to draw the drones.
6. Hand the controls to Panam, who rams into the tunnel with the Turbothrusters. **Cross the border.** In the final scene, V sits on top of the Basilisk, looks at the bullet necklace and lets it go over the desert.

## Who lives and who dies

**Bob**, **Teddy**, **Saul**, several Aldecaldos, many Militech and Arasaka employees, most of the Arasaka board, **Adam Smasher** (your call) and **Hanako** (per Yorinobu) die. Final reputation: **100% (Legend)**.

## Credits calls

- **Viktor:** warns you about dust in your implants and asks you to stop by when you're back.
- **Misty:** draws The Chariot, The Lovers and The Sun ("a good life ahead of you in the Badlands").
- **Mama Welles** (with Heroes): horrified that you're leaving with the nomads.
- **Judy:** if she is your partner, a romantic message (the only voicemail with V's own voice); if you did her chain without romance, she thanks you; if not, she says she's thinking of leaving.
- **Panam:** if she is your partner, a romantic message from the sandstorm. **She doesn't call** if your partner is Judy, Kerry or River.
- **Mitch:** talks about storming Arasaka ("we'd walk through hell and back with you").
- **Kerry and River** (if they are your partner): Kerry mocks your nomad life; River mentions the Badlands pictures and promises to visit.
- **Rogue** (with her chain): still alive, asks you to pass a message to Johnny.
- **Takemura** (if you saved him): "rot in hell".
- **Reed** (Phantom Liberty): berates you for refusing the cure (King of Swords or Pentacles) or calls from Dayton (King of Cups).
- **Peralez** (with Dream On): depending on how it ended.

## Rewards and where you return

After the credits you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1** with:

- Legendary **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk** (if you didn't own them).
- **Aldecaldos rally bolero jacket** and **Amnesty** (if you won it).
- **Access Token** (if you looted it): opens Smasher's secret armory on the **DV Ebunike**, with the **Ba Xing Chong** spec.
- The **Judgement** tarot card (if you took it) and some shards.
- **The Star** achievement.

> **Don't leave without:** **Amnesty** (Cassidy's test before following Panam), the **Access Token** and the **Judgement** card from Smasher's room, and a **save after Smasher** to also do Temperance.
