Boxer **Macidew Coolidge** took **Tyger Claws** money to throw a fight, secretly bet on himself, knocked out his opponent and fled. The Tygers kidnapped his coach, **Roh Chi-Won**, and are holding him in the basement of the **Deravaja Dojo** on Brookland St (Little China, Watson). **Regina Jones** asks you to get him out without tripping the alarm. It is in **set 6 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 5 (Last Login and Small Man, Big Evil). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the dojo (optional: don't raise the alarm) → find out where Roh is held → set him free → talk to him → help him outside → escort him to the fixer's transport → talk to him → collect your reward.

## Getting in

- The courtyard is a public area: the guard at the entrance doesn't react while you're just there.
- On the left side of the building there's a side door with a **Body** check. Above it, on the roof, a window with a **Technical Ability** check. Elsewhere on the roof there's a window with **no check**.
- The **basement** has **two cameras**, one Tyger beating Roh and another watching TV in a different room. Neutralize the one beating him and free Roh.

## The way out

Roh moves **very slowly**: wait for him. When you leave, the courtyard guard is gone. At the transport, Roh sits on a bench and asks who paid for his rescue: tell him it was Macidew or keep it secret. If you tell him, he is surprised (Macidew is why he was taken); V advises him not to see Macidew again and Roh agrees.

## Rewards

- €$5,600, 1,000 XP and 647 Street Cred.
- **Bonus at a Drop Point** if you rescue him without tripping the alarm.
