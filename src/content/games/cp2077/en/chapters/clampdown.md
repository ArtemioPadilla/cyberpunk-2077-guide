**Clampdown** arrived with **Update 2.5** (Cyberpunk: Edgerunners 2 content). It is given by **Wakako Okada**, the Westbrook fixer: after **The Heist** and **two of her gigs**, she messages you with an unusual request. A merc called **King** (Weak Kingsley) asked her for help, and Wakako takes no fee out of respect for him. It is not available yet in Act 1.

> **Order:** Wakako's message → BuryGer (Arroyo) → accept King's job → enter the prison within 24 h → escape your cell → Security Cortex → armory → Crisis Control Room → Darius → blow the morgue wall → lose the NCPD → talk to King.

## The contact

After waiting several hours at the **BuryGer** in Arroyo (Santo Domingo), King holo-calls you: **Miguel**, his crew's techie, is locked up and he needs you to break him out of **Night City Prison**. With the **Nomad** lifepath you can ask whether Miguel counts as family.

## Choices

- **Accept** → you go into the prison while King's crew handles security; **Meshuga**, the crew's netrunner, guides you, helped by a spiderbot. **You have 24 hours** to get in; take longer and the job **fails**.
- **Decline** → King **doubles** the pay. Decline **again** and the job **fails**.

## Inside

You go in through the visitor entrance and end up in a cell with a **Reaper chip**, which lets the guards kill you remotely: if you fight them, defeat them before they trigger it. In the Security Cortex, deactivate Miguel's chip and yours (Wex's is optional). Your gear is waiting in the **armory**. In the Crisis Control Room, jack into the chip server or you will face robots and turrets. At the end, defeat **Darius Bale**.

## Rewards

1,200 XP, 2,500 Street Cred, €$5,000–14,000, the **Coronation** iconic revolver, the **Disjunctor** iconic mod and the King set: King's glasses, King's coat, King's bulletproof vest, King's pants and the ICON America Dust Devil utility boots.
