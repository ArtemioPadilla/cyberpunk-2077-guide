**Only Pain** has no giver: you stumble onto it in an alley in **Vista del Rey** (Madison Street, Heywood). Heywood opens in **Act 2**. Three **NCPD** patrolmen (Corey Aldwyn, Keegan Kelly and William Becker) are beating up a man, but things are not what they seem.

> **Order:** talk to Officer Aldwyn → deal with the cops → wake the corpo → talk to him.

## The cops

Talk to **Aldwyn**:

- **Demand they leave him alone** → they turn hostile; defeat them.
- **Turn a blind eye** and walk away.
- **Streetkid lifepath** → you learn the truth. The man is **Nigel Hamilton**, a wealthy corpo who showed an **XBD** to a girl and left her in a coma. She is the daughter of a friend of Aldwyn's, and since Hamilton was going to dodge the charges, Aldwyn was taking justice into his own hands. You can talk them into leaving by offering to deal with Hamilton yourself.

## Hamilton

However you handle the cops, **wake Hamilton and confront him**; if you leave first, the job **fails**. You can let him go right away, or:

- **Cool 5:** demand payment → €$2,280. Then let him go, or with **Body 5** demand **everything** → €$2,660 more.
- **Reflexes 5:** ask him what the cops wanted. He denies it all. Then let him go, or charge him with **Cool 13** (€$2,280) and take everything with **Body 5** (€$2,660 more).
- **Streetkid:** tell him you know about his spiked XBDs. He confesses he showed it to the girl on purpose. Same outcomes: let him go, Cool 13 and Body 5.
- **Killing him** also completes the job. If you kill him after taking "everything", his body still carries more money: he lied to you.

## Rewards

€$2,280 per the infobox; up to €$4,940 if you take everything, plus whatever he carries if you kill him.
