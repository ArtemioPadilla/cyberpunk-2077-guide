**Gun Music** is a minor job for the **Aldecaldos** in **Arroyo**, **Santo Domingo**, at an industrial complex on MLK Boulevard and Valley. It starts on its own shortly after **Riders on the Storm**, Panam's job where you rescue Saul: Cassidy, Carol and Teddy went into the city to deliver cyberware to a group of **Scavengers**. It is Act 2 content: neither Santo Domingo nor the Aldecaldos open while you are in Watson in Act 1. If you start **With a Little Help from My Friends** before doing this job, Gun Music disappears until that job is finished, so do it first.

> **Order:** Taco stops you at the entrance → see what's going on → talk to Roman and the Aldecaldos → intimidate him (Body) or fight → talk to Carol.

## How to do it

As you approach, Aldecaldo **Taco** fills you in. Go in and interrupt the negotiation: **Roman**, the Scavenger leader, complains that the delivery is three days late (the Aldecaldos blame the dust storm). He agrees to take a cut, but then recognizes you: you are the one who wrecked their business at the Scavenger den during **The Rescue**.

If you would rather fight, you can stealth-kill his whole team except Roman before the dialogue ends: he keeps talking as if nothing happened.

## Choices

- **Intimidate (enough Body)** → the Scavengers close the deal and pay the Aldecaldos, who thank you and head back to the Mobile Camp. The wiki does not give the exact check value.
- **Fight** (or without the check) → a gunfight: neutralize the Scavengers and talk to Carol. She is angry with you, but Cassidy calms her down.

Afterwards, Taco and his twin Jeff thank you at the Mobile Camp. If you are romancing Panam and do this job before **Queen of the Highway**, Cassidy later sends you a text about her.

## Rewards

Per the wiki infobox: 1,438 XP and 3,235 Street Cred. No eddies or items.
