Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. **Mr. Hands** texts it to you after **Firestarter**, and it only appears if you **did not pick up your gear** during that job. Some sources list it on Reed's path only; the wiki only requires that you didn't pick up your gear.

> **Order:** Mr. Hands's message → pay €$20,000 → wait a few hours → collect the gear from the Kress Street Hideout locker.

## Getting your weapons back

Mr. Hands tells you one of Hansen's men sold him weapons he recognized as yours. He offers to return your whole arsenal for a finder's fee of **€$20,000**. Gather the money and send him the payment. A few hours later he texts again: your weapons are in the **locker at the Kress Street Hideout**, in Dogtown. Go and take them.

If you don't want to pay, you can ignore it. Johnny, in the journal, suggests it's a good chance to downsize.

## Rewards

- You get back the gear you left in Firestarter.
- It costs €$20,000; the wiki gives no XP or Street Cred.
