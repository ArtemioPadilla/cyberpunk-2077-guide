**Rogue Amendiares** gives you this gig on Carsten St., **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**, in **Act 2**. It opens after El Capitán's **For My Son** and **Going-away Party**. **Vic Vega** runs **C-Team**, a security outfit that corporations hire to break unions and keep workers in line. Rogue is doing it practically pro bono, "for the public good". What she doesn't spell out: she wants him **alive**, and she doesn't want anyone else in the building touched.

> **Order:** get inside C-Team's building → find Vic Vega → neutralize him → (if knocked out) take the body to the marked spot → put him in the fixer's transport.

## Getting in

The building has **no cameras or turrets**. There are four ways in:

- **Front door:** distract the receptionist to grab the access key from her desk.
- **Garage:** two doors that open with **Technical Ability** or **Body**.
- **Open second-floor window**, reached from the garage roof.
- Vega's office window is open too, but it's hard to reach and he faces it.

## The clean route

Go in through the upstairs window, sneak into Vega's office and **take him down**. The easiest exit is to **drop out of his office window** while carrying him: the fixer's transport is a few steps away. Alternatively, carry him downstairs when the guard patrolling the stairs is away, and cross the security office (one guard) to the garage.

## Choices

- **Vega knocked out and nobody else hurt** → Rogue chalks it up as a win: the community has suffered enough.
- **Vega killed, or extra casualties** → Rogue complains or is unenthusiastic. The **pay is the same** in every case.

€$24,100, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
