Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his third set, which opens when you return to Dogtown some time after **Firestarter**. The client is **Nele Springer**, a chemist in the **Crimson Harvest** terror cell who wants out. She asks to meet at the **EBM Petrochem Stadium**.

> **Order:** talk to Nele → enter the factory (code 1701) → production area in the old swimming pool → upload the software to the Net-nuke → leave → meeting point → get in Nele's car → confront the agents.

## The weapons factory

The factory is the **Nighthawks Rehabilitation Center**, Hansen's armory inside the stadium. Use the code **1701** at the entrance and find a way into the factory. There are BARGHEST and 6th Street enemies; pick stealth or combat. Go down to the **production area in the old swimming pool**, find the **Net-nuke** and upload Nele's software. Before you leave, check the **shooting range and storage area**: a cache holds the iconic smart assault rifle **Hercules 3AX**. If you miss it, Herold Lowe sells it later at the stadium.

Leave, go to the meeting point and get in Nele's car. That's when **Biotechnica** agents show up.

## Choices

- **Fight the Biotechnica agents** → Nele survives and you're paid more. If you part on good terms, days later she texts you for **One Way or Another**.
- **Let them take her** → the gig is completed, but you're paid less.

The exact pay doesn't match between sources: the wiki gives €$7,000 if you defend her and €$4,000 if not, but its infobox only lists €$4,000.

## Rewards

- €$4,000-7,000 depending on the choice
- 1,832 XP
- 167 Street Cred
- Hercules 3AX
