**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, the fixer of **Santo Domingo**, hires you for this theft in **Arroyo**, at the **Kendachi factory** on El Camino Rd. Santo Domingo only opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. It is one of the three gigs in his first set (with **Serious Side Effects** and **Error 404**); finishing them opens the second. One of El Capitán's clients is competing with **Matheus Stove** for a director's job at Kendachi and wants dirt: rumor says Stove has ties to the **Valentinos**.

> **Order:** get onto the factory grounds → get inside the building → reach Matheus's office → find the incriminating data on his computer → leave the factory grounds.

## How to do it

It's a theft: expect security guards, veteran guards and robots. The wiki has no detailed walkthrough; what it does say is that the **metal detector** at the entrance can be hacked and turned friendly, which makes walking in armed easier. Go stealthy, take the files from Stove's computer and leave the grounds to close the gig.

## Afterwards

Once it's done, **Matheus Stove**'s body turns up at the **Grand Imperial Mall** in Coastview (Pacifica), with the shard **Archived Conversation: Matheus Stove and Philip Adler**. Pacifica is Act 2 territory too.
