**The Ripperdoc** starts the morning after The Rescue: V's cyberware has been glitching since the last job and, after a call with **Jackie**, you go see **Viktor Vektor**, V's trusted ripperdoc. On the way, Jackie tells you about the next big gig, fronted by the fixer **Dexter DeShawn**, and gives your car back (he used it for a date with his girlfriend, **Misty**). Viktor's clinic is behind **Misty's Esoterica**, in Little China (Watson). It is a quiet job with no combat: you get your first implants here and learn to use the scanner.

> **Order:** talk to Jackie → meet him outside Megabuilding H10 (optional: check your email and stash) → talk to Jackie → call up your car → drive to the ripperdoc → talk to Viktor → take a seat → install the new cyberware → scan Viktor → talk to Viktor.

> **Get ready:** no gear needed. Before heading down, make use of Megabuilding H10: the **2nd Amendment** gun shop (opposite the elevator) gives you **Dying Night** through the job **The Gun**, and **Coach Fred** offers **Beat on the Brat**. Drive carefully to the clinic: **hitting people or property can bring the NCPD**.

## Megabuilding H10

1. **Talk to Jackie** over the holo.
2. Before heading down, optional: **check your email** on the computer and **check your weapons stash**.
3. As you leave the apartment, **Regina Jones** calls, a fixer who will offer you Watson work.
4. To the **left** of the apartment exit there is a **Drop Point**: you can **sell all your junk** ("Sell All Junk") in one go.
5. Inside the building, **The Gun** (Robert Wilson, **2nd Amendment**, opposite the elevator) and **Beat on the Brat** (Coach Fred) are already available.

## The drive to Little China

1. **Meet Jackie** outside H10 and **talk to him** while he finishes his noodles. He tells you about Dex's job and returns the car.
2. **Call up your car** and **get in the driver's seat**.
3. **Drive to the ripperdoc.** Mind the traffic: crashes and hit pedestrians can bring the police.
4. You reach **Misty's Esoterica**: after a short chat with Misty, **head through to the clinic**.

## Viktor's clinic

1. **Talk to Viktor.** V tells him their operating system is malfunctioning and they want to upgrade their eye and hand implants.
2. Viktor asks **€$21,000** for the work. This is the job's choice (see **Choices**).
3. **Take a seat** and **install the new cyberware**.
4. **Scan Viktor.** It is your first use of the **scanner**, linked to the NCPD database.
5. **Talk to Viktor** and head back to Misty's Esoterica. **The Ride** continues there.

**The cyberware you get.** The wiki lists three pieces: **Basic Kiroshi Optics** (eyes), **Ballistic Coprocessor** (hands) and **Subdermal Armor**; the Subdermal Armor and Ballistic Coprocessor pages both say they are obtained here. Sources disagree: PowerPyx names only the Kiroshi optics and the Ballistic Coprocessor, and IGN has you pick the ocular system and the hands. Subdermal Armor is probably a 2.0 addition; check your cyberware screen afterwards to see what 2.5 installed.

**A known bug.** game8 reports that you can get stuck on "Talk to Viktor" with only the "How's biz?" option. If it happens, save and reload.

## Choices

- **"Pay you once the job's done"** → Viktor agrees, but warns it is the last time. This starts the minor job **Paid in Full**: **Viktor's services are locked until you pay him**. **There is no deadline**. When you do come back to pay, Viktor tells you to keep it, but V insists.
- **Pay the €$21,000 now** → no debt and no Paid in Full. PowerPyx notes you will almost never have that much money this early.
- **Lifepath dialogue (Streetkid)** → a unique chat with Jackie about Dex's gang-war past. No mechanical effect.

> **Don't leave without:** selling your junk at the H10 Drop Point and stopping by **2nd Amendment** for **Dying Night** (The Gun). If you owe Viktor, remember you can't buy anything from him until you close **Paid in Full**.

## Rewards

- **Cyberware** installed by Viktor: Basic Kiroshi Optics and Ballistic Coprocessor, plus Subdermal Armor per the wiki (see above).
- **Ignore old advice:** some guides recommend buying **Titanium Bones** or a Microgenerator from Viktor here; those are pre-2.0 suggestions and don't apply as written in 2.5.
