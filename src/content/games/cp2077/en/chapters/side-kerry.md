The chain of **Kerry Eurodyne**, Samurai's old guitarist, has seven jobs: **Holdin' On**, **Second Conflict**, **A Like Supreme**, **Rebel! Rebel!**, **I Don't Wanna Hear It**, **Off the Leash** and **Boat Drinks**. You always reach it through Johnny, after **Chippin' In**: if you gave Johnny his second chance at the Northern Oil Fields grave, it comes after **Blistering Love**; if not, Johnny sends you straight to Kerry. The first three are Johnny's (reuniting Samurai for one last gig); the last four are Kerry and V's, and lead to the romance.

> **For the ending:** Kerry **opens and closes no ending**. His romance needs a **masculine body and masculine voice** (lifepath and genitalia don't matter); with a feminine V, Kerry kindly declines the kiss. You can hold Kerry's and **Panam**'s romances at the same time with no penalty, but on the rooftop in Nocturne Op55N1 you can call only one partner. Kerry waits for V in **The Sun** and **The Devil**; **The Tower** ends every romance except a possible Kerry reunion. The only failure that cuts the chain (and the romance) is **abandoning Kerry in Rebel! Rebel!**.

> **Order:** **Holdin' On:** North Oak sign → Villa Eurodyne (pills by the guitars) → call Nancy → **Second Conflict:** Totentanz → Nancy's car → Denny's mansion (Denny or Henry) → **A Like Supreme:** Nancy's call → Red Dirt at night (guitar case, pills, gig) → **Rebel! Rebel!:** Rancho Coronado Foodscape (12:30–3:30 AM) → Red Peaks (trap, van) → escape the NCPD → coffee → **I Don't Wanna Hear It:** Riot, 7 PM → Us Cracks → **Off the Leash:** Dark Matter → rooftop → **Boat Drinks:** Gold Beach Marina, 7 PM → the yacht _Seamurai_.

> **Get ready:** **Second Conflict depends on The Pickup** (Act 1): if Royce is alive, the Totentanz is peaceful and **Dum Dum** drops the **Doom Doom**; if you killed Royce and didn't free Brick, you fight. **Rebel! Rebel!** only starts between **12:30 and 3:30 AM**; arrive early and wait seated. In the **Rebel! Rebel!** getaway, **stay in Kerry's car** and don't drive it into the water. For **I Don't Wanna Hear It** there are several ways into Riot: the priciest is €$45,000 in tickets; keep Intelligence or Technical Ability handy if you don't want to pay.

## Holdin' On

Johnny sends you to the **North Oak Sign**: he heard Kerry is depressed and attempted suicide. Go into **Villa Eurodyne**; Kerry is in the shower. Around the house you can scan guitars and album covers, and Johnny shows up to comment on Kerry's life.

1. By the **guitars**, take the **pseudoendotrizine** to give Johnny control.
2. Johnny plays; Kerry jumps out of the shower with his revolver, but recognises him by the way he plays. They talk about the past and **agree to reunite Samurai**.
3. Johnny is to get **Nancy Hartley** (Bes Isis) on board; Kerry handles the rest.
4. **Call Nancy.** Her assistant **Steven** answers: she has been missing for two days after going to the **Totentanz** for a story.

Finishing Holdin' On and then A Like Supreme takes Johnny affinity to 70% (it affects no ending).

## Second Conflict

Go to the **Totentanz** (Northside), Maelstrom turf. Go up to the third floor, to the club entrance: the guards recognise you from **The Pickup**, and what happens depends on how that job ended:

- **Royce alive:** **Dum Dum** takes you to Nancy, who is meeting Royce. It is all peaceful; Dum Dum can drop the **Doom Doom**, his iconic revolver.
- **Royce dead and Brick freed:** **Patricia** takes you to Nancy, who is meeting **Brick**.
- **Royce dead and Brick not freed:** Patricia takes you to Nancy, locked up by the gang. **Fight** to get her out.

Leave the Totentanz, get in **Nancy's car** and convince her on the way to join the Samurai gig. She drops you at **N54 News**. Call Kerry.

**Denny's mansion.** The gate is locked and nobody answers the intercom: let yourself in. In the garden, **Henry** has filled **Denny**'s pool with cement from a truck, and they are arguing. You must pick who plays the gig (Johnny has no preference: Denny is a control freak and Henry can't be trusted):

- **Denny:** Henry leaves angry. Kerry tells Denny about Nancy.
- **Henry:** Henry has no problem with Denny, but she throws everyone out. Kerry takes Henry to his car.

**Gold-plated baseball bat (missable):** Denny throws a golden bat into the pool during the scene; **loot it from the pool** before you leave.

## A Like Supreme

1. Wait **a day** for Nancy's call.
2. Go to the **Red Dirt** bar **in the evening** and open the **guitar case**: it is Johnny's old guitar.
3. Take the **pills in the bathroom** and go on stage as Johnny. There are timed extra actions during the song; if you own pieces of Johnny's outfit, they equip automatically.
4. Afterwards, Johnny gives the guitar to Kerry, and Kerry gives V **Archangel**, his iconic revolver (Overture).

## Rebel! Rebel!

Kerry calls to meet you in the small hours at the **Foodscape** in Rancho Coronado (corner of Manzanita and Mallagra). He only shows up between **12:30 and 3:30 AM**; if you are early, sit on the chair and wait.

1. Kerry arrives in a borrowed **Thorton Galena**. Get in. **Us Cracks**, a J-pop group, want to promote their tour with a cover of one of his songs, and Kerry wants to blow up the van carrying their gear.
2. In **Red Peaks** (Badlands), take the gear out of the trunk and lay the **stinger trap** by the bus stop. Hide.
3. The van stops. Force the occupants out: they cooperate and give you the access card. Open the rear doors and **let the occupants go** (the Us Cracks crew).
4. **Blow up the van with a grenade.**
5. NCPD sirens: **get in the car with Kerry** and escape. If the NCPD catch you, V dies in the shootout.
6. Once you lose them, drop Kerry off on Montenegro street (the dialogue says Carnitas) and **accept the coffee** at Capitán Caliente.

**Money:** if you have the coffee, Kerry sends you **€$21,470** a few minutes later. If you decline, he says he'll pay you later.

**Failure that ends the chain:** if you leave the area **without getting into Kerry's car** (or drive the car into water), Kerry texts you angrily and **never contacts you again**. The chain and the romance are over. If you escape with him and then get out and walk off, nothing happens: he goes home on his own and stays in touch.

## I Don't Wanna Hear It

Kerry meets you at **7 PM** at the **Riot** club in Little China, where Us Cracks are. Ways in:

- **Buy tickets** from a fan: **€$45,000**, or **€$23,000** if you intimidate him with the Streetkid option.
- **Scan two VIPs** for their tickets.
- **Hack the VIP door** (Intelligence).
- Use the **west door and ladder** to get the **Employee Badge**.
- Get past the huscle with a **Technical Ability** check.

Inside, find Kerry and Us Cracks. The choice: **hear them out** or **force them to drop the deal** (see "Choices"). If you hear them out, they become friends with Kerry and you take the photo; a **Cool** check lets you take a selfie.

## Off the Leash

Kerry invites you to **Dark Matter** in Japantown. The elevator bouncer says it is "out of order": tell him you are Kerry's guest.

- **If you heard Us Cracks out:** a promo event is under way; Kerry and Us Cracks announce a single together. Kerry introduces you to the press as his "right hand" and then to **Spector Cheng**, who promises you a "friends and family" discount.
- **If you forced them:** Us Cracks broke their promise and are suing Kerry; the club is empty because Kerry closed it.

Go up to the **rooftop** with Kerry and hear him out: he admits what scared him wasn't selling out to the corps but living in someone's shadow again, as with Johnny in Samurai.

**Romance (masculine body and voice).** Lines as quoted by game8:

1. "You can tell me."
2. "But you did make it."
3. "Maybe it's time you stopped being afraid."
4. [Kiss Kerry] "Yes." or [Kiss Kerry] "Shadows'll fade before you."

With a feminine body, Kerry kindly turns the kiss down. Afterwards, two new joytoys are available in the Dark Matter lounge: **Brittany Hayes** and **Logan Scott**.

## Boat Drinks

About **24 hours** after Off the Leash, Kerry meets you at **7 PM** at **Gold Beach Marina**. You board his yacht, the _Seamurai_.

1. On board you can join Kerry in smashing the **coffee machine**, the **painting** and the **record player**, then break the pole.
2. With a masculine body and voice, the **kiss** triggers the romance scene. game8 also recommends helping wreck the yacht and telling him to "pencil you in for a round 2".
3. The boat burns: **swim ashore**.
4. Choose whether to go home with Kerry.

Finishing it gives the achievement **"To Bad Decisions!"** and a memorabilia item, the **record player**, for your apartment.

## Choices

- **Second Conflict — what you did in The Pickup** → Royce alive: peaceful entry and the **Doom Doom** from Dum Dum; Royce dead: Patricia and, if you didn't free Brick, a fight. Siding with Militech in The Pickup prevents the Doom Doom. Already decided when you arrive.
- **Second Conflict — Denny or Henry** → only changes who plays and the final scene; Johnny has no preference.
- **Rebel! Rebel! — leaving without Kerry or driving into the water** → **Kerry cuts contact: end of the chain and the romance. Permanent.**
- **Rebel! Rebel! — accepting the coffee** → you get the **€$21,470** right away.
- **I Don't Wanna Hear It — hearing Us Cracks out** → they become friends with Kerry; photo (selfie with Cool); a promo event in Off the Leash.
- **I Don't Wanna Hear It — forcing them to drop the deal** → no agreement; in Off the Leash they sue Kerry and Dark Matter is empty. The romance stays available.
- **Off the Leash — kissing Kerry** (masculine body and voice) → the romance starts; it completes in **Boat Drinks**.
- **Boat Drinks — going home with Kerry** → closes the chain. If romanced, Kerry waits for you in The Sun and The Devil.

> **Don't leave without:** the **gold-plated baseball bat** from Denny's pool and the **Doom Doom** from Dum Dum (Second Conflict; if you miss it, Herold Lowe sells the Doom Doom in Phantom Liberty), **Archangel** (A Like Supreme) and the **coffee with Kerry** at the end of Rebel! Rebel!.
