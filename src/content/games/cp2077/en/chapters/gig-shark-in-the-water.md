**Blake Croyle**, a loan shark backed by the **Animals**, used an unpayable debt to seize one of **Roger Wang**'s stores, a clinic at Aoba on O'Malley Place St (Kabuki, Watson). Wang paid **Regina Jones** instead of paying Blake. It is in **set 4 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the three gigs of set 3 (Backs Against the Wall, Woman of La Mancha and Catch a Tyger's Toe). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the store Blake seized → find Blake → neutralize him → if you keep him alive, carry him and put him in the fixer's transport → leave.

## How to do it

- Blake has Medium Subdermal Armor, Charge Jump and flashbangs, and several Animals watch his back.
- The area holds two shards with conversations between Blake and his people.

## Choices

- **Knock him out** and deliver him to the transport → Regina is terse on the final call.
- **Kill him** → Regina sounds much happier: she says she and the client are very pleased.

The wiki shows no difference in pay between the two.

## Rewards

- €$2,600, 1,000 XP and 590 Street Cred.
