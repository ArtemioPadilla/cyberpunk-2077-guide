**Psycho Killer** is the tracker for **Regina Jones**'s **17 Cyberpsycho Sightings**; Regina is the Watson fixer who works out of Kabuki. She calls you after **The Ripperdoc**, and the job activates after your first talk with Jackie in **The Ride**. Regina studies cyberpsychosis and wants the cyberpsychos **alive**, but it isn't required: the final reward comes even if you kill some. No sighting is lost before the game's final point of no return.

> **Order:** go to a sighting → neutralize the cyberpsycho → search the area for information → send it to Regina → repeat until all 17 → meet Regina.

## How each sighting works

1. As you approach the area, Regina calls with what she knows.
2. **Neutralize** the cyberpsycho. To keep them alive: blunt weapons (Gorilla Arms and fists count), the **Pax** mod or **System Collapse**. Once they drop, **don't hit them again**: even with Pax, hitting an unconscious target kills it.
3. **Search the area** for the shard, laptop or computer with their story and **send the information to Regina**. The sighting doesn't close without it.
4. Each Watson sighting pays €$2,300–2,900, 293–369 Street Cred and 300 XP; the five in Act 1 come to about €$13,000.

## Act 1 sightings (Watson)

- **Demons of War** (Kabuki, the unfinished highway bridge; needs **The Pickup**) → entry **Matt Liaw**.
- **Lt. Mower** (Kabuki, the flooded plaza at Allen St and Abraham St) → entry **Lt. Mower**. Lure her to the arena entrance, out of the water, and chain quick melee hits with **Dying Night**.
- **Six Feet Under** (Northside, the abandoned train station) → entry **Lely Hein**.
- **Ticket to the Major Leagues** (Little China, the Bodyweight warehouse) → entry **Alec Johnson**.
- **Where the Bodies Hit the Floor** (Northside, behind Totentanz) → entry **Ellis Carter**.

## Act 2 sightings

- **Bloody Ritual** (Watson): after **Playing for Time**, and only from 8 PM to 5 AM → entry **Zaria Hughes**.
- **The Wasteland** (Badlands): after **Life During Wartime**.
- No extra requirement, as soon as the city opens: Discount Doc and Under the Bridge (Santo Domingo), House on a Hill and Second Chances (Badlands), Letter of the Law and Seaside Cafe (Heywood), Lex Talionis and Smoke on the Water (Pacifica), On Deaf Ears and Phantom of Night City (City Center).

The cyberpsycho in the gig **Occupational Hazard** (Anna Nox) **doesn't count** for this job.

## Rewards

Once all 17 are closed, Regina meets you in person; what she says depends on whether you kept them alive. The reward cache holds, according to the wiki:

- The **Pax** weapon mod (makes any weapon non-lethal).
- A tier 4 or 5 **Cyberware Capacity Shard** (tier 5 needs level 25 or higher).
- The **Trauma Team armor-weave tactical vest**.
- **Chrome Compressor** (an Operating System that adds Cyberware Capacity): sources disagree. The job's infobox lists it, but the implant's own page only gives Airdrops and Dogtown ripperdocs as sources, and there is a reported 2.3 bug (PC, unconfirmed) where it isn't given.
