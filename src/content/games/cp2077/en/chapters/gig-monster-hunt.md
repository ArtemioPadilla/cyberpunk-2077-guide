**Regina Jones** sends you to the **Ho-Oh** club on Allen St (Kabuki, Watson) for **Jotaro Shobo**, a Tyger Claw who records himself torturing joytoys and sells the braindances. **The Mox** tracked him down, but Kabuki is a no-go zone for them. It is in Regina's **set 1 of 8**, the first she offers: her gigs come in sets that unlock one after another, and the next one appears once you finish every gig in the current set. According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the Ho-Oh club → find Jotaro → neutralize him → leave the club (or carry him to the fixer's transport).

## Getting in

- Jotaro is on the **top floor**, and the club is full of **Tyger Claws**. There is a **turret** on the **third floor**.
- With enough **Technical Ability** you can enter the building across the street: go through a gated alley, climb the fire escape and, from the adjacent balcony, scout or take out Jotaro.
- Scan when you arrive to spot the guards and the turret.

## Choices

- **Kill him** → leave the club and get paid.
- **Knock him out** → carry him out of Ho-Oh to the fixer's car, on a street behind the club. He goes to the Mox and you get a small bonus and the jacket.
- If you finish it before **Automatic Love**, in that job you can threaten Woodman with Jotaro's fate.

## Rewards

- €$2,800, 1,000 XP and 647 Street Cred.
- Optional: **Denki-shin thermoset hybrid crystaljock bomber** (for delivering him alive).
