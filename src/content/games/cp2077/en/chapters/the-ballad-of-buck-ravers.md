**The Ballad of Buck Ravers** starts at **Cherry Blossom Market** in Japantown (Westbrook), when V comes across a street musician playing guitar and **Johnny Silverhand** asks whether you like his music. You need Johnny and access to Westbrook, so it is an **Act 2** job.

> **Order:** talk to Johnny → go to the Rainbow Cadenza club → talk to Johnny → ask the staff → talk to the cook → find the vendor selling the tapes → persuade him → talk to Johnny.

## Rainbow Cadenza

Johnny remembers that bootlegs of **Samurai** concerts might still exist, and that **Rainbow Cadenza**, a club they used to play, is nearby. It is a food joint now. Go in and ask the staff: they point you to an old vendor at a nearby stall who keeps asking them about the club.

## Karim Noel

The vendor is **Karim Noel**, a Samurai superfan. He talks about the band for a good while before you can ask about the bootlegs, and he only sells them to you if you convince him you are a true fan:

- **Default:** Karim quizzes you; ask Johnny for help with the answers.
- **Corpo lifepath:** tell him the Arasaka legend about the ghost who splits in two any newbie who misses a deadline (the ghost is Johnny). You skip the quiz.
- **Enough Cool:** say you have been listening to "Chippin' In" since you were a kid; he gives you the recording too. The wiki does not give the exact value.

If you are wearing the **Replica of Johnny's Samurai Jacket**, Karim comments on it.

## Wrap-up

With the **SAMURAI Concert Recording** in hand, Johnny talks about Karim: he knows every lyric, but never understood that Johnny was calling for change, and he is still stuck in the 2020s.

## Rewards

The **SAMURAI Concert Recording**, a quest item (despite its description, it sells for next to nothing). The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred.
