**There Is A Light That Never Goes Out** only starts if, in **Sinnerman**, you talked to **Joshua Stephenson** and got in the NCPD SUV. It's an **Act 2** job set in **Rancho Coronado** (Santo Domingo). Joshua asks you to stick with him for the day and promises you the same pay you'd have got for killing him. Along for the ride are Lieutenant **Vasquez**, who drives, and **Rachel Casich**, from a braindance studio (Fourth Wall Studios). If you ask to be dropped off at the next stop, Joshua won't let you: he tells you the money will be worth it.

> **Order:** talk to Joshua → ride along → follow him to the El-Ahmar house → follow Zuleikha and talk to her → stay or leave → back to the SUV → talk to Rachel → PieZ: refuse or take the bribe → sit at the table and talk to Joshua.

## What you tell Joshua matters

Throughout the chain, how you talk to Joshua changes the outcome of **They Won't Go When I Go**. **Supportive** lines and **honest questions** build his confidence; **criticizing his plan** or **steering the talk away from religion** fills him with doubt. If you want the best pay at the end, be respectful and curious.

## The El-Ahmar house

On the way, Joshua says he was sentenced twelve years ago for armed robbery, among other things, and that he killed several people in cold blood. In prison he had time to think about how to make amends. At the house, **Zuleikha El-Ahmar** greets you: Joshua killed her brother Ruben, but she wrote to him in prison to forgive him, and her letters led Joshua to become a Christian. Here he reveals the plan: Rachel will record his **crucifixion** as a braindance, and he hopes it will inspire people to convert. Then **Gloria**, the mother, arrives; she hasn't forgiven him and throws you both out.

## PieZ

If you stay, Rachel talks to you alone: she thinks Joshua is nuts, but his sincere faith makes for better braindances, and your "probing questions" make him doubt. At the **PieZ** restaurant, before going in, she offers you **double Joshua's pay** to walk away. Johnny objects.

## Choices

- **Leave after talking to Zuleikha** → the job ends and you lose **They Won't Go When I Go**.
- **Refuse the bribe** → you go in, sit at the table with Joshua, Rachel and the clients and keep talking until Rachel gets fed up and storms out. Joshua says goodbye. The pay arrives shortly after, and **a day later** Rachel calls you for the crucifixion (**They Won't Go When I Go**).
- **Take the bribe** → Rachel says goodbye to Joshua for you and later transfers **€$4,600**. You lose the last job of the chain.
- **Ask for more (Corpo lifepath only)** → you tell her you know what a braindance budget looks like; Rachel grudgingly agrees and pays **€$9,300**. You also lose the last job.

## Rewards

- 1,347 XP and 2,021 Street Cred.
- **€$4,600 to €$9,300**, depending on your choice.
