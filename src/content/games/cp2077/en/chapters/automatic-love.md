**Takemura** gives it after Tom's Diner: you need to find **Evelyn Parker**, who got you into the Konpeki heist, in case she knows anything about the Relic. It starts at **Lizzie's Bar** (Kabuki, Watson), where **Judy Álvarez** is waiting, and continues at **Clouds**, the dollhouse on the 12th floor of **Megabuilding H8**, **Tyger Claws** territory. What matters is how you handle the talk with **Woodman** (Oswald Forrest), the dolls' "caretaker": it decides what happens later in Judy's chain.

> **Order:** call Judy (optional) → Lizzie's Bar, Judy's workshop → take the cigarette case → leave Lizzie's → Megabuilding H8 → ride up to Clouds in the evening → reception: jack in and deposit your weapons → pick a doll (Angel or Skye) → (optional) booth 11 → get into the VIP area → find Tom → Woodman's office → interrogate him → talk to Johnny → collect your weapons → ground floor.

> **Get ready:** Clouds **takes all your weapons** at the door. If you fear the Woodman talk will go wrong, remember you'd be fighting with fists or melee weapons (there are some, iconics included, in the restricted area); there's also a **Liberty** pistol behind the reception counter. High **Intelligence**, the **Corpo** lifepath, or having done **Gig: Monster Hunt** first (the Jotaro Shobo business) all help you get Woodman to talk without a fight. Save before entering his office.

## Lizzie's Bar and Judy

You can call Judy first (optional). At **Lizzie's Bar** go to **Judy's workshop**: she doesn't know where Evelyn is, but sends you to **Clouds**, where she used to work. **Take Evelyn's cigarette case** when the objective asks.

**Lizzie**, the iconic Militech M-76e Omaha, is in the **private room next to Judy's workshop**. It has been here since patch 1.5 (it used to sit near the Clouds server room). Grab it before you leave.

## Clouds: reception and dolls

Go to **Megabuilding H8** and take the elevator up to Clouds. It opens **in the evening**: the wiki says 5 pm and game8 says "after 6pm"; if you arrive early, sit on the nearby bench to wait.

1. **Jack in at reception** and **deposit your weapons** in the locker.
2. Pick a doll: **Angel** (booth 6) or **Skye** (booth 9).
3. In the booth, you can cut the session short with the **safe word** or let the doll do the "soul-search". If you use the safe word, you can ask about Evelyn: the doll sends you to **Tom** in the VIP area.

**Booth 11 (optional).** It's Evelyn's. Examine the **missing port** on the wall by the bed, the **blood** on the opposite wall, and **check the security feed**: it shows the client didn't hurt her; Evelyn glitched out in what looks like a remote hack by a netrunner. Johnny has lines here.

## Getting into the VIP area

Entering the VIP area **without an access card makes the Tyger Claws hostile**. Three clean ways in:

- **The bathroom card:** wait outside for a while; a Tyger Claw walks into the nearby bathroom. Knock him out quietly and take his card.
- **The hallway shutter:** in the hallway between the ring of booths and the VIP door there's a shutter onto an **exterior walkway**. Follow it round to a backroom.
- **The Body door:** the same backroom opens from the bathroom area by forcing a door with **Body**.

That backroom holds **a single Tyger Claw** (knock him out and keep his card) and an **elevator** to the VIP area's backroom, which lets you in even without a card. Careful: guards in the main room spot you if you linger in front of the glass window, but they won't comment if you walk out of the backroom into the VIP lounge.

## Tom and the restricted area

Enter **Tom's** booth. He tells you Evelyn had an accident there and ended up at a "special clinic in Oslo", and sends you to **Woodman**, whose office is in a restricted part of the building. On the way there are several side rooms worth checking:

- **Dolls' dressing room:** on the counter of the **first cubby by the door** is **Cocktail Stick**, the iconic katana. Evelyn's jacket is there too (scan only; Johnny comments).
- **Server room** near Woodman's office: the **computer** has information on the accident, and the **"Schedule changes/incident report"** shard is there.
- **Locker room:** the **Evelyn's Schedule** shard and **Evelyn's Handbag**.

## Woodman

Woodman isn't hostile when you walk in, but some answers set him off. Ways to get the information **without a fight**:

- **Intelligence:** offer advice on securing his system better.
- **Corpo:** talk about the Tyger Claws' real bosses.
- **Gig: Monster Hunt done:** mention what happened to Jotaro Shobo; Woodman gives up the information on the spot.
- **With none of the above**, pick this chain:
  1. "You don't understand what's at stake."
  2. "Let's not make this harder than it has to be."
  3. "One way or another, I'll find out..."

He admits he **sold Evelyn to the ripperdoc Fingers** when he couldn't help her. If he turns hostile, defeat him and read the message on his computer: you reach the same lead. Then talk to Johnny, leave, **collect your weapons from the locker** and go down.

## Choices

- **Woodman, the easy way** (Intelligence, Corpo, Monster Hunt or the line chain) → he gives you the information and survives. Even so, **he blacklists you from Clouds**, even if you were polite or paid him. Later, in **Ex-Factor** (Judy's chain), you can take revenge on him with Judy.
- **Woodman, the hard way** (you kill him) → the information comes from his computer. **You lose the Ex-Factor revenge branch** and Maiko is more hostile in that job.
- **Leaving him alive** keeps the most content: you can still kill him with Judy in Ex-Factor if you want.
- **Angel or Skye, safe word or not** → only changes the scene.
- **Entering VIP without a card** → the Tyger Claws turn hostile. If the whole area is on alert, you can no longer talk to Woodman and have to fight him (without your weapons).

## Boss — Woodman (if he turns hostile)

He's not a boss with his own bar, but he's a nuisance because you have no guns.

- He uses **Break Hold**, **Combat Stim** and **Charge Jump**; he has **Subdermal Armor** and a **Malorian Overture**.
- Go for the melee weapons in the restricted area (Cocktail Stick works) or fight with your fists.
- Afterwards, read the message on his computer for the Fingers lead.

## What opens here

- **The Space in Between:** next in the Evelyn line, on Jig-Jig Street.
- **Tapeworm:** per the wiki and IGN, finishing Automatic Love triggers a Tapeworm stage (game8 leaves it out of the triggers; sources disagree).
- **Judy's chain** (Both Sides, Now → Ex-Factor → Talkin' 'bout a Revolution → Pisces → **Pyramid Song**): it opens later, but what you do with Woodman here decides whether the Ex-Factor revenge exists.

> **Don't leave without:** **Lizzie** (room next to Judy's workshop), **Cocktail Stick** (first cubby of the dressing room), the server-room and locker-room shards, and **your weapons** from the locker. If you miss both iconics, Herold Lowe sells them in Phantom Liberty.

## Rewards

- **XP and Street Cred:** the wiki gives 1609 XP and 552 Street Cred; game8 gives 1102 XP, 552 Street Cred and 484 eddies (sources disagree on the XP).
- **Lizzie** and **Cocktail Stick** (if you pick them up).
