Some ripperdocs want a pack of **stolen meds** back, and the GPS tracer hidden inside shows where it is: a run-down housing block on **Columbus St** (Kabuki, Watson). It is in **Regina Jones**'s **set 3 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the three gigs of set 2 (Troublesome Neighbors, Welcome to America, Comrade and Dirty Biz). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the indicated residence → find the stolen meds → retrieve them → deposit them in the Drop Point.

## Finding the meds

1. Go in and head for the **room furthest back** from the entrance.
2. Climb up through the collapsed part of the ceiling to the second level and open the **only door on the left**.
3. **Corporal William Hare** is waiting there: ex-Militech, broken by the war and fixated on killing the mayor.

## Choices

- **Talk him down**, with a **Body** check or by convincing him the meds are useless to him → he lets you take them, but kills himself as soon as you leave the room.
- **Any other answer**, or taking too long to reply (there is no visible timer) → he turns hostile and you have to kill him or knock him out.

Then take the meds to the nearby Drop Point.

## Rewards

- €$6,400, 1,000 XP and 770 Street Cred.
