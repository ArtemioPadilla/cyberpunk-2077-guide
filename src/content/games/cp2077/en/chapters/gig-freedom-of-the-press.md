**Max Jones**, a journalist and old friend of **Regina Jones** (they aren't related), exposed a corporate scam against war veterans and someone put a hit out on him. He won't take Regina's calls, so she sends you to the **old WNS broadcast station** at Drake Ave and Pershing St (Northside, Watson) to talk him round. It is in **set 7 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 6 (Bloodsport and Scrolls before Swine). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** enter the TV studio building → find Max → convince Max to meet with Regina → escort Max outside → leave the area → collect your reward.

## The studio

- Max is expecting a hitman and has filled the building with **mines and turrets**. Scan before every step.
- When he spots you, he plays the veterans' recordings over the sound system. Approach calmly: he is ready to end his own life if pushed.

## Talking him round

Each lifepath has its own pitch, but Max turns them all down: he won't quit journalism. What works is **appealing to his curiosity**: he has no idea what Regina is up to now or what she fights for. Then he gathers his files and follows you to the car. Afterwards he shows up at Regina's Den, annoyed but alive.

## Rewards

- €$10,800, 1,000 XP and 906 Street Cred; per the wiki, Regina adds a generous bonus for bringing him back alive.
