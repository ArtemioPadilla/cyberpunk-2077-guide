**An Inconvenient Killer** is a gig from **Dino Dinovic**, the **City Center** fixer. It takes place at **7th Hell**, a seedy club on the corner of Corporation Street and Ringroad West in **Downtown**, guarded by the **Animals**. It belongs to Dino's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **A Lack of Empathy**. **Jack Mausser**, one of Dino's mercs, hit a Zetatech transport and killed everyone. The corp is hunting him and Dino wants to hand them his head. Mausser has holed up in his club's VIP area. Dino pays more if there is no combat.

> **Order:** get inside 7th Hell → find Mausser (optional: no combat in the club) → reach the VIP area → neutralize him or talk to him → (if knocked out) carry him to the fixer's car → leave the club → collect your reward from the Drop Point.

## Getting in

- **Front door:** the bouncers charge **€$500**.
- **Elevator on the other side:** opens with **Technical Ability** (15 according to the gig page; the club page says 9).
- **Streetkid:** V can bluff the bouncer by saying they are there to recruit talent for illegal braindances.

## Reaching the VIP area

The VIP staircase is guarded. On the second floor, past the security room, a ladder climbs to the scaffolding over the DJ booth; cross it to reach the VIP area without passing the guard.

## Choices

- **Reach him undetected and talk** → you can convince Mausser to leave Night City; Dino is impressed. You can also knock him out after convincing him.
- **If the talk goes wrong**, Mausser turns hostile and you have to neutralize him.
- **Kill him or knock him out:** if he is unconscious, carry him to the fixer's car.

If you do not neutralize him and you have Phantom Liberty, the wiki says he later shows up on a balcony in the Longshore Stacks, in Dogtown.

## Rewards

- €$7,120 (plus the no-combat bonus).
