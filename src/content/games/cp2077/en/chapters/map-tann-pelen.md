It starts on its own when **Double Life** ends: you know the **Voodoo Boys** are behind it all, but they're a **Pacifica** netrunner gang that doesn't talk to outsiders. You call **Mr. Hands**, Pacifica's fixer, and he finds you a contact. It's a short talking job in **Coastview**. The title is Haitian Creole: "M'ap tann pèlen" means "I am waiting for the trap", the name of a song of the Bizango secret society.

> **Order:** call Mr. Hands → pursue other leads until he calls back → talk to Mr. Hands → go to the chapel (Pacifica Serenity) → meet your contact at the altar → go to the butcher shop → ask the vendor for Placide → look into the camera → find Placide in the back of the shop → talk to him → follow him → sit → talk to Placide.

> **Get ready:** there's no combat. The only thing to keep in mind is that **Mr. Hands takes a while to call back**: use the time to push the other lines (Hellman or Takemura) or side jobs. Calling him early also helps Judy's chain, because **Both Sides, Now** doesn't appear until you've made that call.

## The call to Mr. Hands

**Call Mr. Hands** as soon as you leave Judy's apartment. He warns you it's nearly impossible: the Voodoo Boys keep to themselves. He'll try anyway. The objective becomes **"pursue other leads until Mr. Hands calls back"**.

When he calls, he brings the expected news: they're not interested in what you have. But as it happens they have a **job that needs doing**, and Mr. Hands volunteered you: do it right and you'll earn an audience with their leader, **Brigitte**. **Talk to Mr. Hands** to end the call.

## The chapel

Go to the **chapel** at **Pacifica Serenity** in Coastview and **meet your contact at the altar** (Alexandre Batisma). He sends you to the butcher shop.

## Rolland's butcher shop and Placide

Go to **Rolland's Butcher Shop**:

1. **Ask the vendor for Placide** (John Milard).
2. **Look into the camera** when asked.
3. **Find Placide in the back of the shop** and **talk to him**.
4. **Follow him** and **sit**.
5. **Talk to Placide.** He's the Voodoo Boys' second-in-command, despises outsiders and makes no secret of using you. The job: get into the **Grand Imperial Mall**, where the **Animals** have dug in, and find out what they're up to.

**Butcher's Cleaver**, the iconic cleaver, **stays behind after Placide butchers the chicken**. Grab it before leaving: the wiki and the weapon page confirm you can loot it.

## Choices

- **There are no story-changing choices** in this job.
- **When you call Mr. Hands** → the sooner you call, the sooner **Both Sides, Now** (Judy's chain) opens.
- This line's big choice comes in **I Walk the Line**, with the NetWatch agent. Start thinking about which side you want: Placide treats you as a "ranyon" (a disposable rag), and that weighs on the choice.

## What opens here

- **I Walk the Line:** Placide's job at the Grand Imperial Mall.
- **Both Sides, Now** (Judy): available after Double Life **and** the call to Mr. Hands. It's the door to the chain that ends in **Pyramid Song**.

> **Don't leave without:** **Butcher's Cleaver**, which Placide leaves behind after killing the chicken. If you miss it, Herold Lowe sells it in Phantom Liberty.

## Rewards

- game8 lists no rewards for this job. The only thing you take away is the **Butcher's Cleaver**.
