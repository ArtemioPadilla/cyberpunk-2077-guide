**Meetings Along The Edge** is the fourth and last session with the **Zen Master**. He is in **Jackson Plains**, in the Badlands: just south of **Muamar Reyes**'s usual spot, on a west-facing cliff off the paved road. It opens when you finish **Poem Of The Atoms** (Act 2).

> **Order:** approach the Zen Master → talk to him → choose how much to pay → do the meditation → take the altar → talk to Johnny.

## The session

You can open the conversation with several questions, but he only answers one, and he may hint that he knows about Johnny. Then he offers the meditation on his usual terms: pay **nothing** or **€$610**. The meditation is about the element of **air**. When it ends, the monk vanishes.

## What he leaves

A **shard**, his **robes** and his **altar necklace** are left behind. Take the **altar**: it is a decoration for your apartment, hanging from the shelf above your bed. Johnny is sitting next to the robes; from what he says, he never saw the Zen Master, and he suggests you tell **Misty** about the experience.

> **Don't leave without:** the altar and the shard the monk leaves behind.

## Afterwards

Finishing the job opens a new dialogue option with **Misty**.

## Rewards

The **Zen Master's altar** (memorabilia for your apartment). The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred.
