**Takemura** gives it after Tom's Diner, though going to **Rogue** is Johnny's idea: you're looking for **Anders Hellman**, the Relic's creator. At the **Afterlife** (Little China), Rogue, Night City's top fixer and Johnny's ex-lover, charges you for the lead. The job introduces **Panam Palmer**, a nomad who needs help getting her truck back, and ends at the **Sunset Motel**. It's the door to Panam's whole chain, which decides **The Star** ending.

> **Order:** go to the Afterlife → talk to Rogue → pay €$15,000 → wait until she's ready → talk to her (shard) → call Panam → Rancho Coronado container station → ride with her to the nomad camp → Mitch and Scorpion → load the box → Rocky Ridge → set up the trap → wait for the Raffen Shiv on the roof → turn on the traffic lights → recover the truck → decide about Nash → Sunset Motel → sit at the bar with Panam.

> **Get ready:** you need **€$15,000**. If you don't have it, the objective asks you to collect it before returning to Rogue: do gigs or sell gear. If you plan to help Panam with Nash, bring a **shotgun** or a strong melee weapon (game8). Save before the talk with Panam where you decide whether to go after Nash.

## The Afterlife and Rogue

Go to the **Afterlife** and **talk to Rogue** (wait for her to finish her conversation). When you ask about Hellman, she wants **€$15,000**.

1. **Collect €$15,000** if you're short and **return to Rogue** with the money.
2. **Wait until Rogue is ready** and **sit next to her**.
3. She gives you a **shard**: a **Kang Tao AV** is carrying an anonymous passenger, presumably Hellman. To bring it down you need a driver: **Panam Palmer**. Rogue warns you that in return you'll have to help her get her vehicle back.

## Panam and the camp

**Call Panam** and meet her at the **Rancho Coronado Container Freight Station**. You explain the situation; she demands to know where her truck is (the **Thorton Mackinaw "Warhorse"**), and V tells her it's in **Rocky Ridge**. Her former partner, **Nash**, stole it with the cargo and plans to sell it there. Panam calls her client, **Boz** (of 6th Street), to set up a meeting.

**Take the passenger seat** and **go to the nomad camp** of the Aldecaldos. There you meet **Mitch** and **Scorpion**: Saul has ordered them to watch the generators, so they can't come, but Mitch offers you any gear you need. **Follow Panam**, **take the box**, **put it in the trunk** and **go back to the car**.

## Rocky Ridge: the trap

**Go to Rocky Ridge with Panam**, **leave the car** and **discuss the plan**. Panam wants to use the substation to confuse the Raffen Shiv with the intersection's traffic lights.

1. **Scan the devices in the area** (3), the **transformer** and the **power source**.
2. **Wait for Panam by the power substation.**
3. **Take the jumper cables** and **connect them to the battery**.
4. **Restore power in the switchgear:** **break the window and enter**, or force the door with **Body 8** (game8).
5. **Talk to Panam**, **go to the roof** and **get into position**.
6. **Wait for the Raffen Shiv** and **turn on the lights at the intersection**.

**Afterwards:**

- **If no combat starts:** go to the meeting point.
- **If it does:** **defeat the seven Raffen Shiv** (optional: take down the one by Panam's truck), **get the key** to the truck, get in and **wait for Panam**.

**Switch seats with Panam** and **talk to her**: this is where you decide about Nash (see "Choices").

## Nash (optional)

If you choose to help her, you head to the Raffen Shiv hideout at the **Corp-Bud Mine** to **defeat Nash and his people**.

- Take out the **two netrunners** first: they use **Overheat** (game8).
- Nash uses **Combat Stim**, **Sandevistan Mk.2**, **Kerenzikov**, **Charge Jump** and **Biohazard Grenades**.
- When he falls, take his **Widow Maker** and use it on the reinforcements (game8).
- **Join Panam**, wait for her to finish talking and **take the passenger seat**.

## Sunset Motel

**Go to the Sunset Motel with Panam** and **wait for her to strike the deal** with Boz. **Leave the car**, **follow her**, **sit at the bar** and **talk to Panam**. You order from the bartender, **Noah**; Panam tells you the package was **Betahaloperidol** (drugs for cyberpsychos) and that she hopes to start fresh in Night City. You rent rooms for the night.

## Choices

- **Help Panam kill Nash** → you raid the hideout, Nash dies and you take the **Widow Maker**. Rogue mildly scolds you. game8 says it earns romance points for a masculine V.
- **Refuse** ("that wasn't the deal") → Rogue texts approval of your professionalism and gifts you the **Archer Quartz "Bandit"**. Nash survives... until **Queen of the Highway**, where he attacks the camp, dies and drops the **Widow Maker** there.
- **Does it affect the Panam romance?** Sources disagree: the wiki says not helping Panam changes neither the job nor her chain, and its romance pages list no Nash requirement; game8 says skipping Nash locks you out of the romance. **If you want to romance Panam, help her to be safe.**
- **Fighting the Raffen in Rocky Ridge or not** → the wiki says it changes neither the outcome nor Panam's chain.

## What opens here

- **Lightning Breaks:** Panam asks to meet at midnight by the Sunset Motel. It's next in the Hellman line.
- **Panam's chain:** the relationship starts here and continues after Life During Wartime with **Riders on the Storm** → With a Little Help from My Friends → **Queen of the Highway**, which unlocks **The Star** ending. The romance needs a **masculine body** (any voice).
- **Rogue:** what she thinks of you changes with the Nash choice; her own chain comes with **Chippin' In** after Tapeworm.
- **Phantom Liberty:** if you've already finished **Black Steel in the Hour of Chaos**, V can mention having taken out a MaxTac convoy.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Widow Maker** if you went after Nash (or remember it for Queen of the Highway if not), and the **Archer Quartz "Bandit"** if you refused. game8 also mentions a free **Rayfield Caliburn** that appears later in the tunnel past where Nash dies, possibly after leaving and skipping up to three days (unconfirmed). Herold Lowe sells the Widow Maker in Phantom Liberty if you miss it.
