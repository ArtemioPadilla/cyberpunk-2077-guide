Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his third set, which opens when you return to Dogtown some time after **Firestarter**; according to the wiki (unverified), if Run This Town is active you have to finish it first. The client is **Michael Maldonado**, under **The Needle**.

> **Order:** talk to Michael → Heavy Hearts club → find out where Georgina is → VIP 06 → decide → return to Michael.

## The club

Michael says D.A. **Georgina Zembinsky** and her partner **Hank Davis** tortured him into testifying against his son Eric. Mr. Hands asks you not to cause trouble: the club is his base.

- **Bartender** → sends you to **Jack** in the restroom; pay him **€$550** (or convince him with the **Streetkid** lifepath) and he says VIP 04.
- **Waitress** → also tells you VIP 04.
- **Dressing room** (high **Technical Ability**) → its computer has the guest list: VIP 06.
- In VIP 04, a man tells you Georgina is in **VIP 06**.

VIP 06 holds the iconic **Crimestopper** pistol (Tsunami Kappa) in a carry case. If you miss it, Herold Lowe sells it at the EBM Petrochem Stadium.

## Choices

Georgina says she needs Eric in prison to catch Hector Sacristán, of the Valentinos, and that they didn't torture Michael. She offers to anonymize the testimony.

- **Anonymize it** → Michael is angry, but pays €$2,000 when you press him. Mr. Hands isn't happy; he pays €$6,000 if the alarm didn't go off.
- **Make her delete it** → with high **Body** you threaten Hank and he leaves; otherwise, he fights. Michael, happy, pays €$2,000; Mr. Hands, €$6,000 with no alarm or €$4,000 with it.

## Rewards

- €$4,000-8,000 in total
- 1,832 XP
- 167 Street Cred
- Crimestopper
