This page closes the **Reed path**. In **Leave in Silence** you and Reed drive Songbird's body to a **border checkpoint** in the Badlands, where a medical team and President **Myers** are waiting. Then you talk with Reed and the **Phantom Liberty credits** roll. In **Four Score and Seven**, Reed asks to meet where you first met to close the story. What happens depends on your choice in Somewhat Damaged: if you **killed Songbird**, it's the **King of Cups** ending; if you **spared her**, it's **King of Pentacles** and The Tower opens.

> **Order:** Leave in Silence: ride to the border with Reed → get out of the car → approach Reed → take Songbird's body from the car → wait for the surgeons → talk to Myers → talk to Reed → talk to Johnny → credits → Four Score and Seven: wait for Reed to contact you → basketball court → talk to Reed.

> **Get ready:** no combat. If you spared Songbird and want **The Tower**, pick the line **"What now? What about the help I was promised"** with Reed (sources disagree on whether it's needed, but it costs nothing). Save before talking to Reed at the court.

## Leave in Silence

1. You find yourself in the car with **Reed** and **Songbird**, on the way to the **Border Crossing** in the Badlands. Get out of the car and approach Reed.
2. **Take Songbird's body** from the car. A helicopter with a medical team receives her: **wait for the surgeons to take her**.
3. President **Myers** arrives. Talk to her:
   - **If you spared Songbird:** Myers thanks you and offers you the **NUSA distinction** medal, which you can **refuse**. On top of that, **€$30,000** will be waiting at your apartment.
   - **If you granted her wish to die:** Myers offers no thanks; just a token reward of **€$5,000**.
4. **Sit down with Reed** to talk about what happened. Game8 recommends the line **"What now? What about the help I was promised"** to secure The Tower; the wiki says the cure is offered anyway. Pick it if you spared Songbird.
5. Talk to Johnny. Reed walks off into the desert and the **Phantom Liberty credits** roll. The job leads automatically into Four Score and Seven.

## Four Score and Seven

1. **Wait for Reed to contact you.** Shortly after Leave in Silence he asks to meet at the **basketball court where you first met** (in Dogtown: from the Luxor High Wellness Spa Data Term, drive west). If the message doesn't arrive, do Dogtown activities instead of only skipping time.
2. **Meet Reed** and talk to him:
   - **If you spared Songbird:** Reed tells you she survived, but will live on as **Myers' Blackwall puppet**. He keeps his promise of a possible cure and a life after the Relic. **Wait at least a day** for **Who Wants to Live Forever** to start.
   - **If you killed her:** Reed bitterly concedes you were ultimately right to end her life. **Phantom Liberty's story ends here.**

## What comes next

- **This Corrosion** (if you took the Cerberus component and at least one spec): about 24 hours after Somewhat Damaged an unknown number messages you. Go see **Yoko** at **Kabuki Market**; she takes a few hours to decrypt the component. With it you craft **only one** item: **Erebus** or the **Militech Canto Mk.6** cyberdeck. Later, **NetWatch** agents ambush you to confiscate it.
- **MaxTac Mantis Blades:** if in Black Steel you called **Yoko**, accepted her proposal and **sent Mr. Hands the coordinates**, you receive them some time after finishing Leave in Silence.
- **Base-game credits calls:** if you get **King of Cups** and then finish with The Sun, The Star or Temperance, Reed calls you **from Dayton**. If you get **King of Pentacles** but **refuse the cure** and finish with one of those endings, Reed calls to berate you.

## Where your save stands

- **King of Cups:** Alex, Kurt Hansen, BARGHEST soldiers and stadium civilians, a few NCPD officers and one MaxTac unit, and **Song So Mi** die. **No cure and no Tower.** Achievement **King of Cups**.
- **King of Pentacles:** Alex, Hansen and the same NCPD and MaxTac die; **Songbird lives**. **The Tower available** (Who Wants to Live Forever, or the call to Reed from the Nocturne Op55N1 rooftop). Achievement **King of Pentacles**. In The Tower credits, Reed calls to check in on you.

## Choices

- **Accepting or refusing the NUSA distinction** (King of Pentacles) → it's only the medal; the €$30,000 arrives either way.
- **"What about the help I was promised"** → just in case, so as not to close The Tower (sources disagree on whether it's required).
- **What you decided in Somewhat Damaged** → killed Songbird: €$5,000 and the story ends; spared her: medal, €$30,000 and Reed's cure.

> **Don't leave without:** the **€$30,000** at your apartment and the medal (if you spared Songbird). If you still have Mr. Hands gigs or Dogtown jobs, do them before accepting Reed's cure: The Tower is an ending.

## Rewards

- **NUSA distinction** and €$30,000 (King of Pentacles), or €$5,000 (King of Cups).
- **Neural matrix** (the cure, King of Pentacles).
- MaxTac Mantis Blades (depends on Black Steel).
- From the path: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog, Fang, Crowbar, Erebus and Canto specs and the Cerberus component (if you picked them up).
