**Nocturne Op55N1** is the only Act 3 job every route shares. It unlocks after **Tapeworm** (Game8 lists Transmission and Search and Destroy) and is given by **Hanako Arasaka**, who meets you at **Embers**, in The Glen (Heywood). Then you go through Viktor's clinic and end up on the rooftop above **Misty's Esoterica**, where you decide who you go into Mikoshi with. The job itself has no reward: what matters is the ending choice.

> **Before Embers:** as you approach the main entrance of **Embers** the game warns you that this is the **point of no return**: from there on there is no free roam until the credits. Go through this list and make a **manual save** before you walk in. After any ending you come back to this moment, so nothing you leave unfinished is lost for good, but what decides **which endings you have** is locked here.
>
> **Endings that are unlocked (or not) before this point:**
>
> - **The Devil (Hanako / Arasaka):** always available. **The Devil achievement** needs **Takemura saved** in Search and Destroy (inside the ambushed building, turn left at the first chance, crouch under the rubble, go upstairs and kill the enemies around him: he must be with you in the cutscene where you leap out of the door). If he died, the ending still plays, but with no achievement, no Arasaka troops and Hellman in his place in the epilogue.
> - **Oda as an ally (The Devil):** only if you **spared** him in Play It Safe **and** Takemura is alive.
> - **The Star (Panam / Aldecaldos):** finish **Queen of the Highway** (chain Riders on the Storm → With a Little Help from My Friends → Queen of the Highway). In Riders on the Storm don't wander off or skip time (Saul dies and the chain fails), and in With a Little Help **don't tell Saul** (you lose Queen of the Highway and The Star).
> - **The Sun (Rogue):** finish **Blistering Love**. It only appears if at Johnny's grave, at the end of Chippin' In, you picked the right answers (the secret-ending set, below, or "A hard road, but looks like we made it." → "Johnny, no need to bare your soul to me.", the latter per the wiki only). With other answers Johnny sends you to Kerry (Holdin' On) and the Rogue path is closed.
> - **Secret ending ((Don't Fear) The Reaper):** at the Chippin' In grave answer "Nah, fucked that up too." → "What do you want from me?" → "OK. But as second chances go, this is your last.". **It does not depend on Johnny affinity** (the 70% claim is a myth). Sources disagree on whether Blistering Love is also needed: do it anyway, since those same answers unlock it.
> - **Temperance:** needs the Panam, Rogue or secret path open; it is decided inside Mikoshi.
> - **The Tower (Phantom Liberty):** finish Phantom Liberty **surrendering Songbird** to the NUSA (King of Swords or King of Pentacles) and **then accept Reed's offer** (the cure). Game8 also advises confirming with Reed that the surgeons are ready before coming here. Details in [PL 17 · Who Wants to Live Forever](../who-wants-to-live-forever/).
>
> **What changes the epilogues and the credits calls:**
>
> - **Goodbye call on the rooftop:** you can only call a partner whose romance is **complete**: Judy (Pyramid Song), Panam (Queen of the Highway), River (Following the River) or Kerry (Boat Drinks). With none, the only option is "Goodbye's aren't my thing". Who shows up in the epilogue depends on this call.
> - **Heroes** (Jackie's ofrenda): without it, Mama Welles doesn't call in the credits.
> - **Dream On:** decides Peralez's message. **Panam's chain** decides Saul's and Mitch's. In **I Don't Wanna Hear It**, the agreement with Us Cracks changes Kerry's message.
> - **Don't Lose Your Mind** (Delamain chain): decides whether Delamain flies your AV in The Sun epilogue.
> - **Jackie's body** (you chose in The Heist): if it went to Viktor, Arasaka has his engram and you can talk to Jackie in Mikoshi (Hanako path) and in Changes.
>
> **Collectibles and achievements:**
>
> - Johnny's outfit pieces (Breathtaking achievement): collect them all before heading to Arasaka Tower. The pants come from the Psychofan gig (Heywood) and are missable.
> - No source lists side jobs or gigs that fail because you cross this point; still, Phantom Liberty jobs with a deadline are best closed first.
>
> **Saves:** on Switch 2, 2.5 fixed the "Save failed - insufficient space" error and groups saves by playthrough. Keep a manual save **before Embers** and don't overwrite it: it is the one that lets you play the other endings.

> **Order:** Embers → security and elevator to the 13th floor → talk to Hanako → leave the restaurant → Johnny in the elevator (Relic malfunction) → Viktor's clinic → pills and Unity handgun → Misty → elevator to the 9th floor and stairs to the roof → **save** → sit by Misty → Johnny → goodbye call (optional) → **decide what comes next**.

> **Get ready:** level up, upgrade your gear and carry healing: the ending you pick ends against **Adam Smasher** (except the Reed route and the tossed-pills one). Bring electrical or EMP quickhacks and several ammo types. If you want to do more than one ending, save in two places: before Embers and on the rooftop, before sitting next to Misty.

## Embers: dinner with Hanako

Go to **Embers**. As you near the entrance the point-of-no-return warning appears: if you still miss something from the list above, turn back now. Clear security and take the elevator to the **13th floor**.

Hanako is playing the piano (the Chopin nocturne the job is named after). Talk to her and sit with her at the bar. She tells you she knows where **Mikoshi** is, beneath Arasaka Tower, and offers a trade: you testify before the Arasaka board about what you saw at Konpeki Plaza against Yorinobu, and in exchange she helps rid you of Johnny's engram. Johnny interrupts the talk.

- **What you answer Hanako doesn't matter:** the option to ask her for help on the rooftop stays available even if you are hostile to her.
- While you talk, an Arasaka AV flies past the building a couple of times; you can ask her about it.

Leave the restaurant and take the elevator. Johnny stops it and pitches his plan: give him control of the body so he can find **Rogue** and storm Arasaka Tower like in 2023. If your relationship with him is bad, he berates you for ignoring his warnings. The **Relic** malfunctions and V blacks out.

## Viktor's clinic

You wake in **Viktor**'s clinic: Johnny dragged you there. Talk to Johnny and then Viktor, who gives the prognosis: **one more Relic malfunction will likely kill you**. When you stand, take **the last pills** and the **Unity** handgun from the table. Before you leave, **Misty** suggests a better place to think: the spot Jackie went to when he had an important decision to make.

## Misty's rooftop

Follow Misty to the elevator behind her shop, ride up to the **9th floor** and then a few stairs to the roof.

1. **Save now, before sitting next to Misty.** It is the last point where the game lets you save before choosing the ending.
2. Sit by Misty. She remembers Jackie and leaves you alone with Johnny.
3. Talk to Johnny. He suggests calling someone to say goodbye: only partners with a complete romance show up; if you have none, pick "Goodbye's aren't my thing".
4. If you call your partner, talk to them. That call decides who appears in The Star and The Sun epilogues.
5. Decide what comes next (below).

## Choices

Each rooftop option leads to a page of this guide. All are **final for this playthrough** until you reload.

- **Ask Hanako for help**: pick "Think trusting Arasaka's risky but worth it" and take the **Omega Blockers**. Always available. Johnny gets angry and says you'll regret it. Hanako can't come (Yorinobu has her watched at his estate) and sends someone: it's **Hellman**. Downstairs, Misty leaves angry when she learns you are working with Arasaka. → [3.2 · The Devil](../ending-the-devil/) (Last Caress → Totalimmortal → Where is My Mind?).
- **Ask Panam for help**: pick the option to call Panam (on PowerPyx's path: "Gonna ask Panam for help") and take the **Omega Blockers**. Needs **Queen of the Highway**. Panam agrees and comes to pick you up; while you wait, Misty offers a tarot reading. → [3.3 · The Star](../ending-the-star/) (We Gotta Live Together → Forward to Death → Belly of the Beast → Changes → All Along the Watchtower).
- **Go with Johnny's plan and Rogue**: pick "Think you and Rogue should go" and take the **pseudoendotrizine**. Needs **Blistering Love**. You play as Johnny in V's body. → [3.4 · The Sun](../ending-the-sun/) (For Whom the Bell Tolls → Knockin' on Heaven's Door → Changes → Path of Glory).
- **Call Solomon Reed** (Phantom Liberty): only if you finished the expansion surrendering Songbird. Johnny is furious at first, but understands once you apologise; an FIA AV comes to pick you up. → [PL 17 · Who Wants to Live Forever](../who-wants-to-live-forever/) (Things Done Changed, The Tower ending).
- **Pick nothing and wait about 5 minutes** (5 min 8 s, the length of the song): only with the secret Chippin' In answers. Don't touch any option; PowerPyx advises alternating your view between your gun and Johnny about once a minute. Johnny proposes going alone: "Let's hear this plan" → "[Attack Arasaka Tower] Means goin' out with a bang - I like it". One Game8 page says to wait after selecting one of the first three options, but its own secret-ending guide and PowerPyx say to select nothing: select nothing. → [3.6 · (Don't Fear) The Reaper](../ending-the-reaper/).
- **Give up**: pick "Could also just put all this to rest" and then "[Toss pills] I know exactly what we're gonna do". V throws the pills away. Always available, no rewards. → [3.7 · Path of Least Resistance](../ending-least-resistance/).
- **Temperance** is not chosen here: it comes from the Panam, Rogue or secret path, when inside Mikoshi you let Johnny keep the body. → [3.5 · Temperance](../ending-temperance/).

## After the credits

After any ending the game offers to take you back before the point of no return ("One More Gig"): you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1**, in free roam, with earlier jobs as they were. **There is no New Game+.** The ending's rewards (Legendary Gorilla Arms and Militech Berserk, tarot cards, some shards, the route's iconics and the Access Token if you took it) are added to that save. Clothing you get during the epilogues does not come back with you.

> **Don't leave without:** the last pills and the **Unity** handgun from Viktor's table, and a **manual save on the rooftop before sitting next to Misty** (plus the one before Embers). With those two saves you can play every ending you have open without repeating anything.
