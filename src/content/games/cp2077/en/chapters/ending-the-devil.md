The **Hanako Arasaka** route: you pick it on the [Nocturne Op55N1](../nocturne-op55n1/) rooftop with "Think trusting Arasaka's risky but worth it" and the **Omega Blockers**, and it is **always available**. It is three jobs: **Last Caress** (rescue Hanako at the **Arasaka Estate**, Westbrook), **Totalimmortal** (the Arasaka board, Yorinobu's coup and **Adam Smasher** at **Arasaka Tower**) and **Where is My Mind?** (recovery on the **Arasaka Orbital Station**). It is the only Mikoshi route that skips Changes. If you **saved Takemura** in Search and Destroy, he comes along, brings Arasaka troops and gives you the achievement; if not, Hellman takes his place.

> **Order:** Hellman picks you up at Misty's Esoterica → Arasaka Estate (guards, Hellman, Hanako) → elite guards → find Hanako → AV to Arasaka Tower → Mikoshi with Saburo → testimony before the board → fend off the attack → elevator to the upper atrium → Yorinobu's forces → **Adam Smasher** → Yorinobu → elevator down → surgery → orbital station (tests, calls) → **sign or refuse the contract**.

> **Get ready:** without Takemura alive there is no Devil achievement, no Arasaka support and no Oda. If you want to go back for the achievement, load a save from before Search and Destroy. Bring electrical quickhacks (EMP, Short Circuit, Overheat, Cyberware Malfunction), frag grenades and revolver, assault rifle and sniper ammo for Smasher. Save before going in for Smasher.

## Last Caress: the Arasaka Estate

As you come down from the rooftop, Misty offers a reading, but leaves angry when she learns you are working with Arasaka: the corporation caused Jackie's death. The one who walks into the shop to fetch you is **Anders Hellman**, who picks up an earlier conversation with V. He drives you to the estate, where Yorinobu is holding Hanako.

1. **Deal with the guards** at the entrance. Game8 mentions a Body option to do it yourself; if you don't have it, Hellman handles it.
2. Talk to Hellman and to Hanako, and head to the estate.
3. **Neutralize the elite guards.** If you (or Goro, if he is with you) are detected, more show up and you have to kill them too: stealth saves you the fight.
4. **Genjiroh:** before finding Hanako, search the estate's rooms. The iconic smart pistol **Genjiroh** is on a desk in one of them. If you already had it (from Play It Safe, code 2906), you get another after finishing the game.
5. Find Hanako, talk to her and follow her to the **AV**. Fly to Arasaka Tower.

## Totalimmortal: the board and the coup

You reach the tower with Hellman and, if he survived, **Goro Takemura**.

1. Exit the AV and follow Hanako to the elevator. Sit with her: you are going to see **Saburo Arasaka**, whose engram is in Mikoshi.
2. **Jackie:** in Mikoshi you can ask Hanako to talk to **Jackie Welles**'s engram. It only appears if in The Heist you sent his body to Viktor (Arasaka took it afterwards). If shortly before you flip Saburo off in Johnny's name, the talk is cut short and you lose that option; it has no other effect.
3. Follow Hanako to the boardroom, find a seat next to her and **give your testimony** against Yorinobu.
4. Yorinobu tries to wipe out the whole board in one stroke. **Fend off the attackers** and talk to Hanako: now you must reach Yorinobu on her terms (you can't hurt him).
5. **Oda:** if you spared him in Play It Safe and Takemura is alive, he shows up and fights alongside Hanako's troops.
6. **Take the elevator to the upper atrium.** The first elevator is blocked: per Game8, use the lift on the 3rd floor up to floor 140.
7. **Defeat Yorinobu's forces.** Game8 rates the three routes:
   - **Dark Jungle (best):** beside the boardroom table, a lit rock marks the way in. You skip the walkway mech.
   - **Lit Jungle:** follow the red flares; it gets you behind the mech.
   - **Walkway:** fewest soldiers, but you fight a mech.
8. Head to Yorinobu's office. **Adam Smasher** blocks the way (see "Boss — Adam Smasher"). Afterwards, optional: decide his fate and loot the **Access Token**.
9. **Confront Yorinobu.** Pushing his gun away has no consequence.
10. Talk to Hanako and **take the elevator down**. Per PowerPyx, **The Devil** achievement pops here if Takemura is alive; the wiki places it at the end of Where is My Mind?. Totalimmortal gives 387 XP (Game8).

## Boss — Adam Smasher

Since 2.1, Smasher uses a **Sandevistan** and adapts his tactics to your build, so older guides are only partly valid.

- **Phase 1, close combat:** melee and short-range projectiles. Don't stand still.
- **Phase 2, long range:** with his right half destroyed, he keeps his distance and shoots.
- **Phase 3, weakened:** finish him.
- **Weak point:** the right side of his chest, behind armour plating you can shoot off. Shooting off his **right arm** (on your left, facing him) ends his melee; then wreck the **back rocket launcher** and he has little left (PowerPyx tip from before 2.0: verify it).
- **Quickhacks:** **electrical** ones work best; **EMP** briefly blocks most of his attacks.
- **Rockets:** they target where you were: keep moving. They get more aggressive the less health he has.
- **At about 50% health, on this route, a wave of Arasaka ninjas comes in.** They dodge bullets: one or two frag grenades sort them out.
- **Smasher's fate (optional):** at the end you can decide what to do with him; he tells V that mercy is disgusting. The sources don't document what sparing him changes. If you want to be sure of the **Access Token**, finish him and loot it.

## Where is My Mind?: the orbital station

1. **Surgery:** in the tower's medical center they remove the Relic. Talk to Johnny one last time in cyberspace.
2. You wake on the **Arasaka Orbital Station**. Professor **Sachiko Kusama** puts you through tests: reading text, solving a Rubik's cube, responding to keywords and the treadmill. Follow the guard, go to sleep, step onto the treadmill.
3. **Nightmares:** Johnny's voice draws you to the door. Leave the room and investigate the noise. The news reports that Saburo has taken over Yorinobu's body.
4. **Call the people close to you (optional)** once you're allowed to use the comms:
   - **Viktor:** always answers and asks for a checkup when you're back; he says Misty is with family in Warsaw.
   - **Panam** (with Queen of the Highway): with no romance, she demands you come back and hangs up; if she is your partner and you called her on the rooftop, she's angry she heard it from Viktor.
   - **Judy** (with Pyramid Song): with no romance, a recorded message; if she is your partner and you called her, she wants you back within hours.
   - **River** (with Following the River): with no romance, he has no time; if he is your partner and you called him, he wants to fly up and get you.
   - **Kerry** (with Boat Drinks): with no romance, he tells you to just leave; if he is your partner and you called him, he promises to make calls to get you back to Night City.
   - **Hanako:** never answers.
5. Go to sleep and follow the doctor's orders. You dream of the Rubik's cube, which breaks open to show **The Devil**: this route's tarot card.
6. After weeks of tests, V snaps. **Vent your frustration (optional).**
7. After almost **30 days**, Kusama tells you the treatment isn't working. Talk to **Takemura** (if you saved him) or **Hellman** (if not): he offers the **Secure Your Soul** program. Make your final decision.

## Choices

- **Talk to Jackie in Mikoshi** → one last talk with his engram. Only if his body went to Viktor in The Heist; flipping off Saburo cancels it.
- **Push Yorinobu's gun away or not** → no consequence.
- **Smasher's fate** → no documented effect; kill him if you want the Access Token for sure.
- **"[Sign Contract] I want to live"** → V becomes an engram until Arasaka finds them a body. V puts on the bullet necklace and holds it tight. **Final for this ending.**
- **"[Refuse to Sign] I'm going back to Earth"** → V returns to Earth to live out the months left (about six), singing Never Fade Away. Gazing at Earth, V tears the necklace off. Hanako calls in the credits to offer those six months working for Arasaka. Per the wiki, this is the Devil route's way to stay with River or Kerry.
- **Takemura dead** (decided in Search and Destroy) → Hellman instead of Takemura, no troops and no Oda, **no achievement**.

## Who lives and who dies

Most of the Arasaka board and many employees die, plus **Adam Smasher** (your call), **Yorinobu** (his body goes to Saburo's engram) and **Johnny** (the Relic is removed from V). Hanako survives.

## Credits calls

- **Viktor:** asks how Earth looks from up there and offers a checkup when you're back.
- **Misty:** draws The Fool, The Moon and The High Priestess ("keep a close eye on your new friends").
- **Mama Welles** (with Heroes): amused by your new fame, asks you not to forget where you started.
- **Judy:** if she is your partner, she dumps you and leaves Night City; if you did her chain without romance, she thanks you by a campfire; if you didn't do her chain, she says she's thinking of leaving.
- **Panam:** if she is your partner, news from the Badlands and the promise of a long ride; if you did her chain without romance, she thanks you; if not, she asks for work.
- **Saul and Mitch** (with Panam's chain): Saul says you're an Aldecaldo; Mitch says you're welcome at camp.
- **Rogue:** with her chain done, she asks you to pass a message to Johnny; without it, she's impressed by your work with Hellman.
- **Kerry and River** (with their chains): if they are your partner, Kerry wishes you could hang out more and River invites you to Randy's birthday.
- **Hanako:** only if you refused the contract.
- **Peralez** (with Dream On): paranoid if you told him about the brainwashing; if you hid it, he asks for help with a job.

## Rewards and where you return

After the credits you return to the **last save before Nocturne Op55N1**, with these rewards added:

- Legendary **Gorilla Arms** and **Militech Berserk** (if you didn't own them).
- **Genjiroh** (if you took it at the estate; if you already had it, another one).
- **Access Token** (if you looted it from Smasher): opens Smasher's secret office, the stern armory on the **DV Ebunike**, with the **Ba Xing Chong** crafting spec.
- **The Devil** tarot card and some shards.
- **The Devil** achievement (only with Takemura alive).

> **Don't leave without:** the **Genjiroh** from the estate desk, Smasher's **Access Token** and, if you care about the achievement, the certainty that Takemura is alive before you start (if not, go back to a save from before Search and Destroy).
