**Regina Jones**'s client wants the **datashard** of **Mikhail Akulov**, the Soviet fixer whose car you bugged in **Welcome to America, Comrade**: it holds his talks with the Japanese. Akulov is staying in the penthouse of **Hotel Raito** on Adams St (Kabuki, Watson), under heavy protection. It is in **set 8 of 8**, the last one: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 7 (Freedom of the Press and Lousy Kleppers). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** enter Hotel Raito (optional: don't raise the alarm) → gain access to the elevator → go to Akulov's penthouse → obtain the datashard → leave the hotel → call Regina → deliver the shard to her client → collect your bonus from the Drop Point.

## In the hotel

- There are bodyguards in the lobby and the penthouse. To go up you need **elevator access**.
- Akulov's bodyguard, **Nadezhda Tiurina**, ex-KGB, has Mantis Blades, Sandevistan Mk.1, Kerenzikov, Charge Jump, flashbangs and Break Hold (you can't grab her). If you're going for stealth, avoid her.
- You can take the shard without Akulov noticing, or knock him out or kill him.
- Once out, call Regina and take the shard to the client in person.

## Akulov and The Heist

Akulov and Nadezhda also appear in The Heist, at Konpeki Plaza, where they were negotiating with Arasaka. According to an unconfirmed wiki note, if you do this gig before The Heist, you see them there alive even if you killed them here.

## Rewards

- €$6,170, 1,000 XP and 770 Street Cred, plus the Drop Point bonus if nobody spots you.
- Optional: **Skill Shard: Headhunter** (XP for the Headhunter skill).
