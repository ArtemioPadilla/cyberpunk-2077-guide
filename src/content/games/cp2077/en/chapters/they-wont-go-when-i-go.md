**They Won't Go When I Go** ends the **Joshua Stephenson** chain (Act 2). It only comes if, in **There Is A Light That Never Goes Out**, you refused Rachel's bribe at PieZ. Some time later, **Rachel Casich** calls: Joshua wants you with him while he prepares to record his crucifixion braindance. The meeting is at **Fourth Wall Studios** in **Japantown** (Westbrook). (The wiki's job list names Joshua as the giver; the job's infobox names Rachel.)

> **Order:** answer Rachel → go to Fourth Wall Studios → talk to Vasquez → enter the studio → talk to Rachel → talk to Joshua in the dressing room → pray (or don't) → record the braindance → stay with Joshua until he dies → talk to Rachel → wait for the call two days later.

## The call

If you say you don't have time, Joshua pleads with you over the phone and Rachel offers extra pay. If you **refuse again**, the job ends and **you get nothing**.

## The studio

Outside, protesters are rallying against "The Passion of Joshua". **Vasquez** stops you at the gate and sends you to the building down the street, the one with a cop at the door. Inside, talk to Rachel and then to **Joshua** in the dressing room: sit on the stool in front of him. He has doubts and asks for your opinion; it still holds that **supporting him** and **being honest** builds his confidence, while criticizing him makes him doubt. Then he asks you to pray with him: you can let him pray, pray yourself (there are four prayers to choose from, including one of your own) or refuse.

## Choices

- **Crucify him yourself** → take the **hammer and nails**, wait by the cross and nail him while reading the teleprompter lines; then press the button to **raise the cross**. You can **stop halfway**: another actor finishes for you. Stay with Joshua until he dies and talk to Rachel.
- **Don't crucify him, but stay** → another actor does it; sit in the chair, stay until he dies and talk to Rachel.
- **Don't take part and leave** → you walk out of the studio.

**Two days later**, Rachel calls: the braindance is out.

- **If you supported Joshua** → it's a hit; she thanks you and sends a **moderate bonus**.
- **If you didn't** → the braindance came out badly, Rachel is angry and pays you **a small sum**.

## Rewards

- 1,347 XP and 2,021 Street Cred.
- **€$3,100 to €$9,300**, depending on how much you supported Joshua.
