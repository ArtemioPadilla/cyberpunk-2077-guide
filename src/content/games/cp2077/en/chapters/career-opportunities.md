**Career Opportunities** arrived with **Update 2.0** together with **Trauma Drama**, a **Macroware** arcade mini-game in the style of Contra or Metal Slug. If you take **first place** on the leaderboard, Macroware messages you and offers an unpaid internship with **Trauma Team**. You collect the prize at the MedCenter in Little China (Watson). The wiki doesn't say where the Trauma Drama cabinets are or whether any is within reach in Act 1.

> **Order:** set the high score in Trauma Drama → read Macroware's message → open the Trauma Team netpage on your computer → go to the MedCenter → scan your hand → collect your consolation prize.

## Setting the high score

- Playing is free. There are three stages (Westbrook, Pacifica and the Badlands), each with 6 sections and a boss at the end.
- The score to beat is **401,210** (IZZ4L). Reaching the top 10 already gets you a message from Macroware, but the job needs first place.
- Each continue **costs you 10,000 points** and your weapons: don't die. Bosses give 10,000 and the final boss 20,000.
- Rescuing civilians gives you weapons, and picking up the same one twice upgrades it. The infinite-points trick with the helicopters was fixed in 2.01.

## The internship

1. On your apartment computer, open the **Trauma Team** netpage, log in under "Trauma Drama Member Access" and apply on the "We want YOU!" tab.
2. Go to the **MedCenter** (Night City Medical Center, Little China), scan your hand to confirm your identity and collect your prize.

## Rewards

- **Trauma Team uniform** (clothing).
- **Mini Trauma Team AV**, a display piece for your apartment.
