**Regina Jones** has a secret place in the **Cortes and Kennedy** residential block (Kabuki, Watson), and a Tyger Claw, **Taki Kenmochi**, has set up a pachinko parlor right on her doorstep. If the NCPD raids it, they'd find Regina's stuff. Deal with Taki and the other Tygers will clear out. It is in **set 2 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the five gigs of set 1 (Monster Hunt, Playing for Keeps, Hippocratic Oath, Many Ways to Skin a Cat and Rite of Passage). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** approach the pachinko machines → locate Taki Kenmochi → neutralize her → move away from the machines (or carry her to the fixer's transport).

## Enemies

- **Taki Kenmochi:** M221 Saratoga, Combat Stim and EMP grenades.
- **Kenji Sakura**, her partner: Gorilla Arms, Heavy Subdermal Armor, Break Hold (you can't grab him), Charge Jump, EMP grenades and Masking Ink.
- More Tyger Claws around.
- Taki's computer and a shard hold conversations between her and Kenji.

## Choices

- **Kill her** → move away from the pachinko machines and you're done.
- **Knock her out** → pick her up, leave the area and carry her to the fixer's transport.

## Rewards

- €$2,600, 1,000 XP and 590 Street Cred. Taki drops an M221 Saratoga.
