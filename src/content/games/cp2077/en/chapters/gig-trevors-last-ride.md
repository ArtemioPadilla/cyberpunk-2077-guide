**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, gives you this gig in **Biotechnica Flats**, by **Highway 101**. It's from her second set, with **Big Pete's Got Big Problems** and **No Fixers**: it opens after **Dancing on a Minefield** and **Flying Drugs**, in **Act 2**. **Trevor Brass**'s car was spotted, riddled with holes, at a nest of the **Wraiths**, who don't take prisoners. His family, **Aldecaldos**, aren't after revenge: they just want to bury him.

> **Order:** go to Wraiths territory → find Trevor's body → scan the bodies to identify him → retrieve him → carry him out of the outpost → put him in the trunk.

## How to do it

The wiki has no detailed walkthrough. The outpost is full of Wraiths: go in quietly or guns out, **scan** the corpses until you find Trevor and carry him to your car. Put the body in the **trunk** to close the gig.

According to the wiki (it's a bug): if you visited the place before getting the gig, **Johnny**'s and V's lines play all at once when it starts.

## Rewards

€$14,000, 1,000 XP, 1,710 Street Cred and the **Wraiths rally bolero jacket**, found during the gig.
