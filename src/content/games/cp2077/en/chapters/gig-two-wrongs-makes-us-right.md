**Mr. Hands**, the fixer of **Pacifica**, gives you this theft in **West Wind Estate**. Pacifica is **Act 2** territory: you won't have it yet from Watson. It is the base-game Pacifica gig. In the **unfinished NCART station**, an **Animals** den, there's a van loaded with **modified lidocaine** that the Animals had already stolen from **Maelstrom**. Mr. Hands's plan: you steal the van, he resells the lidocaine to ripperdocs.

> **Order:** get to the unfinished NCART station → find the van in the tunnels → get to it → escape the station in the van → park it in the lot by the Pacifica market → get out.

## How to do it

The wiki only gives the objectives. The station is full of **Animals**: work through the tunnels to the van, get in and drive out of the station. Take it to the **lot by the market** in Pacifica and get out to close the gig.

> **Don't leave without:** checking the station's computers (Unfinished NCART Station) and the shard **Important notice to all units**.
