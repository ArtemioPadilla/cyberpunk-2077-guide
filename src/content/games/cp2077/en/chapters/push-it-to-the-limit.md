Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It comes from **Muamar Reyes**, El Capitán, the Santo Domingo fixer. It can trigger as soon as **Spider and the Fly** gives you the objective to find **Solomon Reed**: from then on, while you explore **Dogtown**, vehicles marked with a **blue steering-wheel icon** sometimes appear.

> **Order:** get into a blue-wheel car → El Capitán's call → drive the car to the Arroyo garage → get out and leave → talk to El Capitán in his car.

## The delivery

The first time you get into a marked car, El Capitán calls: he has been tracking that vehicle. He gives you the coordinates of a garage in **Arroyo**, Santo Domingo. On the way, **hostile vehicles** come after you, trying to kill you or block the road: take them out or lose them by getting out of their range before you enter the garage.

Park inside, get out and leave the building. El Capitán is waiting outside by a **Mizutani Shion MZ2**. Get in with him: he explains his new scheme, stealing cars off the street and delivering them to his garages. Midway through, a squad car rolls up and officer **Ayden Daniels** walks over; he's in on the scheme too. When he leaves, El Capitán asks whether you want in; say yes.

## Rewards

- Opens the repeatable job **Just Another Story** (car deliveries with prizes and AUTOFIXER discounts).
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
