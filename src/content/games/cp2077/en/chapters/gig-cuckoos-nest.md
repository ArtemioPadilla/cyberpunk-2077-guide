**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, hires you for this rescue at the **psychiatric ward** (Night City Center for Psychiatric Health) on Sequoia St., in **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his third set, with **Severance Package**: it opens after **Breaking News**, **Hacking the Hacker** and **Family Matters**, in **Act 2**. **Jasmine Dixon**, an NCPD sergeant, overheard something she shouldn't have at the precinct, took it to her supervisor and was committed the next day. Her husband is paying for the job.

> **Order:** get inside the hospital → get into the restricted area → find Jasmine's cell → take the guard's keys → open the cell and talk to her → escort her out of the hospital → take her to the transport → talk to her.

## How to do it

The wiki only gives the objectives. The hospital has **guards**; the key step is getting **a guard's keys** to open the cell (a quiet takedown saves you a fight). Then you must **escort** Jasmine to the transport, so clear your way out before you open the cell.

There are several inmates on the ground floor. In one of the rooms **Johnny Silverhand** recognizes a patient: they served in the same unit, and he remembers what they went through.
