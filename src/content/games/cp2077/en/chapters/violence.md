**Violence** starts shortly after **Search and Destroy** (Act 2), with an anonymous message that sends you to the **No-Tell Motel** (Kabuki). Waiting there is **Lizzy Wizzy**, the chrome star: she suspects her manager and boyfriend, **Liam Northom**, is cheating on her, and wants you to look into it discreetly at the **Riot** club in **Little China**.

> **Order:** motel → Riot at night → surveillance computer → hear the whole conversation → download the footage → (optional) confront Liam → call Lizzy → (part 2) back to the motel.

## Getting into Riot

The club opens **after 8 p.m.**

- **Front door:** intimidate the bouncer with **Body** or have enough **Street Cred** (don't ask him about Liam before intimidating him: he'll shut you out).
- **Fire escape** in the left alley: hit or shoot the ladder to bring it down.
- **Garage on the right:** hack the security room keypad (**Intelligence**; an employee badge is inside) or lift the shutter (**Body**).

You don't need to find Liam: find the **surveillance computer** and check the cameras. Liam is in the VIP lounge with an **Arasaka** employee: it isn't an affair, they're planning a **backup of Lizzy**. Listen to the **whole** conversation. Use the computer again to **download it**.

## Choices

- **Confront Liam** (VIP, third floor; before calling Lizzy) → you can **extort €$30,000** from him and still tell Lizzy everything. If you confront him **before** hearing the conversation, V assumes it's just cheating and you lose part 2.
- **Tell Lizzy it's an affair** → €$1,900 and it ends.
- **Tell her the truth** → €$2,800. If you also have the footage, **days later** she calls you back to the motel: Liam lies dead on the floor. She asks you to get rid of the body.
  - **Agree** → carry it to the garbage chute (if you walk off with it, the job fails). Lizzy pays you.
  - **Refuse** → she sends you away.

If you've started Rebel! Rebel! (Kerry), Liam won't be back until you finish **I Don't Wanna Hear It**. With Phantom Liberty, in **You Know My Name** Lizzy ignores you if you refused to hide the body; otherwise, compliment her show and she gives you the **Amikiri Sound Cutter**.

## Rewards

- 588 XP, 882 Street Cred and **€$1,900 to €$38,500**.
- A Lizzy Wizzy poster for your apartment.
