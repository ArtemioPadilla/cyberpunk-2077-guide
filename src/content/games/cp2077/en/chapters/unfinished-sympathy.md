This page covers the epilogue of the **King of Wands** ending ("take Songbird to the stars"): you helped Songbird in Firestarter and **killed Reed** on the platform in The Killing Moon. Songbird is on her way to the **Moon**. Two short, quiet jobs remain: **Unfinished Sympathy**, a last drink with **Alex** at The Moth, and **From Her to Eternity**, a message from Songbird that sends you to collect her parting gift. Neither has any combat.

> **Order:** Unfinished Sympathy: talk to Alex → meet her at The Moth → sit at the bar → talk to Alex (toast) → _(some time later)_ postcard on your apartment door → From Her to Eternity: message from the unknown number (about 24 h later) → Tranquil Terrace → container by a chair → Quantum Tuner and Metal pin.

> **Get ready:** nothing to prepare. Keep in mind that this ending has **no cure** and **The Tower is not available**: if you want that ending, you need a save from before the choice in The Killing Moon (or in Firestarter).

## Unfinished Sympathy

After The Killing Moon, **Alex** messages you: she wants to meet up.

1. Talk to Alex and **meet her at The Moth**.
2. **Sit at the bar** and talk to her.
3. **The toast:** you can drink to one of the story's dead: **Solomon Reed**, **Jackie Welles**, **Johnny Silverhand**, **T-Bug**, **Evelyn Parker**, **Scorpion** or **Goro Takemura** (only if he died in your playthrough).
   - If you don't toast Reed, you'll get the chance when Alex toasts him.
   - If you toast **Jackie**, Johnny gets jealous.
   - If you toast **Johnny**, he's grateful and toasts you back.

**The postcard.** Some time later a postcard turns up on your apartment door: **"It's Always Sunny in Monte Carlo"**. Its text changes depending on **whether or not you gave Alex the coin**.

## From Her to Eternity

About **24 hours** after Unfinished Sympathy you get a text from an **unknown number**: "Return to the place that reminds me of home and see what you find."

1. **Read the message.** The place is **Tranquil Terrace**, in Dogtown, where you could meet Songbird in Birds with Broken Wings.
2. **Search the area** around the coordinates: there's a **small container next to a chair**.
3. **Collect its contents:** the **Quantum Tuner** and the **Metal pin**. The job ends when you take them.

## Where your save stands

- **Who dies in King of Wands:** Kurt Hansen, many BARGHEST soldiers and stadium civilians, **Albert Murphy** (if you killed him), numerous Orbital Air and Corp-Bud employees and NCX civilians, several NUSA black ops and **Solomon Reed**. **Alex lives.**
- **No Relic cure.** Phantom Liberty's story ends here; your V goes back to the main story with the base game's endings (Nocturne Op55N1 and its paths). There's **no call to Reed** on the Nocturne rooftop and no Tower ending.
- **Achievement:** **King of Wands**.

## Choices

- **Who you toast** → only changes the dialogue (Johnny's jealousy or gratitude).
- **Alex's coin** → changes the postcard's text.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Quantum Tuner** and **Metal pin** from the Tranquil Terrace container, and the **postcard** on your apartment door. Any Mr. Hands gigs or Dogtown jobs you have left are still available.

## Rewards

- Postcard "It's Always Sunny in Monte Carlo".
- **Quantum Tuner** and **Metal pin**.
- From the path: Bald Eagle, Wild Dog, Murphy's Law and **Pariah** (if you picked them up).
