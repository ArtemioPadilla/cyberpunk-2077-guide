**Stairway To Heaven** is the second session with the **Zen Master**, the monk from **Imagine**. This time he appears in **Japantown** (Westbrook), so you need to be in **Act 2**. It opens when you finish Imagine.

> **Order:** approach the Zen Master → talk to him → do the meditation.

## What is known

The wiki page has no walkthrough yet: it only gives the district and the objectives (approach the Zen Master and talk to him). Look for his marker in Japantown. Going by the later sessions, each time the monk offers you a braindance meditation, lets you pay what you think is fair (nothing included) and vanishes when it ends. Johnny still suspects the whole thing is an advertising gimmick.

## What comes next

Finishing it opens **Poem Of The Atoms**, in North Oak.
