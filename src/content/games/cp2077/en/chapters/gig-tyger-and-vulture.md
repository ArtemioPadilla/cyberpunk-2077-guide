**Tyger and Vulture** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place in the **Tyger Claws' hidden casino**, tucked into a culvert on the **Charter Hill** waterfront, by Longshore South. It belongs to Wakako's **first set**, so it does not show up while you are in Watson in Act 1: it opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. The story: a dealer called **Vortex** rigged the casino machines to skim a little off every win. The Tyger Claws caught her and she died under interrogation without saying where she hid the money. Wakako wants you to find it.

> **Order:** enter the casino → search the office → find Vortex's credchip → take it → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## Getting in

The door is inside a drainage culvert. The guard at the entrance lets you through if you pay **€$1,700**; if you do not want to pay, sneak past behind him. After a corridor with two guards you reach the gaming floor: a bar, four roulette tables and arcade machines. To the right of the entrance is a server room with the computer that controls the casino's cameras.

## Reaching the upper floor

The offices are on the upper floor, overlooking the gaming floor. You have two ways up:

- **The guarded stairwell:** going this way starts a fight with the Tyger Claws.
- **The hole in a storeroom ceiling:** the stealth route.

**Vortex's credchip** is in a Tyger Claws netrunner's room. From that room a skywalk crosses the entrance culvert: that is the casino's exit. Then take the credchip to the Drop Point.
