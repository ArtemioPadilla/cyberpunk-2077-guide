**Sr. Ladrillo's Private Collection** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at **Dicky Twister**, a gay strip club on the corner of Congress and Pajaro Streets in **Vista del Rey**. It belongs to Padre's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the second (**Psychofan**, **Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta** and **Old Friends**). The owner, the pimp **Sr. Ladrillo** of the **Valentinos**, records his clients in the VIP rooms: politicians, corpos, crime bosses and celebrities. The NCPD is planning a raid; if it gets those recordings, the district falls into chaos. Ladrillo is out of town.

> **Order:** go to Dicky Twister → get to Sr. Ladrillo's office (optional: undetected) → steal the compromising material → get out of Dicky Twister.

## Getting in

- **Front door:** pay **€$100** or talk the guard round with **Technical Ability** (18 according to the club page).
- **Back alley:** a locked door that needs very high Technical Ability (18) leads to a storage room past the main guards.
- **With high Body** (18): open a double door in the alley, or climb to the roof via a container and open the door that leads straight into the office.

## The office

It is the **glass room** above the dance floor; you reach it by the stairs in the hostile area at the back. The upper floor has guards and cameras. In the office, **Saulo Capon**, one of Ladrillo's lieutenants, stands by the computer: neutralize him before stealing the data. You can also disable any remaining cameras from that terminal. Leave through the nearby doors to finish.

During the gig, Johnny may mention having been at the club before with Kerry.

## Rewards

- €$18,200 (plus the bonus for staying undetected).
