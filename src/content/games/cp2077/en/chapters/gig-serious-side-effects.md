**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, sends you to the **Dewdrop Inn**, a motel in **Arroyo (Santo Domingo)**. Santo Domingo only opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. It is one of the three gigs in his first set (with **Race to the Top** and **Error 404**). Denver Doug, a ripperdoc, lost a gallon of **beta acid** after patching up a **6th Street** ganger. He hired Booker Updike to find it, and Booker vanished too; he was last seen at the Dewdrop Inn.

> **Order:** go to the Dewdrop Inn → (optional) room 103: find out what happened to Booker and search it → go to room 203 → look for the acid in the lab → take the container → get out of the motel → leave the acid at the Drop Point.

## How to do it

The motel is held by **6th Street**. The wiki has no detailed walkthrough, only the objectives. Stop at **room 103** first if you want to know what became of Booker (optional), then head up to **203**, where the lab with the acid is. Leave with the container and take it to the Drop Point to close the gig.
