**Hole in the Sky** starts the moment you jump down the chute at the end of Dog Eat Dog. **Space Force One** has just come down in Dogtown, near the **Golden Pacific**, and **BARGHEST** is already heading for the wreck. Songbird guides you over the radio: reach the plane, get into the wreckage, find President **Rosalind Myers** and hold off the attack. It's short, but it's the Phantom Liberty job with the harshest failure: **fail it and you lose the whole expansion**, including its side jobs and the Dogtown gigs.

> **Order:** go to the crash site (optional laptop) → the crane → talk to Songbird → get onto the crane arm → the wreckage → enter the wreckage → find Myers → talk to Myers → defeat the attackers.

> **Get ready:** make a manual save **before the chute** in Dog Eat Dog and don't overwrite it until this job is done. Go straight there: this is no time to explore Dogtown.

## The one rule you must not break

This job can fail in **only** one way: **straying too far from the street that leads straight from the chute to the crash site**.

- When you go down the chute, the in-game clock starts at **8:00 PM**.
- If you stay on that street, time runs until **12:00 AM** (about 4 in-game hours) and then freezes until you finish the job. There's no real rush.
- If you leave the street, at any hour, Songbird gives you a **first warning**. After **10 in-game minutes** comes the **second warning**, and after another **15 minutes** (25 in total) the **third warning fails the job**.
- **Failing Hole in the Sky stops you from progressing in Phantom Liberty**: no more story, no side jobs and no gigs from the Dogtown fixers.

If you hear Songbird warn you, turn around and get back to the street at once. If you fail it, load the save you made before the chute.

## The crash site and the crane

1. **Go to the crash site** along the street. On the way there's a **laptop** you can hack (optional); it's Space Force One's only computer and just has messages to read.
2. **Go to the crane** and talk to Songbird.
3. **Get onto the crane arm** and cross it to reach the plane's wreckage.

## Inside the plane

1. **Go to the wreckage** and **enter** it.
2. Search the interior before looking for the President: a **suitcase** in the plane holds the **NUSA suit**, an outfit you won't be able to pick up later.
3. **Find Rosalind Myers** and talk to her.

## Defending Myers

When you talk to Myers, BARGHEST arrives: **defeat the attackers**. It's a gunfight against militia soldiers in the plane's wreckage. Use the cover the fuselage gives you, stay close to Myers and don't throw grenades around carelessly: the next job, Spider and the Fly, starts instantly and ends with a boss.

When the last attacker falls, Myers has survived Kurt Hansen's attempt on her life, which nearly killed her. The job ends and Spider and the Fly begins.

## Choices

- **Leaving the direct street** → three warnings from Songbird and, at the third, the job fails and **Phantom Liberty is blocked for good** on that save. It's the only way to fail Hole in the Sky.
- **Hacking the laptop** → optional; it only gives information.

> **Don't leave without:** the **NUSA suit** from the suitcase inside Space Force One. And keep your pre-chute save until you've finished.

## Rewards

- NUSA suit (missable).
