Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is available after **Spider and the Fly**. The marker is by a BARGHEST garrison in front of the **Black Sapphire** hotel in Dogtown. A drunk recruit, **Paco Torres**, calls you over while he drinks with his partner **Babs Okoye**. **Careful:** the job and its characters disappear once you finish **Firestarter**, and it fails if still active.

> **Order:** talk to Paco and Babs → first Deep Dive (fistfight) → second Deep Dive (convoy) → advise them → wait → pick up the reward.

## Hansen's memories

Paco offers you a **Deep Dive**: you inhale a drug and relive his story as **Kurt Hansen**. In the first one, talk to **Yuri Bychkov**, go down to the lobby and beat Paco in a fistfight. In the second you ride shotgun in a truck Paco drives: take out the Scavenger vehicles with the **Bald Eagle** revolver and, at the barricade, finish the rest with only that revolver and the **Fang** knife (BARGHEST soldiers back you up). Back in the present, Paco admits he stole and sold the convoy's generators.

## Choices

- **Frame Yuri** → the next day, under The Needle, Yuri attacks you. Kill him to take his weapons and a shard from Hansen. Paco ends up hanged; Babs tells you she fled to Kenya.
- **Tell them to leave Dogtown** → put Paco in the trunk of his car and drive him to the **Grand Imperial Mall**. 48 hours later he asks to meet by the **Afterlife**: he has joined the Scavengers and leaves you the **Carmen** on a chair.
- **Call your friends** (River, Panam or Rogue, depending on the jobs you finished) → Panam smuggles them out or River finds generators in an NCPD evidence locker; Rogue sends you to River. After 24 hours they leave a crate in their tent at the garrison with the Carmen, a **Skill Shard: Solo** and €$8,000.

## Rewards

- 1,832 XP and 167 Street Cred
- Depending on the choice: Carmen, Skill Shard: Solo and €$8,000
