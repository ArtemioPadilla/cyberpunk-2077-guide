**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, sends you to the **Horrigan family home** on Gibson St., **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his second set: it opens after **Race to the Top**, **Serious Side Effects** and **Error 404**, in **Act 2**. **Juliet Horrigan**, one of his favorite netrunners, steals data from **Zetatech** for him, but she has gone silent since her last heist two weeks ago. The data comes first; finding out what happened comes second.

> **Order:** go to Juliet's home → look for the chip → (optional) find out what happened to Juliet → find a way to open the safe → open it → leave the datashard at the Drop Point.

## Getting in

- **Front door:** takes **Technical Ability**.
- **Back door:** takes **Body**.
- **Neither:** climb onto the front roof and go in through the open upstairs bathroom window.

Search the ground and upper floors: you'll find a **locked safe** holding Juliet's shard, and signs that something terrible happened. The key can only be in the **basement**. If you use the computer near the safe, a basement camera shows you what happened.

## The basement

**Rose**, Juliet's sister, wanders down there, lost to **cyberpsychosis**. Your only option is to **neutralize** her. Take the key next to Juliet's body, go back to the safe and take the shard. The notes around the house tell how Juliet tried to care for her to the end.
