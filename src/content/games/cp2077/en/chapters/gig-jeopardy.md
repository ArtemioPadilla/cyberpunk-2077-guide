**Jeopardy** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at **La Catrina**, a funeral home and crematorium on the corner of Phalarope and Plover Streets in **Vista del Rey**. It belongs to Padre's **first set** and opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. **Jim Greyer** smuggled unstable shards slotted inside his own head; on his last run the daemon got loose and fried his brain. His body ended up at La Catrina with the client's shard still inside, and it has to come out before he is cremated. The funeral home is legal, but the **Valentinos** control it and use it to dispose of bodies and traffic goods.

> **Order:** go to the funeral home → get inside the morgue → look for Jim Greyer's body → find him with your scanner → pull the shard from his body → leave the crematorium → deposit the shard in the Drop Point.

## Getting in

The main entrance on Plover Street opens onto a lobby and a chapel. The two employee-only doors to the morgue, one in the lobby and one in the chapel, have Valentinos guards you must **distract** to get past. The other option is the **back door** or the **garage**, on Phalarope Street.

## The morgue

It is in the basement, with two Valentinos and several bodies on the tables. Use your **scanner** to find which one is Greyer, pull the shard and leave the crematorium. Finish by depositing it in the Drop Point.

> **Don't leave without:** to the right of the lobby there is a refrigerated room behind a Technical Ability door; it holds weapons and components.
