**Raymond Chandler Evening** is given by **Pepe Najarro**, the bartender at **El Coyote Cojo** in The Glen (Heywood). It starts when you walk into the bar after **Heroes**, in Act 2. Johnny narrates the whole job like a noir detective.

> **Order:** El Coyote Cojo → the Valentinos → Pepe → scan Cynthia and tail her from a distance → talk to her → leave and call Pepe.

## The Valentinos

Two **Valentinos**, Juan and Pedro, are harassing a drunk corpo, **Joseph Odman**. Talk them into leaving or fight; with the **Streetkid** lifepath you can tell them Mama Welles would not like blood in her bar. **If you let them beat Joseph up, the job fails.** The corpo pays you for defending him, and Pepe offers you a drink and asks you to find out who his wife, **Cynthia**, is cheating with.

## Tailing Cynthia

Leave the bar and head right (northeast). Scan Cynthia and **follow her from a distance**: if you get too close, she notices and the job fails (Johnny warns you at about 15 m). If you start shooting at a nearby **Assault in Progress**, the gunfire scares her off too. And do not dawdle: the job can fail if you leave it too long.

## In the building

Cynthia argues with a man. You can cut the conversation short or hear her out: the man is not her lover but **Juan, her ripperdoc**. Her whole appearance is artificial, the result of implants.

## Choices

Leave the building and call Pepe:

- **Tell him the truth** → Pepe is shocked; then you tell him he **has a right to feel cheated** or that **Cynthia loves him and he should not give up**.
- **Tell him to talk to Cynthia himself.**

Pepe pays you either way. If you told him to **save his marriage**, a day or two later Cynthia calls to thank you and sends you **€$2,210** more. Otherwise, she tells you Pepe threw her out and she now lives on the street.

## Rewards

576 XP, 864 Street Cred and €$2,200–7,840 (plus Cynthia's €$2,210).
