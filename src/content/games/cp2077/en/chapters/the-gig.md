**The Gig** wraps up **The Rescue**. On the elevator ride down after saving **Sandra Dorsett** from the Scavengers, V calls the fixer **Wakako Okada** to confirm the job, and she tells you to come by for your pay when you can. The job sits in your journal from then on, but **you can't finish it in Act 1**: while the NCPD lockdown holds Watson, you can't reach Westbrook. It opens at the start of **Act 2**, once V recovers from **The Heist** and the lockdown is lifted. There is no combat and no choice; you just collect.

> **Order:** wait for the lockdown to end → go to Wakako's pachinko parlor (Jig-Jig Street, Japantown) → get paid → go to Cassius Ryder's clinic → take the free tattoo from his inventory.

## Collecting from Wakako

Go to Wakako's **pachinko parlor** on **Jig-Jig Street** in **Japantown** (Westbrook). Talk to her: she transfers **€$3,380** for Sandra's rescue and tells you there's an extra gift waiting for you at **Cassius Ryder**'s clinic. It's a good moment to ask her for more gigs: Wakako is Westbrook's fixer.

## Cassius Ryder's tattoo

Visit the ripperdoc **Cassius Ryder** (Ryder's Clinic, Pershing Street, Northside, Watson). His inventory lists the **Tattoo: Tyger Claws Dermal Imprint** at **zero cost**: take it and have it installed. It goes in the **hands** slot and uses no cyberware capacity. It gives the **Smart Link** function: smart weapons lock on faster and their reticle gets smaller. If you use smart weapons it's worth having from the start of Act 2; if not, it's still free.

## Rewards

- **€$3,380** (from Wakako).
- **Tattoo: Tyger Claws Dermal Imprint**, free at Cassius Ryder's clinic.
- The wiki gives no XP or Street Cred for this job.
