**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, gives you this sabotage job in **Rancho Coronado (Santo Domingo)**, at a garage on Oak St. Santo Domingo only opens in **Act 2**, after **The Heist**. It is one of the three gigs in his first set (with **Race to the Top** and **Serious Side Effects**); finishing them opens the second. **6th Street** backs **Jefferson Peralez** for mayor and runs servers that spew propaganda against **Weldon Holt**. El Capitán's client wants that machine gone.

> **Order:** enter the garage area → find the garage with the servers → install the malware → leave the area.

## How to do it

The area is full of **6th Street** members: most in an open yard, plus one on the **roof** overlooking it. Spot him before you move, because he sees you from above. The garage with the servers is in that same area. Install the malware on the servers and leave the area to close the gig. The wiki gives no more detail on the route.
