**Sacrum Profanum** (also **Losing My Religion**) is an encounter at the **Cargo Bay** in the **Northside** docks (Watson), available after **The Rescue**. A monk with forcibly installed cyberware tells you that **Maelstrom** kidnapped him and his brother and gave them implants, which their religion forbids. They let him go, but his brother is still held in a nearby abandoned warehouse. He asks you to hurry and **not to kill** the Maelstromers.

> **Order:** talk to the monk → go to the abandoned warehouse → defeat the gangoons → free the captive monk → talk to him.

## The warehouse

It is surrounded by shipping containers and has three ways in:

- A **grate** on the south side, with a **Technical Ability** check.
- **Climb the containers** on the west or east side to sneak past.
- The **front gate**: it alerts them at once.

Inside, deal with the Maelstromers without killing them if you want to please the monk: non-lethal takedowns, blunt weapons or the Pax mod. Free the captive. **To his left** is **Fenrir**, an iconic M221 Saratoga.

## Choices

- **Kill no one** → the monk thanks you and hopes you stay on the path of peace.
- **Kill any of them** → he calls you evil: his suffering doesn't justify the deaths of others.
- **Take too long** → the job fails, but Fenrir is still in the warehouse.

You can also start the job by walking straight into the warehouse: the dialogue changes slightly and it ends when you free the monk.

If you saved them, later (Act 2) you can find both monks at the Cherry Blossom Market near Jig-Jig Street, talking about whether a digital copy of a person is a person; Johnny has an opinion.

> **Don't leave without:** **Fenrir**, to the left of the captive monk.

## Rewards

- 872 XP and 1,306 Street Cred.
- **Fenrir** (optional): its rounds deal electrical damage with a chance to disable enemy cyberware, and it has +20% Shock chance.
