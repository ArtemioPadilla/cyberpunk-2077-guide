**God Bless This Mess** is the gift from **Sebastian "Padre" Ibarra**, the Heywood fixer, when you finish **all his gigs** in the district. It was added in Patch 1.5. His gigs open after **The Heist**, in **Act 2**.

> **Order:** finish Padre's 11 gigs → read his message → open your stash → take the Seraph.

## The gigs that count

- **Set 1:** Fifth Column, Jeopardy, Life's Work, On a Tight Leash.
- **Set 2:** Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta, Old Friends, Psychofan.
- **Set 3:** Eye for an Eye, Sr. Ladrillo's Private Collection, The Lord Giveth and Taketh Away.
- **Set 4:** Going Up or Down? (the last one).

Each set opens when you finish the one before it.

## Collecting the pistol

Padre texts you to say thanks. Open the **stash** and take the **Seraph**.

## Rewards

**Seraph**, an iconic variant of the Constitutional Arms Liberty pistol. Each hit raises the chance to set the enemy **Burning**, and once they burn, your bullets deal more damage to them. It is a quest reward, so it cannot be missed.
