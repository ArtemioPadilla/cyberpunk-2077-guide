**Brigitte**, the Voodoo Boys' leader, gives it right after I Walk the Line. She takes you to the **Maglev tunnel beneath the Pacifica Serenity Bible Church**, the physical home of their data fortress, **Rezo Agwe**. She wants to use Johnny's engram as bait to contact **Alt Cunningham** beyond the **Blackwall**. Inside you play **Never Fade Away**: a Johnny memory from **2013**, with the Samurai gig, Alt's abduction and the raid on **Arasaka Tower**. When you come back, everything depends on what you did with the NetWatch agent at the GIM.

> **Order:** follow Brigitte → talk to her → get in the ice bath → wait for the connection → talk to Brigitte → investigate the source of the noises → unblock Johnny's memories (Never Fade Away) → talk to Brigitte → touch the Blackwall → talk to Alt → talk to Brigitte → leave the chapel (in peace, guns blazing, or fighting Placide).

> **Get ready:** if in I Walk the Line you **made the deal with Mosley**, a **mandatory fight against Placide** and the remaining Voodoo Boys awaits at the end: have weapons and healing ready, and quickhacks for Breach Protocol. In Never Fade Away you use Johnny's gear, not yours. **Save before coming back from cyberspace**, in case you want to try both exits on the Voodoo Boys branch.

## Rezo Agwe

**Follow Brigitte** to the Maglev tunnel, **talk to her**, **get in the ice bath** and **wait for the connection**. In cyberspace, **talk to Brigitte**, **investigate the source of the noises** and **unblock Johnny's memories**. This is where Never Fade Away begins.

## Never Fade Away (Johnny, 2013)

You control Johnny Silverhand in one of his memories. It's his version of events: **it isn't faithful to what really happened**, and Alt will tell you so later.

**The gig and the dressing room.**

1. **Grab the microphone.** Scenes from the Samurai gig flash past, ending with Johnny firing his Malorian.
2. **Go to the dressing room** and **talk to Kerry**: they argue because Kerry says Samurai isn't a one-man show.
3. **Talk to Alt.** There's an automatic intimate scene; afterwards Alt tells him she's leaving Samurai.
4. **Look for drugs** (optional) and **talk to Alt** again.

**The alley.** Alt leaves first; Johnny catches up. Some thugs close in.

1. **Protect Alt from the thugs.** You get a few shots off, but a thug with **Mantis Blades** cuts Johnny down.
2. **Fight for your life.** You watch Alt being taken away in a van.

**Milt Nauman's clinic.** The **media Lyle Thompson** gets you to the ripperdoc **Milt Nauman**. **Talk to the ripperdoc**, **talk to Thompson** (Alt was working on software for ITS that Arasaka wanted: Soulkiller) and **leave the clinic**.

**The Atlantis.** **Go to the Atlantis**; Thompson stays in the car.

1. **Find Rogue.** You can **ask around for her** (optional). Talk to the **bouncer, Engels**, on the second floor.
2. **Sit next to Rogue**, who's waiting with the nomad **Santiago**, and **convince her** to help you raid Arasaka Tower.
3. **Arasaka agents** storm the place: **fight for your life** and **defeat the agents**.
4. **Escape the Atlantis:** call the elevator, get in, **wait for Santiago and Rogue** and ride down to the parking lot. **Defeat the agents** waiting below.
5. **Get in Johnny's Porsche** and **escape** the Arasaka agents.

**The plan.** **Plan your next move** and **talk to the rest of the crew**: you stage a diversion in front of the tower (Johnny's fans and a call to Nancy).

**Arasaka Tower.**

1. **Find the mainframe.** On the way: **destroy the turret**, then **the turret by the mainframe door**.
2. **Open the mainframe door**, **arm the explosives** and **find Alt**.
3. **Defeat the Arasaka agents.** The mainframe is guarded by **Akira**, Arasaka's bodyguard (see "Boss — Akira").
4. **Check on Alt.** Johnny kills **Toshiro**, her handler, and unplugs Alt too early: **that kills her**.
5. **Talk to Thompson.** Johnny lashes out at Lyle, who recorded everything, and Rogue stops him. Rogue confirms Alt is dead.

## The Blackwall and Alt

You return to Rezo Agwe. **Talk to Johnny** about the memory, then to Brigitte. She takes you to the Blackwall with her netrunners: **touch the Blackwall** and **talk to Alt**. What happens next depends on I Walk the Line:

**If you made the deal with Mosley (NetWatch):**

- Mosley's shard carried a **NetWatch tracker**. When the Voodoo Boys contact Alt, NetWatch moves in; Alt takes them for NetWatch and **kills Brigitte and all her connected netrunners**.
- Leaving cyberspace, Placide and the remaining Voodoo Boys are on alert. **You have to defeat Placide**: he carries the **Chapel Access Token** that opens the church door. game8 says the fight happens "regardless of if you stealthed".

**If you followed Placide's plan (the Voodoo Boys):**

- With Alt's help, Brigitte's netrunners **cross the Blackwall**.
- Afterwards, Brigitte makes you leave. **Choose:** leave in peace or **settle the score** by force (see "Choices").

**Talk to Brigitte** and **leave the chapel**.

## Choices

The main choice (NetWatch or Voodoo Boys) was already made in **I Walk the Line**. There's only one more here, and only on the Voodoo Boys branch:

- **Leave in peace** → you walk out of the church under Brigitte's protection. Placide, disappointed he couldn't kill you, texts you later to watch your back.
- **Settle the score (violence)** → you kill the Voodoo Boys in the church. **Generic Voodoo Boys stop spawning on the streets** (except in **Dogtown**), and Phantom Liberty's **The Damned** gets extra dialogue and options. If you leave Placide alive, he messages you over the Net to watch your back.
- **NetWatch branch: the Placide fight** → mandatory. Brigitte and her netrunners die no matter what.
- **What you tell Johnny after leaving the church** → IGN says it "can impact your relationship with him", with no further detail. Johnny affinity doesn't change any ending (see Tapeworm).

## Boss — Akira

He's the Arasaka bodyguard guarding the mainframe in Johnny's memory. You face him with the gear the memory gives you, alongside Rogue and Santiago. Sources don't detail his attacks: treat him as an elite enemy, use cover and let your companions draw his attention while you shoot.

## Boss — Placide (NetWatch branch)

- **His abilities:** Combat Stim, Sandevistan Mk.2, Charge Jump, Kerenzikov and EMP grenades.
- **He's immune to the System Collapse quickhack**: don't waste RAM on it.
- **game8 tip:** lower his resistances with **Breach Protocol** at an access point. He **tends to stand in place a lot**, so headshots work well.
- When he falls, take the **Chapel Access Token** to open the church door and loot his body.

## What opens here

- **Tapeworm:** finishing Transmission triggers a stage. After this job V takes the **Omega blockers**.
- **Phantom Liberty (Dog Eat Dog):** **Songbird calls you** once the meeting with the Voodoo Boys and Alt is resolved. It's the earliest point to start the expansion.
- **Send in the Clowns:** the wiki lists it as a next job, but exactly what unlocks it is unclear.
- **The Damned** (Phantom Liberty): changes depending on how you left the church.

> **Don't leave without:** if you fought Placide, loot his body: game8 says he drops the **Timeworn Trench Coat**, a missable legendary, and the wiki's equipment table for Placide lists it. And before leaving the chapel, think calmly about leaving in peace or guns blazing: Voodoo-Boy-free streets are permanent.
