**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, hires you for this theft at the **Cytech factory** on Olivia St., in **Arroyo (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his third set, with **Cuckoo's Nest**: it opens after **Breaking News**, **Hacking the Hacker** and **Family Matters**, in **Act 2**. **Rihanna Kumar** was a star **Cytech** engineer until the company threw her out. Now she wants the data from the projects she worked on to relaunch her career: her "severance package".

> **Order:** enter the factory grounds → go into the factory → get to the second floor → reach the server room → retrieve Rihanna's data files → leave the factory.

## How to do it

The wiki only lists the objectives, with no walkthrough. What it does say is that **veteran guards** watch the building: go in carefully, get up to the **second floor** and find the **server room**. Download the files and leave the grounds to close the gig.
