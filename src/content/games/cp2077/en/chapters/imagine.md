**Imagine** is your first meeting with the **Zen Master** (also called the Strange Monk), a monk who appears in **Reconciliation Park**, Corpo Plaza (City Center). It opens after **Love Like Fire**, at the start of **Act 2**. It is the first of four meditations.

> **Order:** approach the stranger → talk to him → do the braindance meditation → leave.

## The meditation

Talk to the monk about meditation. He offers you a **meditative braindance** devoted to the element of **earth**. There is no combat and no attribute check: follow it to the end and leave. Johnny distrusts the monk from the start.

## The chain

- **Imagine:** Reconciliation Park (City Center), earth.
- **Stairway To Heaven:** Japantown (Westbrook).
- **Poem Of The Atoms:** North Oak (Westbrook), fire.
- **Meetings Along The Edge:** Jackson Plains (Badlands), air. At the end the monk leaves you an **altar** for your apartment.

Each session appears on the map after you finish the one before.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred. The chain's reward comes with the last meditation.
