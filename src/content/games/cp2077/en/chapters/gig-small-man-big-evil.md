**Jae-Hyun Lee** picks out people who are alone with nowhere to go, kidnaps them and hands them to the **Scavengers**, who strip their biomonitors and other implants; the victims end up as "property" of men like **Jotaro Shobo**, the target of Monster Hunt. **Regina Jones** wants this head of the hydra chopped off. Jae-Hyun is in the **rooftop slums** near Allen St (Kabuki, Watson). It is in **set 5 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 4 (Shark in the Water and Flight of the Cheetah). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to the slums → find Jae-Hyun in the restricted part of the slums → neutralize him → pick him up and leave the area → carry the body to the fixer's vehicle.

## How to do it

- Scavengers guard the restricted part of the slums. Jae-Hyun has Combat Stim and frag grenades.
- After you neutralize him you have to **carry him** to the fixer's vehicle: clear the way out first, or slip back out the way you came.
- If you kill him, his niche later shows up at the Columbarium.

## Rewards

- €$6,400, 1,000 XP and 770 Street Cred.
