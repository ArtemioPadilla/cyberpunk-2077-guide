Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It comes from **Leon Rinder**, the ex-BARGHEST soldier from the gig **The Man Who Killed Jason Foreman**. If you spare him and take his deal, he sends you the coordinates of his garage near **Terra Cognita**, in Dogtown, and a text with the code **1975** for the door.

> **Order:** spare Rinder → go to the garage → code 1975 → take his things.

## The garage

Go to the marker and enter **1975** on the door keypad. With a cyberdeck you can also open it with the terminal's Remote Deactivation hack. Inside are **Leon Rinder's Computer** and a cache with the **Dezerter**, the iconic DB-2 Testera. Also take the shard **Leon Rinder's notes**.

The door has no power until you start Rinder's gig, so you can't get there early.

## Known bugs

According to the wiki (patch 2.02, PC), the job can complete on its own when you pass near the cache even if you never started it, and the keypad may not respond; with the job active, Remote Deactivation gets around it. If you looted the garage before getting Rinder's location, the job can no longer be completed.

> **Don't leave without:** the Dezerter and Rinder's notes shard.

## Rewards

- Dezerter (iconic DB-2 Testera)
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
