The son of **Arati Kapoor**, co-owner of the Masala Studios restaurant chain, died of a **glitter** overdose, a drug said to hook you for life. She wants the lab where it is cooked wiped off the map: it's in the **basement** of a building on **Rovinj St** (Little China, Watson), and she doesn't mind if some of the cooks go down with it. **Regina Jones** warns that **Tyger Claws** may be guarding it. It is in **set 8 of 8**, the last one: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 7 (Freedom of the Press and Lousy Kleppers). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** enter the building → enter the basement → find the lab → destroy the equipment used to make glitter → leave the area.

## How to do it

- The wiki doesn't detail the route: scan the building when you arrive to spot the Tyger Claws and cameras before heading down.
- In the basement, find the lab and **destroy all the marked equipment**.
- Leave the area to close the gig.

## Rewards

- €$8,440, 1,000 XP and 647 Street Cred.
