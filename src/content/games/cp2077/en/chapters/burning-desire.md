**Burning Desire** is an encounter on **Farrier** street, north of **Megabuilding H10** (Little China, Watson), available after **The Rescue**. You hear a pantsless man moaning and clutching his crotch: it's **Jesse Johnson**, and his **Mr. Studd** implant is malfunctioning. He needs a ripperdoc now. It is the same job in both acts: in Act 1 it's called Burning Desire and, if you find it after The Heist, **Night Moves**.

> **Order:** find out what's wrong → talk to him → decide whether to help → grab a car and pick him up → take him to a ripperdoc → stop in front of the clinic → wait for his call → talk to him.

## The drive

1. If you agree, the last car you used appears (if you have none available, a stand-in car).
2. Get him in and drive to **Cassius Ryder**'s clinic in Northside. There are **several roadblocks** on the way: find detours without losing time.
3. Stop right in front of the clinic. Then go about your business until Jesse calls.

## Choices

- **Help him and arrive in time** → Jesse survives, calls you after the operation and transfers €$1,940.
- **Help him and don't make it** → the implant explodes on the way and Jesse dies.
- **Leave him** → he dies from the malfunction.

If he dies, a niche with his name later appears at the Columbarium in North Oak (Westbrook).

## Rewards

- €$1,940 (only if he survives), 261 XP and 391 Street Cred.
