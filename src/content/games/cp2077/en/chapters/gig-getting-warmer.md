**Getting Warmer...** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer. It takes place in **8ug8ear's apartment** on Salinas Street in **Japantown**. It belongs to Wakako's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the second set (**Until Death Do Us Part**, **Greed Never Pays** and **Olive Branch**). **8ug8ear**, a netrunner who works for Wakako, left the **Tyger Claws** hanging on a Kiroshi warehouse job. They came to her place and she hid in the Net, shielded by software that kills her if she is disconnected. The Tyger Claws are just waiting for her to come out.

> **Order:** enter the building → find 8ug8ear → find a way to disconnect her safely → take the coolant → lower her body temperature → disconnect her → carry her to the fixer's car.

## The building

The whole building is full of Tyger Claws, and the residents comment on the situation. Sneak or fight your way to 8ug8ear's room.

## Disconnecting her

You find her unconscious in her netrunner chair. Since she cannot speak, she writes to you on the screens: before pulling her out of the Net you must inject her with the **coolant from the bathroom**, or she overheats and dies. Take the coolant, apply it, and only then disconnect her. Wakako was clear: "She's useless to me dead." If she dies, her niche later appears at the Columbarium.

## Getting her out

Carry her unconscious body to Wakako's contact, who is waiting with a car, and put her in the trunk to complete the gig.

## Rewards

- €$9,300, 1,000 XP and 1,309 Street Cred.
- **Skill Shard: Netrunner** (experience for the Netrunner skill).
