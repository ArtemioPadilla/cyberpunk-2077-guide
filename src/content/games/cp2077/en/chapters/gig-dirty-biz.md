Televangelist **Bryce Stone**, a friend of **Regina Jones**, lost his son Bobby: he was kidnapped and murdered, and the NCPD dropped the case for lack of evidence. The braindance of the murder is circulating in edited form; Regina wants the **raw cut** to identify the killer. It was made by two XBD editors, **Gottfrid** and **Fredrik Persson** (father and son), in buildings off Shipyard Way (Northside, Watson), guarded by **Maelstrom**. A netrunner Regina hired earlier, L33tHax, died trying. It is in **set 2 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the five gigs of set 1 (Monster Hunt, Playing for Keeps, Hippocratic Oath, Many Ways to Skin a Cat and Rite of Passage). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** find Gottfrid and Fredrik's studio → find the XBD recording → take recording BD 9430 → leave the area → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## At the studio

- Gottfrid has plenty of Maelstromers guarding him: scan before you push in.
- In the studio you find the Perssons editing, off guard. They beg for their lives and swear they only edit what Maelstrom hands them.
- Recording **BD 9430** is on a shelf in the editing room. Take it yourself or make them tell you where it is.

## Choices

- Before you leave you can **kill one, both or neither**; the gig doesn't require it. If you kill only Fredrik, Gottfrid breaks down.

## Rewards

- €$1,930, 314 XP and 707 Street Cred.
