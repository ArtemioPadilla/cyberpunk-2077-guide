**Hwangbo** did small jobs for the **Tyger Claws** and stole from them; now he's marked for death. **Regina Jones** has already found a nomad smuggler: you have to get Hwangbo from the **motel on Martin St** (Northside, Watson) to the meeting point without him being spotted on the street. It is in **set 4 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the three gigs of set 3 (Backs Against the Wall, Woman of La Mancha and Catch a Tyger's Toe). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** find Hwangbo → meet him in room 1237 → talk to him → escort him (optional: use his car) → go to the meeting point → talk to him before leaving.

## How to do it

1. Go up to **room 1237** and talk to Hwangbo.
2. Shortly after, Tyger Claws show up: take them down or sneak past.
3. Optional: use **Hwangbo's car**, parked a short distance from the motel.
4. Drive to the meeting point, where a group of **Aldecaldos** is waiting. Talk to Hwangbo; once he gets out of the car, the gig is done.

## Rewards

- €$3,000, 1,000 XP and 707 Street Cred.
