You don't accept **Tapeworm**: **Johnny Silverhand** gives it, and it's a main job that **triggers on its own at several points in Act 2**, disappears from the journal and comes back later. In each stage you talk to Johnny and the two of you come to understand each other (or not). Where each talk happens depends on the order you play the lines in; the last stage is always at the **Hotel Pistis Sophia** (West Wind Estate, Pacifica). Completing it **starts Act 3** (Nocturne Op55N1) and Johnny asks you to see Rogue, which opens **Chippin' In**. This page also explains **Johnny affinity** and why it doesn't do what many people think.

> **Order:** stage 1 (talk to Johnny) → stage 2 (talk to Johnny; optional pills) → stage 3 (sit and rest; talk to Johnny) → stage 4 (talk to Johnny) → final stage: wake up at the Hotel Pistis Sophia → enter through the window → open the cache → talk to Johnny → leave the hotel through the window.

> **Get ready:** there's nothing to prepare: the stages fire on their own. The only thing that matters is **listening to Johnny**: if you don't take the **pseudoendotrizine** and keep talking to him, you learn his motives. Before the next job (**Chippin' In**) **make a manual save**: your answers at the grave decide endings.

## When each stage fires

Per the wiki and IGN, a Tapeworm stage triggers right after you finish each of these jobs:

- **Automatic Love** (Evelyn line).
- **Life During Wartime** (Hellman line).
- **Search and Destroy** (Takemura line).
- **Transmission** (Voodoo Boys line).

game8 counts only three triggers (Transmission, Life During Wartime and Search and Destroy) and leaves out Automatic Love: sources disagree, but in practice it doesn't matter, since you have to finish all four lines anyway.

## The stages

- **Stage 1:** talk to Johnny. At first he's aggressive and argumentative.
- **Stage 2:** talk to Johnny. You can **take the pseudoendotrizine** (optional) to shut him off... or not take it and keep talking.
- **Stage 3:** **sit and rest** and talk to Johnny. The location changes with your order: outside the Voodoo Boys' chapel, at the Sunset Motel or at the Megabuilding (IGN).
- **Stage 4:** talk to Johnny.

**Final stage: the Hotel Pistis Sophia.** You wake up in Pacifica, by the old hotel on **Santa Maria St.** It's where Johnny stayed in 2003 after deserting from the army, before he became Johnny Silverhand.

1. **Enter through the window.**
2. **Open the cache:** it's the **vent under the counter**. Inside are his army **dog tags**.
3. **Talk to Johnny.**
4. **Leave the hotel through the window.**

## Choices

- **Taking the pseudoendotrizine (stage 2) or talking more** → IGN says blocking Johnny with the pills or listening "will have no effect on the story". The wiki adds that if you don't take them and talk more, you learn his motives (his hatred of corporations). **It changes no ending:** listen if you care about the character.
- **Pacifist run** → if you killed no one yourself when you had the option, Johnny gets unique dialogue: he says V is the gentle type who doesn't kill but lets others do the killing.

## Johnny affinity

Your relationship with Johnny has an internal affinity percentage that rises at fixed story points. Milestones per the wiki:

- **1%, "Play it Again":** Love Like Fire.
- **10%, "Breaking the Ice":** Tapeworm phase 1.
- **15%, "Metamorphosis":** Tapeworm phase 2.
- **20%, "Mikoshi":** Tapeworm phase 3.
- **25%, "Fucking entropy":** the last phase of Tapeworm.
- **30%, "Double Life":** A Cool Metal Fire (during Chippin' In).
- **40%, "Synergy":** a specific dialogue in Chippin' In; otherwise Blistering Love or Holdin' On.
- **50%, "Shadows of the Past":** Blistering Love; otherwise Holdin' On or A Like Supreme.
- **60–70%, "You Only Live Once":** Holdin' On (60%) and A Like Supreme (70%); otherwise only 60% via A Like Supreme.
- Phantom Liberty adds more milestones, such as "Fuck the System" (85%).

**What affinity does:** per the wiki, it **doesn't seem to have any major impact on the game**.

**The 70% myth.** Many people believe you need 70% affinity for the **secret ending** ((Don't Fear) The Reaper). **It's false.** The wiki (on Chippin' In and on Johnny's page) and game8 agree: **the secret ending depends only on your answers in the grave conversation in Chippin' In**, not on affinity. Get that conversation right and forget the percentage.

## What opens here

- **Nocturne Op55N1** (Act 3): starts when Tapeworm completes. The **point of no return** comes when you approach the Embers entrance, not here: you can still finish everything outstanding.
- **Chippin' In** (Johnny and Rogue's chain): Johnny asks you to see Rogue. Your answers at the grave decide whether **Blistering Love** follows (needed for **The Sun** via Rogue) and the **secret ending**; get them wrong and he sends you to Kerry (**Holdin' On**). **Save before the grave.**
- **Before Act 3, check the ending gates:**
  - **The Devil:** always available; the achievement needs Takemura alive (Search and Destroy).
  - **The Star:** complete **Queen of the Highway** (Panam).
  - **The Sun:** the right answers in Chippin' In, then **Blistering Love**.
  - **Temperance:** have the Panam or Rogue route open (or the secret one) and let Johnny keep the body.
  - **Secret:** the Chippin' In answers, then wait about 5 minutes on Misty's rooftop.

> **Don't leave without:** opening the **cache under the counter** at the Hotel Pistis Sophia, which is part of the objective, and saving before moving on to Chippin' In.

- **Johnny's tank top.**
