**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, gives you this gig at the **Warbler Lake Hazardous Waste Facility** on Anderson St., in **Red Peaks**. It's from her third set, with **MIA**: it opens after **No Fixers**, **Big Pete's Got Big Problems** and **Trevor's Last Ride**, in **Act 2**. Red Joe, a Badlands kid with a gift for boxing, has no coach. A next-gen sparring bot that still works was dumped at that facility: download its software before it gets recycled. Dakota warns you that the man running the landfill is a real piece of work.

> **Order:** go to the landfill → find the broken bot → get to it → pull the shard from the bot → leave it at the Drop Point.

## How to do it

The possible enemy is the Red Peaks **junk vendor** who runs the place. If you **kill or insult** him during the gig, he's gone for good as a vendor.

The wiki shows no walkthrough, but its page source has hidden notes (unconfirmed):

- There's an easier **back entrance** than the one the game points you to: a tiny office with one guy and a camera above its door. Disable the camera, neutralize the guy, sneak to the van, take the shard and leave the same way.
- You can also **buy the bot** from the vendor for €$37,000, or half that with a **Cool** check, and skip the fight.

## Rewards
