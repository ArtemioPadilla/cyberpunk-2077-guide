**Small Man, Big Mouth** exists only with the **Streetkid** lifepath. After Jackie's ofrenda in **Heroes**, **Kirk Sawyer**, an old acquaintance of V's, calls with a job and asks to meet at **El Coyote Cojo** (The Glen, Heywood). It is an Act 2 job; the wiki suggests level 34.

> **Order:** meet Kirk at El Coyote Cojo → go to Tirempire Auto Shop (Arroyo) → defeat the Valentinos → open the van → look for Kirk and Big Joe → decide what to do with Thomas Hays.

## Kirk's plan

Kirk says experimental **Militech** cyberware, fresh from a lab in Shanghai, is up for grabs. He sends you the coordinates: **Tirempire Auto Shop** in Arroyo (Santo Domingo), where he and **Big Joe** are waiting. Your part is to pull the cyberware out of the back of a delivery van. Kirk swears the place is clear and that the take will be split evenly.

## The auto shop

It is not clear: several **Valentinos** thugs are there. Take them out, reach the van and open the back doors. There is no cyberware, only a **fake Samurai jacket**. Take it.

## Choices

You hear gunshots in the distance. At the meeting point, the Valentino **Thomas Hays** and two thugs are waiting: they lured Kirk into a trap to kill him, and Kirk and Big Joe are already dead.

- **Walk away** → the job ends.
- **Fight** Hays and his thugs → the job also ends.

## Rewards

The **SEMURAI crystaljock bomber**, a cheap knock-off of the Samurai jacket (blue and pink, with the Crystaljock logo), which you take from the van. The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred.
