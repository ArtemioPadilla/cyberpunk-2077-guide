**Happy Together** happens in your own building. From the start of **Act 2** (after **Love Like Fire**), on the **floor below your apartment** in **Megabuilding H10** (Little China), two cops, **Nadia Petrova** and **Juan Mendez**, are knocking on **Barry Lewis**'s door. Barry is an ex-cop who left the force, sunk in depression after a friend's death, and he won't open up. They ask you to see what's going on.

> **Order:** talk to the cops → knock on Barry's door → come back a few hours later → talk to Barry (all dialogue) → visit Andrew's niche at the columbarium → talk to the cops → check on Barry.

## Barry

The first time, Barry won't open for you either; the game asks you to **come back in a few hours**. When you return, he lets you in and talks about a very ugly case that got swept under the rug and about losing his friend **Andrew**. **Go through all of his dialogue options**: it's the only way to get the optional objective to visit Andrew's grave. Otherwise, the options won't appear at the columbarium.

## Choices

- **Visit Andrew at the Columbarium** (**North Oak**) before going back to the cops → you learn Andrew wasn't a person but **his pet turtle**. Tell the cops: Mendez opens up to Barry about his own tough cases and Barry finally lets them in.
- **Talk to the cops without visiting the columbarium** → some time later **Barry takes his own life**, and you find Mendez distraught outside his door. It's permanent.
