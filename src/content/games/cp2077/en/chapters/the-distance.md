**The Distance** is a **repeatable** job added in Patch 2.1 (the wiki classes it as a side job). It opens once you finish **The Beast In Me**, Claire's racing chain, and let several days pass: **Jim Jarry**, the street-race organizer, texts you. It is Act 2 content.

> **Order:** accept Jim's offer → pick one of the 4 crossed-flag markers on the map → sit on the marked bench → pay €$2,000 → race → open the chest at the finish line if you won.

## How to do it

Once you accept, **four races** appear on the map, marked with **crossed flags**. Each repeats a track from The Beast In Me:

- **City Center**
- **Badlands**
- **Santo Domingo**
- **The Big Race**

The tracks run through the Badlands, Heywood (Vista del Rey), Santo Domingo (Arroyo) and Westbrook (Charter Hill). At each flag, sit on the marked bench and pay the **€$2,000** entry fee.

## Rewards

Per the wiki infobox, **€$10,000**; and if you finish first, the **AUTOFIXER'S DISCOUNT EXTRAVAGANZZZA!** shard from the chest by the finish line. Use it on the AUTOFIXER netpage: several shards combine on a single purchase, with diminishing returns (1 = 13%, 2 = 24%, 5 = 45%, 10 = 66%; 25 reach 100%).
