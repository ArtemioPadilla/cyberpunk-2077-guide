**Dakota Smith**, the fixer of **the Badlands**, hires you for this hit at the **New American Autoworks** garage on Sage Gulch Road, in **Jackson Plains**. It's from her second set, with **No Fixers** and **Trevor's Last Ride**: it opens after **Dancing on a Minefield** and **Flying Drugs**, in **Act 2**. **Big Pete** was a techie in Night City, made powerful enemies and now fixes cars for the **Wraiths**. Dakota owes those enemies a favor.

> **Order:** get inside Big Pete's garage → find him → neutralize him (or not) → leave the area.

## How to do it

The garage has **two turrets** (facing the road), **six cameras** (four outside, two inside) and **nine Wraiths** (five outside, four inside). A firefight ends it fast; stealth works too if you deal with the interior cameras and enemies quietly. The **basement** door is on the left side of the interior. Read the garage computers: one holds messages from his brother **Tiny Mike** begging for money.

To leave, the basement has a **utility tunnel** that comes out behind a communications tower, out of sight of enemies and cameras.

## Choices

Pete spots you and begs: he's wanted for selling a faulty signal jammer, and he offers **double** your pay to say you killed him. (If you read about Tiny Mike, you can point out he's broke; he claims that's no longer true.)

- **Spare him and take the bribe** → he wires you the eddies and promises to leave for good. Dakota is surprised but closes the contract.
- **Spare him and refuse** → he still leaves; Dakota closes the contract.
- **Kill him** → contract fulfilled and Dakota is pleased.

If you've done **Concrete Cage Trap**, Pete recognizes you for helping his brother, and you can talk about it with Tiny Mike at the Afterlife afterwards.

## Rewards

€$13,500, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred, plus the bribe if you take it.
