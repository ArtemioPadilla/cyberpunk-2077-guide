**Judy Álvarez** gives it as soon as Woodman tells you where Evelyn ended up. You go to **Jig-Jig Street** in Japantown (Westbrook), to the clinic of **Fingers** (Finn Gerstatt), the ripperdoc Woodman sold Evelyn to. You can let Judy know first, but it doesn't matter: **she waits in front of Fingers' office whether you call her or not**. It's a short job, with an easy-to-miss iconic and a single choice.

> **Order:** go to Jig-Jig Street → find Fingers' clinic → talk to the thugs → enter the clinic → talk to Judy → (optional) sit and talk to the joytoys → get into Fingers' office → talk to Fingers → interrogate him.

> **Get ready:** no fight is required. With enough **Body** you can force doors instead of waiting or talking (game8 gives **Body 5** for the clinic door and **Body 7** for Fingers' door). If you plan to use Fingers as a ripperdoc, decide beforehand what you'll do in the interrogation.

## Jig-Jig Street and the way in

Find Fingers' clinic on Jig-Jig Street. There are some **thugs** at the door: talk to them. You have several ways past:

- **Talk** to the thugs until they let you in.
- **Force the clinic door** with **Body 5** (game8).
- **Side entrance:** game8 notes a second entrance on the **left side of the building** that avoids the thugs.

## The waiting room and the office

Inside, talk to **Judy**. Fingers' door is shut and there's a queue:

- **Sit and talk to the joytoys** (optional): that's how you skip the queue.
- **Force Fingers' door** with **Body 7** (game8).

**Cottonmouth**, the iconic one-handed club, is in Fingers' office, but sources describe the spot differently: the wiki says "on the bed inside Fingers' office", the weapon page says "on a coffee table in Fingers' clinic" and game8 says "in Finger's bedroom". **Check the bed, the tables and the bedroom** before you carry on with the interrogation.

## The interrogation

Talk to Fingers, then **interrogate him** about Evelyn's whereabouts. At one point in the interrogation Fingers gets dragged onto the floor. There are several dialogue paths, but **in the end V and Judy always get the information**, and the lead sends you looking for an **XBD** (an illegal braindance) in the next job.

## Choices

- **Punching Fingers while he's on the floor** → Fingers is **no longer available as a ripperdoc** for good. Since Update 2.0 he no longer sells unique cyberware, so in practice you lose little (game8 still advises not punching him for his Sandevistans, but that advice looks like 1.x).
- **Not punching him** → he stays one more ripperdoc in Japantown.
- **How you get in** (talk, Body or side entrance) → doesn't change the outcome.

## What opens here

- **Disasterpiece:** Judy and V go looking for Evelyn's XBD. It's the next job in the line.
- It doesn't open or close any side chain. Judy's chain (ending in **Pyramid Song**) comes after Double Life.

> **Don't leave without:** **Cottonmouth**. It's easy to leave without it because it isn't on the objective's path: check the office and the bedroom before you leave the clinic.
