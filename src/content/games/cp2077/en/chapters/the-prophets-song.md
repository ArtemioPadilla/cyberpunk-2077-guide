**The Prophet's Song** starts with **Garry the Prophet** (Gerald Winkler), the conspiracy preacher on **Urmland Street**, next to **Misty's Esoterica** (Watson; the wiki files the job under Kabuki and places Garry in Little China). You can make progress in Act 1 by listening to his rants, but the full job only opens after **Playing for Time**: Johnny takes part in the second half. Garry sleeps until the afternoon; if he isn't there, come back later.

> **Order:** listen to the five rants → deal with the Aldecaldos → go to the old factory → talk to Johnny → hide and keep a lookout → seize the conspirators' chip → search the body → show the chip to the prophet → talk to his disciple → talk to Johnny.

## The rants

1. Talk to Garry **five times**, on separate visits, whenever he has a new topic. Giving him money is optional; the most you can donate goes up as you progress. Donating enough helps at the end.
2. During the fifth rant (nomads are werewolves), **two Aldecaldos** show up, offended. If you are a **Nomad** or have already finished **Queen of the Highway** and acknowledge them as allies, they leave without a fight. Otherwise you help Garry fight them off.
3. Garry tells you about a mysterious meeting in Kabuki.

## The meeting

1. Go to the marked **old factory** in Kabuki, talk to Johnny and hide at the spot the job marks.
2. Wait: some **Maelstrom** members and two corpos, **Jane Doe** and **John Doe**, show up, and Maelstrom hands them a chip.
3. **Don't let the corpos leave.** Take the **Mysterious Chip** and search the body. The wiki says in one place that it is on Jane Doe's body and in another that John Doe or a Maelstrom member carries it: check them all.

## Choices

- **Stop the corpos** → you get the chip. You go back to Garry, but he is gone: his disciple (Sue Glover) says he was taken away. If you donated enough to Garry, she tells you what she knows about the chip; if not, donate to her now to make her talk. Then talk to Johnny and the job ends.
- **Let them escape** → **the job fails**.
- Decrypting the chip from the shard menu is optional.
