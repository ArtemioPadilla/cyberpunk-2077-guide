**The Streetkid** is the Streetkid lifepath prologue, and you only play it if you pick that lifepath when creating V. The other two lifepaths have their own prologue (The Nomad and The Corpo-Rat); all three end by introducing you to **Jackie Welles** and lead into the same job, The Rescue. V grew up on the streets of Heywood, left for Atlanta and comes back years later no better off. The job comes from **Pepe Najarro**, the bartender at **El Coyote Cojo** (The Glen, Heywood): Pepe owes money to **Kirk Sawyer**, a small-time fixer who is threatening to break his legs. To settle that debt, V will have to steal a car at **Embers** (Westbrook). It is a short, linear job with no combat.

> **Order:** find Kirk on the second floor of El Coyote Cojo → talk to Kirk → leave the bar → talk to Padre and get in his car → ride with him to Embers → take the elevator to the garage → find the Rayfield (optional: find Rick) → disable the lock with Kirk's device → get in and "steal" the Rayfield → talk to the thief (Jackie) → talk to the police → talk to Jackie.

> **Get ready (creating V):** **difficulty** (Easy, Normal, Hard, Very Hard) can be changed whenever you like; since 2.1 it lives in the Accessibility tab. Every attribute starts at 3 and you spend **7 free points** (maximum 6 per attribute at creation; later they go up to 20). Each level gives +1 Attribute point and +1 Perk point. **Attribute points are permanent**, but perks can be refunded one by one for free. Romances depend on body and voice: **Judy** needs a feminine body and voice, **River** a feminine body, **Panam** a masculine body and **Kerry** a masculine body and voice (lifepath doesn't matter). The lifepath only changes the prologue and some dialogue; **none is mechanically better**. If you own Phantom Liberty, creation offers to **skip ahead to Phantom Liberty**: that auto-completes the prologue and every main job through Transmission, so you skip all of Act 1 and its choices.

## El Coyote Cojo

V starts at the bar of El Coyote Cojo, setting their broken nose. Pepe tells you about his trouble with Kirk and asks you to talk Kirk into forgetting the debt or settling it some other way.

1. **Find Kirk on the second floor** of the bar.
2. **Talk to Kirk.** Kirk Sawyer and **Big Joe** join you at the booth. Kirk won't forgive the debt, but he offers a deal: steal a **Rayfield Aerondight** belonging to an Arasaka corpo and he forgets what Pepe owes and gives you a cut on top. He hands you a **device** (a Rayfield service key) to open the car without tripping the alarm.
3. **Leave the bar.**

## The ride with Padre

**Padre**, a neighborhood fixer and an old acquaintance of V's, is waiting outside.

1. **Talk to Padre** and **get in his car**.
2. **Ride with him to Embers.** On the way there is a brief run-in with **Sam Carter** of the 6th Street gang: no combat. When you part, Padre hands you his card.
3. **Get out of the car.**

## The Embers garage

1. **Take the elevator to the garage** (the -01 Parking level, per IGN).
2. **Find the Rayfield.**
3. **Optional: find Rick**, the man in the garage booth. It is an optional objective with no documented reward.
4. **Disable the lock** with Kirk's device, **get in** and start it: the device stops the alarm from alerting the NCPD.

## Jackie and the NCPD

Just as everything seems to be going fine, someone opens the door from outside: it's **Jackie Welles**, pointing a gun at you, because he came for that car too.

1. **Talk to the thief** (Jackie).
2. The NCPD arrives with **Inspector Stints**, who pins you both to the ground. **Talk to the police officers.** The car's owner, the Arasaka corpo **Kaoru Fujioka**, orders Stints to break your legs and dump you in the river. Stints knocks you out but, since he can't stand corpos either, just leaves you in an alley.
3. **Talk to Jackie** when you wake up in the alley. Jackie tells you Kirk isn't as dangerous as he seems and invites you to breakfast. The Night City introduction montage begins, leading into **The Rescue**.

## Choices

- **This prologue has no documented choices with consequences.** What you tell Pepe, Kirk, Padre or the police only changes the conversation.
- **The Streetkid lifepath in the long run** → gives you unique dialogue all game (the "Stanislavski's Method" achievement asks for 10 lifepath lines). Some Act 1 examples: in **The Ripperdoc** you talk with Jackie about Dex's gang-war past; in **The Pickup** you recognize the **Black Lace** Dum Dum offers and, if you leave on good terms with Maelstrom, **Dum Dum gives you an extra one**; in **The Gun**, Robert Wilson has special "rite of passage" dialogue.
- **Exclusive job:** **Small Man, Big Mouth**, with Kirk, comes after the side job **Heroes** and is only available to this lifepath.

> **Don't leave without:** talking to Rick if you want to complete the optional garage objective. Beyond that, this prologue has no documented missable items.

## Rewards

- **Memorabilia:** a **Night City diorama** for V's apartment. The official guide wrongly says it is the Arasaka clock; that one comes from the Corpo prologue.
- **Lifepath starting gear:** the **Unity** pistol and street clothing.
