**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, calls you as you near **Megabuilding H6** in **Arroyo (Santo Domingo)**. It's from his second set: it opens after **Race to the Top**, **Serious Side Effects** and **Error 404**, in **Act 2**. **6th Street** jumped a **Militech** transport and wants to jailbreak the weapons; El Capitán gives you the door code and a virus for the computer they use to crack them. He'd also like **Lucius Thoran**, the techie behind the plan, out of the picture.

> **Order:** enter the megabuilding → take the elevator to the 19th floor → get inside the hideout → (optional) kill Lucius → upload the virus to the netrunner's computer → leave the hideout → go to the Drop Point.

## How to do it

The whole **19th floor** is hostile. Outside the elevator, **two gangers have their backs to you**: a good moment for quiet takedowns. The computer is on the highest floor of the hideout, directly across the megabuilding, and **Lucius** is in that same room.

There is a **stealth route** through the abandoned rooms on the right, which takes **Body** and **Technical Ability**.

When you're done, go back down by elevator: some gangers may be waiting outside. After El Capitán calls, go to the Drop Point at the foot of the building to close the gig.

> **Don't leave without:** checking Lucius Thoran's computer and the shard **Cargo 400925/MIL**.
