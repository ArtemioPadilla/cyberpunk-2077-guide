**Olive Branch** is a gig from **Wakako Okada**, the **Westbrook** fixer, in **Japantown**. It belongs to Wakako's **second set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Tyger and Vulture** and **We Have Your Wife**. The client is **Sergei Karasinsky**, a solo who landed on the **Tyger Claws'** blacklist and wants to make peace with a gift. You meet him at Silk Road and he unlocks a garage on Redwood Street holding the car you must drive to the Tyger Claws' restaurant. He hints that the gift is more than the car, but does not explain.

> **Order:** meet Sergei at the marked location → talk to him → get into the garage → get in the car → drive to the Tyger Claws' restaurant → (optional) talk to the man in the trunk.

## The trunk

While you drive you hear pleas from the back. If you open the trunk before arriving, you find **Alex Pushkin**, a **Biotechnica** employee who turned down an offer from the Tyger Claws; Sergei kidnapped him to buy his pardon. With a **Nomad** V who has already done **Guinea Pigs** (and talked to the hotel clerk), an extra dialogue appears: Alex worked with Joanne Koch on the test that killed dozens of the Red Ocher clan, and he has no remorse.

## Choices

- **Deliver Alex and the car** → gig complete; Wakako praises your discretion. Later Alex's body turns up in a storm drain in Charter Hill, near the Tyger and Vulture casino.
- **Free Alex** → he pays you **double** what Sergei offered; Wakako is surprised but closes the contract. The Tyger Claws kill Sergei: his body appears in an alley in Vista del Rey, behind Dicky Twister.
- **Antagonize the Tyger Claws** during the delivery → they turn hostile; after you beat them, you can only finish by freeing Alex.
- **Kill Alex** or **drive him out of Night City** → the gig **fails**.

## Rewards

- €$2,720 and 550 XP (double the eddies if you free Alex).
