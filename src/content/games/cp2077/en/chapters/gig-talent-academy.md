Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It is a **Mr. Hands** gig from his second set, which opens during **Get It Together**. The target: the data of **Fiona Vargas**'s clients, scouts who dodge taxes when signing athletes, at the **Center for Neuromotor Development, Site 341**, in Luxor Heights.

> **Order:** meet Baird → enter the exhibition hall → reach Fiona's office → download the data → talk to Fiona → leave.

## Infiltration

Mr. Hands's netrunner, **Baird**, waits near the entrance. Your cover is a recruiter for a San Diego team, but it only gets you in: inside, don't talk about yourself.

- **If you tell the bartender you work for the San Diego team**, another man asks you questions you can't answer, you're taken to another security check, you fail and the whole academy turns hostile.
- **If you tell him you don't want to chat**, a kid cracks the glass in front of him, the bartender leaves and nobody watches the **Personnel only** door.
- **Stealth:** as you leave the elevator to the exhibition hall, go right. A guard watches a technical back office with a computer that shuts down surveillance; a vent leads into a shaft that drops into a lab. To get back up through the ceiling you need **double jump**.

## Choices

Fiona's office is one floor down; the door locks behind you. **Tommie Walker**, a young athlete, is inside. The download fails and Fiona walks in: she offers to leak some low-importance data. You can ask about Tommie's chances and accept so he gets signed. The reward doesn't change. You can also **kill Fiona**: Johnny approves and you take her elevator key. Once it's over, the elevator is disabled and the hall stays closed.

Later you find Tommie with his dad at the EBM Petrochem Stadium; if you shut the academy down and tell his dad, he resents you for it.
