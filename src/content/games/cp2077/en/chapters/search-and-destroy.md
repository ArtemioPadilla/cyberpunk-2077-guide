**Takemura** gives it right after you escape the parade. He has taken **Hanako Arasaka** to his safe house on **Vine Street** in **Vista del Rey** (Heywood): an abandoned apartment building. You go in, talk to Hanako, and Arasaka storms the building with **Adam Smasher** in the lead. What really matters comes after: there's a **hidden objective to save Takemura** that doesn't appear until you find the way. If you don't save him, you lose **The Devil** achievement for good on this playthrough.

> **Order:** go to the hideout → knock on the door (4 times) → talk to Takemura → talk to Hanako → check the door → return to Takemura → ambush and collapse → (hidden) find Takemura and get him out → leave the building → Sunset Motel → talk to Johnny → approach the door → talk to the stranger → talk to Johnny.

> **Get ready:** **make a manual save at the start of the job** (PowerPyx). Bring a **thermal or night-vision scope** for the final hallway: the squad's lights blind you. Have weapons for a straight gunfight; IGN recommends fighting the first soldiers openly.

## Arrival: apartment 303

Before you leave **Redwood Market**, where the parade ended, you can run into **Gillean Jordan** of N54 News recording a piece on the attack (optional).

Go to the Vine Street safe house and head up to **apartment 303** on the third floor. **Knock exactly four times.** The door is rigged with explosives: knock **once or three times and it blows up, killing V**.

Inside, **talk to Takemura**, then **sit with Hanako**: you explain how her father died, but she refuses to listen. Your dialogue choice with Takemura has no effect (IGN).

**The High Priestess**, the tarot card representing Hanako, is on the building's **fourth floor**.

## The ambush

There are noises in the hallway. **Check the door**, **return to Takemura**... and Arasaka bursts in. **Adam Smasher** takes Hanako away in an AV and **the floor collapses** under V.

## Saving Takemura (hidden objective)

You wake on a pile of rubble several floors down, and Takemura is missing. The objective says "leave the building" and Johnny tells you to run. **There's no marker for saving Takemura until you find the stairwell.** Do it like this:

1. **In the corridor where you wake**, find the room with the **large blue cross**. **Turn left** and squeeze through the **small hole in the wall**.
2. **Clear the soldiers** on the other side (up to five). IGN recommends a straight gunfight.
3. Take the **stairwell in the top-right corner by the elevators** and **climb back up to 303**. The optional objective "**Try to save Goro Takemura**" appears as you climb.
4. A **large squad** ambushes you in the hallway. Their lights are blinding: use the **thermal or night-vision scope**.
5. **Help Takemura** in 303 and **lead him out of the building**. **Takemura must appear in the exit cutscene**: if he walks out with you, you've done it.

Even if you follow Johnny's advice to run at first, **you can still go back for Takemura as long as you haven't left the building**.

## Sunset Motel

Once outside, V takes refuge at the **Sunset Motel** in Red Peaks (Badlands). Talk to **Johnny**. A car arrives, a door is heard and someone knocks: **approach the door** and **talk to the stranger**. It's a **proxy** Hanako is using to relay a message. If you don't own an **M2038 Tactician**, the game gives you one for the scene. **Talk to Johnny** to wrap up the job.

## Choices

- **Save Takemura** → Takemura survives. In **Nocturne Op55N1**, if you choose Hanako (**The Devil** route), he joins you with Arasaka troops and, if you spared Oda in Play It Safe, Oda too. **The Devil achievement needs Takemura alive**; per PowerPyx it pops at the end of **Totalimmortal**, after Yorinobu and the elevator.
- **Let Takemura die** (leave the building without him) → he's absent from The Devil ending: **no Arasaka troops and no Oda**, and **Hellman** offers you the final choice instead. **The Devil achievement can no longer be earned** on this playthrough.
- **Knocking 1 or 3 times** → V dies (back to the checkpoint).
- **What you tell Takemura in 303** → no effect.

## What opens here

- **Tapeworm:** finishing Search and Destroy triggers a stage.
- This is the last job in Takemura's line. The next step with Hanako comes in Act 3 (**Nocturne Op55N1**).
- It doesn't open or close side chains, but **it's the Act 2 choice that weighs most on an ending**.

> **Don't leave without:** **Takemura**. Follow the steps above even though you can't see the objective. And if you're collecting tarot, **The High Priestess** on the fourth floor.

## Rewards

- **1580 XP** (game8).
