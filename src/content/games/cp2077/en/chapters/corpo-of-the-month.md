Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It follows Mr. Hands's gig **Treating Symptoms**, and only appears if you meet two conditions: when you find **Alan Noël**, the undercover NetWatch agent, you pick the **Corpo lifepath** dialogue option, and you leave **Milko Alexis** alive.

> **Order:** finish Treating Symptoms with Milko alive → wait a few days → Alan Noël's message → collect the stash.

## The stash

A few days after the gig, Alan Noël texts you: NetWatch caught the mastermind behind Milko. As thanks he sends you the location of a **hidden stash** at the **Luxor High Wellness Spa**, the former Voodoo Boys base in Luxor Heights. Go to the marker and open it.

Without the Corpo option, Noël's message still arrives, but with only an extra €$2,000 and no stash.

Johnny, in the journal, mocks everyone: the fixer is happy because NetWatch owes him a favor, NetWatch is happy because its op went well, and V is happy because the money's good.

## Rewards

- According to the Treating Symptoms page, the stash holds more money and a **Skill Shard: Netrunner**; the wiki gives no amount.
- The wiki gives no XP or Street Cred for this job.
