**Family Heirloom** is a gig from **Rogue Amendiares**, the Afterlife's fixer, set in a **parking lot on Crockett Street** (Acorn Towers) in **Charter Hill**, **Westbrook**. It opens in **Act 2** once you finish **A Shrine Defiled**, Wakako's last gig (the wiki does not say whether you need to have dealt with Rogue in the story first). The client is **Dan**, son of **Nancy**, Samurai's keyboardist. Dan has a gambling problem and lost his car, a **Rayfield Caliburn**, in a card game. In the trunk was a **one-of-a-kind Samurai bootleg recording**. **Wyatt Alken**, a **6th Street** bootlicker, has it. Rogue pays extra if you also bring back the car.

> **Order:** go to the parking lot → find the bootleg recording → retrieve it → (optional) retrieve Dan's car → leave the parking lot → (optional) deliver the car to Dan → exit the car → deliver the recording to Dan → leave the area → collect your reward from the Drop Point.

## The parking lot

Wyatt stands in view of most of the 6th Street members, so running into him usually ends in a firefight. You can kill him, knock him out or simply sneak past. The recording (**Bootleg Shard**) is in a **locker at the back** of the lot, together with the **car keys** and **Johnny's shoes**.

> **Don't leave without:** the iconic **Johnny's shoes** from the locker. They are only available in this gig and are required for the **Breathtaking** achievement. Grab the keys too if you want the car bonus.

## Rewards

- €$16,600, 1,000 XP and 1,710 Street Cred.
- **Second Conflict flight jacket**: reward for the optional car objective.
- **Johnny's shoes** (if you take them from the locker).
