**Goro Takemura** gives it with a call some time after Playing for Time. It's the first job in his line: he wants to reach **Hanako Arasaka**, and to do that he tries to bring his former pupil **Sandayu Oda**, Hanako's bodyguard, onto his side. You visit the **Japantown docks** and **Jig-Jig Street**, where **Wakako Okada** is. It's short and dialogue-based, with no combat and no choices that change anything, but it sets up the next three.

> **Order:** talk to Takemura → meet him at the docks → lean on the barrier and wait → talk to Oda → talk to Takemura → get in his van → go to Wakako → talk to Wakako → talk to Takemura → wait a day for his call.

> **Get ready:** the meeting is at **11:30 PM**; if you arrive early, skip time. If you haven't been paid for **The Rescue** yet, here's an easy way: in the talk with Wakako, **remind her about the Sandra Dorsett gig** (game8).

## The Japantown docks

Go to the Japantown docks and **lean on the barrier** to wait for Takemura. At 11:30 PM he shows up and, shortly after, **Oda**.

**Oda comes alone and by car**, not cloaked, as a sign of trust towards his mentor. He listens to Takemura and V, but as soon as he realises V really saw what happened at Konpeki Plaza, **he cuts you off and won't hear more**. Before leaving he drops a hint about trouble at the upcoming **parade**, and a warning: if he sees you again, he won't be so courteous. **Whatever you say, nothing changes this scene.**

## Wakako

**Talk to Takemura**, **get in his van** and ride with him to **Wakako** on Jig-Jig Street. With some persuasion, Wakako hands over the information on the **parade's security measures**. Use the chance to remind her about the **Sandra Dorsett** gig and collect what she owed you for The Rescue (game8).

Takemura needs to prepare, so you can't act yet: **talk to him** and **wait a day** for his call.

## Choices

- **What you say to Oda** → changes nothing: Oda refuses to listen either way.
- **Reminding Wakako about Sandra Dorsett** → you collect the pending payment for The Rescue. No other consequence.
- The big choice about Oda comes in **Play It Safe**: kill him or spare him.

## What opens here

- **Gimme Danger:** Takemura calls after a day for the next step, at Redwood Market.
- The rest of the line is **Play It Safe** (the parade) and **Search and Destroy**, where saving Takemura decides **The Devil** achievement.
- It doesn't open or close any side chain.

> **Don't leave without:** collecting the Sandra Dorsett payment from Wakako if you haven't yet, and taking a good look at Oda: you'll meet him again at the parade, with Mantis Blades and optical camo.

## Rewards

- **100 XP** and **115 Street Cred** (game8).
