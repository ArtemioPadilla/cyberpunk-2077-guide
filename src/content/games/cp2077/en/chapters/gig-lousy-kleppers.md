**Maelstrom** stole a **Malorian Arms** van on Sir Francis Drake Ave. One of **Regina Jones**'s informants tailed it with a drone to a gang chop shop, a warehouse on Offshore St (Northside, Watson). The valuable part isn't the cargo: it's a **databank** with Malorian's transport route data, and the gangers have no idea. It is in **set 7 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 6 (Bloodsport and Scrolls before Swine). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** get inside the Maelstrom warehouse → collect the databank → leave the area → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## How to do it

- Every door opens with an attribute check or a quickhack. The access card isn't available on site: don't waste time looking for it.
- The databank is in the stolen van, inside the warehouse.
- Leave the area and take it to the Drop Point.

## Rewards

- €$10,800, 1,000 XP and 906 Street Cred.
