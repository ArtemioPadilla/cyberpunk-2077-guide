**Muamar Reyes, El Capitán**, calls you as you near the **Kendachi factory** (Almunecar) in **Santo Domingo**. The gig's infobox places it in Rancho Coronado, but the factory's own page puts it in Arroyo. It's from his second set: it opens after **Race to the Top**, **Serious Side Effects** and **Error 404**, in **Act 2**. **Ted Fox**, an N54 reporter, is working on an exposé about Kendachi cooperating with **6th Street** and needs proof.

> **Order:** meet Ted Fox → get in his car and talk to him → take the tracker → get to the parking lot → (optional) remain undetected → install the tracker → leave the parking lot → collect your reward.

## How to do it

The grounds are small. There are only **two cameras**, both outside the main building, but several guards patrol the parking lot. Two more are inside: one in the garage with the van and one in the office next door. Plant the tracker through the van's **driver-side door panel** and slip out quietly.

## Choices

- **Nobody detects you** → El Capitán is pleased and leaves a **bonus** in a nearby dropbox.
- **An alarm goes off** → Ted still gets his proof, but El Capitán grades you "C minus" and there's no bonus.

## Rewards

€$6,340, plus the dropbox bonus if you stay undetected.
