**Going Up or Down?** is the last gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer, and this time the client is Padre himself. It takes place in **Megabuilding H1** on Congress Street in **the Glen**. It belongs to the **fourth set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish **Eye for an Eye** and **The Lord Giveth and Taketh Away** (and the rest of the third set). **El Gallo** (Juan Troy) is the techie who has kept H1 from falling apart, but he has a serious syn-coke problem. He got it into his head that a set of **scandium rods** would fix everything and stole them from a corporate warehouse. They are rare and very expensive; Padre wants them before El Gallo pawns them. He does not care how you do it. Access to the building is already taken care of.

> **Order:** go to El Gallo's workshop → look for the scandium rods → (optional) talk to El Gallo → take them from the cache or steal them → go outside → deposit them in the Drop Point.

## The workshop

It is in H1's sublevels, where the building's elevators are repaired.

## Choices

- **Talk to El Gallo** → with enough **Technical Ability** you convince him he does not need the rods to fix the elevator. He lets you take them from the cache **without a fight**.
- **Steal them without talking** → if El Gallo sees you after the theft, he gets suspicious first and then turns hostile. Neutralizing him is an optional objective.

## What it unlocks

Wrapping up Padre's gigs triggers **God Bless This Mess**. This gig also unlocks **Hot Merchandise**, a gig from Rogue.
