**Motorbreath** arrived with **Update 2.3**. **River Ward** gives it to you some time after you finish **The Hunt** and **The Beast In Me**, so it's an **Act 2** job. Since 2.31 it won't trigger if River died in The Hunt; if The Hunt failed, the car can be bought on AUTOFIXER. River has heard of an ultra-rare supercar, a **Yaiba ARV-Q340 Semimaru**, impounded by the NCPD and then stolen by two street racers who slid into cyberpsychosis, **Nova MacCaster** and **Jax Forgrave**. Rather than leave it to the cops, River would rather you put it to better use. (The wiki page is a stub: it only lists the objectives.)

> **Order:** check the Watson highway → look for the red Semimaru → chase it and stop it → defeat the drivers → investigate the Semimaru → send the info to River.

## The chase

Drive the **Night City Ringroad** in **Watson**, near **Kabuki**, until you spot a **red Semimaru**. Chase it in a fast car or on a bike and force it to stop. When they get out, Nova and Jax fight: **defeat them**. Then **investigate the car** and **send the data to River** to close the job. The Semimaru is yours.

## What's next

The car comes with its **CrystalCoat** system broken. That opens **Where Eagles Dare**, with Claire Russell, to fix it.

## Rewards

- **Yaiba ARV-Q340 Semimaru** (all-wheel-drive sports car).
- The wiki gives no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
