**Lucy Thackery** bought her brother Bertie's freedom with three months of cyberware repair for **Maelstrom**, and five have already gone by. Bertie is paying for you to get her out of the **Clean Cut** clinic on Longshore North St (Northside, Watson). It is in **Regina Jones**'s **set 1 of 8**, the first she offers: her gigs come in sets that unlock one after another, and the next one appears once you finish every gig in the current set. According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** go to Clean Cut → find Lucy → talk to her → persuade her to leave → optional: scan the patient, pick up the synthetic blood and inject it → escort her outside → escort her to the fixer's transport.

## The clinic

- There are **three cameras** and several Maelstromers on patrol: spot them with the scanner before going in.
- Lucy is in the **far back left room**, trying to save a badly injured ganger, Hans Otto. She won't leave until she's done.

## Choices

- **Help her** → scan the patient, take the **synthetic blood** from the nearby table and inject him. She agrees to leave.
- **Don't help** → you can put the patient out of his misery; that also convinces her to leave.

Then escort her outside to the fixer's transport. Later (Act 2), Lucy shows up at Octavio's Clinic in Rancho Coronado. Finishing the gig opens the NCPD Scanner Hustle **No License, No Problem**.

## Rewards

- €$3,000, 1,000 XP and 707 Street Cred.
