**Sweet Dreams** is offered by **Stefan**, a street seller hawking an insane braindance outside **Cherry Blossom Market** in Japantown (Westbrook). It appears after **I Fought the Law**, so it is an Act 2 job.

> **Order:** talk to Stefan → buy the braindance → pay to use his gear → follow him → use his BD player → escape the scavenger hideout → get your equipment back → confront Stefan.

## The braindance

Pay Stefan to get the **Unlabeled Braindance**. To watch it you have to pay **again** to use his wreath, which is in the building behind him. If you try to play it with your own gear from the inventory, it does not work: the format is old, as he warned you. Go back to him and choose: ask for a **refund** or pay to use his wreath.

## The trap

When you use his player you black out. You wake up in a very familiar bathroom, the same one where V and Jackie found **Sandra Dorsett**, but this time you are the victim. Johnny mocks you. Your **weapons, clothes and eddies** are gone; you keep your **quickhacks**, your **cyberware** and quest items.

## The hideout

There are about **15 scavengers** spread around the place. Work your way with quickhacks and cyberware to the **main room**: your equipment is in a **locker**. Grab it before you leave.

## Choices

Go back to the market. Stefan makes up an excuse and returns your money.

- **Either dialogue option** → you let him live.
- **Aim a weapon at him** → opens another dialogue where he begs for mercy; you can **kill** him or **spare** him.

All of them complete the job. If you finish it before starting **Dazed and Confused**, V mentions it in a text to Viktor.

## Rewards

2,303 XP, 3,453 Street Cred and €$25,600/32,000 (wiki figures).
