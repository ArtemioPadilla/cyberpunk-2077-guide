**Black Steel In The Hour of Chaos** opens the **Reed path**: it only exists if you chose to **help Reed capture Songbird** in Firestarter. Alex is dead and **MaxTac** has taken Songbird. Reed won't give up: you have to get Songbird out of the MaxTac convoy before she's buried for good. That takes intel on the convoy, and a **netrunner** from your contacts gets it; depending on your earlier jobs you'll have different ones, and each asks for something different. Then comes an ambush from an abandoned hotel with Reed and the **6th Street** gang.

> **Order:** call a netrunner → wait for their data (6 to 12 hours) → send Reed the details → wait for his text → abandoned hotel → talk to Reed and 6th Street → review the plan (optional) → wait for the convoy → defeat the NCPD escort → open the truck's rear doors → defeat the MaxTac operators → follow Songbird's traces.

> **Get ready:** check which netrunners you have available (below). If you want the **MaxTac Mantis Blades**, pick **Yoko** (Mr. Hands' netrunner, €$15,000) and **accept her proposal**. The final fight is against four MaxTac officers with different roles: bring plenty of healing and something to cut off an enemy netrunner's hacks.

## Choosing a netrunner

**Call a netrunner** to help you breach MaxTac. The options depend on what you did before:

- **Carol Emeka:** requires Life During Wartime and Queen of the Highway siding with Panam (the wiki flags this requirement as unconfirmed). **She refuses to give you the data**, but sends you a **weapon or a quickhack** for the MaxTac fight; collect it from the stash (optional). You'll have to call another contact for the data.
- **Chang-Hoon Nam:** requires the gig **Wakako's Favorite** with him alive. He delivers the data **after 12 hours** at his shop in **Japantown**. The hack leaves him with **aphasia** and he leaves Night City.
- **Nix:** requires **KOLD MIRAGE** and **Spellbound** with Nix alive. He delivers the data **after 6 hours** at the **Arasaka Memorial** and gives you the **Self-ICE** implant for the fight.
- **Sandra Dorsett:** requires **Full Disclosure** resolved favorably. She delivers the data **after 8 hours** at **Reconciliation Park**; in return she asks you to **install a trojan** in MaxTac.
- **Yoko, Mr. Hands' netrunner:** always available if you **pay Mr. Hands €$15,000**. She delivers the data **in 10 hours** in **Kabuki**. **Listen to her proposal** (optional) and accept it: later, during the ambush, you'll have to **send Mr. Hands the site's coordinates**. That gets you the **MaxTac Mantis Blades** some time after finishing Leave in Silence.

Once you have the data, **send Reed a message** with the details and **wait for his reply**.

## The abandoned hotel

1. **Meet Reed at the abandoned hotel** in Pacifica, overlooking a skybridge. You can talk to Johnny (optional).
2. Talk to Reed and the **6th Street** crew. Their leader, **Will Gunner**, recognizes you if you did **Stadium Love**: if you solved it **without violence**, he compliments your gun handling; if it was violent, he has less kind things to say.
3. **Review the plan (optional):** a 6th Street soldier goes over the ambush. Take the shard and choose:
   - **Turrets or mines** for the kill zone.
   - **One of 3 starting positions** for the fight.
4. Talk to Reed and to Johnny, and **wait for the convoy**.
5. **If you picked Yoko:** **send Mr. Hands the ambush coordinates** (optional). Without this, no Mantis Blades.

## The ambush

1. **Defeat the NCPD escort.** The turrets or mines you chose do part of the work.
2. **Open the truck's rear doors.**
3. **Defeat the MaxTac operators.** There are four, each with a role:
   - **Faye Russo:** assault.
   - **Asher Peterson:** **Mantis Blades**, comes at you in melee.
   - **Dallas Foster:** heavy.
   - **Jasper Collins:** **netrunner**; hacks you from the back line.

**Tips for MaxTac:** take out **Jasper Collins**, the netrunner, first if his quickhacks are burning you down, or **Peterson** if his blades are reaching you; don't let them surround you. If you called Nix, **Self-ICE** helps against the hacks; Carol's gift is useful here too. Reed fights beside you: use him to split the enemies' attention.

## Songbird's trail

In the chaos, **Songbird escapes in the MaxTac truck**. **Follow the traces** she leaves; if you like, **check on Reed** and talk to him (optional). The trail leads to an abandoned underground facility: **Somewhat Damaged** begins.

## Choices

- **Which netrunner you call** → changes the wait, the place and the extra: **Carol** (weapon or quickhack, no data), **Chang-Hoon** (leaves the city with aphasia), **Nix** (Self-ICE implant), **Sandra** (trojan in MaxTac), **Yoko** (€$15,000; with her proposal and the coordinates, **MaxTac Mantis Blades**).
- **Turrets or mines, and starting position** → only changes how the fight begins.
- **Stadium Love** (base game) → how Will Gunner treats you.

> **Don't leave without:** sending the **coordinates to Mr. Hands** if you went with Yoko and collecting **Carol's** gift if you called her. The iconic **Crowbar** often linked to this path is at the start of Somewhat Damaged, past the first sealed gate.

## Rewards

- Self-ICE (if you call Nix).
- Carol's weapon or quickhack (if you call her).
- MaxTac Mantis Blades (later, via Yoko and Mr. Hands).
