**You Know My Name** is the **Black Sapphire** infiltration, Kurt Hansen's hotel. You go in wearing a diving suit through the flooded tunnels that lead to the maintenance area, cover **Reed** from a sniper nest with the **Rasetsu** rifle, and go up together to Hansen's party, packed with Night City celebrities. There Songbird tells you Hansen has the cure and that she'll work on it with two netrunner guests, the **Aurore and Aymeric Cassel** twins. To pass yourself off as one of them later, you have to **scan their behavior** by playing roulette at their table.

> **Order:** flooded tunnels (diving suit) → panel to open the path → warehouse guards → CCTV to track Reed → entry gate → elevator to maintenance → meeting point → neutralize the sniper → **Rasetsu nest**: cover Reed → join Reed → elevator to the top floor → eveningwear → party → Songbird and the shard in the glass → buy chips (**€$80,000**) → roulette with the Cassels → confrontation with Hansen → exit through the lobby.

> **Get ready:** if you want the **Amikiri Sound Cutter**, you must have met **Lizzy Wizzy** in the minor job **Violence** without refusing to dispose of Liam's body. Save before roulette and **note whether the twins win or lose the final bet**: Hansen will ask about it in Firestarter.

## The flooded tunnels and the warehouse

1. **Enter the flooded tunnels.** **Put the diving suit on before diving.**
2. Go down the tunnels. Find a way forward and **use the panel to open the path**.
3. **Defeat the warehouse guards.**
4. **Connect to the CCTV** from the security computer and **switch between cameras to track Reed**.
5. **Open the entry gate** and take the **elevator to the maintenance area**.

## The sniper nest (Rasetsu)

1. Go to the meeting point and **neutralize the sniper**. Jack in and track down Reed. Talk to him.
2. **Use your Kiroshi to find the meeting point.** Optional: **scan the floor** for threats. There are **6**: **mine, guards, main elevator, sniper, bridge and cameras**. The more you flag for Reed, the better prepared he is to get through by stealth.
3. Talk to Reed. You now control a **mounted Rasetsu** rifle and your job is to **identify immediate threats** to Reed and clear his way to the panel that lowers the bridge:
   - Wait until the patrol walks past Reed.
   - Find a way to open the passage for him: **destroy the power source** for the gate's security systems.
   - Find the route he can take.
   - **Take out the guard**, **handle the sniper on the balcony** and the guard blocking his way (optional: locate him with the CCTV).
   - **Deal with the incoming guards.**
4. **You can do it without killing anyone**, even though Reed suggests shooting. Scan, then shoot or hack environment objects (crates, air conditioners, transformers, forklifts) to **distract or knock out** the guards and let Reed through.
5. Wait for Reed to pass and join him. When it's over, V keeps the rifle: the iconic **Tsunami Rasetsu**.

## Hansen's party

1. Take the elevator with Reed to the **top floor**. Follow Reed, **grab your outfit** and **put on your eveningwear**.
2. Go to the party and **join Reed at the bar** when you're ready.
3. **Enjoy the party until Songbird makes contact.** There are plenty of familiar faces: Gillean Jordan (N54), Arif Iqbal (WNS), Ziggy Q, Ruth Dzeng, Deputy Mayor Weldon Holt, NCPD Commissioner Jerry Fawlter, Mr. Hands, Lizzy Wizzy and, depending on your earlier choices, Tyger Claws figures or Maiko Maeda.
4. **Lizzy Wizzy:** if you met her at the motel in **Violence** and didn't refuse to dispose of Liam's body, she recognizes you. **Compliment her performance** and she gives you her **Amikiri Sound Cutter**. If you refused, she ignores you.
5. **Read Songbird's message**, approach her and talk. She tells you Hansen has the cure and that she'll work on it with two netrunners who are at the party. Then **take the shard from the glass**. Hansen whisks her away, but she re-establishes the connection through the Relic.

## Roulette with the Cassels

1. Go back to Reed at the bar and talk to him.
2. **Buy at least €$80,000 worth of casino chips** for roulette.
3. **Play roulette with the netrunners.** If a round is in progress, wait until it's over to join.
4. To scan their behavioral imprints, **ask them questions** that provoke emotions: the scan progresses with their reactions.
5. **Important:** note whether the twins **win or lose the final bet**. In Firestarter, Hansen asks you about gambling at the Black Sapphire and you have to answer according to that result.

## The confrontation and the exit

1. As the roulette game ends, **Hansen** arrives: he says he knows V is an NUS agent, threatens you and throws you out of the hotel. **Get through the confrontation** and talk to Alex.
2. **Leave the Black Sapphire through the lobby.** Optional: **cash out your winnings**.
3. Talk to the guards and follow them to the exit:
   - **If you leave peacefully:** follow Reed and talk to him.
   - **If a fight breaks out in the lobby:** exit the Black Sapphire, leave the area, **lose your tail** and talk to Reed.

## Choices

- **Scanning all 6 threats** → Reed gets through better prepared and by stealth.
- **Killing the guards from the nest or not** → you can get through without kills using distractions; it doesn't change the story.
- **Complimenting Lizzy Wizzy** (if you meet the Violence condition) → **Amikiri Sound Cutter**. Afterwards other guests and the bartender comment on the gift.
- **Winning or losing at roulette** → doesn't change this job, but **you must remember it** for the Firestarter interview; two false answers there is a game over.
- **Leaving peacefully or fighting in the lobby** → if you fight, you have to lose your pursuers before meeting Reed.

> **Don't leave without:** the **Tsunami Rasetsu**, the **Amikiri Sound Cutter** if you qualify, and **cashing out your chips**. Remember the roulette result. Afterwards Alex sends a **bootleg BD** of Lizzy Wizzy's show to your apartment, and Mr. Hands offers you the side job **Dazed and Confused**.

## Rewards

- Tactical diving suit (and its broken version).
- **Tsunami Rasetsu** (iconic sniper rifle).
- Stylish suit.
- **Amikiri Sound Cutter** (depends on Violence).
- Bootleg BD from Lizzy Wizzy's Black Sapphire show (mailed to your apartment).
