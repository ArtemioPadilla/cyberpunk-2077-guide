Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. It comes from **Muamar Reyes**, El Capitán, after you complete **Just Another Story at least 7 times**. He calls about a special job and asks to meet at his spot by the dam, in **Rancho Coronado** (Santo Domingo). You can keep doing normal deliveries in the meantime.

> **Order:** meet by the dam → talk to El Capitán → Arasaka docks → talk to Daniels → board the ship → find the truck → drive it to the Arroyo garage → talk to El Capitán.

## The port job

When you arrive, El Capitán is arguing with officer **Ayden Daniels**. The plan: steal an **Arasaka Behemoth** truck full of medicine from the **Arasaka Port**, in Corpo Plaza (City Center), with Daniels as backup. Johnny suggests following El Capitán to ask what's wrong; do it.

At the docks, talk to Daniels first. The truck is aboard the docked ship and guards watch the compound; you can get there by stealth or by fighting, but once you get in **the alarm goes off** and Arasaka squads arrive. According to the wiki (unverified), there are two locked containers that open with **Technical Ability**; the harder one is emptied once the job ends.

## Choices

- **Get out to save Daniels** → he lives, but Arasaka has time to set up roadblocks and some of the medical gear gets damaged. Days later Daniels texts you, angry.
- **Leave him** → Daniels dies, the drive back meets little resistance and the cargo arrives intact. El Capitán's celebration feels hollow.

Either way, drive the truck to the **Arroyo** garage and take out the pursuers. If the truck is destroyed by damage or you drive it into the water, the job fails.

## Rewards

- Herrera Outlaw "Weiler" (car)
- €$5,000
- 500 XP
- 300 Street Cred
