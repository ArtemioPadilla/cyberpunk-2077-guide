**Professional Widow** is the gift from **Wakako Okada**, the Westbrook fixer, when you finish **all her gigs** in the district. It was added in Patch 1.5. Her Westbrook gigs open after **The Heist**, in **Act 2**: in Watson you cannot work through this list yet.

> **Order:** finish Wakako's 8 Westbrook gigs → read her message → open your stash → take the Byakko.

## The gigs that count

- **Set 1:** Tyger and Vulture, We Have Your Wife.
- **Set 2:** Greed Never Pays, Olive Branch, Until Death Do Us Part.
- **Set 3:** Getting Warmer..., Wakako's Favorite.
- **Set 4:** A Shrine Defiled (the last one).

Each set opens when you finish the one before it.

## Collecting the katana

Wakako texts you to say thanks. The marker points to the stash in your **Megabuilding H10** apartment, but according to the wiki **any stash** works, including your vehicles'.

## Rewards

**Byakko**, an iconic katana (Blade). Its effect: you leap at an enemy with full force, and killing them lets you perform a rapid combo of attacks for a short time. It is a quest reward, so it cannot be missed.
