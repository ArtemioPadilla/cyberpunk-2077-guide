**The Lord Giveth and Taketh Away** is a gig from **Padre** (Sebastian Ibarra), the **Heywood** fixer. It takes place at a **Valentinos auto shop** on Russell Street in **Wellsprings**. It belongs to Padre's **third set**: it opens in **Act 2** once you finish the second (**Psychofan**, **Bring Me the Head of Gustavo Orta** and **Old Friends**). Some **nomads** bought a stolen **Militech** SUV from the **Valentinos** without going through a fixer. The gang kept the eddies, beat them up and told them to get lost. Their rep, **Dakota Smith**, asked Padre for help, and he sends you to get it back. Getting back at the Valentinos is not part of the job: he will handle that.

> **Order:** go to the Valentinos' auto shop → find the SUV → enter it → escape with it → deliver it to the nomads → park it in the garage → exit the garage → leave the area.

## The auto shop

You can get in by **climbing the perimeter fence**. Several Valentinos patrol outside and more are inside the shop. Reach the SUV quietly or by force, get in and drive out. Take it to the nomads' garage, park it inside, get out and walk away to finish.

According to the wiki, **fighting the Valentinos does not change** the final dialogue or reward, so pick whichever approach you like.

Known bug: the Aldecaldo at the garage does not despawn after the gig ends.
