**Freedom** is a minor job added in **Update 2.3**. **Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes** texts it to you once you have finished **Transmission**, **Search and Destroy** and the first three races of **The Beast In Me** (City Center, Badlands and Santo Domingo), all Act 2 content. He wants **Rayfield** corporate intel and sends you coordinates next to **Coronado Dam**, in **Rancho Coronado**. The wiki marks this page as incomplete.

> **Order:** read the message → pile of wrecked cars by the dam → scan Harry Bell and read his shard → construction site at Bullard and Sequoia → take out the private security → laptop next to the car → send the data → decide: leave or steal the Mordred.

## How to do it

Among the burning wrecks, find engineer **Harry Bell** next to a research table in the middle. His shard is a chat with **Arlene Anwyl** about an off-protocol AutoDrive update on a very valuable car, and it says where the car is: the construction site on the corner of **Bullard and Sequoia**.

The site is full of **private security**; clearing everyone is optional. Use the laptop on the table to the right of the car: the **Caliburn "Mordred"** starts up and drives off on its own. The files say the owner is **Yorinobu Arasaka**; when you send them to Muamar he panics, tells you to abort and forbids you to steal the car.

## Choices

- **Leave** → the job fails and the Mordred can no longer be obtained. (The wiki reports an unconfirmed bug on Xbox, 2.3: the journal does not mark it as finished.)
- **Keep going** → on the other side of the site, on a workbench with wrenches, is the "skeleton key"; nearby are a transcript between Yorinobu and Anders Hellman and a palm reader. Connecting to add yourself to the car's whitelist is optional, but it spares you a 2-star wanted level when you steal it. The Mordred circles some nearby houses: stop it and get in. Without the whitelist, escape the **NCPD**.

## Rewards

The **Rayfield Caliburn "Mordred"**, if you steal it. The infobox gives no XP or eddies.
