**The Beast In Me** is the racing chain of **Claire Russell**, the **Afterlife** bartender. She texts you some time after **Double Life**, so it's an **Act 2** job. She asks you to her garage on Valley Street in **Arroyo** (Santo Domingo) **during the day**: in the evening or at night the door is shut. Claire is the gunner and needs a driver. If you **don't agree** to drive for her, the job fails.

> **Order:** garage by day → wait for her text → City Center (next morning) → Badlands (two days later) → Santo Domingo (a day later) and the overlook talk → the Big Race (about two days later) → follow Sampson or finish the race.

There are three qualifying races and a final. To reach the final you must finish **in the top three in at least two**. In each one: reach the start, call Claire, wait on the bench and talk to her.

## City Center

Starts at MLK Boulevard and Skyline East in **Vista del Rey** (Heywood), runs through **Corpo Plaza** and ends at the **Wellsprings** docks. You can use your own car or Claire's "Beast"; it changes nothing. Claire hands you a route shard (**RoutesNC RMaster v77**): slot it to see the course.

## Badlands

On Brookland Street by the **Laguna Bend** dam. Here **you can only use the Beast**.

## Santo Domingo

At Brandon and Wollesen, **Arroyo**; you pick the vehicle. If you qualify, Claire asks you to pull over at an overlook: her husband **Dean** died in last year's final because **Peter Sampson**, a Militech corpo, braked on purpose in front of them. Claire wants to **kill Sampson** in the final, even if it costs the win.

## The Big Race

Starts on Pardey Street in **Charter Hill** (Westbrook); it's a highway race, so bring a fast car. Stay close to Sampson: two thirds in, his car breaks down and leaves the highway, and Claire asks you to follow.

## Choices

- **At the overlook:** **help her kill him** or say **your priority is winning** → you go on to the final. **Refuse to help her** → Claire drops you from the team, leaves the Afterlife and the chain **fails**.
- **To have Claire spare Sampson:** at the overlook pick "You sure it was murder?" and stay on only to win. The wiki gives two versions of the dialogue: "My priority's winning.", or "Sorry, Claire, but I can't help." followed by "Okay, I'll still drive. But only to win.". When Claire texts you after the race, reply "Don't worry about it. Just remember what you promised me.". In the confrontation, take every timed option: "What's he mean?", "Wait, wanna hear this.", "Claire, that true?" and "Yeah. Let him go.".
- **Follow Sampson** → he crashes and Claire confronts him.
  - **If you met the conditions above** → she spares him. Claire gives you the **Beast**, and about 7 to 10 days later Sampson gives you his **Cthulhu** for free.
  - **If not** → Claire kills him and gives you the **Beast**. Muamar Reyes later sells the Cthulhu on AUTOFIXER for **€$67,000**.
- **Finish the race** → Claire is furious, **you get no vehicle** (the Cthulhu goes on sale on AUTOFIXER) and she stops serving you at the Afterlife.

Afterwards you can replay races (flag icons) for eddies and AUTOFIXER discounts. This chain, together with **The Hunt**, unlocks **Motorbreath**.

## Rewards

- Each qualifier: 486 XP and 728 Street Cred; **€$3,860** if you win.
- The Big Race: 728 XP and 1,092 Street Cred; **€$5,790** if you win it.
- **Thorton Mackinaw "Beast"** and, if you save Sampson, **Quadra Type-66 "Cthulhu"**.
