**Poem Of The Atoms** is the third session with the **Zen Master**. You find him in **North Oak** (Westbrook, Act 2): under the phoenix-like statue in the middle of the roundabout below the North Oak sign, where one of Delamain's cabs also hangs around. It opens when you finish **Stairway To Heaven**.

> **Order:** go to the North Oak roundabout → talk to the Zen Master → choose how much to pay → do the meditation.

## The session

Tell him you want to continue the meditations. Once again he lets you **pay what you think is fair**: this time **nothing** or **€$377**. The wiki does not say the payment changes anything. The braindance meditation is about the element of **fire**. When it ends, the monk is gone, just like in the earlier sessions. Johnny keeps making fun of your meditations.

## What comes next

It opens **Meetings Along The Edge**, the last session, in the Badlands.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred (the only money involved is what you choose to pay).
