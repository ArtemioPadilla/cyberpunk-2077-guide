**Alois Daquin**, a corpo who double-crossed **Regina Jones**, supposedly skipped Night City and left his gear behind. Twenty minutes ago his computer logged into an **Eisenhower St** localnet (Kabuki, Watson): Regina wants that laptop. It is in **set 5 of 8**: the sets unlock one after another, and this one arrives once you finish the two gigs of set 4 (Shark in the Water and Flight of the Cheetah). According to players' reports, all 23 Watson gigs can be done before **The Heist**.

> **Order:** enter the building where the laptop connected to the Net → find the laptop → optional: confront Charles Bucks → deposit it in the Drop Point.

## In the building

- **Scavengers** guard it. On the main floor a joytoy is watching TV with a Scav, and another is scrolling a braindance in the next room.
- Alois didn't skip town: he was killed and stripped for parts. His body is on a table in the **basement**, with the **laptop** nearby. A Scav in the basement carries a **Clinic Access Token**.
- The computer near the body has a message from Charles Bucks proving he knew everything.
- Once you have the laptop you can already deposit it and get paid.
- The shard for the job **Full Disclosure** is in this same building: if it is active, do both at once.

## Choices

If you leave by the back stairs, you come up into the clinic of **Charles Bucks**, a Kabuki ripperdoc, who begs for his life.

- **Believe him** → his clinic stays open.
- **Call out his lies** → he offers a discount. **Accept** and you get a **permanent 20% discount** with him. If you **refuse**, he tries to flee and, whether he escapes or not, you can no longer buy from him.
- **Neutralize him** → you lose Charles Bucks as a ripperdoc.

## Rewards

- €$6,400, 1,000 XP and 770 Street Cred.
