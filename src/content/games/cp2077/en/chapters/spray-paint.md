**Spray Paint** is the second part of the chain of **Brendan**, the AI vending machine at **Megabuilding H8** in **Japantown** (Westbrook, Act 2). It starts right after **I Can See Clearly Now**.

> **Order:** talk to Brendan → wait a day or more → return to the marker → stop the vandal → talk to Brendan.

## First talk

Talk to Brendan. He mentions **Theo Price**, a girl who just talked to him; V can say whether they saw her or not. Then the marker disappears from the map.

## The vandal

After some time (it can be a day or more), the marker returns. Go back and you will find a **vandal** spray-painting Brendan. Deal with him however you like: the wiki does not ask for a specific method.

## The last talk

Brendan thanks you and chats for a while, until he suddenly makes an unsettling remark: that you are dying. Then he explains that he cannot read minds; he just picks up everything he hears and works out what is going on with each person. The chain ends with **Coin Operated Boy**, given by Theo.

## Rewards

The wiki lists no eddies, XP or Street Cred for this job.
