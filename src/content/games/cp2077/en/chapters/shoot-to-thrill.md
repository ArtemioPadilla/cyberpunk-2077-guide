**Shoot To Thrill** comes from **Robert Wilson**, owner of the **2nd Amendment** gun shop in **Megabuilding H10** (Little China), the same building as your apartment. He calls some time after **Playing for Time**, in **Act 2**: he's hosting a shooting contest to drum up business. You compete against five other shooters; one gets kicked out for breaking Wilson's rule: **pistols only**.

> **Order:** talk to Wilson → go to the shooting range → enter → listen to the instructions → get into position → draw and aim → wait for the start → shoot the targets → talk to Wilson → collect your prize.

## How to win

- Bring a pistol with a **high fire rate** and a **large magazine**.
- You can **shoot targets in other lanes**, not just yours: take advantage of it.
- Pistols that fire several projectiles per shot count each projectile as a point (for example **Lizzie** or **Death and Taxes**).
- **40 hits** win it. Going past that gives no extra prize.

## Afterwards

- **First place** → Wilson gives you the **Lexington x-MOD2** and eddies.
- **Second place** → €$500.

With enough points in **Reflexes**, you can ask him how business is going: he laments that sales are still down and the contest didn't help. Before 2.0 the prize was the **Dying Night**, which you now get in **The Gun**.

## Rewards

- 266 XP and 397 Street Cred.
- First place: **Lexington x-MOD2** (iconic Power pistol with two mod slots and a fixed scope) and **€$1,200** per the wiki infobox (the same wiki's text says €$1,500).
- Second place: **€$500**.
