**Practice Makes Perfect** is the game's optional tutorial. **Jackie and T-Bug** offer it at the start of **The Rescue**, in Japantown (Westbrook): it is a **Militech** virtual-reality training shard, narrated by T-Bug. It has **four modules** (combat basics, hacking, stealth and advanced combat), but **only the first two are required** to finish it. **It gives no XP or Street Cred**, so do it only if you want to practice the controls. If you already know the game, you can skip it and lose nothing.

> **Order:** talk to the drill sergeant → module 1, combat basics → module 2, hacking → (optional) module 3, stealth → (optional) module 4, advanced combat → exit the simulation through the door.

> **Get ready:** nothing to prepare: the simulation hands you the weapons. If you skip it now, since **Update 2.3** you can replay it whenever you like from **Settings > Controls**, as long as you are not in combat or in the middle of something important. Careful: if you skipped it in The Rescue and open it later, **you will have to complete the first two modules** before you can leave.

## Module 1 — Combat Basics (required)

1. **Pick up the weapon**, the **M-10AF Lexington** pistol.
2. **Shoot all the targets**, wait for the next round and shoot them again.
3. **Pick up the second weapon**, the **M251s Ajax** assault rifle, and **eliminate all enemies**.
4. **Take the inhaler** (a **MaxDoc Mk.1**) and **use it to regain Health**. It is the quick heal you will use all game.
5. **Enter the training area** and **eliminate all enemies**.
6. **Step onto the platform** to move to the next module.

## Module 2 — Hacking (required)

1. **Approach the window** and **open your scanner**.
2. **Scan the two objects highlighted in gold.**
3. Go back to the window and **use the TV screen to distract** the enemies.
4. **Sneak up to the guard** and **eliminate him**. **Pick up the body** and **hide it**.
5. **Sneak up to the second guard**, take him down and hide the body.
6. **Enter the training area** and **take control of the camera**.
7. From the camera, **hack one of the guards with the Detonate Grenade quickhack** (it sets off his grenade).
8. **Exit camera view** and **connect to the Access Point**.
9. **Step onto the platform.** You can now **exit the simulation through the door** (optional): at this point the job counts as complete.

**Two useful things about hacking today:** since 2.0, **Breach Protocol** (the code minigame) is used on **access points**, not on enemies. On Switch 2 you can play the Breach Protocol minigame with the **touchscreen**, and since 2.1 its timer can be disabled in the settings. Quickhacks spend **RAM**, which regenerates on its own.

## Module 3 — Stealth (optional)

1. **Go to the window** and **tag all the guards**.
2. **Enter the training area**, **hide from the guards** and **sneak to the exit**.
3. **Enter the next area**, **tag the camera** and **reach the exit without being detected by the camera**.
4. **Step onto the platform.**

## Module 4 — Advanced Combat (optional)

This is the melee module. Follow the on-screen objectives in the arena:

1. **Hit the enemy 3 times with Fast Attacks**, then **land a combo**, then **a Strong Attack**.
2. **Hit the blocking enemy 3 times with Fast Attacks** and **break their block with a Strong Attack**.
3. **Block 3 attacks**, **block 2 Strong Attacks**, **counter 2 times** and **dodge 3 attacks**.
4. **Defeat the enemy.**
5. **Take the katana** and **defeat the next enemy**.
6. **Step into the training area** and **defeat all enemies**.
7. **Step onto the platform** and **exit the simulation through the door**.

## Choices

- **Doing it or skipping it** → changes nothing in the story or your character: no XP, Street Cred or items. (IGN says you earn experience "in various categories"; that is probably pre-2.0 text, and the wiki says there is no reward.)
- **Doing only modules 1 and 2** → the job still counts as complete. Modules 3 and 4 are extra practice.

> **Don't leave without:** nothing. Whatever you pick up in the simulation stays in the simulation.

## Rewards

- **None:** this job gives no level XP or Street Cred.
