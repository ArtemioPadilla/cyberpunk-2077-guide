Needs **Phantom Liberty**: Dogtown opens in Act 2 with Dog Eat Dog, after Transmission. **Mr. Hands** gives it to you after **Get It Together**. It starts at **Bree Whitney**'s apartment in Wellsprings (Heywood) and continues under Dogtown, at **Cynosure Site D**.

> **Order:** Bree's apartment → Militech card → write to Mr. Hands → under the Kress Street building → follow Bree → Cynosure Site D → control room → decide.

## The apartment

Force the door open and find the **bamboo plant** in the bedroom: under it is a safe box with a **Militech card**. On your way out, a neighbor worried about Bree stops you (optional). Write to Mr. Hands: he sends you to meet Bree under the **Kress Street Hideout** building, after dark (in daytime she only complains).

## Cynosure Site D

Take the elevator to the lower level and scan the footprints to find Bree in a cave. The card won't open one door, so you take another route: behind each door are **cameras, turrets and laser mines**. Scan the four threats and deactivate the four defense systems; the turrets only switch on if a camera detects you. In the control room Bree finds **Evan McRay**'s video logs, and on your way out Militech agent **Dante Caruso** confronts you.

## Choices

- **Side with Bree** → kill Dante and take his **Ol' Reliable** revolver; the **Riskit** pistol is in the boxes on the right. Mr. Hands pays you, but later reveals Bree was working for NetWatch: days later a NetWatch hit squad attacks you in the street.
- **Side with Dante** → Bree dies, the Riskit lies on the floor, Dante gives you a shard that exposes her and pays you. Mr. Hands is displeased.
- **Data for Mr. Hands** → with Bree dead, kill Dante before he takes the data; pick up **Database: Cynosure Site D**, the Riskit and Ol' Reliable, and leave the shard at the Drop Point.

## Rewards

- €$5,000 (from Mr. Hands or Dante)
- 1,832 XP and 167 Street Cred
- Depending on the choice: Riskit, Ol' Reliable
