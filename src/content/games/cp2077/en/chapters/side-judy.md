**Judy Álvarez**'s chain continues the Evelyn Parker line and has five jobs: **Both Sides, Now**, **Ex-Factor**, **Talkin' 'bout a Revolution**, **Pisces** and **Pyramid Song**. Both Sides, Now opens after **Double Life**, but only once you have called **Mr. Hands** in **M'ap Tann Pèlen**. Judy wants revenge for Evelyn and has a plan: take **Clouds** from the **Tyger Claws** from the inside. game8 advises **always answering Judy's calls straight away**.

> **For the ending:** Judy **opens and closes no ending**; this chain is story and romance only. Her romance needs a **feminine body and feminine voice** (lifepath and genitalia don't matter). A feminine V can hold Judy and **River** at the same time with no penalty, but on the rooftop you call only one partner. **Judy stays with V only in The Star**; in the other endings she leaves, and The Tower ends the romance. What cuts the chain: telling her the plan is a bad idea in Ex-Factor, siding with Maiko **and taking the money** in Pisces, and in Pyramid Song picking "A pleasant distraction" or leaving while she sleeps.

> **Order:** **Both Sides, Now:** Judy's apartment (Evelyn) → rooftop → **Ex-Factor:** accept the plan → Clouds balcony at dawn → Maiko's office (Death and Taxes) → Woodman (if alive) → **Talkin' 'bout a Revolution:** pizza with Tom, Roxanne and Maiko → stay the night → **Pisces:** maintenance level → rooftop → Hiromi's balcony (Tsumetogi) → the Maiko choice → **Pyramid Song:** the lake shack at Laguna Bend at sunrise → the dive → generator → the next morning.

> **Get ready:** **Evelyn always dies**: no earlier choice prevents it. What you do carry over is **Woodman**: if he survived **Automatic Love**, Ex-Factor lets you take revenge on him; if you killed him there, that branch doesn't exist and Maiko is more hostile. **Save before Pisces**: the final choice decides the romance. For Pisces, bring weapons to clear the Tyger Claws on the maintenance level.

## Both Sides, Now

Judy asks you to come to her apartment (Charter street, Northside) as soon as possible.

1. In the **bathroom** you find **Evelyn dead in the bathtub**. Judy calls the NCPD, who won't come.
2. **Carry Evelyn's body** to the bedroom and leave Judy alone with her.
3. **Talk to Judy on the roof.** You give her a cigarette from **Evelyn's cigarette case** and Judy lets you keep it.

Judy tells you what she found: **Woodman** (Oswald Forrest, the Clouds manager) kept Evelyn for three days, left her with the ripperdoc **Fingers** as payment, and Fingers sold her to the **Scavengers**. Judy needs time to think and promises to call. The next day she texts that she buried Evelyn at the **Columbarium**.

## Ex-Factor

A few days later, Judy tells you her plan: do to Clouds what the Mox did to Lizzie's Bar. She needs an ally inside: **Maiko Maeda**, the Clouds manager.

**The first choice is the most dangerous one: accept.** If you tell her it's a **bad idea**, the job disappears from the journal and **every remaining Judy job and her romance are gone**. Permanent.

1. Meet Judy **early in the morning** on the balcony by Clouds (12th floor of Megabuilding H8). Let her do the talking: she knows Maiko better than you.
2. Judy opens the door by hacking a maintenance panel. Go into **Maiko's office**.
3. **Death and Taxes** (iconic Nue pistol) is on a **table near the entrance** of the office. If you miss it, you can pick it up later at Judy's apartment after Maiko messages you.
4. Maiko refuses to join.

**Revenge on Woodman** (only if he survived Automatic Love). If you let Judy talk, Maiko tells you Woodman is on a break on the **maintenance level**. Go down with Judy: he regrets nothing and dies there. If you let **Judy** kill him, she will hate herself for having become a killer. Fandom adds, unconfirmed, that **threatening Maiko** removes the option to kill Woodman: don't threaten her if you want that scene.

If you killed Woodman in Automatic Love, Judy decides to cool off and call Maiko the next day. On the way out you can ask about her past with Maiko (they used to be together). Judy plans to recruit former Clouds dolls and will call you.

## Talkin' 'bout a Revolution

A pizza meeting with **Tom**, **Roxanne** and **Maiko**.

- **Reflexes** and **Body** checks add dialogue.
- The "sparring" with Tom is scripted: it can't be won.
- **Commit** to the plan: "You can count on me". If you back out, you can recover by calling Judy.
- **game8's romance tips:** refuse the pay ("Out of the question") and don't burden her with the Relic ("Forget about it. It's passed."). Fandom only notes you can tell her about the Relic.
- **Stay the night** on the couch.

## Pisces

The day of the takeover. **Save before you start.**

1. **Clear the Tyger Claws** on the Megabuilding maintenance level.
2. Take the **freight elevator** to the roof and **drop onto Hiromi's balcony**.
3. In the office, the **Tsumetogi** (iconic katana) is on a display: **grab it before the dialogue starts**, or take it from Hiromi if you neutralise him.
4. **Maiko betrays the plan** in front of the bosses **Hiromi Sato**, **Jun Azegami** and **Marcus Ichida**. She offers you money: €$27,900 base, **€$37,200** with the Streetkid haggle.

**The choice** (details under "Choices"): of the four outcomes, **only siding with Maiko and taking the money locks Pyramid Song**. Killing Maiko is optional; game8 recommends a line that ends with killing her, but its own Pisces page confirms that not taking the money is enough.

## Pyramid Song

If you didn't take Maiko's money, Judy invites you to the **lake shack at Laguna Bend at sunrise**.

1. **Calibrate the dive suit.**
2. **Dive to the drowned town.** Scan the **restaurant**, **her home**, the **gas station** and the **church**. Give Judy the camera.
3. **The Relic malfunctions** underwater; Judy performs CPR.
4. Start the **generator** and talk to Judy in the bathroom.

**Underwater (rewards):** the **Heart of Laguna Bend** and a **cross necklace** on the church steps.

**Romance (feminine body and voice):**

1. "Why not" when she asks you to stay the night.
2. **Kiss her.**
3. In the morning, anything **except "A pleasant distraction"**. game8 suggests "The beginning of something amazing."

**Don't leave while she sleeps** even if Johnny tells you to: Judy disappears and deletes you from her life.

## Choices

- **Automatic Love — kill Woodman or not** (Act 2) → if he lives, Ex-Factor offers revenge; if he died, that branch doesn't exist and Maiko is more hostile.
- **Ex-Factor — say the plan is a bad idea** → **end of the chain and the romance. Permanent.**
- **Ex-Factor — kill Woodman** → he dies unrepentant; if you let Judy do it, she'll carry having become a killer.
- **Ex-Factor — threaten Maiko** → per Fandom (unconfirmed), the option to kill Woodman disappears.
- **Talkin' 'bout a Revolution — back out** → recoverable by calling Judy.
- **Pisces — side with Maiko and take the money** → Judy cuts ties and leaves Night City; Clouds is no better under Maiko; **Pyramid Song is locked and there is no romance. Permanent.**
- **Pisces — side with Maiko and refuse the money** → Judy is disappointed but accepts it; Clouds is no better; Pyramid Song stays open (Judy is sad later).
- **Pisces — side with Judy and kill Maiko** → Judy is shocked; the Tyger Claws retake Clouds, Tom is killed and Roxanne goes into hiding; Pyramid Song open.
- **Pisces — side with Judy and leave Maiko alive** → Judy is pleased (a feminine V gets a cheek kiss); the dolls run Clouds, though Tom is still killed in retaliation and Roxanne flees; Pyramid Song open. It is the kindest outcome.
- **Pyramid Song — "A pleasant distraction"** → Judy leaves Night City and deletes you. **Permanent.**
- **Pyramid Song — full romance** → Judy stays and you get access to her apartment.
- **Pyramid Song — not romanced or masculine V, staying until morning** → Judy leaves Night City and gives you her apartment.
- **Pyramid Song — leave while she sleeps** → Judy disappears and deletes you. **Permanent.**

> **Don't leave without:** **Death and Taxes** (Maiko's office, Ex-Factor; recoverable at Judy's), the **Tsumetogi** (Hiromi's display, Pisces; take it before the dialogue), the **Heart of Laguna Bend** and the church necklace (Pyramid Song) and **Evelyn's cigarette case** (Both Sides, Now). If you lose Death and Taxes or the Tsumetogi, Herold Lowe sells them in Phantom Liberty.

## Rewards

- **Both Sides, Now:** 578 XP, 866 Street Cred and Evelyn's cigarette case.
- **Ex-Factor:** 578 XP, 866 Street Cred and **Death and Taxes**.
- **Talkin' 'bout a Revolution:** 578 XP, 866 Street Cred.
- **Pisces:** 578 XP, 866 Street Cred, **Tsumetogi** and, if you accept, Maiko's bribe (up to €$37,200; it locks the romance).
- **Pyramid Song:** the **Mox** shotgun on Judy's kitchen counter (right away if not romanced; if romanced, after a text a few days later); romance only, the **MaxTac helmet, jacket and pants** at her apartment; memorabilia: the diving box and the Laguna Bend photo; underwater, the **Heart of Laguna Bend** and the cross necklace.
