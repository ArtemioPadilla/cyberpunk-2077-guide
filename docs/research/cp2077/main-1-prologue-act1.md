## 0. Job list and order (verified)

Per [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Main Jobs] and its category:

- **Prologue.** Only one runs, based on the lifepath you pick.
  - The Corpo-Rat (`q000_corpo`)
  - The Nomad (`q000_nomad`)
  - The Streetkid (`q000_street_kid`)
  - Wiki title is "The Streetkid". IGN calls it "The Street Kid". [W][IGN]
- **Act 1**, in wiki order:
  1. Practice Makes Perfect (optional, `q000_tutorial`)
  2. The Rescue (`q001_intro`)
  3. The Ripperdoc (`q001_01_victor`)
  4. The Ride (`q001_02_dex`)
  5. The Pickup (`q003_maelstrom`)
  6. The Information (`q004_braindance`)
  7. The Heist (`q005_heist`)
- The Pickup and The Information can be done in either order. Both are needed for The Heist. [W:The Ride][G8][PPX]
- **Interlude:** Love Like Fire (`q101_01_firestorm`). [W]
- **Act 2 starts:** Playing for Time (`q101_resurrection`). [W]
- Labeling differences:
  - IGN files The Rescue under "Prologue" and calls The Ripperdoc "the first mission in Act 1". [IGN]
  - The wiki puts Practice Makes Perfect and The Rescue in Act 1. [W]
- PowerPyx numbering: The Pickup is the "5th main story mission" and The Heist the "7th". [PPX]
- Exploration is limited to Watson during Act 1. [W:Main Jobs]
- **Point of no return:** once V gets into the Delamain cab in The Heist, V cannot go back to Night City or finish other missions until Act 2. [W:The Heist]

## 1. The Nomad (Nomad lifepath prologue)

- Quest giver Willie McCoy. Badlands, Los Padres County: Yucca, Dry Creek's Farm, SoCal Border Crossing, Biotechnica Flats. [W]
- Reward: a car model memorabilia on the desk in V's apartment. [W]

**Objectives in order** [W]:

1. Talk to the mechanic, fix the engine, get in the car, start the engine.
2. Talk to the sheriff, leave the garage.
3. Go to the telecom tower, climb it, connect your sat phone, talk to McCoy.
4. Drive to the meeting place (Dry Creek's Farm) and meet Jackie. Follow him and open the trunk.
5. Drive to the border checkpoint. Deposit your weapons at the front desk, go to room 2, sit, talk to the border guard.
6. Collect your weapons, return to the car, depart.
7. Fight off pursuers, go to the safe location, talk to Jackie.

**Walkthrough tips and loot** [IGN]:

- The garage holds the first shard ("The Unification War... For Gonks!") plus consumables.
- After you exit the garage, enter the mechanic's office from the front. A drawer behind his desk holds €$100.
- Between the trailers near a guitar player there is a shard and €$61.
- The telecom tower gate needs a kick. A side platform has common crafting materials.
- Inside Jackie's trailer: €$45 on the middle shelf under the bed, plus junk and a shard.
- At the border V gives the guard the declaration, and can slip in the bribe (credit chip) with it. [IGN][W]
- Car-chase combat: ammo is unlimited (you still reload). Shoot the car grilles between the headlights to blow up pursuers. About three pursuing vehicles. [IGN]
- In the garage there are common item components in a crate near the front-left tire. [IGN]
- The cargo is a live iguana. [W][IGN]

**Choices:**

- Dialogue with the mechanic and the sheriff has no listed consequences. [IGN]
- PowerPyx: lifepath choice only changes the prologue and some dialogue. No lifepath is mechanically better. [PPX:story-choices]

**Starting gear (regular start):** DR5 Nova; V's vest with or without the Bakkers patch; nomad pants and boots. [W:Nomad (Lifepath)]

**Lifepath side job later:** "These Boots Are Made for Walkin'". [W:Nomad (Lifepath)]

## 2. The Streetkid (Streetkid lifepath prologue)

- Quest giver Pepe Najarro. El Coyote Cojo (The Glen, Heywood) and Embers (Westbrook). [W]
- Reward: a Night City diorama memorabilia. The official guide wrongly says Arasaka clock. [W]

**Objectives in order** [W]:

1. Find Kirk on the second floor, talk to Kirk, leave the bar.
2. Talk to Padre, ride with him to Embers.
3. Take the elevator to the garage (-01 Parking per IGN).
4. Find the Rayfield. Optional: find Rick (the man in the booth).
5. Disable the lock with Kirk's device, get in, "steal" the Rayfield.
6. Talk to the thief (Jackie), the police, then Jackie.

**Notes:**

- Kirk Sawyer and Big Joe join at the booth. The deal: steal a Rayfield Aerondight to clear Pepe's debt and earn a cut. [IGN][W]
- There is a brief 6th Street encounter (Sam Carter) during Padre's ride. No combat. Padre hands V his card. [W][IGN]
- Inspector Stints dumps V and Jackie in an alley instead of following the Arasaka corpo's order. [W]
- No consequential choices are documented. [PPX:story-choices]

**Starting gear:** Unity pistol, street clothing. [W:Streetkid (Lifepath)]

**Lifepath side job later:** "Small Man, Big Mouth". It follows Heroes and is Streetkid only. [W:Heroes][W:Streetkid (Lifepath)]

## 3. The Corpo-Rat (Corpo lifepath prologue)

- Quest giver Arthur Jenkins. Arasaka Tower (Corpo Plaza) and Lizzie's Bar. [W]
- Reward: an Arasaka clock memorabilia. [W]

**Objectives in order** [W]:

1. Leave the bathroom.
2. Head to Jenkins' office. Optional: talk to your old acquaintance (Frank Nostra).
3. Sit, watch the vote, talk to Jenkins. Take the datashard and the money.
4. Leave the office, go to the AV garage. Talk to Jackie. Optional: read the report on your personal terminal.
5. Get in Jenkins' AV, set course for Lizzie's, fly there, exit the AV.
6. Deal with the locals (the basketball players). V can subdue them with a lightning attack or threaten them. [W]
7. Head to Lizzie's, talk to the bouncer, meet and sit with Jackie.
8. Talk to the agents, hand over the datashard, talk to Jackie.

**Notes:**

- IGN: Jenkins hands V **€$45,800**. [IGN]
- **(CONFLICT/UNVERIFIED) Is the money kept?** IGN says V keeps "the bundle of cash Jenkins gave you" after the agents strip their assets. The wiki only says the agents strip V's corporate privileges and cyberware. Verify in-game whether Corpo V starts The Rescue with extra eddies.
- If you wait out the agents' timer, V's cyberware really does shut off and the HUD disappears. [W]
- Optional choice at Harry's cubicle (pull the BioTechnica-exposed spy or keep him digging): "no impact on the story". [IGN]
- Frank: "no repercussions". [IGN]
- Loot: lore shards in the lobby and offices, booze, a roll of bills on a gossip table. There is a clothing container behind the fallen hoop on the basketball court and a pink cabinet with clothing in Lizzie's DJ hallway. [IGN]

**Starting gear:** Unity pistol, office clothing. [W:Corpo (Lifepath)]

**Lifepath side job later:** "War Pigs". [W:Corpo (Lifepath)]

## 4. Practice Makes Perfect (optional tutorial)

- Offered by Jackie and T-Bug at the start of The Rescue. It is a Militech VR training shard. [W][IGN]
- Four modules: Combat Basics, Hacking, Stealth, Advanced Combat (melee, katana). Only the **first two are required** to finish. [W]
  - (CONFLICT, minor) IGN marks Hacking as mandatory and Stealth as optional. That is consistent with the wiki.
- **No XP or Street Cred reward.** [W]
- (CONFLICT) IGN says you "earn experience in various categories". That is likely pre-2.0 skill XP. (UNVERIFIED for 2.5)
- **Update 2.3** added a replay option in Settings > Controls, usable any time outside combat. If you skipped the job, the first two modules are required before you can exit. [W]

## 5. The Rescue

- Quest giver Wakako Okada. Scavenger Den (Japantown, room 1237) and V's apartment (Megabuilding H10). [W]
- Wiki rewards: 1,460 XP and 1,167 Street Cred (as listed). [W]
- IGN: you earn your first 2 Street Cred on exiting the elevator, and reach level 2 on arriving home. [IGN]

**Objectives in order (main line)** [W]:

1. Talk to Jackie, follow him, take the elevator, talk to T-Bug.
2. Head to the apartment, hack the door, examine the woman's body.
3. Find Sandra Dorsett. Optional stealth steps:
   - Sneak up on the first scav and neutralize him.
   - Hide the body in the freezer.
   - Hide from the scavs and wait unnoticed.
   - Use the path T-Bug marks.
4. Defeat the scavenger leader. Optional: go onto the terrace and distract him.
5. Examine the body, check vitals, save Sandra with the AirHypo.
6. Carry her to the terrace, wait for Trauma Team (wait by the door), place her on the stretcher.
7. Take the elevator and call Wakako.
8. Ride back with Jackie, defeat the scavengers in the van.
9. Go home and go to bed. Optional: check your weapons stash.

**Walkthrough details:**

- Five scavs in the main room plus the Scav Leader in the next room. [W]
- Stealth route: let the two door guards walk into your doorway and take them down with Jackie. Hug the right wall into the side bathroom to bypass the others. [IGN]
- Scav Leader options: [IGN][G8]
  - Hack the lamp and wait until he stares into it.
  - Use Reboot Optics, then go for headshots.
  - Climb through the left-side window to flank him.
- Garage: a suitcase between two leather chairs holds armor (requires level 2). There is a MaxDoc Mk.1 to the right of the elevator exit. [IGN]
- Trauma Team will not land if V stands too close. Step back by the door. [IGN]
- Van ambush: four scavs (two at the rear doors, one at the side door, the driver). Shoot the driver to end it quickly. The van crashes and kills them all anyway. [W][G8][IGN]
- Choices: none consequential (easing the woman back into her apartment changes nothing). [IGN][PPX]

**Unlocks during and after:** [W:Side Jobs][W:The Gig][W:The Gift][IGN]

- **The Gig** (Wakako): collect your reward after the Watson lockdown lifts, i.e. in Act 2.
  - Wakako pays €$3,380.
  - Then collect a free Tattoo: Tyger Claws Dermal Imprint at Cassius Ryder's clinic.
- **The Gift** (T-Bug calls on the way up to the apartment): a free Ping quickhack from Yoko Tsuru's netrunner shop at Kabuki Roundabout.
- **The Gun**: Robert Wilson at 2nd Amendment in Megabuilding H10 gives you the Iconic Dying Night pistol free.
  - Streetkid V gets special "rite of passage" dialogue.
  - If Shoot To Thrill is active, Dying Night can't be bought until that quest is done. [W:The Gun]
  - Dying Night received a new iconic modifier in Patch 2.0. [W:Dying Night]

## 6. The Ripperdoc

- Quest giver Jackie. Misty's Esoterica and Viktor's Clinic (Little China). [W]
- **Reward cyberware** (wiki infobox): Basic Kiroshi Optics, Ballistic Coprocessor, Subdermal Armor. [W]
  - Wiki item pages for Subdermal Armor and Ballistic Coprocessor both say "Obtained during The Ripperdoc". [W]
  - (CONFLICT) PowerPyx lists only "Kiroshi Optics MK.1 and Ballistic Coprocessor". [PPX]
  - (CONFLICT) IGN says pick Ocular System (Kiroshi) and Hands (Ballistic Coprocessor). [IGN]
  - Subdermal Armor is probably a 2.0 addition. (UNVERIFIED which set 2.5 installs)

**Objectives in order** [W]:

1. Talk to Jackie, meet him outside H10. Optional: check email and your weapons stash.
2. Call up your car, drive to the ripperdoc.
3. Talk to Viktor, take a seat, install the new cyberware.
4. Scan Viktor, talk to Viktor.

**Choices and consequences:**

- **Payment, €$21,000:**
  - "Pay you once the job's done" starts the minor job **Paid in Full**. Viktor's services are unavailable until you pay. There is no deadline. [W:Paid in Full][IGN][PPX]
  - Paying up front avoids it. PowerPyx says you will almost never have the money this early. [PPX]
  - If you do pay later, Viktor tells you to keep it, but V insists. [W:Paid in Full]
- Streetkid has unique dialogue about Dex's gang-war past with Jackie. [W:Streetkid dialogue]

**Tips:**

- Leaving the apartment: Regina Jones calls about Watson work. There is a Drop Point to the left, where you can "Sell All Junk". [IGN]
- In H10: Coach Fred's "Beat on the Brat" can be started now. Wilson's "The Gun" is opposite the elevator. [IGN]
- IGN's suggested extra buys (Titanium Bones, Microgenerator at €$1,000) are **pre-2.0 era**. Titanium Bones may no longer exist in that form. (UNVERIFIED for 2.5; do not reuse without checking)
- Drive carefully. Hitting people or property can trigger NCPD. [G8]
- Known bug: stuck on "Talk to Viktor" with only "How's biz?" available. Save and reload. [G8] (UNVERIFIED)

## 7. The Ride

- Quest giver Jackie. Misty's Esoterica to Dex's limo (Chevillon Thrax), dropped off at Kabuki Roundabout. [W]
- Wiki rewards: 1,516 XP and 167 Street Cred. [W]

**Objectives** [W]: talk to Jackie, meet Dex, get in the limo, talk to Dex, exit, call Jackie, talk to Jackie.

**Content:**

- Dex briefs the Arasaka biochip job.
- Maelstrom ambushed a Militech convoy and took the **Flathead**.

**After the job:**

- You can call Regina Jones about Royce. [W]
- IGN suggests doing **The Gift** now, since you are dropped off near Kabuki Roundabout. [IGN]

## 8. The Pickup (the big branching job)

- Quest giver Dex. All Foods Factory (Plant), Talbot Place, Northside, Watson. [W]
- Rewards: 1,547 XP and 1,727 Street Cred. **€$3,000 to €$13,000**, depending on the path. [W]
- Items: Iconic **Chaos** pistol (dependent). **Black Lace** (Streetkid only). [W]
- PowerPyx also lists the **Spiked Maelstrom Short-Sleeve Jacket** from Royce. IGN calls it "Epic". [PPX][IGN]
- Recommended level about 3. [IGN]

### 8.1 Objectives (core order) [W]

1. Meet Jackie. If you arrive first, wait for him by All Foods.
2. Optional Militech branch:
   - Call Meredith Stout, meet her in the storm channel under the overpass, talk.
   - Corpo only: if V says they know the chip is spiked, the optional "Remove the virus from the chip" appears.
3. Talk to Jackie, go to the gate, use the intercom, talk to Maelstrom (Dum Dum), get to the main production floor.
4. Friendly path:
   - Get in the elevator, wait, talk to the Maelstromers.
   - Sit on the couch, talk, take the Flathead, talk again.
5. Then one of these:
   - **Peaceful exit.** Take the elevator down with Dum Dum, follow him, talk, leave.
   - **Militech assault.** Pass through the maintenance tunnel and turn on the production line. Optionally free Brick. Defeat the Militech agents, follow Dum Dum, escape, talk to **Anthony Gilchrist**.
   - **Hostile path.** Defeat the Maelstromers and talk to Jackie. If combat started before the Flathead was shown, find and open the case. Take the Flathead, go through the maintenance tunnel and production line, optionally free Brick.
     - Then either "Sneak past Royce" (optional), or "Defeat Royce and his crew" if you are spotted.
     - If you met Militech: leave and talk to **Meredith Stout**.
6. Follow Jackie, talk to Jackie, call Dex.
   - If The Information is not done, Dex sends you to Lizzie's.
   - If it is done, he sends you to the Afterlife (The Heist).

### 8.2 Meredith Stout meeting (optional)

- A bodyguard jacks into V and runs a lie-detector interrogation. Gilchrist is present as her suspect. [W:The Pickup][W:Anthony Gilchrist][IGN]
- Deal: V pays Maelstrom with her **Militech credchip** (Militech Datashard). It secretly carries a daemon (malware). [W]
- Lifepath lines here:
  - Corpo can say she is in no position to negotiate, and that they know she spiked the chip. [W:Corpo dialogue]
  - Nomad can offer to help find the mole and patch up the convoys' security. Stout isn't interested. [W:Nomad dialogue]
- **Hacking the chip** (Shards menu, Breach Protocol):
  - **Copy the malware:** Synapse Burnout quickhack. [W][IGN][G8]
  - **Neutralize the malware:** €$10,000, decrypted later from the Backpack ("Hacked Militech Datashard"). [W:The Pickup]
  - **Both are possible.** IGN's sequence: solve the bottom code first (BD BD FF 55), then the top (55 1C E9), i.e. BD BD FF 55 1C E9. Save first. [IGN][W]
  - (CONFLICT) The wiki's Militech Datashard page says the copy only works if done **before** neutralizing. It also says the €$10,000 only pays out if the chip is hacked **after** The Pickup is completed. [W:Militech Datashard]
  - (CONFLICT) IGN: "not certain if you are able to keep the Militech Shard money". [IGN]
  - (CONFLICT) game8 says copying leaves the malware in and leads to a Maelstrom fight. [G8]
  - Treat the exact €$10k mechanics as (UNVERIFIED for 2.5).

### 8.3 Inside All Foods: before Royce

- Militech mines on the stairs and doorway, and turrets and cameras, are inactive. **Shooting or disabling security turns Maelstrom hostile.** [IGN][W]
  - Exception: turning off cameras through the catwalk computer on the main production floor does not alert them. [W note]
- Loot on the way in: a crate with uncommon item components and a shard behind the shelves on the right at the entrance. You can grab grenades and ammo in the lobby before the elevator. [IGN]
- **Dum Dum's demand that Jackie sit:** you must tell Jackie to sit, or Maelstrom turns hostile. [W][IGN][G8]
- Dum Dum offers an inhaler (Black Lace). Taking or refusing it doesn't matter. [IGN]
  - Streetkid V can identify it as Black Lace. [W:Streetkid dialogue]
  - Streetkid also gets an extra Black Lace from Dum Dum at the end of the peaceful or Maelstrom-friendly outcomes, even if they refused earlier. [W:Black Lace][W:The Pickup]
- **Royce's timed dialogue:**
  - Letting the timer run out on "how are you going to pay" gets V shot in the head. Reload. [W][PPX]
  - PowerPyx: there is an autosave just before Royce comes out. [PPX]
  - Key lines: "Fuck Brick. Let's cut a new deal" (timed). Then pay options or "[Draw weapon]" / "[Take down Royce]". [PPX]

### 8.4 Outcome matrix (wiki "Paths")

**Path 2: Peaceful transaction.** All of these must hold:

- You never met Meredith.
- No weapon pointed at Maelstrom, no attacks.
- You told Jackie to sit.
- You paid Royce **€$10,000 of your own**.

Results: Dum Dum escorts you out. Streetkid gets Black Lace. This is the **only** way to unlock the **Maelstrom Weapon Vendor** at All Foods afterwards. It also keeps Dum Dum alive, so he carries **Doom Doom** in Second Conflict (Act 2). [W][W:Weapon Vendor (Maelstrom)]

- IGN: you lose out on looting the factory. Not recommended unless you want to stay friendly with everyone. [IGN]

**Path 3: Fight Militech (side with Maelstrom).** All of these must hold:

- You met Meredith (chip taken or not).
- No hostility, and you told Jackie to sit.
- You paid with one of: the chip after removing its malware; the chip with a heads-up warning to Royce (**Corpo only**); or your own €$10,000.

Results: [W]

- Militech storms the plant. Dum Dum leads you through. Fight Militech, including mechs in the last room alongside Royce.
- You can free Brick, but in this path you never see him again.
- **Chaos** is possible only if Royce dies in the Militech attack. Let him fight alone.
- Gilchrist meets you at the gate. He later has Meredith killed. Her body can be found off a north Watson pier between Ebunike Docks and the Oil Fields fast travel points. [W:Meredith Stout]
- **Nomad only:** find Gilchrist's "Transports - LOA" message on a computer in the red-lit side room of the first big hall after the negotiation room. Confront Gilchrist and he leaves a **crate of Militech gear in V's apartment in Act 2**. [W][IGN][G8]

**Path 4: Fight Maelstrom (side with Militech).** Triggered by **any** of these:

| Trigger                                                    | Royce at the end        | Royce kill reward |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ----------------- |
| Point a weapon at or attack Maelstrom                      | strong boss fight       | €$3,000           |
| Tamper with cameras or turrets (except via the catwalk PC) | strong boss fight       | €$3,000           |
| Don't sit, or don't tell Jackie to sit                     | strong boss fight       | €$3,000           |
| Pay with the infected chip, no warning                     | **weakened** boss fight | €$3,000           |
| Knock out or shoot Royce during the talk                   | no boss fight           | no extra money    |

- Royce scans the chip and hides. [IGN]
- PowerPyx: if you killed Royce early, only three regular Maelstromers wait in the last room. [PPX]
- Chaos drops from Royce in the negotiation room or after the boss fight. You can free Brick. [W]
- With Path 4, Maelstrom ambushes V while driving in Watson afterwards. [W note]
- Knockout requirement: "6 BODY [Take down Royce]" per PowerPyx and IGN. A game8 commenter reports "[12 Str]". (UNVERIFIED for 2.5)

### 8.5 Who waits at the gate (Meredith vs Gilchrist)

- **Wiki:** Meredith appears if you met her (deal or not) **and** Maelstrom became hostile before you got the Flathead. [W]
