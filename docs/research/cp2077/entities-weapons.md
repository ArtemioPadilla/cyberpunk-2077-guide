# Research notes: weapons (iconics, models, classes)

Target: Cyberpunk 2077: Ultimate Edition (base game + Phantom Liberty), Nintendo Switch 2, Update 2.5. Notes compiled 2026-10-30.
Scope: **every iconic weapon** (the 115 pages in Fandom's `Category:Cyberpunk 2077 Iconic weapons`, plus the iconic grenade Ozob's Nose, which is not in that category). Also **every standard weapon model** in the weapon-type categories (74 pages: 54 guns and 20 melee weapons, plus the unobtainable Kolac and the VR-only toy gun), and **25 class/type rows**: 6 technologies (Power, Tech, Smart, Blade, Blunt, Throwable) and 19 weapon types. **Total: 215 rows.**

## Conventions

- `kind`: `iconic` | `model` | `class`. Class/type rows have ids `class-*` / `type-*`. Model ids use the short common name (`unity`, `lexington`, `overture`…). `name_en` is the infobox `title` (exact in-game name, e.g. `Osprey`, `Rasetsu`, `3516`, `M-10AF Lexington`).
- `wiki_image` = the infobox `image =` value with spaces turned into underscores. On some model pages the API `pageimage` is a different variant (e.g. `Liberty_Military1.png` vs infobox `Liberty_Default.png`). I used the infobox value. Class/type rows have no own page: they point to sections of *Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons* and have no image.
- `category`: pistol, revolver, smg, shotgun, `shotgun (double-barrel)`, ar, lmg, hmg, precision-rifle, sniper-rifle, katana, sword, knife, machete, chainsword, axe, one-handed-club, two-handed-club, hammer, grenade.
- `class`: Power / Tech / Smart for guns. Blade / Blunt / Throwable for melee (the wiki infobox writes "Thrown").
- `jobs`: only guide job ids that exist as files in `chapters/`. Gigs, NCPD activities and most PL side jobs have no guide file yet, so the column is blank for them.
- `content`: `base` or `PL`, from the `{{CP|2077}}` / `{{CP|2077PL}}` tag in the page lead.
- `name_es_mx`: **left blank for every row**. No Latin-American Spanish names could be verified. The Spanish wiki is almost empty. Its only weapon page gives Death and Taxes = "Muerte e impuestos" and names the Iconic rarity "Emblemática". Both are **es-ES and from a 2020 page (UNVERIFIED for es-MX)**.
- **No prices.** Fandom lists no buy price for Herold Lowe's iconics (Order, Yasha, the resales), for the two melee-vendor iconics (Blue Fang, Headhunter) or for the standard models. game8 does not list them either. The only priced item is the Slaught-O-Matic (€$100 at a Budget Arms vending machine). Every "price" is therefore **(UNVERIFIED)** and needs an in-game check.
- Tags: **(UNVERIFIED)** = one source, or my reading of an absent mention. **(CONFLICT)** = sources disagree. **(inference)** = derived by me. **PRE-2.0** = text that describes 1.x behaviour.

## Key findings for the guide

### Herold Lowe (PL black market, EBM Petrochem Stadium, Dogtown)

Rule (Herold Lowe page): he sells **only iconics you had the chance to pick up and missed**. He does not sell iconics from quests you have not finished, or ones a choice made unavailable. Exception: he sells **Apparition** even if V is not Corpo [W:Herold Lowe]. Patch 2.1 added the five airdrop Techtronika iconics to his stock if not retrieved [W:Patch 2.1].

**Confirmed resales (34)**, each weapon page saying "can be purchased from Herold Lowe… if not looted": Alabai, Apparition, Bald Eagle, BFC 9000, Borzaya, Butcher's Cleaver, Carmen, Chaos (conditions still apply), Cocktail Stick, Crimestopper, Death and Taxes, Dezerter, Doom Doom, Errata, Hercules 3AX, Jinchu-maru, Kongou, La Chingona Dorada, Laika, Lizzie, Murphy's Law (Songbird path only), Nehan, Osprey, Plan B, Problem Solver, Sasquatch's Hammer, Satori, Sir John Phallustiff, Taigan, Tinker Bell, Tsumetogi, Volkodav, Widow Maker, Wild Dog.

**Herold-only stock (2):** **Order** (Brick's Satara, always for sale) and **Yasha** (after the 2.5 job *Welcome to Paradise*). He also sells BARGHEST variants of the HA-4 Grit, HA-7 Warden, M2038 Tactician and Kyubi, plus the Grit and Warden crafting specs [W:Herold Lowe/Store, model pages].

The rendered store page (built from pages linking to him) lists the same ranged iconics. Its DPL tables cover only ranged categories, so the melee resales are confirmed only on their own pages.

### Iconics with no resale documented (truly one-shot, or flagged)

- **Choice-locked:**
  - Fang, Crowbar and Erebus: Reed path. Erebus also excludes the iconic Militech Canto cyberdeck.
  - Pariah: you kill Reed in The Killing Moon.
  - Chaos and Doom Doom: mutually exclusive in The Pickup (both resold, but the conditions still apply).
  - Baby Boomer: only if Tool's letter was not read.
  - Carmen: help Paco (resold).
  - Cheetah, Riskit and Pizdets: depend on how the target NPC ends.
- **Flagged missable by Fandom:**
  - Hawk (Lucretia My Reflection).
  - Gold-plated baseball bat (Second Conflict).
  - Scalpel (Big in Japan).
  - Fenrir (Sacrum Profanum). The quest page says that even if the job fails, the gun can still be taken from the warehouse.
  - Kongou, Problem Solver, Hercules 3AX and Caretaker's Spade are also flagged, but Herold or the post-game regrant covers them.
- **No resale mentioned (UNVERIFIED whether missable):** Cottonmouth, Prototype: Shingen Mark V, Divided We Stand, O'Five, Lexington x-MOD2, Ambition, Agaou, Coronation (Clampdown is timed: it fails if you take more than 24 h to enter the prison).
- **Regranted after the credits** if you got them once ("Point of No Return" reward): Amnesty, Prejudice (since 2.0), Pride and Caretaker's Spade.
- **Always recoverable:**
  - Plan B: Dex's body, any time after Act 1.
  - Death and Taxes: Judy's apartment later.
  - Osprey: the safehouse back door.
  - Skippy: Regina returns it, but a 2.0 re-obtained copy has no AI and no mod slots.
  - Genjiroh: second copy in the Arasaka Estate.
- **Account rewards** (appear in the stash): Black Unicorn (GOG/CDPR link), Gwynbleidd (Witcher 3 + PL), Scorch (GWENT + PL).
- **Former Prime Gaming BARGHEST six:** since 2.1 they drop from regular Dogtown airdrops from **level 41**, in a fixed order: Chesapeake → Pit Bull → Amstaff → Chinook → Catahoula → Foxhound [W:Airdrops]. **(CONFLICT)** Chesapeake's own page says level 40.

### Timeline: when each weapon was added or changed (what still applies in 2.5)

- **1.5:**
  - Added: Bloody Maria, Byakko, Seraph (quest rewards), Guillotine and DA8 Umbra.
  - Changed: Lizzie moved to the private room next to Judy's den (it used to sit by the server room: PRE-1.5); Skippy lost its unique effect.
- **1.6** (Edgerunners content / New Weapons DLC): Guts, Hypercritical, Blue Fang and Headhunter, plus the Kappa, Pozhar, Kyubi, MA70 HB, Senkoh LX, Kolac, Cut-o-Matic, Claw, Punknife, Neurotoxin Knife and Razor models.
- **2.0 (and PL):**
  - New Throwable class; Nehan and Errata added.
  - New modifiers: Dying Night, Satori, Prejudice and Pride.
  - Black Unicorn became Iconic quality.
  - Changed effects: Fenrir (pre-2.0 Thermal/Burn), Bloody Maria and Death and Taxes (pre-2.0 split projectiles). Prejudice became a post-game reward. **Any pre-2.0 description of these is obsolete.**
  - PL added all the x-MOD2 weapons with 2 guaranteed mod slots, iconics up to T5++ from airdrops, and Herold Lowe.
- **2.1:** Achilles x-MOD2, BFC 9000, Claw x-MOD2 and MA70 HB x-MOD2 added. The ex-Prime BARGHEST iconics went into airdrops; the airdrop Techtronika iconics went into Herold's stock. Crafted weapons can have up to 2 mod slots.
- **2.11:** BFC 9000 made obtainable for players who missed the crashed AV; Catahoula can no longer take attachments.
- **2.5:**
  - **Coronation** (Tech Burya) and the **Disjunctor** iconic Tech weapon mod: Clampdown reward. Disjunctor gives +50% Bolt-shot damage and +10% charge speed after a fully charged headshot, stacking 5 times.
  - **Boar Carver**: a non-iconic throwable knife from Ariadne's messages, like Sihil.
  - **Yasha** tied to Welcome to Paradise.
  - **La Chingona Dorada** now grants Crit Chance instead of Crit Damage.
  - **Double Trouble** (Cool 9): dual-wield **Power pistols and revolvers**, at −37.5% damage each.

### Conflicts noted

- **O'Five:** infobox source says *Beat on the Brat: Rancho Coronado*, but the acquisition text and the Arroyo quest page say **Beat on the Brat: Arroyo** (Buck Arnold, Body 8 to raise the bet to €$18,000). The quest page wins.
- **Cottonmouth:** coffee table (weapon page) vs bed/bedroom (quest page, game8). Already flagged in the-space-in-between.md.
- **Clampdown unlock:** "The Heist + 2 Wakako gigs" (Update 2.5 patch notes) vs "start of Act 2 + 2 gigs" (quest page). **Welcome to Paradise:** "after The Heist + Street Cred 15" (patch notes) vs "Act 2 + 15 Street Cred" (quest page).
- **Update 2.5 Switch 2 date:** 26 Oct (infobox) vs 27 Oct (body). Same conflict as in 00-overview.
- **game8's PL weapons list** labels Bloody Maria and Seraph as PL weapons. Fandom says both are base-game 1.5 rewards. It also lists Errata, Sasquatch's Hammer and Order under "Black Market Vendor", which matches Herold Lowe's resale rule rather than a PL-only source.
- **Bald Eagle and Wild Dog** are "dropped by Kurt Hansen" [W]. The guide's firestarter.md has you loot them on the Songbird path, and Reed's path fights Hansen, so I record "both paths" **(inference)**. Fang is Reed-only and Murphy's Law Songbird-only [W].

### Not rows here

Arm cyberware (Mantis Blades, Gorilla Arms, Monowire, Projectile Launch System) is listed by the wiki under "Cyberware Weapons" → *Cyberpunk 2077 Cyberware*. All four can be bought from any ripperdoc from level 10. Gorilla Arms are also an ending reward, and 2.5 adds Glamdoc skins for all four [W:each page, W:Update 2.5]. They belong in the cyberware file. Grenades beyond Ozob's Nose belong in the items file.

## Classes and types

- **Power** (`class-power`): conventional firearms using traditional ammo. 5% base ricochet chance, raised to 100% (66% for shotguns) by the **Ballistic Coprocessor** (Hands cyberware). Most take a muzzle and a scope. **Kongou** ricochets without the cyberware. Only Power pistols and revolvers can be dual-wielded (Double Trouble, 2.5). [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons] [W:Double Trouble]
- **Tech** (`class-tech`): railguns. Hold to charge; charged shots penetrate obstacles. No silencer, most take a sight. Tech perks are in Technical Ability, and use gives Engineer skill XP. Some fire full auto when fully charged (Senkoh LX, DR-12 Quasar, HA-4 Grit; buffed about 15% in 2.1). [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons] [W:Patch 2.1]
- **Smart** (`class-smart`): guided gyrojet rounds that curve around cover but fly slower. Lock-on needs the **Smart Link** cyberware (Skippy is the documented exception). No suppressor or scope. Tyger Claws' masking-ink tattoos give smart rounds a 50% miss chance. Perks are in Intelligence. [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons] [W:Skippy]
- **Blade** (`class-blade`), **Blunt** (`class-blunt`, non-lethal, can stun), **Throwable** (`class-throwable`, added in 2.0: thrown, then auto-returned after the throw-recovery time, or at once if V is in melee range). [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons]
- **Ammo families:** Handgun Ammo is shared by pistols, revolvers and SMGs, all usable while driving. Heavy Ammo is shared by ARs, LMGs and precision rifles. Shotguns use Shotgun Ammo, sniper rifles Sniper Ammo. HMGs come only from turrets (Body check) or enemies: they cannot be holstered, block sprinting, hold 300 rounds and overheat. [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons]
- **Tiers:** 11 qualities, T1 → T5++. Unlock levels: T2 at 9, T3 at 17, T4 at 25, T5 at 33, T5++ at 51 (PL). Mod slots start at T2. Iconics sold by vendors or found as specs scale to T5 (level 33); rewarded or world iconics scale to T5+ (level 41). **x-MOD2** iconics (PL) have 2 guaranteed mod slots and better handling. Other iconics normally have no mod slots and carry a unique iconic modifier. [W:Cyberpunk 2077 Weapons]
- The 19 `type-*` rows mirror the `category` values. Swords exist only as the iconics Gwynbleidd and Sihil. Knives are throwable, except Butcher's Cleaver, which "is the only Knife which cannot be thrown" (and uses the axe finisher). [W:Butcher's Cleaver]
