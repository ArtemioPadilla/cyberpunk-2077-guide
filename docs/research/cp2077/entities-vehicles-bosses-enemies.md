# Research notes: vehicles, bosses and cyberpsychos, enemies and factions

Target: Cyberpunk 2077: Ultimate Edition (base game + Phantom Liberty), Nintendo Switch 2, Update 2.5. Notes compiled 2026-10-30.

Scope: three tables. (1) **Vehicles:** all 87 ownable vehicles on the wiki's "Ownable Vehicles" list (73 cars, 14 motorcycles). That covers every Autofixer purchase, quest reward, PL car-theft unlock and armed car. (2) **Bosses:** 21 boss and fist-fight encounters, the **17 cyberpsychos** and the **11 Phantom Liberty cyberjunkies** (PL has no Regina cyberpsychos; the cyberjunkies are its equivalent). (3) **Enemies:** 21 gangs and factions and 14 enemy archetypes. Total: **87 + 49 + 35 = 171 rows.**

Markers: **(UNVERIFIED)** means one weak source, a snippet only, or my inference. **(CONFLICT: A vs B)** means sources disagree; the preferred reading comes first. **(PRE-2.0)** means the data comes from a 1.x source and may not apply in 2.5. Wiki citations use the short form `[FW: Page]` = `https://cyberpunk.fandom.com/wiki/Page`. Guide job ids are the file names in `src/content/games/cp2077/en/chapters/`. `—` in a `jobs` cell means the guide has no page for that job; "(prereq)" means the job only unlocks the source.

`name_es_mx` is **blank everywhere**. I found no source for Latin-American Spanish in-game names. The Spanish wiki is es-ES, thin, and mostly uses Cyberpunk 2020 tabletop names. Where it had a page, I put its title in prose or in the `es_es_wiki` column of table 3 as **es-ES wiki naming, not verified in-game**. Langlinks (`prop=langlinks&lllang=es`) for all 186 English pages found only 19 Spanish interwikis (gangs, Royce, Sasquatch, Placide, Smasher, Netrunner).

`wiki_image` is the API `pageimages` name (underscored) where one exists. Otherwise it is the infobox `image =` value. When both are empty it is the Autofixer icon from the vehicle list, prefixed `icon:`.

## 1. Vehicles

### 1.1 System facts (2.x)

- **Ownership:** owned vehicles can be summoned. A destroyed owned vehicle is replaced for an insurance charge of **€$100**, a figure marked "Cite" on the wiki **(UNVERIFIED)**. Stolen vehicles despawn and cannot be kept. Every owned vehicle has a stash (car trunk or bike compartment) [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Vehicles].
- **Stealing:** taking an occupied car needs Body 4–10, and breaking into a parked car needs Technical Ability 4–10, both depending on class and make [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Vehicles].
- **Vehicle combat (since 2.0):** you can fight from the driver's seat in first or third person with pistols, revolvers and SMGs, use health items, and fight with one-handed melee weapons on motorcycles. Without a suitable gun you automatically get a Unity. Tires can be shot or slashed. CrystalDome cars take less damage. Perks add vehicle quickhacks, ramming damage, mounted-weapon boosts and Sandevistan/Kerenzikov while driving [FW: Patch 2.0] [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Vehicles].
- **Mounted weapons:** some cars have **machine guns**, and some have machine guns **plus missiles**. Ammo is infinite but the magazines need reloading. Kills with vehicle weapons give reduced XP and **no skill XP** [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Vehicles]. Front-mounted guns aim where the car points; the Hoon page says "requiring the driver to turn the car towards its target" [FW: Quadra Type-66 "Hoon"]. The **Wingate** is the exception: its guns sit on the rear, over the back wheels [FW: Quadra Type-66 "Wingate"].
- **Armed ownable vehicles (15):** **MG only:** Archer Quartz "Specter", Chevillon Legatus 450 Aquila (2.3), Herrera Outlaw "Weiler", Mizutani Shion "Coyote", Quadra Type-66 "Hoon", Quadra Type-66 "Javelina", Quadra Type-66 "Wingate", Thorton Colby "Little Mule" and Thorton Galena "Gecko". **MG plus missiles:** Archer Quartz "Sidewinder", Mahir Supron "Trailbruiser", Militech Hellhound, Mizutani Shion "Samum", Thorton Colby "Vulture" and Thorton Galena "Locust". The source is each page's "Upgrades" list or its overview text. **Base-game players get 7 armed cars:** Coyote, Javelina, Little Mule and Gecko (all Autofixer, all Autojock), the Specter (Autofixer, 2.0), the Legatus 450 Aquila (Autofixer, 2.3) and the Hoon (a quest). That is consistent with fextralife's note about the updated Aldecaldos cars. No motorcycle has mounted guns.
- **CrystalCoat / TWINTONE (2.2):** you can repaint Rayfield, Herrera, Mizutani, Quadra and Villefort cars. **Repainting lowers your NCPD wanted level** ("Pro tip" in the patch notes) [FW: Patch 2.2].
- **Races (2.1):** after The Beast in Me you can replay races, marked with flag icons. They pay eddies plus an **Autofixer discount** [FW: Patch 2.1].
- **Car chases (2.1):** gigs that involve stealing a vehicle can turn into chases, and gangs you anger in gigs may hunt you later [FW: Patch 2.1].

### 1.2 Autofixer and the Autojock achievement

- **Autofixer** is Muamar "El Capitán" Reyes's netpage. Base-game cars unlock as your **Street Cred** rises; PL cars unlock **one per completion of Just Another Story** (14 completions unlock them all) [FW: AUTOFIXER] [FW: Just Another Story].
- **Autojock** (Silver, 30G): **buy the 23 base-game cars and 3 bikes** on the Autofixer list. PL, patch-added, Nomad-only and quest-reward vehicles don't count. The full set cost **€$1,379,000 as of 2.31** [FW: Autojock]. The `autojock` column marks the 26 vehicles. Switch 2 has no achievement system (see 00-overview), so this is only a checklist goal there.
- **Discount tickets:** each Just Another Story gives an **AUTOFIXER'S DISCOUNT EXTRAVAGANZZZA** ticket. Tickets stack: 13 % for 1, 24 % for 2, 32 % for 3, 50 % for 6, 70 % for 11, and **100 % at 25 tickets** (a free car) [FW: AUTOFIXER]. Race wins also give discounts [FW: Patch 2.1].
- **Price conflicts:** the vehicle list says the Chevillon Thrax 388 Jefferson costs €$38,000, but AUTOFIXER, Autojock and its infobox all say **€$34,000** (CONFLICT; 34,000 preferred). For Street Cred, the main list and some infoboxes disagree: Turbo-R 740 (42 vs 34), Type-66 Avenger (42 vs 48), Colby C125 (none vs 25), Supron FS3 (none vs 12). I used the main list and flagged each one.

### 1.3 Phantom Liberty car theft ("Autojock/Overdrive contracts")

- The brief's "Autojock/Overdrive contracts" don't exist under those names. **Autojock** is the base-game achievement. A wiki search for "Overdrive" returned only clothing and the "Turbo Overdrive" page, with no vehicle job. The PL car-theft activity is: tutorial **Push It to the Limit** (Muamar Reyes, Dogtown; after Spider and the Fly) → repeatable **Just Another Story** → after enough runs, the side job **Baby Let Me Take You**, which rewards the **Herrera Outlaw "Weiler"** [FW: Push It to the Limit] [FW: Just Another Story] [FW: Baby Let Me Take You].
- **Just Another Story:** look for a car marked with a **blue steering-wheel icon** (it's on the minimap). It may be parked and guarded, or moving, in which case you have to stop it and pull out the driver. There are three variants: (1) delivery with pursuers you must lose by leaving the yellow area or kill; (2) no pursuers, optional "good condition" bonus; (3) drive a hacking **Mizutani Shion "Blackbird"** next to a moving target until it stops, then steal the target. An optional timer runs from 90 s to 5.5 min. There are **13 destinations**. Rewards: discount ticket, 1–2 weapons, 1–2 attachments, money, a skill shard, components, and **the next PL car unlocks on Autofixer**. Rewards are rolled when the marked car appears, so reloading a save made **before getting in** rerolls them [FW: Just Another Story].
- **PL Autofixer unlock order** (number of completions): 1 Thorton Colby BARGHEST · 2 Makigai Tanishi "Kuma" · 3 Villefort Deleon "Vindicator" · 4 Mizutani Hozuki "Hoseki" · 5 Villefort Alvarado V4FH 570 Herman · 6 Thorton Merrimac "Warlock" · 7 Thorton Colby "Vulture" · 8 Thorton Galena "Locust" · 9 Mizutani Shion "Samum" · 10 Archer Quartz "Sidewinder" · 11 Mahir Supron "Trailbruiser" · 12 Quadra Type-66 "Wingate" · 13 Quadra Sport R-7 "Chiaroscuro" · 14 Herrera Riptide "Terrier" [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Vehicles]. Other PL Autofixer cars without a theft count: **Militech Hellhound (€$150,000)**, Quadra Sport R-7 "Vigilante" (€$86,000; pre-order bonus version exists), Mizutani Hozuki MH2 (42 SC) and Villefort Deleon V410-S Coupé (42 SC).

## 2. Bosses, cyberpsychos and cyberjunkies

### 2.1 Cyberpsycho system facts

- **Psycho Killer** (Regina Jones, Kabuki): it unlocks after Regina's call (after **The Ripperdoc**) **and** Jackie's talk in **The Ride**. It tracks all **17 Cyberpsycho Sightings**. The five Watson sightings are Act 1; the other twelve open in Act 2 [FW: Psycho Killer]. Extra prerequisites: **Demons of War** needs The Pickup; **Bloody Ritual** needs Playing for Time **and runs only from 8 PM to 5 AM**; **The Wasteland** needs Life During Wartime [FW: Psycho Killer]. GamerGuides lists The Rescue for most sightings, **Playing for Time for Lex Talionis** and **The Heist for Seaside Cafe**, which is consistent with the Act 2 split.
- **Non-lethal is optional in 2.x.** The wiki says "The reward is given even if some of the cyberpsychos are killed instead of incapacitated." If none die, Regina is grateful and comments on it [FW: Psycho Killer], and GamerGuides agrees. **(CONFLICT with the brief's "must be kept alive for Regina's reward? yes": the sources say no.)** Keeping them alive only changes Regina's texts and the final meeting. The `nonlethal` column therefore says whether a non-lethal takedown is **possible and recommended**, plus any catch.
- **Final reward** (infobox): **Chrome Compressor**, **Pax**, a **Cyberware Capacity Shard** and a **Trauma Team armor-weave tactical vest** [FW: Psycho Killer]. (CONFLICT: the Chrome Compressor page lists only Airdrops and Dogtown ripperdocs as sources, and a 2.3 bug note says it may not be given; already flagged in systems-early-game-builds.md.)
- **Achievement:** **I Am The Law**, "Complete all Cyberpsycho Sightings" (Bronze) [FW: Cyberpunk 2077/Achievements]. Kills count.
- **How to take them down non-lethally in 2.x:** **blunt weapons** ("heavy, slow and non-lethal": hammers, clubs, **Gorilla Arms, bare fists**) [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Blunt Weapons]. The **Pax** weapon mod (added 2.0) makes any weapon non-lethal; hitting an unconscious target with a Pax weapon **kills** it [FW: Pax]. The ultimate quickhack **System Collapse** "knocks out the enemy" silently [FW: System Collapse]. **Stop attacking once the body drops.** Pre-2.0 advice to use "System Reset" or the Tyger Claws Dermal Imprint (epicdope) is **(PRE-2.0)**: System Reset is now System Collapse, and the tattoo trick isn't verified for 2.5.
- **Pay rate (from the infoboxes):** every sighting gives 300 XP. Watson ones pay €$2,300–2,900 and 293–369 SC; the others pay €$5,400–7,950 and 650 SC. The Wasteland pays 1,949 SC.
- **After each fight,** search the area for a shard or computer and "send the information to Regina" to complete the gig [FW: sighting pages].

### 2.2 Cyberjunkies (PL)

The **11 cyberjunkies** are Dogtown's version: cyberware users hooked on drugs into something like cyberpsychosis. Most appear **as soon as V leaves the Kress Street Hideout during Lucretia My Reflection**. Spawn rules: **Jacob Bernard and Sean McMillan** spawn after 4 others are down; **Chester Hamilton** spawns after David Dover is down and 6 in-game hours pass; **Greg Wilson** after Gig: Roads to Redemption; **Andrew Newman** after Balls to the Wall [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Cyberjunkies]. There is no fixer, no non-lethal reward and no achievement; they drop loot and a shard each. Several drop a **Cyberware Capacity Shard** (Wesley Hunt, Garry Bates, Jacqueline Peele, Andrew Newman) [FW: each page]. Defeating Garry Bates makes new **Airdrops** spawn at Brave Atlas [FW: Garry Bates].

### 2.3 Not bosses / not in the table

- **Woodman** (Oswald Forrest, Automatic Love) is not a boss: he has no boss health bar. If he turns hostile he has Break Hold, Combat Stim and Charge Jump [FW: Oswald Forrest] [guide `automatic-love`].
- **Punchin' Bag** is the training robot that opens Beat on the Brat (Break Hold) [FW: Punchin' Bag].
- The **Animals Boss** is a Tier 2 named enemy in I Fought the Law (Red Queen's Race) [FW: Animals Boss], not a boss fight.
- **Dum Dum** is not fought in The Pickup [FW: Dum Dum].
- **NCPD Scanner Hustles** "Suspected Organized Crime Activity" (base, 17 per 00-overview) and PL **"Increased Criminal Activity"** each have a **powerful leader enemy** with subordinates [FW: Cyberpsycho Sighting hub]. These are mini-bosses, not listed individually here.
- The **Smasher-route Arasaka commanding officer** (`ending-the-sun`) is already a pill in that job and has no wiki page of its own.

## 3. Enemies: gangs, factions and archetypes

### 3.1 2.x rules that change how weaknesses work

- **"All resistances for regular NPCs have been removed"** in Patch 2.0. Also: **all NPCs now scale to your level** (zone difficulty is gone), **enemies have different tiers depending on their faction**, and **some archetypes have Low, Medium or High armor** [FW: Patch 2.0]. So the per-faction "weak to Thermal / resistant to Chemical" tables in 1.x guides (Piggyback via eip.gg) are **(PRE-2.0)** and no longer apply to ordinary enemies. I keep them below only as context, clearly marked.
- In 2.x, the useful "weakness" of a faction is its **tier spread** (T1 grunts to T3 elites), its **armor** (Light, Medium or Heavy **Subdermal Armor** on the heavies: favour armour penetration), and its **abilities**. Abilities come from each enemy page's `{{Ab2077}}` list: Sandevistan, Kerenzikov, Break Hold (can't be grabbed), Masking Ink, Second Heart, Optical Camo, and **Thermal/Electrical/Chemical Mods**, which mean their attacks apply that damage type.
- Damage types in 2.x: Physical (armor-mitigated), Chemical (Poison), Electrical (Shock, can cause EMP), Thermal (Burn), Explosive (knockdown) [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Damage Types].
- **Netrunner AI was improved in 2.0**, enemies with Sandevistan **counter-activate it** when you use yours, grenade use improved, and flanks are noticed faster [FW: Patch 2.0].
- **Police (2.0):** the NCPD chases you on foot and in vehicles. Response scales with wanted level up to roadblocks and **MaxTac AVs at 5 stars**. You can now quickhack NCPD officers [FW: Patch 2.0]. A **CrystalCoat repaint lowers your wanted level** [FW: Patch 2.2]. MaxTac squads have four roles with strong armor and Sandevistan Mk.3 [FW: MaxTac]. PL achievement "The APB is Not Enough": become Dogtown's most wanted (00-overview).
- **Machines** (robots, mechs, drones, turrets) **can only be destroyed**, never knocked out [FW: Cyberpunk 2077 Enemies]. **Short Circuit** deals extra damage to mechs, robots, drones and turrets [FW: Short Circuit]. **Cyberware Malfunction** disables enemy cyberware for 10 s, and at T3 two stacks disable it permanently: this is the answer to Sandevistan and Kerenzikov users [FW: Cyberware Malfunction]. **Self-ICE** negates one enemy quickhack, with a 45–20 s cooldown, from any ripperdoc at level 20+ [FW: Self-ICE].
- **(PRE-2.0) faction affinities** (Piggyback via eip.gg), for reference only. Thermal: Scavengers very weak; Animals, NCPD, Tyger Claws, Voodoo Boys and Wraiths weak; Militech and Valentinos strong. Chemical: Wraiths very strong; Animals, Maelstrom, Scavengers, TC and VDB strong; 6th St, Aldecaldos, NCPD and Mox weak. Electrical: Kang Tao very strong; TC and VDB strong; Animals, Maelstrom, NCPD, Scavengers and Valentinos weak. Hacking: Kang Tao and VDB very strong; 6th St and Scavengers very weak; Aldecaldos, Animals, Valentinos and Wraiths weak.
