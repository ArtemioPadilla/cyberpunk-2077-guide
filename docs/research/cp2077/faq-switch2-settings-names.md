# Topic A: Nintendo Switch 2

### Q: ¿Ya tengo la actualización 2.5 en mi Switch 2? ¿Cuándo salió?
- **Short answer:** Yes. 2.5 reached Switch 2 on **27 Oct 2026**, five days after PC and the other consoles (22 Oct). If you don't have it, update the game from the HOME menu.
- **Details:**
  - (CONFLICT, resolved) CDPR-52120 (21 Oct) said Switch 2 would get it "in the days to follow". CDPR-52125 (22 Oct) says "available now on PC, Mac, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2". That was wrong, or the text was edited later. Every later source puts the Switch 2 release on 27 Oct: the official CDPR YouTube post ("v.2.5 Release Sequence: COMPLETE … now available on Nintendo Switch 2", posted "2 days ago" on 30 Oct), RPG Site ("The Switch 2 patch is live as of October 27"), perfectly-nintendo ("October 27th 2026") and the wiki's body text. The wiki infobox says Oct 26, probably a time-zone or editing difference. LatAm YouTubers posted Switch 2 2.5 videos on 27 and 28 Oct (TechDawDo `Iy4Ra-lJIrc`, KarussoTV `FWa2b3wATOA`).
  - The es-MX page [CDPR-ER2-MX] still says "próximamente en Nintendo Switch 2" and "estará disponible para Nintendo Switch 2 … más adelante". The page is out of date.
  - How to check: the game's main menu shows the version. CDPR-SUP 2425 says it is "visible from the game's main menu".
  - The 2.5 download size on Switch 2 was not found.
- **Sources:** CDPR-52120, CDPR-52125, CDPR-YT, RPGSITE, PN, W:2.5, CDPR-ER2-MX, CDPR-SUP 2734.
- **Confidence:** high that it is out. Medium on the exact day (27 Oct; 26 Oct per the wiki infobox).
- **es-MX names:** "actualización Edgerunners 2 (2.5)" [CDPR-ER2-MX].

### Q: ¿Qué trae la 2.5 en Switch 2? ¿Le falta algo respecto a PS5/PC?
- **Short answer:** The same content: the Clampdown and Welcome to Paradise quests, dual wield, glamdocs, the new tarot cards, the radio station, mods and the console FOV slider. The PC-only items are graphics tech (DLSS 4.5, FSR, Samsung 3D, MetalFX).
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-52120 lists cross-platform mods (mod.io) as available on "PC, Mac, PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S, and Nintendo Switch 2". The support page "Cross-Platform Mod Support - how to", updated 2 days ago, also names Switch 2. (CONFLICT, minor) RPG Site, quoting the platform list on 27 Oct, omits Switch 2. The CDPR text may have been edited. Trust the current CDPR pages.
  - Mods disable trophies and achievements on consoles. Modded saves show a cog icon. Mods need a CD PROJEKT RED account linked to mod.io [CDPR-SUP 3034].
  - The only 2.5 fix that names Switch 2: "Fixed an issue on Nintendo Switch 2 where saving the game could result in a 'Save failed - insufficient space' error" [CDPR-52120].
  - Also in 2.5 for all platforms: saves in "Load Game" are grouped by playthrough, the apartment and vehicle stashes get filters, and "Added an FOV (Field Of View) slider on consoles" [CDPR-52120].
  - New quests: Clampdown unlocks after The Heist plus two gigs for Wakako. Welcome to Paradise unlocks after The Heist and **Street Cred 15**. So **in Act 1 you can't do either yet**.
- **Sources:** CDPR-52120, CDPR-SUP 3034, RPGSITE.
- **Confidence:** high for the content list. Medium for mods on Switch 2 (the later sources agree).
- **es-MX names:** Dual wield = **"Empuñadura doble"** [CDPR-ER2-MX]. The eShop MX copy says "blandir un arma con cada mano"; KarussoTV says "doble empuñadura" (fan wording). Glamdocs = "glamdocs" [CDPR-ER2-MX, ESHOP-MX]. Mods = "mods multiplataforma" [CDPR-ER2-MX]. The quest names Clampdown and Welcome to Paradise in es-MX were not verified. A YouTube title says "Bienvenidos al Paraíso" (superantonio, region unclear). (UNVERIFIED)

### Q: ¿Modo calidad o modo rendimiento en Switch 2?
- **Short answer:** **Handheld: Performance** (40 fps target at 720p, with VRR) feels smoother. **On a TV: Quality** (30 fps at 1080p), unless your TV is 120 Hz.
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-SUP 2816: two frame-rate modes in the game Settings, **Quality** and **Performance**. Performance targets "up to 40 FPS at 1080p" in TV mode and "up to 40 FPS at 720p" in handheld. Quality runs at "30 FPS at 1080p in both TV and handheld modes". VRR in handheld mode.
  - Nintendo Life: Performance "only works in docked mode if you have a 120hz TV or monitor" [NL].
  - Nintendo Wire: the Switch 2 has no VRR when docked, so 40 fps on a TV "winds up being uglier in motion". Stay at 30 on a TV. In handheld, Performance "typically runs just above 30 fps" and VRR hides the tearing [NW].
  - Internal resolutions (Digital Foundry, via NE): docked Quality 720p–1080p, docked Performance 540p–1080p, handheld Quality 450p–810p, handheld Performance 360p–720p. All use DLSS reconstruction. "Phantom Liberty expansion causes more abundant 30 FPS drops and hitches". Performance drops to 30 or lower in shootouts and driving.
  - 2.5 on Switch 2: KarussoTV says there are fps drops in some areas. The patch notes list no Switch 2 performance change. (UNVERIFIED)
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2816, NL, NW, NE, YouTube `FWa2b3wATOA`.
- **Confidence:** high (official mode targets). Medium for the DF numbers, which are secondhand.
- **es-MX names:** The es-ES support page uses "Calidad" / "Rendimiento". The in-game es-MX strings were not seen. (UNVERIFIED, probably the same)

### Q: ¿Cómo activo el giroscopio solo cuando apunto?
- **Short answer:** Go to Settings → Controls → **Gyroscope Mode** and pick **"When holding aim button"**. Gyro then only works while you aim with a ranged weapon.
- **Details:**
  - The 5 options [CDPR-SUP 2822]: **Disabled**; **Always enabled** (menus and gameplay); **Always enabled except in menus**; **With ranged weapons** (when a ranged weapon is equipped); **When holding aim button** (while aiming a ranged weapon).
  - Gyro uses the right Joy-Con 2 [CDPR-SUP 2822].
  - There are also settings for sensitivity and for "how much movement is dampened" to avoid twitchy aim [NL]. The exact menu names were not found.
  - Reviewers: gyro is meant to fine-tune on top of the sticks [GN]. With weak aim, combine it with Aim Assist (see below).
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2822 (EN, es-ES), NL, GN.
- **Confidence:** high (official option list).
- **es-MX names:** es-ES support: "Ajustes → Controles → **Modo Giroscopio**", options "Desactivado / Siempre activado / Siempre activado excepto en menús / Con armas a distancia / **Al mantener el botón de apuntar**". This is es-ES support text, not the es-MX game text. (UNVERIFIED for es-MX)

### Q: ¿Cómo uso el modo mouse con el Joy-Con 2?
- **Short answer:** Turn it on in Settings → Controls (mouse sensor option). Then rest the right Joy-Con 2 on its side on a flat surface and move it like a mouse to aim and to pick menu or dialogue options.
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-SUP 2822 (EN): "Mouse sensor allows you to use the Joy-Con 2 on a surface as a mouse device. You can enable this feature in game Settings, in Controls → **Mouse Patterns**". "Mouse Patterns" looks like a copy error from "Motion Patterns". The es-ES page says "Controles → **Sensor del ratón**".
  - Mouse mode works "very similarly to the PC version" and aims weapons and selects dialogue options [GN, quoting CDPR's Charles Tremblay].
  - Known problems: "hitting the 'Y' button just didn't always initiate a reload" in this mode [NL]. Gizmodo: you have to move your hand a lot to reach the face buttons (via search summary, page not opened).
  - Supported peripherals listed by CDPR: only the **Nintendo Switch 2 Pro Controller** [CDPR-SUP 2722]. Nintendo Wire says the game also supports a full keyboard and mouse, rebindable [NW]. CDPR doesn't list them. (CONFLICT, minor)
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2822, GN, NL, NW, CDPR-SUP 2722.
- **Confidence:** high that the option exists. Medium on its exact menu name.
- **es-MX names:** The Nintendo eShop MX text says "usa tu Joy-Con™ 2 (R) como un **mouse**" [ESHOP-MX]. The es-MX game probably says "mouse", while es-ES says "ratón". (UNVERIFIED)

### Q: ¿Para qué sirven los gestos de movimiento? ¿Por qué la opción aparece gris?
- **Short answer:** **Motion Patterns** let you dodge, heal, melee and reload by moving the Joy-Con 2. The option stays **grayed out until you finish its tutorial in The Rescue** (Jackie gives you a tutorial shard).
- **Details:**
  - The gestures [CDPR-SUP 2822]: dodge/dash = rotate the left Joy-Con 2 toward the direction. Consumable = tilt the left one back toward you. Fast melee = turn the right one inward. Strong melee = tilt the right one back, then slap it down. Charge attack / throw knife = raise the right one, wait for the rumble, return. **Reload = rotate the right Joy-Con 2 clockwise.**
  - The option is in Settings → Controls → Motion Patterns. You can replay the tutorial from Controls [CDPR-SUP 2825].
  - Only with two Joy-Con 2 and no grip [NL]. A Checkpoint Gaming report (via search summary) says the reload twist often pointed the camera at the ceiling because gyro aim is forced in that mode. (UNVERIFIED)
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2822, CDPR-SUP 2825, NL.
- **Confidence:** high.
- **es-MX names:** es-ES support: "Patrones de movimiento", "pincho del tutorial", "El rescate". The quest **El rescate** is verified es-MX (glossary).

### Q: ¿Puedo jugar el Protocolo de invasión con la pantalla táctil? ¿Qué más se toca?
- **Short answer:** Touch works in menus, the map, the perk tree, the inventory, in-game screens (computers, elevator panels), Photo Mode and the Roach Race arcade. The launch announcement says you can also play the hacking minigame by touch.
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-SUP 2822's list: map (including fast travel), character preview (creation and inventory), **perk tree**, Roach Race, in-game displays (computers, elevator panels…), menus (switching tabs), Photo Mode.
  - CDPR-51566: "touch screen capabilities to swipe through menus, select character skills, or **play the hacking mini-game like a real netrunner**".
  - (CONFLICT, minor) The support list does not name Breach Protocol. GoNintendo (pre-launch) only mentions menus. Keep the site's claim, but say "según CDPR".
  - Breach Protocol without a timer: since 2.1 the Accessibility tab can remove the timer (systems note, CDPR 49591).
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2822, CDPR-51566, GN.
- **Confidence:** high for the list, medium for Breach Protocol by touch.
- **es-MX names:** Breach Protocol = **Protocolo de invasión** (STEAM-MX, high). Perk tree = "árbol de ventajas" (STEAM-MX).

### Q: ¿Qué ajustes me ayudan si apunto mal?
- **Short answer:** In **Accessibility**: Aim Assist (ranged) on **Substantial**, **Snap to Target** on. In Controls: gyro only while aiming, lower sensitivity, raise "Zoom Sensitivity Reduction". You can also lower the difficulty at any time.
- **Details:**
  - Aim Assist has three settings: ranged (off / light / standard / substantial), melee (off / light / standard) and vehicle combat (on/off). Snap to Target "allows target snapping when aiming with firearms" [CDPR-SUP 2864 = the 2.1 accessibility list, reposted on the Switch 2 page].
  - Camera: horizontal/vertical sensitivity, **Zoom Sensitivity Reduction**, inner/outer dead zone, response curve, turning bonus, and turning bonus while aiming (ADS) [G8].
  - Switch 2-specific aim assist **defaults: not found** in any source.
  - For a hacker, the real fix is not to need to aim: quickhacks only need a scan and a choice, and smart weapons with Smart Link do the tracking (see systems-early-game-builds.md §4.3).
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2864 (EN, es-ES), G8.
- **Confidence:** high (options). Defaults not found.
- **es-MX names:** Not verified in es-MX. **es-ES** (CDPR support in Spanish, which looks copied from the game strings): Aim Assist = "**Ayuda al apuntar**" (levels "No / Mínima / Estándar / Significativa"). Snap to Target = "**Fijación rápida del objetivo**". Additive Camera Motions = "Movimientos añadidos de cámara". Weapon Sway = "Inestabilidad del arma". The tab is "Accesibilidad". A Spain YouTuber also says "ayuda al apuntar" (ZONAGAMI `SaZ4j-nxBSM`, España). One Mexican stream title says "Sin Ayuda de Apuntado" (Alfred Blavatsky `OgOJrz153ms`, México): (UNVERIFIED). The site's guess "Asistencia de apuntado" has **no support**.

### Q: ¿Cómo cambio el idioma de las voces y de los textos? ¿Puedo tener textos en español latino y voces en inglés?
- **Short answer:** Yes, and on Switch 2 that is the default: **the game has no Latin-American Spanish voices on Switch 2.** es-MX is text only. Voice options are English (included) or a free pack you download, e.g. Spain Spanish ("castellano").
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-SUP 2810 (global version): text and voice on the card or initial download: **English**. Text included, voice pack download required: Brazilian Portuguese, Chinese, French, German, Italian, Korean, Polish, **Spanish (Spain)**. **Text only: … Latin American Spanish …** (also Arabic, Czech, Hungarian, Russian, Simplified/Traditional Chinese, Thai, Turkish, Ukrainian). "Additional language packs may require up to 2.9 GB each", for both card and digital.
  - The eShop MX lists the free packs (0 MXN), for example "**Pack de voces en castellano de Cyberpunk 2077: Edición definitiva**", plus French, German, Italian, Korean, Polish, Brazilian, Simplified Chinese and Russian (base game). There is no Latin-American Spanish pack [ESHOP-MX].
  - In-game menu: Settings → **Language** has separate **voiceover**, **subtitles** and **interface** (text) settings. They "only appear in the Main Menu settings" [G8, 2023, not Switch 2-specific]. (UNVERIFIED for Switch 2, but the Switch 2 support page shows that voice and text are separate.)

  - Known UI issue: some UI shows English strings "mistakenly pulled from the credits" in Arabic, Czech, Hungarian, Korean, Thai, Traditional Chinese and Turkish. **Spanish is not affected** [CDPR-SUP 2885].
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2810, ESHOP-MX, CDPR-SUP 2807, G8, CDPR-SUP 2885.

### Q: ¿Cómo hago un guardado manual? ¿Cuántas ranuras hay? ¿Se guarda en la nube?
- **Short answer:** Menu → Save. There are **20 manual**, **20 auto** and **10 quick** slots. Cloud saves work with **Nintendo Switch Online**, and CDPR's cross-progression adds its own cloud copy.
- **Details:**
  - Slots: "20 Auto Saves, 10 Quick Saves, 20 Manual Saves". This does not count Point of No Return or end-game saves [CDPR-SUP 1751].
  - CDPR: "We recommend saving often and having more than one save file as backup" [CDPR-SUP 1745].
  - eShop: NSO feature "**Guardado de datos en la nube**" (Save Data Cloud) [ESHOP-MX, ESHOP-US].
  - Cross progression: link a CD PROJEKT RED account (Load Game → button in the bottom-left). After that, new saves upload automatically (cloud icon). Toggle in Settings → Gameplay → "Enable cross-platform Saves". It keeps the latest save of each type [CDPR-SUP 2425]. Saves made with mods only carry over if both platforms have the same mods [CDPR-SUP 3034].
  - 2.5: saves are grouped per playthrough in Load Game, and the "Save failed - insufficient space" error on Switch 2 was fixed [CDPR-52120]. If you still get it, free up internal or microSD space. (inference)
  - You can't save in combat (site card; not re-checked).
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 1751, 1745, 2425, 3034; CDPR-52120; ESHOP-MX.
- **Confidence:** high.

### Q: El juego se cierra / se congela en Switch 2. ¿Qué hago?
- **Short answer:** First check for corrupt data, then clear the cache, restart and load an earlier save. If the console freezes, force it off (hold Power for 12 s).
- **Details:**
  - CDPR-SUP 2837, step by step: System Settings → **Data Management** → Manage Software → Cyberpunk 2077 → **Check for Corrupt Data**. Then System Settings → System → **Formatting Options → Clear Cache** (this does not delete saves), restart. Then try an earlier save. If frozen: undock (Joy-Con attached), hold Power ≥12 s, take out the game card and/or microSD, power on, update the system, then retry.
  - Reported bugs on Switch 2 that are still current: Y sometimes fails to reload in mouse mode [NL, 2025]. Stutter when loading areas while driving, hitches and odd geometry in Dogtown [NL, 2025].
  - On 2.5: a GodisaGeek headline, "Cyberpunk 2077 Update 2.5 Hits Switch 2 With Crash Reports", seen only in search results (the site returned 403). I could not read the details. (UNVERIFIED)
- **Sources:** CDPR-SUP 2837, NL; godisageek.com/2026/10/cyberpunk-2077-update-2-5-crashes/ (not readable).
- **Confidence:** high for the official steps. Low for 2.5-specific crashes.
