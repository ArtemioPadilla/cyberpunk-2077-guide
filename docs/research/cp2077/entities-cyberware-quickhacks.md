# Research notes: cyberware, cyberdecks, quickhacks and ripperdocs (2.x system)

Target: Cyberpunk 2077: Ultimate Edition (base game + Phantom Liberty), Nintendo Switch 2, Update 2.5. Notes compiled 2026-10-30.
Scope: only the 2.0+ cyberware system (capacity, tiers T1-T5++, 10 body slots). 1.x stats, Street Cred gating and cut implants are flagged and left out of the table.

How to read these notes:

- Source tags: **[W:Page]** = Cyberpunk Fandom wiki page, read through the API (wikitext). **[G8:id]** = game8.co article id. **(UNVERIFIED)** = one source, a `{{Verify}}` tag, or my inference. **(CONFLICT)** = two sources (or two parts of one wiki page) disagree; both sides are given. **(PRE-2.0, OBSOLETE)** = describes the 1.x game. **(inference)** = my own deduction.
- Numbers like `10-20%` read "value at the item's lowest tier to value at T5". The wiki infoboxes give the low value at the item's starting tier and the high one at T5 (sometimes at T5++).
- `name_es_mx` is **blank on every row**. The Spanish Fandom wiki (`cyberpunk.fandom.com/es`, es-ES anyway) has only 518 articles and **no** 2077 cyberware, quickhack or ripperdoc item pages (checked `list=allpages`, the categories `Ciberequipo de Cyberpunk 2077`, which holds only "Hebras PEM", and searches). The English wiki pages carry no `es:` interlanguage links. The only es-ES term found is the article title **"Matasanos"** (= ripperdoc). The Latin-American in-game names have to come from the game itself.
- `wiki_image` = the infobox `image =` value (spaces, not underscores). It matches `prop=pageimages` except that pageimages returns underscores. Many cyberware pages reuse one icon (e.g. all Mantis Blades variants → `MantisBlades.png`; all generic quickhacks without art → `GenericProgram.png`).

## 2.x system facts the guide needs

- **Slots (10 types):** Frontal Cortex 3, Operating System 1, Arms 1, Face 1 (a **2nd Face slot unlocks in Birds with Broken Wings**), Skeleton 2 (a 3rd with **License To Chrome**), Hands 1 (a 2nd with **Ambidextrous**), Nervous System 3, Circulatory System 3, Integumentary System 3, Legs 1. One implant in each of the 10 slot types unlocks the achievement **Full Body Conversion**. [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Cyberware]
- **Operating System** = exactly one of: a **cyberdeck** (quickhacks), a **Sandevistan** (slow time), a **Berserk** (melee tank, Superhero Landing), or the **Chrome Compressor** (capacity). [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Cyberware]
- **Cyberware Capacity:** 24 at level 1, +3 per level, 201 at level 60. Extra from Cyberware Capacity Shards (max +74), Engineer skill (the Stats page says +15 total; the Cyberware page breaks it into +5 at level 10 and +10 at level 30), **Edgerunner** perk (+50), **Renaissance Punk** (up to +20), Chrome Compressor (+70 at T5). Hard cap 450. Reachable maximum 430 with the Compressor, 360 without. [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Cyberware] [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Stats]
- **`capacity_cost` caveat:** the wiki infobox gives one capacity number per item. That is the value stored for the item, not a per-tier list, so I cannot say whether the cost rises with tier (UNVERIFIED). Patch 2.1 lowered the cost of 13 implants; the wiki values should already include that.
- **Tiers and when they show up in shops:** T1 from level 1, T2 from 10, T3 from 20, T4 from 30, T5 from 40 (CDPR support page cited by the wiki). A ripperdoc's stock refreshes **every 10 levels**, not daily. You upgrade an implant's tier at a ripperdoc with **Item Components**; T5+ and T5++ are further upgrades. Each implant gets one random stat bonus at T1, a second at T2, and a third with the **Driver Update** perk; **Chipware Connoisseur** lets you reroll them. [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Cyberware]
- **Street Cred:** none of the 2.x cyberware pages has a `street_cred` value, and the Cyberware page gates stock by **level** only. The `where` column therefore gives a level, not a Street Cred number. The 1.x Street Cred gating is (PRE-2.0, OBSOLETE).
- **Prices:** the wiki infoboxes almost never fill the price fields, so prices are given only where a page lists one (Mantis Blades / Electrifying Mantis Blades T3 €$28,783; Toxic PLS T3 €$29,483; Adrenaline Converter T3 €$5,273; Apogee €$120,000). Everything else is blank on purpose.
- **Iconic cyberware (PL):** 15 iconic implants are sold only by **Dogtown ripperdocs** and also form the guaranteed-iconic pool of repeating **Airdrops** (each one guarantees one until the pool runs out): Chrome Compressor, RAM Reallocator, COX-2, Axolotl, Kiroshi "Cockatrice", Rara Avis, Immovable Force, Adreno-trigger, Revulsor, Deep-field Visual Interface, Isometric Stabilizer, Electromag Recycler, Peripheral Inverse, Chitin, Leeroy Ligament System. Other iconics: Militech "Apogee" (any ripperdoc, level 40), Militech Berserk (level 30 or ending reward), Militech Canto Mk.6 (crafted), Quantum Tuner (quest), Behavioral Imprint-synced Faceplate (quest), NetWatch Netdriver (flagged Iconic in its infobox), Militech "Falcon" (CONFLICT, see its row). Airdrops start in Lucretia My Reflection. [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Airdrops]
- **Quickhacks:** 4 Combat, 4 Control, 5 Covert, 5 Ultimate (incl. Blackwall Gateway), plus device hacks (Breach Protocol, Take Control; the Quickhacks page also lists Distract Enemies, Initiate Overload, Friendly Mode, Assist Mode, Remote Deactivation, which have no item pages) and 3-4 vehicle hacks (the Carhacker perk; Take Control also works on vehicles). Netrunner vendors sell hacks and crafting specs: Yoko Tsuru (Kabuki), Chang Hoon Nam (Japantown), Nix (Little China), Netrunner (Coastview / Japantown / Vista del Rey), and in PL Sammy Taylor (EBM Petrochem Stadium, who sells all the **Iconic** quickhack variants). Hacks also drop from enemy netrunners, Breach Protocol datamines and Airdrops (iconic specs). Monowire can hold Control hacks with the **Jailbreak** Relic perk. [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Quickhacks] [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Vendors]
- **Update 2.5 (the target build):** adds **5 Glamdocs** (spray-paint icon on the map) that restyle **arm cyberware** (Mantis Blades, Gorilla Arms, Monowire, Projectile Launch System): Issam Zahir (Kabuki), Shō Gun Rose (Charter Hill), Zéna Delicé (West Wind Estate), Spite Vondell (Rancho Coronado), Serena Zhao (Downtown). They are cosmetic only, not ripperdocs, so they get no table rows. Update_2.5 lists no other cyberware or quickhack balance change. [W:Update_2.5] [W:Cyberpunk_2077_Vendors]
- **Update 2.1** is the last big cyberware rebalance: it added Feen-X and Cogito Lattice, changed ~20 implants (noted per row) and nerfed Synapse Burnout. 2.2 added 8 cosmetic face-cyberware options in the character creator and fixed the Iconic Synapse Burnout / System Collapse RAM buff. 2.3 only fixed Capacity Shard drops. [W:Update_2.1] [W:Update_2.2] [W:Update_2.3]
