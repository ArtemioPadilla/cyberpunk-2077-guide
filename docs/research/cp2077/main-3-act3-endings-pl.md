## 0. Version notes (Switch 2, Update 2.5)

- Update 2.5, the "Edgerunners 2 Update", released 2026-10-22 on PS5 [PS25]. The web-search summary said Switch 2 followed "days later" (UNVERIFIED: no allowed source gives a date).
- 2.5 fixes that matter for this file [PS25]:
  - Switch 2: fixed the "Save failed - insufficient space" error. This matters for the advice to keep manual saves before the point of no return.
  - Things Done Changed: fixed the Delamain cab getting stuck after a crash, which blocked progress on the Tower ending.
  - Saves are now grouped by playthrough. This helps with keeping separate saves for each ending.
- Mod support (mod.io) in 2.5 is listed for PC, Mac, PS5 and Xbox only, not Switch 2 [PS25]. On consoles, a modded save cannot earn achievements or trophies [PS25].
- 2.5 adds no Act 3 or PL story changes [PS25].
- Patch 2.1 reworked the Adam Smasher fight. He can now use a Sandevistan, and his tactics adapt to V's build [G8:Patch-Notes-2-1]. Pre-2.1 Smasher guides, including PowerPyx's 2020 boss guide, are only partly valid.

## 1. Act 3: Nocturne Op55N1 (the point of no return)

### Unlock and basics

- Previous quest: Tapeworm [W:Nocturne_Op55N1]. Game8's unlock line says "Complete Transmission and Search and Destroy" [G8:Nocturne]. The quest giver is Hanako Arasaka. Locations: Embers (Heywood, The Glen), then Viktor's Clinic, then Misty's Esoterica [W:Nocturne_Op55N1].
- No rewards [G8:Nocturne].
- Approaching the main entrance of Embers shows an on-screen warning that this is the point of no return. After it you can no longer free-roam Night City [W:Nocturne_Op55N1 note] [PP:nocturne].
- "One More Gig": after the credits of any ending, the game puts you back before the point of no return, in free roam, so you can finish other content and replay other endings [PP:nocturne] [PP:endings].
  - Rewards from the ending are added to your inventory in the last save before Nocturne [W:Endings §Rewards].
  - Previous quests are not reset [search summary of game8 "New Game Plus" page].
  - There is no true NG+ [G8:314736].

### What to finish before entering Embers

Ending gates (all from [W:Endings] [PP:endings]):

- **The Devil achievement:** save Takemura in Search and Destroy. This is highly missable.
  - Inside the ambushed building, turn left at the first chance, crouch under the rubble, go upstairs and kill the enemies around Takemura. He must appear in the cutscene as you leap out of the exit door [PP:endings].
  - If Takemura is dead you can still play the Devil ending, but the achievement does not unlock and Hellman replaces Takemura in the epilogue [W:Endings §Variations] [PP:endings].
- **Oda ally in Totalimmortal:** spare Sandayu Oda in Play It Safe [W:Totalimmortal notes]. He only appears if Takemura is alive [W:Endings §Variations].
- **Panam / The Star:** complete Queen of the Highway [W:Nocturne note] [W:Endings].
  - Chain: Riders on the Storm, then With a Little Help From My Friends, then Queen of the Highway [PP:endings].
  - Riders on the Storm can be failed. After taking the SuperJet from Mitch, drive with Panam straight to the enemy camp. If you drive the wrong way she kicks you out, and skipping time then fails the quest [PP:endings].
- **Rogue / The Sun:** complete Blistering Love [W:Nocturne note].
  - Blistering Love only unlocks after the right dialogue at Johnny's grave in Chippin' In: "A hard road, but looks like we made it." then "Johnny, no need to bare your soul to me.", or the secret-ending lines [W:Blistering_Love].
  - Any other lines lead to Holdin' On instead, and the Rogue path is locked [W:Blistering_Love] [W:Chippin' In].
- **Secret ending:** choose the three Chippin' In lines in the secret ending section below.
- **Tower (needs PL):**
  - Finish PL with Songbird surrendered to the NUSA (King of Swords or King of Pentacles) [W:Nocturne note].
  - Game8 also says to confirm with Reed that the surgeons are ready before Nocturne [G8:428843].
- **Romance goodbye call on the rooftop:**
  - Only partners whose romance is complete can be called (Judy, Panam, River, Kerry). Otherwise "Goodbye's aren't my thing" is the only option [W:Nocturne note].
  - Who appears in each epilogue depends on this call [W:Path_of_Glory] [W:All_Along_the_Watchtower].
- **Jackie's body choice (made earlier, in The Heist):**
  - If his body went to Viktor, Arasaka took his engram. You can then talk to Jackie in Mikoshi on the Devil path (ask Hanako), and he appears in Changes [W:Totalimmortal notes] [W:Changes].
  - On the Devil path, flipping off Saburo cuts that conversation short [W:Totalimmortal notes].
- **"Heroes" (ofrenda) side job:** affects Mama Welles' credits message [W:Endings §Epilogue].
- **Johnny's outfit pieces (Breathtaking achievement):** collect them all "before heading off to Arasaka Tower (or to the labs in Langley)" [G8:Missables].

### What fails or vanishes at Embers

- None of the sources lists a side quest or gig that fails permanently because you went through the point of no return. You return to the pre-Nocturne save afterwards, so free-roam content stays available [PP:nocturne] [PP:endings] [G8:Nocturne]. (UNVERIFIED that nothing is lost: no source has an explicit list.)
- Per PowerPyx's PL guide, some side jobs are timed and can fail if not done promptly [PP:dlc-walkthrough]. Those are PL-specific; see §3 missables.
- The Arasaka path is the only Mikoshi path that skips "Changes" [W:Changes].

### Walkthrough (objectives in order) [W:Nocturne_Op55N1] [G8:Nocturne] [PP:nocturne]

1. Meet Hanako at Embers. Clear security, then take the elevator to the 13th floor [W].
2. Talk to Hanako, then sit with her. Your answers to her do not matter; the Hanako option stays available even if you are hostile [PP:nocturne].
3. Leave the restaurant. In the elevator Johnny proposes the Rogue plan, then the Relic malfunctions [W].
4. You wake at Viktor's clinic. Viktor warns that one more malfunction will likely kill V. Take the last pills and the Unity handgun from the table [W].
5. Talk to Misty and follow her to the elevator behind the shop, up to the 9th floor, then up the stairs to the roof. Sit by Misty [W].
6. **Last manual save before the ending choice:** just before sitting next to Misty [W note] [G8:Nocturne].
7. Talk to Johnny, optionally call a romance, then decide what comes next.

## 2. Ending paths

### 2.1 The Devil (Arasaka): Last Caress, Totalimmortal, Where is My Mind?

**Last Caress** [W:Last_Caress] [G8:Nocturne]

- Hellman drives you to the Arasaka Estate. Deal with the guards: Game8 says you can smash a guard's head with 7 Body, otherwise Hellman handles him (UNVERIFIED for 2.x, where the attribute scale differs).
- Then: talk to Hellman and Hanako, kill the elite guards (more spawn if detected), find Hanako, follow her to the AV and fly to Arasaka Tower.
- Missable: the iconic **Genjiroh** in one of the estate rooms. If you took it, another is granted after the game [W:Endings §Rewards].

**Totalimmortal** [W:Totalimmortal] [G8:Totalimmortal]

1. Follow Hanako to Saburo/Mikoshi and sit for the talk. Optionally ask to talk to Jackie's engram (see conditions above).
2. Give testimony at the board meeting, then fend off the attackers.
3. Take the elevator to the upper atrium. The first elevator is blocked; take the lift on the 3rd floor to floor 140 [G8].
   - Route ratings [G8]: Dark Jungle 5/5 (beside the boardroom table, a lit rock leads in; skips the walkway mech), Lit Jungle 4/5 (red flares; gets you behind the mech), Walkway 3/5 (fewest soldiers but you fight a mech).
   - Game8: "get the VIP card" to pass most of the upper level without a fight (UNVERIFIED detail).
4. Fight **Adam Smasher** before Yorinobu's office. Optional: decide Smasher's fate. Loot the **Access Token** (missable).
5. Confront Yorinobu. Pushing his gun away has no consequence [W notes].
6. Take the elevator down. The Devil achievement unlocks here, after Yorinobu, at the elevator, if Takemura is alive [PP:endings].

- Oda joins Hanako's forces if spared in Play It Safe and Takemura is alive [W] [W:Endings §Variations].
- Without Takemura there is no Arasaka troop support and no Oda, and Hellman appears instead [W:Endings].
- Reward: 387 XP [G8].

**Where is My Mind?** [W:Where_is_My_Mind?] [PP:endings]

- Surgery: last talk with Johnny in cyberspace. Then Arasaka Orbital Station (about 30 days of tests with Sachiko Kusama).
- Optional calls (Viktor, Panam, Judy, River, Kerry, Hanako). Responses depend on completed chains and the rooftop romance call; Hanako never answers [W].
- Optional "Vent your frustration" [W].
- Final scene: Takemura (alive) or Hellman offers Secure Your Soul:
  - **"[Sign Contract] I want to live"**: V becomes an engram until a body is found.
  - **"[Refuse to Sign] I'm going back to Earth"**: V leaves to live out about 6 months [W] [PP:endings].
- Devil tarot card: the Rubik's cube dream [W].

### 2.2 The Star (Aldecaldos): We Gotta Live Together, Forward to Death, Belly of the Beast, Changes, All Along the Watchtower

**We Gotta Live Together** [W]

- Objectives: Follow Mitch, Saul, Dakota, "Get in the tub", Saul again, then optional "Talk to the nomads": Dakota, Saul, the Bob/Carol/Teddy table (wait until they finish talking), and Cassidy.
- Extra nomads count if their jobs were done: Jake (Fortunate Son), Benedict (Gig: MIA), Bruce (Gig: Goodbye, Night City), Anna Hamill (Gig: Woman of La Mancha, Nomad lifepath only).
- Missable: **Amnesty** revolver. Hit 12 or more bottles in Cassidy's shooting game; crouch and aim before he says go.
- Reward: **Aldecaldos rally bolero jacket** [W] [W:Endings].

**Forward to Death** [W]

- Drone scan, then a Basilisk panzer assault on Militech at the Maglev tunnel construction site.
- Sequence: protect the panzer during repair; 11 kills; ram the bridge (x2) and the gate; on foot, 8 kills; escort Mitch and Carol; 20 kills plus 13 reinforcements; strike team of 11; then drive into the tunnel.

**Belly of the Beast** [W]

- SERC climb into Arasaka Tower, maintenance shaft, security-room shard for Alt, netrunner's nest, Mikoshi.
- **Adam Smasher** fight: he crushes Saul's head first [W:Adam_Smasher]. Decide his fate (optional). Loot the Access Token (optional).
- Then the Mikoshi access point and Changes.

**Changes, then the choice** [W:Changes] [PP:endings]

- "Wanna live out whatever life I got left", then enter the **Well**: All Along the Watchtower (**The Star**).
- "Body's Johnny's. I'll go", then cross the **Bridge**: New Dawn Fades (**Temperance**).
- Keep a manual save after Smasher to do both [PP:endings].

**All Along the Watchtower** [W]

- Weeks later at the Coronado Dam: talk to Panam, drive to the solar arrays, meet Cassidy and Carol.
- If a romance was called:
  - Judy is brought by Carol and **leaves with V**.
  - Kerry and River come but **stay** in Night City.
  - If Panam is the romance, she is already with V.
- With high Body there is an extra border-security dialogue line.
- Basilisk border run through the sandstorm; Cassidy and Carol peel off as decoys. Ending cinematic: V tosses Misty's necklace.

### 2.3 The Sun (Rogue): For Whom the Bell Tolls, Knockin' on Heaven's Door, Changes, Path of Glory

**For Whom the Bell Tolls** (played as Johnny) [W]

- Meet Rogue, follow Weyland, take the **Retrothrusters**, gear up (optional), put on the netrunner suit, talk to Alt, then take the AV from the roof.
- Missable: **Prejudice** [W] [W:Endings].

**Knockin' on Heaven's Door** [W] [G8:Knockin]

1. Optionally follow Rogue, take down a guard and talk to Rogue and Weyland.
2. Find the commanding officer, on the far left of the floor; use the jungle for cover. Defeat him and search him for the elevator access token [G8].
3. Elevator to the 68th-floor atrium. Drones; drop over the edges with the Retrothrusters to the security floor. Watch the camera above the security-room door [G8].
4. Open the server room and connect Alt. Elevator to the netrunner's nest, then Mikoshi, then open the gate.
5. **Smasher** kills Rogue in a scripted scene: she plants a grenade in his chest. Then he fights Johnny and Weyland. He starts at about 80% HP on this route [W:Adam_Smasher] [G8:Knockin].
6. Optional: pick up Rogue's gun, **Pride** (missable), near her body or where it was [W] [G8:Knockin].
7. Optional: decide Smasher's fate. Then the Mikoshi access point, Changes.

- Missables [W] [G8]:
  - **Caretaker's Spade**: jungle area, near the conference table, against a tree.
  - **HJKE-11 Yukimura** smart pistol: on the commanding officer's table (G8 only).
  - **Judgement tarot card**: on a wall of the Smasher room.
  - **Access Token**: from Smasher.
- Reward: 501 XP [G8].

**Changes (as Johnny)**

- "Goin' with Alt, you live on", then cross the bridge: Path of Glory (**The Sun**).
- Enter the well instead: New Dawn Fades (**Temperance**) [PP:endings] [W:Endings].
- (CONFLICT/error) Game8's Knockin' choice table labels "Cross the Bridge" as the Star ending path. The wiki and PowerPyx are clear that on the Rogue path, crossing the bridge as Johnny gives The Sun. Treat Game8's table as wrong [G8:Knockin] vs [W:Endings] [PP:endings].

**Path of Glory** [W]

- Wake in the penthouse. The romance called on the rooftop is there; Panam is already in the shower.
- Outcomes are fixed by dialogue: Kerry and River wait for V; Panam is ambiguous but hopeful; Judy ends the relationship.
- Johnny's Samurai jacket hangs on the wall. The AV pilot is Delamain, depending on Don't Lose Your Mind.
- At the Afterlife: Claire's special drink (optional), then meet Mr. Blue Eyes in Rogue's old booth about a Crystal Palace job.
- Spaceship airlock finale: the Sun tarot. "A few months" have passed [W notes].

**Rules of the run** [W:(Don't Fear) The Reaper]

- **No manual saves.** Autosaves happen at the mission start, after descending to the mainframe level, and right before Smasher.
- V's max Health drops 25% at the start, then 1% more at intervals ("Relic Malfunction Detected").
- Dying rolls the credits with the suicide-ending messages. There is no "load last save" prompt, but you can resume from those autosaves [W]. PowerPyx says "one try only" but you can reload the balcony save [PP:endings].

**Sequence** [W]

1. Front entrance: 2 Samurai and 3 Special Agents at max level, then 2 mechs.
2. Loot the access token from the lobby spawns, then hold the lobby until the lift comes. Juggernauts spawn.
3. Board immediately and choose the Netrunner floor. A mech spawns before the doors close.
4. Control room and mainframe-room fights. Expose the mainframe and connect Alt.
5. Smasher, who calls in 6 Snipers on the balcony and 3 Juggernauts [W].

- Cross the bridge as V: **Temperance** [W:Endings] [W:Changes objectives].
- PowerPyx and Game8 word this as the well leading to Temperance, which is reversed. (CONFLICT, wording) PowerPyx: "enter the Well to get Temperance or cross the bridge to get The Sun" [PP:endings]. The wiki says Reaper + well = The Sun and Reaper + bridge = Temperance [W:Endings table] [W:(Don't Fear) The Reaper objectives "Enter the well to return your body"]. Wiki logic is consistent: the well returns your body. Use the wiki.
- Practise the tower layout on the Rogue path first [G8:314736].
- Rewards: Access Token, Judgement card, Gorilla Arms, Militech Berserk [W:Endings].
- Achievements: none of its own; you can earn The Sun or Temperance [PP:endings].

### 2.8 Boss tactics, Act 3

**Adam Smasher** (Totalimmortal, Belly of the Beast, Knockin' on Heaven's Door, Reaper)

- Since 2.1 he can Sandevistan and adapts to your build [G8:Patch-Notes-2-1].
- Three phases [G8:Totalimmortal]: close combat (melee plus short-range projectiles), then long range (his right half is destroyed and he keeps his distance), then a weakened state.
- Weak point: right side of his chest, behind armour plating you can shoot off [G8:Totalimmortal].
- Shoot off his right arm (on your left as you face him). It deals big damage and ends his melee. Then shoot off the back rocket launcher and he has little left [PP:boss] (pre-2.0, verify).
- Electrical quickhacks work best. EMP briefly disables most of his attacks [G8:Totalimmortal]. Game8 also lists Cyberware Malfunction, Short Circuit and Overheat (from the search summary).
- At about 50% HP: an Arasaka ninja wave on the Hanako path (Totalimmortal) [G8:Totalimmortal], or snipers on the upper level (Rogue path) [G8:Knockin]. Ninjas dodge bullets, so 1-2 frag grenades work [PP:boss].

### 3.9 Boss tactics, PL

- **Militech Chimera** (Spider and the Fly) [PP:spider] [G8:427838] [W:Chimera]
  - Shoot the glowing yellow weak points; scan to find any left (there is an optional "destroy all").
  - Jump over the sweeping front laser.
  - At about 70% repair drones appear; kill them (optional objective) or it heals.
  - At about 40% it floods the floor with toxic gas; Myers warns you, so get to high ground.
  - Dodge missile barrages. Rotate cover, because it destroys cover [G8].
  - At 2% it stops; jump on it and "Finish it off".
  - Lore: Arasaka engineers called it vulnerable to attacks from above [W:Chimera].
